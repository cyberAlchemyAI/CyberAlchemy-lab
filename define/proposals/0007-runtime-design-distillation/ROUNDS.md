## Final reconciliation

- **Accept:** smaller pure unit, three responsibilities, concrete adapter, classified inputs, separate later-use observation, exact retained closure and explicit attributable handling.
- **Revise:** premature module/rewrite prescription, staged-review ordering and acceptance labels. Preserve C06; bytes to be signed are not a signing grant.
- **Reject:** any interpretation that new workspace staging alone closes review, snapshot output proves live integration, or a root digest proves reconstruction.
- **Defer:** final file/language layout of compatible pure transformations and general multi-backend abstractions.
- **Route:** actual provisioning/review policy and owner/profile standing; retention resolver/cold-read proof; existing host/representation/consumer qualification.

### Stable disagreement resolved at different levels

Proposer favors a first user-visible integrated episode; Balancer preserves a fixture-backed core as the first implementation step. Both fit: do not postpone useful core work, and do not label it successful live integration. No additional round is warranted to rename this distinction.

## Closure and cycle guard

The pure unit has one responsibility, named explicit inputs, a frozen output or truthful diagnostics, and no dependency on imagined authority. Splitting it into independent disposition/parameter/assessment services would add coordination without preserving a complete user outcome.

Full recomposition has named pending joins, so its verdict stays FLAG. Stop because the two-round budget is reached and further reduction would either repeat the same split or remove provenance/meaning fidelity. The next route is design revision at the named joins, not another unbounded optimization loop.
