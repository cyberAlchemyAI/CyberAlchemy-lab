# Step 2 — CyberAlchemy Define

Candidate `cal.definition.cyberalchemy-define@1.0.0-candidate.3` — authored for meaning review; admission pending.

## Revised governing meaning

CyberAlchemy Define is the Lab capability to make the meaning required for one bounded Goal and its originating Intent explicit for a named consumer and intended use. It examines selected existing meanings and their source standing, identifies the declared required concepts, exposes ambiguous or missing meaning and ownership gaps, and prepares a reviewable Definition Pack with exactly one current disposition—reuse, specialize, propose or open—for each declared required concept. The result preserves the originating Intent, explicit Goal and concept-set revisions, exact source bindings and unresolved obligations. It establishes coverage of the declared concept set, not exhaustive discovery of every relevant concept. A recorded open question may be part of a conforming result; an interrupted or failed attempt does not by itself produce one. Define does not select the consumer solution, appoint an owner, admit a Definition, achieve the Goal or grant execution authority. Its Product Capability instance separately binds exactly one Product, scope, owner and outcome contract; a Method specifies the governed transformation, and CLI or agent-based implementations may realize the capability.

## What changes from the planning draft

The capability describes an enabled outcome. Its Product Capability instance binds one Product and accountable owner. A Method defines the transformation; an Implementation supplies the technical realization; a Definition Pack carries the result. Each remains separately reviewable.

The formal expression now specifies a conforming result rather than promising that every attempt yields a Pack. An open question is valid recorded content. A failure without a conforming Pack is an unsuccessful attempt. Concept coverage is limited to the explicit required set and its attributable revisions.

## The same incident, from request to result

For INC-1042, an agent needs to investigate missing rows using the saved Northstar export, `status=active`, snapshot S-1042 and output E-1042. Its Goal belongs to one illustrative investigation Project. None of these fixture labels creates a canonical Project, Goal, owner assignment or production observation.

1. Preserve the request and scope: investigate omissions from saved evidence.
2. Inspect the selected admitted meanings and their exact versions.
3. Separate identifier coverage from account/filter exclusivity.
4. Prepare one handling disposition for each required concept.
5. Give the human reviewer the exact references, proposals and unresolved obligations.

| Required concept | Handling | What the reviewer receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incident | Reuse | Exact admitted Incident meaning; no incident closure effect. |
| Investigation Work | Specialize | Work applied to this investigation through explicit parameters; its normative meaning is unchanged. |
| Export identifier completeness | Propose | Candidate criterion: every required ID for the same account, filters and snapshot appears in the saved output. |
| Account exclusivity | Open | Whether the output must contain only matching Northstar records; missing criterion and owner route stay visible. |

With 12,000 required IDs and 11,800 matched required IDs in the synthetic fixture, 200 are missing. Under the proposed criterion, coverage would fail. This illustrates the meaning; it is not an executed investigation, accepted Finding or selected repair. The exclusivity question remains independent.

The reviewer can discuss the Pack despite that gap. A complete export-contract claim remains blocked. Production repair, candidate admission and Goal achievement require their own applicable routes.
