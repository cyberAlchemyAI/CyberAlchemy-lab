## Blocking finding R01 — wall-clock currentness after cold-open

The original `cold.validate_period` checks exact period fields and identity binding but not `now`; `SelectedReader.guard()` and `SignedSources.guard()` recheck bytes/freshness, not the episode clock. The design requires only pre-dispatch period currentness. A Reader that begins before 2026-10-21 08:20 UTC and finishes after that instant could publish PASS for a no-longer-current episode. Require an independently selected clock guard immediately before Reader import/open, after the `open_signed` context closes, and immediately before terminal PASS. Test expiry during the yielded Reader work and at postclose/commit, including `-O`; retain the consumed attempt as UNKNOWN or failure without renewing the Source identity.

## Blocking finding R02 — ambiguous module-name projection

Historical Reader `base.py` hardcodes both `PRODUCER_ROOT='legacy-session-fixture-source-v1-action-0148'` and `NAME='_cal_legacy_session_fixture_producer_0257'`. The design says the path literal is the only behavior change but also requires a unique imported package name. These must be reconciled as an exact, counted projection or an explicit unchanged-alias decision. Because `base.py` writes `sys.modules[NAME]` and then imports `NAME+'.base'`, a stale preloaded submodule under the reused alias can bypass the fresh Source module origin unless the namespace is unique and checked. Pin the new alias and its submodule origins, or reject/clear preloaded collisions before import; demonstrate the negative with a preloaded `NAME.base` and no unverified reread. An AST/preimage inverse must account for every changed literal, not just the producer path.

## Implementation boundary to make explicit

The phrase “same repository-shaped staged root” must not authorize writing new Reader files into the **consumed** Source attempt. Select a new Reader attempt with exact Source inputs/package copied under explicit private scope, or mount the retained Source tree read-only with an independently selected read-only Reader-role overlay. In either case the Source package bytes and original attempt evidence remain unchanged, the new private archive is not baked into the image, and only a fresh private Reader receipt directory plus bounded tmpfs are writable. Test wrong path+digest pairs, extra/nonregular entries and Source/package mutation independently. Specify the 180-second or other finite Reader deadline and complete no-retry daemon cleanup before effect, rather than inheriting Source controller 0006 by name alone.

After R01/R02 are fixed and the new stage/controller/selection are frozen, the required negative suite is otherwise well scoped: independent wrong Source/package/Reader pins, trusted-selector substitution, module-origin swaps, archive privacy, timeout/transport/signal/cleanup faults, and causal missing-carrier reporting. A future observed PASS can establish only that this disposable Source package was cold-opened and consumed by a fresh exact-pinned Reader, producing a bounded private diagnostic receipt. It cannot complete the original runtime qualification or 36-task ledger.
