# Inactive historical Source builder probe

This candidate executes the original Source `build.main` in a disposable
Bubblewrap namespace. It is a fixture exercise of one historical constructor,
not an installed T35 effect capability or a protected Source8 qualification.
The run at source SHA-256 `bcefa9f936ed6f6621f6eba3ce5c9ffef56e69db6088852785e0caecf8f338ff`
completed with the bounded result in `source-probe-result-0002.json`. The
earlier 180-second timeout is preserved separately in
`source-probe-attempt-0001.json`; it was not promoted to PASS.

The harness held and hashed 1,703 exact repository-relative files selected by
the historical Source/current-package manifests and their engine, admission,
schema, correction, history and mapping dependency bindings. It copied those
bytes into a sparse snapshot mounted read-only at `/work/snapshot`. The input
selection digest is
`fc14342a83f49047288fc3201a9322e6abf0adbcb2f884b0865f6930adb2eb01`.
It rendered 27 roles and three stage controls under a disposable external
family, then selected a separate effect-capable Source variant. The production
candidate's 27 generated roles remain effect-sealed. The Source manifest is
another preexisting read-only leaf; the only writable family mount in the
namespace is `source/packages` and `/tmp` is private. The builder's signer and
owned store live in that private `/tmp` and are absent at completion.

The successful process returned the historical Source PASS shape, 23 original
targets and 21 records. The harness checked the package's exact 36 member
names and digests, its five core bindings, private visibility, no public

The namespace binds the selected Bubblewrap binary (SHA-256
`523da3e7399044be5163aee6f57a77a6bef7454376e28f0a0627920bae1b76b6`),

not cryptographically pin their full bytes. The scratch controller and bridge

The package bytes are intentionally ephemeral and depend on a fresh fixture
signer and time. The receipt binds its observed package manifest SHA but does
not claim an independently replayable protected-family package. Reader,
mapper and Context historical builders remain unexecuted in this slice.
Formal LC-U02-05 acceptance, host owner/exclusion, protected issuers and
grants, runner selection of newer cases, and activation remain false.
