successor impact, enforce the original schema/core and permission checks, verify

declared signed policy. It must journal exact candidate outcomes, prevent duplicate
effects and expose pending/indeterminate keys without redispatch. Full original
positive/negative/retry/recovery, scope, budget, expiry and tamper cases are still
required. These unit cases do not satisfy those public obligations.

this preparation. Candidate readiness leaves unregistered connections unwritable.
