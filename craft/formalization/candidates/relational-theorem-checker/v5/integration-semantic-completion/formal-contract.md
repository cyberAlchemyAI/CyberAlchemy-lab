N00: shared formal contract

This model is the first implementation step of the successor plan. Its claims
concern exact typed values and explicitly supplied trusted context. It establishes
no new inherited semantic discharge and no authenticated owner identity.

An artifact identity contains owner namespace, artifact ID and revision. Its
reference also contains a content digest. A frame fixes profile, subject, target,
context, scope, snapshot, epoch and time domain. Equality never drops a component.
Catalog non-equivocation requires two entries with one revision key to be the
same full definition, including body, raw content and review reference. Duplicate
identical rows are harmless in this model; the later closed schema can forbid
them. Resolution requires exact reference equality and membership in a separate
trusted approval set. A proposal cannot populate that set through a boolean.

The catalog extension theorem proves preservation across append-only,
non-equivocating extensions. Replacing a selected revision's body is not a legal
extension. Historical proofs remain historical; a changed source is a new target
requiring new qualification. These theorems do not prove a hashing algorithm or
that arbitrary bytes decode to the stored body.

An expression has an independent positive/negative rail meaning. Evaluation uses
the inherited three-valued truth tables. The agreement theorem proves that only
an exact resolved approved expression with a positive rail can activate a
potential norm origin. Unknown cannot activate it. This models activation, not
authorization, opposing-norm resolution or the truth of external leaf facts.

A requirement fixes a nonempty, duplicate-free list of exact criterion
references. Successful fulfillment requires the submitted list to equal this
owned list and every criterion to have exact positive support in the same frame.
Order is part of this first contract: clients use the owner's canonical order.
A future order-insensitive contract must be separately defined and proved. The
positive fulfillment theorem is independent of the boolean checking algorithm;
it quantifies over every owned criterion. Negative/unknown verdict semantics,
issuer policy and action/output provenance are later N06/N08 requirements.

Required obligations are independently supplied by the owned policy context.
Submitted obligations must be exactly that nonempty set, without duplicate
identities. Verified evidence must match the complete challenge, accepted method
and current snapshot. A challenge includes exact definitions, frame,
dependencies, statement and premises. Caller assertion and retirement methods
cannot discharge it. A claimed eligibility flag cannot change qualification.
Semantic truth follows only under the separately stated verifier soundness
premise; the coverage algorithm does not itself establish that premise.

The explicit Review → Ready → Review inductive example has no derivation with
an empty seed set. This is a theorem about these two rules. General relation
closure and lifecycle invariant preservation require later relation proofs.

The formal source inventory lists all 263 original pending IDs and proves its
count. Exact string uniqueness and source partition are checked by the bound
source audit; no dedicated string-uniqueness Lean theorem is claimed.
FullInherited requires exactly their identities. Merely
listing them does not discharge their meanings. Twelve synthetic witness
theorems demonstrate satisfiable positive cases and exact replacement,
omission, frame and empty-evidence refusals. Fixture review references are
synthetic data, not actual approval evidence.

Acceptance requires fresh compilation and separate replay with the pinned
Lean 4.33.1 compiler, inspection of every new theorem's axioms, and current source
bindings. Allowed foundational axioms are propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice;
sorryAx and arbitrary custom axioms are rejected. Independent checking of these
closures is still a successor N11 requirement.
