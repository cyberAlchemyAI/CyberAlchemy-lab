**Notation.** `W` is an independently supplied semantic world, `e` an exact candidate edge, `s`/`t` its endpoint revisions, `q` its raw qualifiers, `f` the pinned frame and `w` an independent witness record. An exact reference contains kind, identity, revision and a content commitment. Equality never follows from spelling similarity. `∃` means there is a witness; `∧` means all conditions must hold; `∨` means one stated constructor; `→` states a conditional requirement; `∈` means membership in an independently supplied set.

**Common meaning.** Each relation requires one declared ordered pair, exact endpoint and witness joins, a closed qualifier object with unique typed interpretation, matching frame and provenance appropriate to its claim tier. Recording, as-of checking, actual local execution and observed external effects remain distinct claim tiers. A raw nonempty string or supplied Boolean cannot establish those stronger facts.

**Unknown and conflict.** Missing/ambiguous inputs yield unknown. Conflicting admissible evidence is reported as conflict. A failed decoder is a refusal to establish the meaning, not a proof that an external fact is false. The checker cannot quietly strengthen a historical observation into live currentness.

**Optional links and graph inference.** Defining a relation does not require every eligible endpoint to have that relation. In particular an inquiry needs no Improvement Target. Inverse, transitive or authority-propagating edges require explicit reviewed rules. Recursive positive rules have least-fixed-point semantics: `Review → Ready → Review` preserves an existing seed but produces nothing from an empty seed.

## Shared policy choices

These are concrete proposals for review, rather than defaults that have been accepted on your behalf.

### D01 — Exact ownership and scope

Resolve role-bearing tokens through immutable owner-reviewed catalogs; scope inclusion uses the owned relation and preserves all ancestor restrictions. Unresolved provenance remains unknown.

**Consequence:** Neither a role label nor a scope token authenticates a grant or owner.

**Alternative:** Provide a different explicit owner resolution/inclusion policy before its affected definitions can be accepted.

### D02 — Periods and time domains

Support both exact epoch intervals and owner-resolved session windows. Finite intervals use start <= epoch < stop; a session window uses the exact session's independently observed active period in one time domain.

**Consequence:** Legacy session-valued effective_period strings are not parsed as dates or silently rejected as a narrower finished profile.

**Alternative:** Choose interval-only meaning and supply an exact lawful session-to-interval mapping for every session-period source; omitted session cases remain unfinished.

### D03 — Conflicting norms and instruction precedence

Retain all independently active norm origins. Opposing active requirements produce an explicit conflict state; no permission/fulfillment is issued until an exact reviewed resolution policy applies. Lesson precedence resolves an owned policy with override evidence.

**Consequence:** The AST's positive rail activates a norm but cannot select a winner or grant authority.

**Alternative:** Specify and review an explicit priority/override policy, then prove its ordering and scope before using it.

### D04 — Roles and operation postures

Use relation-specific owned code catalogs and endpoint-pair interpretations. Preserve proposed, attempted, completed, rejected, failed, applied and deliberately-not-used as distinct postures; claim actual observation/use/production only with its exact witness.

**Consequence:** Identical code spelling in two kinds does not identify the same role or outcome.

**Alternative:** Supply different explicit role/posture domains and source mappings for affected kinds.

### D05 — Evidence aggregation and negative verdicts

Preserve the legacy any-exact-accepted-positive-support profile unchanged. Use a separately versioned successor fulfillment policy: an admissible exact negative assessment refutes its criterion; negative evidence from an unaccepted action/output is inadmissible; opposing admissible positive and negative assessments produce conflict; missing admissible support remains unknown.

**Consequence:** Conflict is a diagnostic state outside the yes/no/unknown truth value; it is never permission.

**Alternative:** Choose a separately versioned quorum, issuer hierarchy or alternative negative-admissibility policy, then prove it without relabeling existing any-support evidence.

### D06 — Criterion completeness and Context Request fulfillment

Require the exact owned complete criterion set. Context Request criteria cover selectors, exclusions, freshness at resolution, budget and output constraints; intended fulfillment without completed support cannot count as fulfilled. Canonical criterion order is retained in the first successor contract.

**Consequence:** The generic E03 model needs an explicit Context Request/View source bridge.

**Alternative:** Propose an explicit different interpretation of fulfillment or order policy for review; a smaller caller-selected set cannot close the original completeness claim.

### D07 — Provenance and historical/live scope

Label each claim recorded, checked as-of, locally executed or externally observed. Exact receipts and source adapters establish their named provenance. Historical fulfillment/conformance does not establish later live currentness.

**Consequence:** Fake fixture approval, observed bytes or a success label cannot become actual owner or execution evidence.

**Alternative:** Provide the stronger authenticated source and clock model required for a chosen live/external claim.

### D08 — Forbidden inference and protected-state boundaries

Review every kind-scoped conclusion as a distinct typed proposition. Prove non-entailment with a valid antecedent and absent/false conclusion. Also prove full-rule closure/state preservation for authority, identity and ownership boundaries where required. Materialization consumes prior authorization; supersession additionally requires supersedes and qualified replacement.

**Consequence:** A valid relation can coexist with an independently established conclusion; exclusions do not mean the conclusion is always false.

**Alternative:** Identify a different exact intended conclusion or legitimate extra inference rule; re-formalize its affected theory and preservation obligations before acceptance.

## Seven extension definitions and their bridges

### E00 — Condition expressions

Conditions use yes/no/unknown with strong Kleene conjunction, disjunction and negation. Yes activates a norm, no deactivates it, unknown retains a potential obligation. Conflict is a separate diagnostic state. No activation result supplies an authorization root.

**Relation bridges:** K00, K21, K23.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Define exact AST grammar, typed environment lookup, truth tables, total evaluation and norm-origin preservation; prove source/environment refinement.

### E01 — Multiple evidence references

Resolve exact bounded evidence references under the chosen profile. Duplicate normalized references count once under the proposed successor set policy. For a criterion, admissible positive only gives yes, negative only no, neither unknown, and both conflict. Unaccepted action/output evidence is inadmissible. Legacy any-positive F0 remains separately versioned.

**Relation bridges:** K03, K10, K18, K20, K40.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Freeze reference normalization, limits and policy selection; prove criterion verdicts, duplicate invariance, exact joins and legacy compatibility.

### E02 — Redelegation

A child use is permitted only within the intersection of all complete independently established ancestor envelopes, gates, windows and unrevoked observations in one domain. Cyclic ancestry is invalid. Missing ancestry is unknown. Child declarations cannot enlarge the root grant.

**Relation bridges:** K00, K35.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Specify chain derivation and completeness, lawful scope inclusion, time intersections, cycle diagnostics and authentic grant/revocation providers; prove no privilege amplification.

### E03 — Fulfillment

The owned exact requirement derivation, rather than a caller-supplied criterion list, determines the complete set. All active mandatory criteria must be yes; potential or conflicting mandatory criteria prevent completion. An empty set is allowed only if the owned derivation yields it. Request fulfillment checks selectors, exclusions, freshness at resolution, budget and output constraints.

**Relation bridges:** K10, K20, K23, K33.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Build pair-specific criterion derivations and an explicit request/E03 bridge; prove completeness, selection and evidence admissibility under the pinned policy.

### E04 — Lifecycle

Every historical relation keeps its original exact witness and frame. Ending, replacement and expiration affect live eligibility through exact qualified transitions; they never rewrite prior history. Continues does not replace. Norm origins and conflicts survive projection. Recursive positive rules use least-fixed-point closure, so an unseeded cycle derives no starting fact.

**Relation bridges:** K06, K21, K23, K26, K36.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Define historical/live projections, ending targets and revision applicability; prove monotonic positive closure and preservation, then specify any nonmonotone live-state selection separately.

### E05 — Checked IR

A typed intermediate representation binds the exact selected definition/policy revision, all references, source digests, normalized qualifier values, frame, criterion derivations and evidence. It contains no executable effect authority. Bounded verification rejects unsupported variants and stale commitments; a well-typed IR is not proof of raw source authenticity.

**Relation bridges:** K08, K10, K14, K20, K25.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Specify closed IR grammar and resource limits; prove decode/type/source interpretation bridges and replay correspondence for all implemented constructors.

### E06 — Independent checker

An independently implemented checker validates the supported proof/certificate language against the exact theory, sources and resource profile. Same-toolchain replay is reproduction, not independent verification. Capability coverage is explicit; unsupported proof constructs remain unsupported, never implicitly accepted.

**Relation bridges:** K00, K04, K10, K20, K25, K40.

**Implementation/proof work still required:** Finish N11A substantial core/semantic sample and N11B required coverage; document separate implementation trust assumptions and replay disagreements.

## Findings and limits to consider

1. **Raw strings are not the proposed semantic values.** Original qualifier contracts often require only nonempty strings. These definitions require versioned owned reference/code maps. The exact token-to-role implementation and authenticity boundary remain unfinished.
2. **The endpoint matrix is complete, its proof is not.** All 550 declared pairs are listed, including 100 challenge targets and 100 same-kind succession pairs. Pair-specific source mappings and source-refinement proofs still need implementation.
3. **No full source countermodel is claimed.** The 148 forbidden conclusions have independent definitions and exact inherited IDs. Their full-theory non-entailment targets are specified; existing local synthetic models do not prove every composed source boundary.
4. **Lesson-use draft and proposed time policy differ.** K04 synthetic code uses an inclusive end; D02 proposes a half-open end. Existing application/lesson fixture identifiers also disagree. Resolve these through a new versioned adapter and model; retain the old proof scope.
5. **A proposition inside an artifact needs a selector.** K18 may target a finding, hypothesis or assessment containing multiple claims. An owned unique proposition selector is required; selecting a target artifact alone is insufficient.
6. **Historical and live state differ.** Assignment, membership, scope and succession records need explicit as-of/live profiles. Recording a supersedes declaration cannot automatically update current state.
7. **Origin, derivation and succession remain independent.** No inferred transformation, identity equality or replacement follows just from lineage. Cycles in positive inference cannot create facts; policy for self/cyclic succession declarations should be decided explicitly during review.
8. **Profile compatibility matters.** K10, K14 and K25 retain their legacy F0 predicate separately as LegacyQualified. Their stronger new Meaning explicitly requires typedSuccessor. Typed scopes, evidence collections, receipts and consequences need explicit profile selection and compatibility proofs; the two claim strengths are not equated.
9. **Local recording is a narrow effect.** Append/idempotence/conflict-refusal postconditions preserve the independent meaning world. Full inference closure, local atomic materialization and external observations still require separate proofs and providers.
10. **Approval of meanings does not complete the full plan.** Owner assessment, satisfiability, exact source refinement, all inherited discharges, implementation, independent checking and composed measurements remain distinct exit criteria.

**Endpoint source schema inventory:** All 109 expected schema locations were found and inventoried; inventorying fields is not proof of their interpretation.
