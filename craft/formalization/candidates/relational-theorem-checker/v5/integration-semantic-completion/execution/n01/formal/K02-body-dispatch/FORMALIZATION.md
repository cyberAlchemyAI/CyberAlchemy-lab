Heterogeneous kind dispatch and source alignment — formalization first

The seven complete body kinds are the Intent endpoint and six permitted subject
kinds of addresses-intent. They are not the complete family of supporting payload
roles. Keep decision, satisfaction-test, excluded subject and other artifact
providers unresolved until their complete contracts are formalized and realized.
Do not label a seven-kind candidate entrypoint the complete original checker.

A selector relates an ordered raw JSON object to one of the seven kinds using
unique object keys and its exact source discriminant. The six kind-bearing
formats use their original kind string; Goal has no kind field and uses its
exact cyberalchemy-lab-goal/v1 schema_version. Duplicate discriminants, unknown
kind strings, missing discriminants and invalid Goal versions refuse. Other
object fields are interpreted only by the complete selected record decoder.

Body meaning combines independent selector evidence with the selected complete
record meaning. No caller-selected kind or schema definition is accepted. This
records exact shape interpretation only; schema admission and source ownership
remain separate premises. Success implies the resulting Body constructor is
exactly the selected kind. Refusal means no output satisfies this meaning.

Catalog alignment requires every complete payload artifact, including unused
records and root approval occurrences, to match its retained sourceKind to its
Body constructor and its revision key artifact token to the complete body identity.
Goal uses goal_id; the other six use node_id. The full record, raw bytes, source
reference and owner review remain retained. Token equality is not proof of actual
identity ownership, authentic admission or an authoritative frame.

Compose the automatic decoder and whole-catalog alignment guard with the retained
entrypoint. Its independent meaning is the original FullMeaning for this decoder
AND alignment of all payload artifact occurrences. It must preserve the complete
original meaning, all byte/digest/nested/resource checks and all eighteen missing
non-parser policy premises. This is a candidate integration component. Complete
supporting-role providers are required before any usable full-scope recommendation.

Required targets: exact source-kind token decoding; exact selector; exact body
dispatch; constructor/kind consistency; refusal characterization; complete
catalog alignment; exact guarded full entry; conditional original interpretation.
Finite original-source controls complement these general proofs. They do not prove
general JSON Schema compilation, Python/Lean correspondence or reconstruction.
No owner acceptance, independent checking or inherited discharge is inferred.
