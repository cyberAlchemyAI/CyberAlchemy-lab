K02 candidate formalization review

The reviewed candidate means: an independently owned Intent Resolution selects
this exact subject to address this exact Intent, in a matching owned addressing
role; an excluded subject cannot establish the relation even when also selected.
This prepares N01. It does not accept the meaning, discharge an inherited ID,
freeze N02/N03 contracts, or promote authority.

The source signature permits six source kinds: Agent Assignment, Goal, Product,
Product Capability, Project and Research Inquiry. All target Intent. Six positive
models and four non-entailment families cover every source branch in the typed
fragment. SourceSignature.lean checks the captured signature against this model.
The captured member-of signature separately allows five relevant source/container
pairs; selection-list membership never manufactures such a graph relation.

Both qualifiers are bound independently: intent_resolution_ref denotes the exact
owned resolution; addressing_role denotes the exact owned role for this source
kind and recorded selection. The raw Intent Resolution carrier has no role mapping;
the typed Selection supplies that mapping as a proposed owned witness. Its capture
and raw-source bridge remain obligations, rather than assumed source fields.

The source audit uses actual JSON Schema probes. It accepts empty tests, no selected
subjects, and selected/excluded overlap; it refuses duplicate tests and a missing
owner decision. A nonempty arbitrary role string also passes raw qualifier shape.
These facts show where semantic interpretation must go beyond shape validation.
Addresses requires selection and absence from the excluded list. The candidate
Satisfied predicate requires all exact tests, a nonempty distinct list, positive
support, and no matching negative support. Empty-test and conflict handling are
proposed successor policies; global D05/D06 assessment is still separate.

Four outcome predicates have independent evidence: satisfaction tests, complete
work requirements, delegated authority, and graph membership. A valid addressing
model lacks every outcome. Other models allow separately evidenced satisfaction
or membership alongside addressing. Thus non-entailment is not universal falsity.

Review caught an existential contract-choice gap in completion. Completed now
requires at least one applicable owned WorkContract and fulfillment of every
applicable contract in the exact subject/frame. Each contract has nonempty distinct
requirements and every requirement uses complete owned criteria through the shared
FulfillmentMeaning. A general theorem refuses completion when an applicable contract
is unfinished even if an easier contract is fulfilled. Completeness of the captured
contract universe and its raw applicability bridge remain explicit trust gaps.
The archived earlier fragment is under repair-attempts/02-completion-contract-choice.

Recording is a pure append of Selection. It preserves test assessments, fulfillment
assessments, graph memberships, and the K00 protected state. It is not the complete
qualified, immutable, idempotent public operation required by the definition.

Verification is fresh pinned Lean compilation plus separate fresh replay. Exact
source/output hashes, declarations, allowed axioms and logs are in verification.json.
This is replay using the same pinned kernel; independent checking remains outstanding.
