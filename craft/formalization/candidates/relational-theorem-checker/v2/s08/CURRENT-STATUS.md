## Worked semantic boundary

`ConformsTo(capability@v1, contract-A@v1, scope-A)` means that an assertion in this graph has an accepted conformance assessment for those exact endpoints, revision and scope. `TargetKind(contract-A@v1, contract)` independently retains its kind. A rule may read both observations and derive an informational `ReviewSignal`. It cannot manufacture either observation or an independent root.

`Conforms(capability@v1)` is an existential summary. It says there is some qualifying target and scope. It does not prove conformance to contract-B, another scope, or another revision. The explanation API names the exact source assertions and assessments and retains multiple supports for one deduplicated observation.

For an inherited norm, source mapping first establishes a binding and contract origin. That supplies a **potential** norm. A matching positive, consistent condition evaluation also supplies an **active** norm. Missing/unknown/false/conflicting selections supply no active norm. Active require/forbid conflicts are separately represented and do not allow arbitrary conclusions. Neither potential nor active norms prove that a requirement has been fulfilled.

## Logical boundary

Every old model expands only under the checked fresh-head, fixed-frame, positive rule hypotheses. It need not equal the least closure and may contain extra ordinary facts. The stronger source-faithful subclass fixes sealed observations exactly to the source; it must be named explicitly. A checked query certificate proves a derivation for that query. A complete closure also checks all valid ground instances and certificates for every retained fact; partial search cannot prove absence.
