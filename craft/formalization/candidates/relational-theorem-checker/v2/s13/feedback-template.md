# Actual human feedback

Review [review-packet.md](review-packet.md). Feedback must come from an actual human response; blank or inferred answers cannot complete S14.

1. **Meaning:** Do the distinctions between exact source conformance, derived information, potential/active norms, and fulfillment match the intended model? Identify any meaning that should change.
2. **Diagnostics:** Does the wrong-scope worked example explain why the fact is absent? Are unknown/conflicting-condition explanations clear? Identify wording or traces that need improvement.
3. **Bounded use:** Given supplied external truth, tested adapters, the local resource envelope, and cooperative shadow-write limits, is this candidate suitable for bounded local shadow use, or should that recommendation remain deferred?

Record the original response verbatim, its scope and date, the reviewed packet hash, requested changes, and the resulting candidate recommendation. This assessment does not complete original v1 R06/G4 unless the response expressly reviews those artifacts.
