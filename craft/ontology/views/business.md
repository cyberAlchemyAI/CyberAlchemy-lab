# DomainSpec Product Ontology — Business View

This is a human projection of `nodes.json` and `relations.json`. The machine records remain authoritative for
package state; product positioning remains in `../PRODUCT-THESIS.md` and term semantics remain in definitions.

- `dsv2.product.checked-system-map` — preserve meaning across structure, realization, observation, and evidence.
- `dsv2.product.typed-domain-graph` — express concepts with DS-D1 and relationships with DS-D8.
- `dsv2.product.exact-realization` — bind one selected implementation form by exact path and digest.
- `dsv2.product.non-inflating-evidence` — never claim beyond exact observed support.
- `dsv2.product.workflow-compensation` — umbrella forward recovery through exact Operations outside Workflow evaluation.
- `dsv2.compensation.intent` — immutable semantic recovery identity and required/satisfied/unresolved state.
- `dsv2.compensation.dispatch` — durable frozen-plan progress and coordination state.
- `dsv2.compensation.attempt` — one succeeded, failed, or unknown execution episode.
- `dsv2.compensation.invocation-identity` — stable replay key with at-most-once semantic application.
- `dsv2.compensation.claim` — exclusive fenced authority to advance one dispatch.
- `dsv2.compensation.claim-lease` — renewable time bound on one Claim.
- `dsv2.compensation.retry` — bounded successor-attempt scheduling under explicit continuation.
- `dsv2.compensation.terminal-disposition` — completed, exhausted, or escalated whole-recovery result.
- `dsv2.integration.dependency-contract` — one responsibility-specific consumer seam, never a universal Port.
- `dsv2.integration.persistence-binding` — one logical persistence concern bound to an exact realization and evidence boundary.
- `dsv2.integration.transaction-guarantee-binding` — one exact local Unit of Work and participant set.
- `dsv2.integration.failure-outcome-binding` — retryable, non-retryable, or unknown integration evidence without continuation authority.
- `dsv2.integration.durable-workflow-runtime-binding` — exact durable-profile composition of persistence, transaction, failure, time, wakeup, replay, restart, and version surfaces.
- `dsv2.language.meta-types` — semantic concept roles and their independent maturity.
- `dsv2.language.relationships` — typed relationship signatures and construction limits.

The business view owns intent and language. It does not pretend that implementation exists without a bridge
edge and system evidence.

The compensation subgraph preserves three non-collapse boundaries: Claim safety is distinct from Lease
liveness; both are distinct from Invocation Identity replay protection; and Attempt outcome is distinct from
Terminal Disposition.

The integration-boundary subgraph is the canonical projection of D45-D49. The infrastructure-binding Tower
and System Integration Revision 2 are provenance, while the explicit owner admission supplies commitment.
Live system evidence now proves provider-neutral contract and binding formation exists in three language
profiles; neither research record nor that formation selects a provider or proves runtime guarantees.
