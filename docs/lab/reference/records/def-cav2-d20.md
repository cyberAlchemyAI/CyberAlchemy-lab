# Craft Method

A governed way of building systems through smaller construction steps whose results can be recombined, while preserving purpose, choices, evidence and unresolved work.

**When it applies**

CyberAlchemy's governed recursive system-building Method from owner-controlled Product intent through meaning, specification, realization, construction, conformance, operation, recovery, evolution, residue, and continuation.

**Record formats**

[Method binding · v1](type-cal-artifact-type-method-binding-v1.md#type-cal-artifact-type-method-binding-v1)

**A stated exclusion**

The Craft Product or Project.

### Formal definition, source explanation and boundaries

The formal statement governs the meaning. The short introduction above helps you read it; it does not replace its requirements.

<a id="def-cav2-d20-section-2"></a>

## Source explanatory wording

Craft Method is how CyberAlchemy builds systems without losing why they exist, how they were selected, what was proved, or what still remains.

<a id="def-cav2-d20-section-3"></a>

## Exact governing statement

Craft Method specializes Method as a recursive construction chain that preserves typed identity, canonical source authority, separate construction and conformance, generated-view derivation, typed residue, child-to-parent recomposition, and owner-controlled closure. Its ledger preserves continuity but is not the Method itself.

````
M_Craft(S) = (context, product_intent, domain_meaning, system_specification, realization_selection, construction, conformance, operation, recovery_and_evolution, owner_continuation, ledger_state).
````

- Every implementation-bearing construction follows CAV2-D65 or carries typed prerequisite residue.
- Construction, conformance, runtime evidence, adoption, and permission remain separate claims.
- Canonical state and generated views remain directionally distinct.
