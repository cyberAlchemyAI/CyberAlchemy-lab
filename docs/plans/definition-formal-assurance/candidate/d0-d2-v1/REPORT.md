## D1: exact semantics and evidence contracts

[contracts.json](contracts.json) defines all six relation classes, their formula shapes, required inhabitants and claim strengths. Legitimate aliases require equivalence, not a difference witness. Specializations require inclusion and subtype inhabitation; properness is demanded only when declared. Overlapping-distinct concepts preserve an intersection. Disjoint concepts must both be inhabited. Independent axes identify specific forbidden inferences and allowed couplings; Cartesian independence is not assumed from an axis label. Unresolved carrier alignment or meanings cannot produce a pass.

Authority and Provenance are compared through a **shared object projection**: whether an object has recorded provenance, and whether it has a scoped owner-qualified authority grant. This does not equate the natural-language concepts, make them nominally disjoint, or compare unrelated types.

The pilot requires a countermodel to `P(x) -> A(x)` and preserves an overlap witness `P(x) and A(x)`. It deliberately does not require `A(x) and not P(x)`. The selected admission-use policy includes the explicit coupling `A(x) -> P(x)`. Thus an extensional specialization in this workflow can coexist with a governance rule forbidding provenance from creating authority. The stronger coupling is a candidate assumption, not a new global canonical implication.

The exact partial translations, source selectors, aliases, omissions, assumptions, bounds and external roots are in [interpretation.json](interpretation.json). Definitions, relation classes and assumptions are not inferred from labels. Required dependencies are fixed by the candidate provider profile; deleting both a dependency and its declaration does not bypass the check.

The preflight recomputes source hashes, compares exact selected registry versions and source entries, checks required translations, and binds reuse inputs. Each case stores its complete input and digest. The evidence bundle binds the interpreter executable hash, version, checker, interpretation, contracts, queue, context and schema. The [schema](evidence.schema.json) constrains record shape; [verify_evidence.py](verify_evidence.py) checks the exact complete case/obligation set, current bytes and reproduced outcomes, then independently replays witnesses.

The full normative dependency closure is **unresolved**. Actor accountability, authority boundaries, real decisions, arbitrary provenance graphs and move permissions are abstract premises or omitted meanings here. The selected three-definition projection has explicit dependency closure; that does not establish the complete closure of all three natural-language definitions.
