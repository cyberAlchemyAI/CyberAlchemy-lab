## Execution policy

Edges are prerequisites, not Lab Relation Kinds. All semantic dependencies are explicit reviewed declarations, with SCC joint compilation. The conservative all-review barrier can later be narrowed only by a sealed SCC-derived successor slice. Conditional provider nodes require actual activation/non-applicability records without deleting required obligations.

Owner units require real attributable decisions on concrete bytes. They are not satisfied by a script, silence or an implementer label. Candidate evidence may be produced before review; semantic readiness may not. H03 is only a future selected admission-owner handoff, with a no-effect branch by default.

No subagents were spawned. Parallel branch opportunities are scheduling information, not delegation permission. No implementation command runs while validating this plan.
