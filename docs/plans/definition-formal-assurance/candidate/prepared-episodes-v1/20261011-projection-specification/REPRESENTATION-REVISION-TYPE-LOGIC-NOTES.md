These are preparatory notes for the next unit. They create no native interpretation, model, correspondence, accepted map, revision instance or authority effect. The embedded local source references select exact Definition and Bounded Context/v2 bytes; their selection is not owner acceptance of a future interpretation.

The source's formal relation has three arguments: an immutable revision record r, exact predecessor representation a0 and candidate postimage representation a1. It requires equal semantic identity, Semantic Epoch and normative meaning, unequal canonical bytes, and exact predecessor/postimage binding. The normative paragraph additionally names a revision identity, representation delta, equivalence evidence, validation, owner selection and rollback. Boundaries require immutable predecessor history, exclude meaning change, and forbid semantic-successor and context lineage for representation-only changes.

A useful type design should separate the revision record from its endpoint representations. Distinct proposed sorts can cover RevisionReference, RepresentationReference, SemanticIdentityReference, SemanticEpochReference, NormativeMeaningReference, CanonicalStateReference, DigestReference, DeltaReference, EquivalenceEvidenceReference, ValidationReference, OwnerSelectionReference and RollbackReference. Scope, artifact-type contract and schema references may need separate sorts when grounded by individual source clauses. Exact signature names and declaration counts must be derived during native authoring; this list is neither a canonical ontology nor an executed signature.

Total endpoint selectors cannot alone certify present, exact or authenticated fields. A selected revision needs explicit field declarations and exact relation bindings for its nominated endpoints, identity and support records. Applicability must be scoped so unrelated artifacts or revisions do not inherit guards silently. The predecessor should remain a distinct retained record with declared historical immutability, and the new canonical record must not be an in-place update of decision-bound bytes. Actual persistent storage guarantees need separate evidence rather than a predicate named immutable.

The next theory should keep canonical-state equality distinct from digest equality. The formal expression requires canonical bytes to differ; the testable conditions additionally require exact and distinct pre/post digests. A declared state-to-digest function and selected digest labels can represent necessary consistency guards. They do not execute a digest algorithm or prove collision freedom. Source review must decide how declared canonical-state tokens correspond to actual byte sequences and how exact digests relate to those sequences. Equal bytes, unequal labels, opaque references or syntactically valid hashes must not be mistaken for actual revision correctness.

Likewise, equal NormativeMeaningReference values are an interpretation of a declaration, not an accepted semantic equivalence proof. The source explicitly requires evidence and owner selection for meaning preservation. The candidate may require nonempty exact evidence/selection records and declared preservation posture. Actual equivalence, source-owner selection authenticity, validation adequacy and rollback behavior should remain separate query targets. A model satisfying the necessary record guards must be allowed to leave those outcomes false unless independently supplied evidence supports them.

The formal iff and the additional normative/boundary requirements need an explicit precedence decision. The future source finding should say whether the classification relation includes all support-record guards or whether those guards constrain revisions classified by the formal relation. A selected record with equal semantic references and changed bytes but absent evidence, validation, owner selection or rollback must not pass a complete declaration model merely because a short formula omits those fields. Conversely, the candidate must expose any interpretation it adds rather than silently making the displayed formula stronger.

The normative list permits changes in schema binding, artifact-type representation contract, canonical field layout or encoding. A future declaration model may represent four change dimensions with an inclusive disjunction. Whether actual byte inequality itself is sufficient to witness one of those dimensions remains an owner-reviewed map. The theory should avoid requiring all four changes, and should not classify human projection refreshes with unchanged canonical input as Representation Revisions. Those are explicitly outside this Definition's applicability.
