# I02: obligations for additions and revisions

I02 derives revision requirements from independently selected baseline and proposal bytes. It regenerates both required sets, types their formulas, classifies actual changes, propagates invalidation and retains the complete requirement history. A declared label is explanatory input. It cannot replace comparison of sources, signatures, statements, premises, couplings, populations and scope.

All 33 final tests passed in 183.339 seconds. All three source-bound worked controls were freshly regenerated and matched their retained outputs. Earlier attempts remain preserved with their original implementation, tests and logs. Actual outcomes are retained in numbered attempts and the unit receipt. This review explains the formalization aspects and the resulting claim ceiling. It supplies no owner fidelity decision, proof of a proposed revision or completion of the full episode.

## Baseline, proposal and effective theory

Each side independently selects an input epoch, theory, relation requests, population policies, endpoint bindings and formal assumption document. I02 reconstructs the epoch, checks that the theory's sources belong to it, reads selected carrier maps and validates the formal assumption context. Baseline inputs can be historical; the proposal epoch must still pass live source/byte checks at the closing boundary.

The proposed theory's change metadata is not authoritative. I02 produces a separate effective candidate theory that binds its prior subject to the actually selected baseline and uses the derived policy class. The original theory file stays unchanged. Prior change-policy requirements remain explicitly required, so changing an additive metadata label to initial cannot erase the earlier conservativity requirement. Declared labels can add review/proof requirements; they cannot shrink the independently regenerated logical denominator.

Logical context includes the actual theory, assumptions, relations, populations, endpoint bindings and epoch. A query may keep the same statement while its context changes. Its history row then records a replaced context or target, rather than unchanged evidence. This distinction is essential for consistency and population queries, whose bare formulas can be identical across very different theories.

## Cross-theory proof contracts

Preservation, conservativity and identity equivalence are represented as explicit theorem descriptors. They bind the selected baseline/proposal theories, effective proposal hash, signatures, contexts, target IDs and any selected embedding data. Their minimum strength is a mechanized theorem. Their status is required, with proof encoding pending an explicit cross-theory contract. The generator does not fabricate a first-order formula for a model-class quantification or assert that a proposed mapping is correct.

Conservativity ranges over the complete old signature, rather than a conveniently selected subset of old targets. The model-expansion property says that every admissible old model can be extended while preserving its reviewed reduct. Baseline consistency and joint populations are required in addition: otherwise that property can hold vacuously when there are no admissible old models. Proposal consistency and populations remain in the regenerated A06 set. The corresponding witness gates cannot replace the universal theorem, and the theorem cannot replace their nonvacuity checks.

Identity claims select their endpoints by side. The same version reference may appear on both sides because two candidate interpretations can revise the same source bytes. The descriptor preserves exact source references and distinct theory/context bindings. It still requires a reviewed interpretation mapping and a theorem. No identity claim is automatically emitted merely because a source or theory was renamed.

## Obsolete requirements and evidence

The ledger includes the union of old and new logical obligation IDs, gate IDs and query metadata IDs. A removed row retains its complete baseline requirement and context, with a pending retirement-review disposition. Changed rows retain both sides. New rows are explicit. Prior evidence is never marked inherited, even when a bare formula is unchanged. Earlier change-policy gates are retained with their original subjects and strengths.

Exact submission comparison regenerates the whole result and rejects omission, weakening, substituted contexts, forged readiness or altered claim denominators. Selected bytes are reread at the end. A changed input, selection file, proposal epoch or active generator/parser/type/policy build returns an explicit unresolved result through the selected-input API. Resource limits also remain execution failures, never negative mathematical results.
