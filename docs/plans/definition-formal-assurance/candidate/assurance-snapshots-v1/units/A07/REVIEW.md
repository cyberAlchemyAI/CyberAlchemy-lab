## What changes from the predecessor

The predecessor omitted the responsibility variant, used nonempty provenance only, generated claims together with active roots, and checked detached posture vectors. It already documented these limits and preserved authority/provenance distinctions in its bounded cases. The successor makes the missing distinctions expressible and binds their changes to actual model records.

Permit grants and responsibility assignments are independent pools. Claims assert positions but cannot materialize either pool. A qualified grant can exist without a separate claim. A complete claim can exist without a grant. A duty assignment can exist without a claim or permission, and rights and duties can coexist with distinct tags. Authority's broader right-or-responsibility position is therefore separated from permission to perform a selected operation.
