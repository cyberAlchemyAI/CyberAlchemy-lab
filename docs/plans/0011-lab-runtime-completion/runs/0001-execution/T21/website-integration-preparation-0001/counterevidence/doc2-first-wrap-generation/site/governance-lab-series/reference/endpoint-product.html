Product

An endpoint is the thing at either end of a connection. This entry identifies a permitted kind and the record-type contract that represents it.

Record format · carrier: Product identity · v2

Connection rules where it may be the source: addresses-intent · member-of · successor-of · uses-method

Connection rules where it may be the target: affects · challenges · contributes-to · evaluates · frames · instance-of · member-of · successor-of

These lists show where to look. Open the connection rule to find its allowed opposite endpoint and required versions and qualifiers. Being listed here does not allow every possible connection.
