## What the Lab organizes

A software company wants customers to export data reliably. Intent records the outcome and constraints before a solution is chosen. Intent Resolution records the owner’s response and success assessment. That response might be product work, a project or a research question. Choosing it gives direction, with delivery still to establish.

The Product is the continuing offering, such as the customer platform. Its Product Thesis explains the audience, problem, value and propositions needing evidence. A Product Capability states an outcome such as reliable export. A service, command or agent workflow could realize it, each with its own identity and lifecycle.

A Capability Realization Binding links specific capability and implementation versions, roles, compatibility, ownership, adoption posture and replacement conditions. Several implementations can participate, and delivery can change while the capability keeps its identity. The binding leaves adoption, installation, activation, conformance and customer benefit to the appropriate decision or evidence.

A Project organizes an effort with purpose, scope, exclusions, goals, responsibility, evidence and lifecycle. Its export-repair Goal might require no omitted identifiers under a selected comparison. Work names a bounded activity and its inputs, participants, result and constraints. That activity can contribute to the Goal, whose conditions and owner-controlled state change still govern achievement.

The platform continues beyond the repair Project. The Project can examine several implementations before any product adoption. Membership, adoption and demonstrated value therefore need separate records. Later work can recover what was investigated, selected and observed.

A Method describes repeatable transformation of inputs into results, with actions, roles, checkpoints and unresolved work. A Craft Method specializes construction into smaller governed tasks and their recombination. Define makes behavior and limits explicit. Design selects an arrangement; Plan describes how work proceeds. The resulting records use the selected contracts.

Implementation identifies a technical realization with sources, interfaces, build or deployment materials and evidence. Workflow orders software Operations and their connections, decisions and recovery. Construction continues through conformance, operation, recovery and later change. Smaller tasks need owners, recombination and stop conditions. Residue keeps meaningful unfinished or unpreserved work visible.

A Coordination Context saves why information is needed and what the work concerns. It has exactly one Product anchor and optionally one Project and one primary Goal. A Project might have several goals while this context selects one. A material change to those anchors or purpose needs a successor context identity. Consumers and source-selection rules belong in the record too.

Sales and billing might both use “customer” while referring to different facts. A Bounded Context establishes consistent local meaning, and a Context Map records directional relationships between particular model versions. Domain-driven design gives a useful account of those coherent meanings and explicit model connections. [Martin Fowler, Bounded Context](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BoundedContext.html)

A Context Profile supplies reusable construction and resolution rules. A Context Request identifies task or consumer needs, selection and freshness. A Context View supplies the fixed result with exact sources, exclusions and expiry. These records keep separate identities, so a refresh leaves the purpose to its own governing change route.

Project State retains records, definitions, connections, decisions, evidence and histories. A Ledger is an ordered collection with entry, integrity, correction and reconciliation rules. Some histories add append-only requirements and event reconstruction. The general Ledger meaning makes no blanket claim that every collection is immutable or every entry true, accepted or authoritative.

The export agent now has a route through the need, capability, remaining project conditions, method, software and evidence. The reference library supplies exact meanings and record requirements. We can follow that route while learning the identifiers along the way.
