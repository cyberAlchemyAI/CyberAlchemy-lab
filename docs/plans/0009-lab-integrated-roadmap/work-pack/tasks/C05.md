## Implementation detail: ordered rules

1. Verify old branch independently and retain it
2. Capture new baseline
3. Classify selected results as already-promoted, eligible, conflicting or excluded
4. Revalidate eligible targets under new current contracts
5. Review exact transition effects and initialize separate identified successor branch
6. Record provenance without copying old signatures or rebasing transactions
