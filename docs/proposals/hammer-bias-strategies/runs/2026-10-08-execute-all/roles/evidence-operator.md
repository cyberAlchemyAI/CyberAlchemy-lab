3. **Missing evidence has ambiguous stop semantics.** S07 is intended to investigate an uncertain cause, yet its experiment step says to stop on missing causal evidence (`inputs/dispatches/s07.dispatch.json#/steps/1/stop_conditions/1`). Interpreting that as “do not choose a winner without support” is coherent. Interpreting it as “do not test while the outcome observation is missing” defeats the route. Execution should distinguish a missing indispensable test input from the observation the test is designed to produce.
4. **Two tempting attacks fail current inspection.** Gate inversion is already repaired: S10's pass condition is affirmative and its failure test is separate. S14's direct exit is preserved both in prose and in `inputs/dispatches/s14.dispatch.json#/applicability_exit`, before s1, skipping both stages. Structural validation explicitly sets `effectiveness_tested` to false. Reporting these as current defects would manufacture a problem from stale expectations.
5. **Construction obligations are countable; recurring utility is not yet measured.** The catalog contains 47 authored steps, but step count is neither measured cost nor decision quality. S11 requests operating effort and ownership as inputs without supplying an operating dataset (`inputs/dispatches/s11.dispatch.json#/strategy/inputs`). A rich route and a minimal causal record therefore remain incomparable in general. Executing all methods now satisfies the user's research request; it does not establish that all methods should become mandatory operational ceremony.

## S07: distinguish structure from causal evidence

The two proposed explanations are:

- **H-structure:** additional method structure independently improves fit. At equal evidence, a structured route should preserve more constraints and choose correctly or defer appropriately more often.
- **H-evidence:** tool-independent discriminating observations are the main contributor. At equal method, adding those observations should improve choice; more structure may tie while adding burden.

Use a crossed controlled design: plain causal card versus structured route, each with capability-only information versus discriminating causal evidence. Keep the problem, model, constraints, answer fields, budget, and tool access fixed. Include an ownership bottleneck, an information bottleneck, a removable obligation, and an ambiguous case for which “inconclusive” is the correct result. Vary order across independent agents where feasible.

Measure feasible choice, constraint preservation, causal fit or correct deferral, unsupported claims, and burden separately. A toy case's stipulated causal rules provide a toy scoring key, not evidence that those rules describe a real organization. Control prompt length, unequal information, label-sensitive rubrics, shared-model correlation, same-agent memory, and author-favored scenarios. Ties, interaction effects, improved accuracy with higher burden, and insufficient discrimination are valid outcomes. This role proposes the experiment; the parent owns actual execution and joining its outputs.

The logical inversion is typed as **removal of presumed necessity**: “Under which evidence conditions can a plain causal record preserve fit with fewer handoffs?” It does not assert that the inverse of “rich methods improve fit” follows logically.

## Attack with the goal of expansion

The observed weaknesses open three useful freedoms: choose evidence states independently of capabilities; derive acceptance checks from the outcome obligations; and allow missing evidence to lead to measurement, deferral, or a constrained reversible action according to what is actually missing. Each can support a repaired rich method, a smaller causal record, or direct inspection. These are different mechanisms only where they change how a decision becomes justified; renaming a template does not count.

Some attacks should end without expansion. Gate inversion and missing direct exits were rejected because current evidence contradicts them. The outcome of criticism can be to preserve a sound design and avoid needless work.
