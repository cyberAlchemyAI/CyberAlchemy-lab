## Round 3 of 3: repair to the smallest coherent unit

**U1 — one bounded intervention decision episode.** Its responsibility is to turn one unchanged problem and a contested intervention claim into a justified next action or an explicit unresolved disposition, while retaining the evidence needed to revisit it.

Inputs: fixed outcome and constraints; incumbent claim; at least one mechanism-distinct alternative where feasible; source-linked observations and uncertainties; decision owner; remaining shared round budget. Output: claim dispositions, surviving mechanism set, the smallest discriminating next test, residue owners, and expiry. Abstraction: workflow/process.

The record contains: (1) problem and constraints; (2) mechanism and causal prediction for each surviving option; (3) attack with provenance, exposed assumption, and any opening; (4) test or reason evidence cannot yet discriminate; (5) solved versus reframed assessment and local bridge verdict; (6) decision consumer, residue owner, expiry, and remaining budget. It retains a no-feasible-opening or tie result as a valid decision state.

**Recomposition:** parent synthesis consumes U1, checks it against the unchanged problem brief and independent Lane A/Balancer outputs, and chooses a reversible research test. The user remains the eventual operational adoption owner. Missing production data is explicitly carried as residue; it is not filled by the workflow. A proposed test needs a named executor and an actual evidence receipt before the disposition can advance from proposed to observed.

**Minimality check:** removing the problem permits goal substitution; removing mechanism permits cosmetic alternatives; removing provenance permits invented counterexamples; removing the test permits preference without discrimination; removing the disposition permits indefinite investigation; removing consumer/residue/expiry permits hidden coordination and stale reuse. Agent count, route names, and detailed chronology can remain external to U1 as execution metadata. Further reduction to “choose the best alternative” loses its decision responsibility.

**Loss ledger:** U1 intentionally loses the full discussion transcript and duplicate rhetoric, with original sources retained by reference. It retains constraints, disagreements, evidence class, failed openings, and next evidence. It does not compress several distinct strategy executions into one completion claim. Evolution: add a concern only when a failed check names a missing responsibility; remove a field only after recomposition remains possible. Expiry: changed outcome, changed constraint, invalidated evidence, new mechanism, or reached round budget.
