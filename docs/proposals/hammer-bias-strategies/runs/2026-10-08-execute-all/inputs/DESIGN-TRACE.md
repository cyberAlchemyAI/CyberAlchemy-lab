# Deep design trace

Scope: create at least ten different strategies for avoiding tool-driven problem framing. Final artifact: fourteen candidate strategy cards, fourteen descriptive dispatch drafts, a structured catalog, source fingerprints, and structural validation results. No concrete user problem was investigated, no strategy was executed, and no reusable spell or canonical discipline was promoted.

The user selected Deep mode. Budget: two tracks, three finite rounds for the whole catalog, followed by reconciliation. Track 1 covers reasoning and reframing; Track 2 covers evidence and workflow. This is a portfolio design, not a tournament selecting one universally best strategy.

## Discovery baseline

The user supplied the central concern: producing hammers encourages interpreting every problem as a nail. They requested robot-talks, two-lane disciplines, distillation, and dispatch-spec from the sibling repository. No actual incident, success measure, operating constraints, or evidence set was supplied. Those omissions do not block strategy design; they do prevent claims about which strategy solves a real situation.

The source search corrected an initial provisional interpretation of two lanes as discovery/delivery. The local active-pattern discipline means Lane Z builds a hypothesis and attacks a real counterexample, while Lane A proposes a genuinely different mechanism against the same solution-independent problem. The candidate human/machine representation discipline is separate. Construction/operational support is also a separate classification.

## Proposer report and three rounds

One read-only native helper acted as Proposer. It received a conceptual brief and did not inspect or modify repository files. Its first final report supplied twelve mechanisms and the first two rounds; a follow-up completed Deep-mode correction and the third round. Both reports were received before final reconciliation.

1. Generation: problem-first framing, incumbent/challenger contrast, outcome/mechanism zigzag, intervention families, hammer-free toy case, construction/support contrast, reversibility, causal testing, benefit/burden comparison, recomposition, exit menus, and contrasting-case transfer.
2. Reduction: reduce each proposal to a bounded decision operation with named inputs, outputs, owner, assumptions, and a recomposition target. Reject a bare slogan as too small to retain decision behavior.
3. Correction: enforce exact Lane Z/A meanings, preserve the underlying outcome, require counterexample provenance, distinguish solved from reframed claims, and keep bridge decisions local. Add concern-frame interrogation and claim-to-evidence checks; record a shared premortem and expiry.

Proposer claim: independently assigned opposition can make tool fit inspectable. Assumption: an underlying outcome can be stated independently of the incumbent. Proposer caveat: invented examples are test scenarios, not real counterexamples. Its design verdict was pass with actual-run evidence and feasibility flags.

Parent reconciliation retained fourteen tactics rather than copying every proposed item as a separate route. Contrast-case transfer became a falsification/expiry check across tactics; claim-to-evidence became a common synthesis contract. Separate final cards expose compression loss, human/machine representation loss, and the applicability of the investigation method itself. This avoids inflating common checks into more strategies while preserving the requested diversity.

## Distill result

- Target context: a reusable catalog for selecting anti-bias investigations before committing to an intervention.
- Mode/budget: Deep; two tracks; three design rounds completed; one reconciliation; true native Proposer and Balancer helpers used sequentially.
- Verdict: pass for bounded catalog design; flag for empirical effectiveness and selected-run readiness.
- Current smallest coherent unit: one strategy decision card with applicability, one responsibility, named inputs/output, a discriminating check, recomposition, residue, and expiry.
- Optimization point: smaller than an entire universal investigation workflow, larger than a technique label that hides how to decide.
- Concept map: purpose (outcome-directed action) → capability (inspect intervention fit) → workflow (one strategy) → artifact (decision card and descriptive dispatch).
- Recomposition: a named decision owner connects a card’s result to the unchanged outcome, constraints, evidence limitations, and residue. Exact consumer and interfaces must be bound for a real run; missing glue remains flagged.
- Evolution profile: new task families may need different evidence and alternative mechanisms. Preserve the card and route interface; add a strategy only for a named uncovered failure mode.
- Deferred complexity: executable orchestrator, capability-bound wave contracts, runtime registration, canonical vocabulary promotion, automated semantic equivalence, and outcome benchmarks.
- Premortem: the portfolio becomes mandatory ceremony; two options retain the same mechanism; metrics reward making tools; attractive synthesis passes shape validation and is mistaken for causal evidence. Guards: direct exit, mechanism-distance test, declared outcome criteria, provenance, and evidence ceilings.
- Frame expiry: changed outcome, constraints, available mechanisms, evidence, or integration obligations trigger reconsideration; each selected run also has a finite three-round budget.
- Navigation: start with S01 if the need is tool-shaped, S02 if an incumbent is favored, S03 for cross-layer contradictions, S05 for rival mechanisms, and S14 before escalating the method.
- Next route: select the minimal suitable card for a concrete problem; use its named capability owners after binding the entry contract. No automatic implementation successor.

## User-requested extension: inversion and attack to expand

The user added logical inversion and destructive-to-creative analysis: attacking with the goal of expansion. The parent added S15 (typed inversion) and S16 (attack-to-opening conversion), bringing the catalog from fourteen to sixteen cards. The historical three-round helper reports above refer to the original fourteen-card design; the extension is a bounded parent-authored revision, not an additional multi-agent run.

Logical inversion generates hypotheses about presumed necessities, roles, or causes while preserving the desired outcome. Formal inference is distinguished from exploratory reversal; converse and inverse are not treated as automatic implications. Attack-to-expand records a critique's assumption, any new degree of freedom, candidate mechanisms, and discriminating evidence. Invalid attacks and hard constraints need no fabricated creative alternative.

The parent also applied a conditional expansion contract to existing Robot Talks and residuality steps. This adds a critique-to-creation output obligation without requiring a new full workflow. Distill follows expansion, candidate count is bounded, and the original shared route-wide critique budget remains unchanged. Local-extension technique references point to ANALYSIS-LENSES.md, with explicit validation expectations. All changed and new dispatch bytes are structurally revalidated; this establishes no empirical effectiveness claim.
