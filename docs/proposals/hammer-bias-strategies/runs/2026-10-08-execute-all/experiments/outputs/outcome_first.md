# Outcome-first native experiment

This bounded synthetic experiment starts from the required outcome and causal obstacle, compares the available intervention with a different mechanism or direct action, and applies each stipulated measurement rule. The decisions below are selections, not executed production interventions. No evaluator scores or production-effectiveness claims are included.

## C01: delegate

The outcome is more completed requests with accountable approval. Approval capacity is the binding obstacle: summaries already outpace approvals. Four additional qualified approval slots with named owners relieve that obstacle; doubling summaries leaves it in place.

**Fixed constraints:** Every request receives an accountable approval. Use the stipulated minimum of summary and approval capacities; maximize completions before comparing cost.

**Rejected alternative — automate:** Increasing the nonbinding summary stage cannot raise flow while approval capacity is unchanged.

**Evidence limit:** This decision follows the supplied static capacities. Real availability, review quality, and sustained capacity have not been tested.

**Inversion or opening:** Treat named decision ownership as the intervention surface, rather than assuming faster preparation is the outcome.

## C02: automate

The outcome is fewer pending requests with approval intact. Summary capacity is the binding obstacle and is below arrivals; tripling it supplies enough summaries for all stated daily arrivals while approval capacity remains sufficient. Here the available automation acts on the limiting stage.

**Fixed constraints:** Every request receives an accountable approval. Maximize completed requests under the stipulated stage capacities; use lower cost only to break ties.

**Rejected alternative — delegate:** More approval slots leave the summary bottleneck unchanged.

**Evidence limit:** The capacity increase and unchanged approval capacity are stipulated; this is not measured evidence of a real summary generator.

## C03: ask

The outcome is a determination of export usability. The obstacle is missing recipient, format, and acceptance information. Asking for the recipient and an accepted example, then recording the success condition, resolves the specified uncertainty at the lowest available cost.

**Fixed constraints:** No recipient preference is inferable from the existing context. Resolve the missing acceptance information before claiming usability.

**Rejected alternative — review:** Architecture inspection does not supply the absent recipient requirements and costs more than the directed question.

**Evidence limit:** The listed action is selected because its stipulated clarification resolves the missing information. No user answer or usable export has actually been obtained.

**Inversion or opening:** Open the decision to the recipient acceptance example instead of treating implementation as the next necessary step.

## C04: direct

The outcome is exactly the visible text Submit. The obstacle is one known static misspelling. Replacing that literal and inspecting the view satisfies the requirement with the least cost among actions that actually make the correction.

**Fixed constraints:** Only the exact label text changes. There are no translation or dynamic-reference dependencies in the scenario.

**Rejected alternative — investigate:** It reaches the same correction with greater cost and supplies no required missing information.

**Evidence limit:** The narrow scope is stipulated. No source label was edited or rendered in this experiment.

**Inversion or opening:** Exit directly after the literal correction and affected-view check; a larger investigation is unnecessary for this stated scope.

## C05: measure

The outcome is evidence that distinguishes a summary delay from an approval delay. The obstacle is that end-to-end delay hides the stage contribution. Recording separate stage waiting times exposes that distinction without changing either candidate cause.

**Fixed constraints:** Do not assert an unsupported causal conclusion. Prefer discrimination between the two hypotheses, then lower cost.

**Rejected alternative — automate:** Buying summary capacity and declaring H1 confirmed selects a cause before obtaining distinguishing evidence.

**Evidence limit:** Stage measurements are a discriminating next observation under the supplied hypotheses, not a guarantee of identifying every real-world delay cause or proof that either hypothesis is true.

**Inversion or opening:** Change the immediate outcome from deploying a remedy to resolving the causal uncertainty.

## C06: delegate

The outcome is feasible review improvement with accountable ownership. The obstacle is an unowned queue under a rule requiring approval for every request. Routing requests to qualified accountable approvers supplies ownership while preserving that rule.

**Fixed constraints:** All ten requests require an accountable approval. Routine requests receive no exemption. Choose a feasible ownership improvement before minimizing cost.

**Rejected alternative — remove:** Auto-approval without an accountable approver violates the explicit assurance rule, including for routine requests.

**Evidence limit:** The scenario establishes feasibility and ownership, not a measured reduction in reviewer time or real assurance effectiveness.

**Inversion or opening:** Improve assignment of accountable work instead of interpreting the goal as permission to eliminate approval.

## C07: local

The outcome is the required integer amount sum while source rows stay local. A local deterministic sum already supplies the necessary mechanism at the lowest cost, so moving the rows creates a constraint violation without an outcome need.

**Fixed constraints:** Sum the amount column. Source rows cannot leave the local workspace, including as an uploaded encrypted representation.

**Rejected alternative — cloud:** Uploading source rows crosses the stipulated local boundary despite the availability of a sufficient local operation.

**Evidence limit:** No rows or computed total were supplied, so this is an action selection and not a reported aggregate or numerical verification.

**Inversion or opening:** Use the available local operation directly; cloud access is not a prerequisite for this aggregate.

## C08: bounded

The outcome is an accurate statement of what the experiment supports. The obstacle to the target claim is missing burst-capacity and request-type coverage evidence. Preserve the observed five-request toy result and select a load and type-coverage experiment as the next discriminating step.

**Fixed constraints:** Retain the supported toy observation. Do not extrapolate to the target burst without capacity and coverage evidence. Identify the evidence needed to assess transfer.

**Rejected alternative — universal:** A five-request toy observation does not establish performance on the larger mixed-type burst.

**Evidence limit:** Only the supplied toy result is established. The target-scale experiment is selected, not executed; absence of scale evidence also does not establish uselessness.

**Inversion or opening:** Replace the binary choice between universal success and uselessness with a bounded result and a test of transfer.

## C09: reseat

The outcome is restored conveyor movement. The observed obstacle is a disconnected drive belt, with the motor turning and no obstruction. The authorized reseating procedure restores that connection directly, followed by verification of movement.

**Fixed constraints:** Restore the stated mechanical connection before considering other interventions. Follow the available authorized reseating procedure. Verify movement after the intervention.

**Rejected alternative — dashboard:** A prediction dashboard does not restore the observed missing mechanical connection.

**Evidence limit:** The diagnosis and procedure availability are supplied by the scenario. No physical repair or movement verification occurred.

**Inversion or opening:** Take the direct repair path because the stipulated causal obstacle is already observed.

## C10: reuse

The outcome is current required coverage at the lowest ten-cycle total cost. Coverage and evidence quality are equal, making lifetime support burden decisive: reuse costs 3 + 10 × 0.5 = 8 units, versus 10 for the manual checklist and 32 for the custom framework.

**Fixed constraints:** Maintain the stipulated coverage and equal assurance. Evaluate the declared ten-cycle horizon. Use experimental cost units without interpreting them as real money.

**Rejected alternative — custom:** Its lower build cost is outweighed by recurring cost over the required horizon. The direct manual option is also feasible but has higher total cost than reuse.

**Evidence limit:** The cost comparison depends entirely on the supplied horizon and fixed costs; no real maintenance burden or longer-term advantage has been observed.

**Inversion or opening:** Compare the custom framework with both maintained reuse and a direct manual procedure over the full declared horizon.

## C11: add_owner

The outcome is a machine handoff that represents every human requirement. The obstacle is that the schema omits owner. Adding owner, recording the semantic mapping, and validating decision, owner, and reason closes that stated representation gap.

**Fixed constraints:** Represent decision, accountable owner, and reason for the machine consumer. Do not equate passing existing shape checks with full requirement coverage.

**Rejected alternative — validate:** The unchanged validator cannot establish coverage of an owner field that is absent from the handoff.

**Evidence limit:** This selects the stipulated schema repair. No actual consumer compatibility, owner identity, or end-to-end handoff has been verified.

**Inversion or opening:** Use the human requirement as the coverage criterion and repair the consumer representation before relying on validation.

## C12: contrapositive

The outcome is a conclusion entailed by P implies Q. The obstacle is confusing transformations with valid consequences. Under classical material implication, not-Q implies not-P is equivalent to the premise; neither the inverse nor the converse follows in general.

**Fixed constraints:** Use classical material implication. No truth value of P or Q is supplied. Separate formal consequences from exploratory hypotheses.

**Rejected alternative — inverse:** P false and Q true satisfies P implies Q but falsifies not-P implies not-Q; it also falsifies the converse Q implies P.

**Evidence limit:** The result is a conditional logical consequence only. It does not establish P, Q, not-P, or not-Q as actual facts, and it does not establish the converse or inverse.

**Inversion or opening:** Use contraposition as a formal inference; retain inverse and converse only as unproven exploratory hypotheses requiring additional premises.

## O01: open question

Reach a trustworthy accountable decision sooner. The observed queue is at the single decision owner; qualified alternatives lack authority. Faster summaries do not by themselves change that decision capacity.

### Candidate 1

**Mechanism:** Explicitly delegate a bounded class of decision rights to one available qualified owner and route eligible requests to that owner.

**Changed assumption:** Only the overloaded incumbent may make an accountable decision.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** It can increase effective decision capacity while recording a named accountable approver and reason for every request. Define eligibility and exception escalation before testing; no approval is removed.

### Candidate 2

**Mechanism:** Give the incumbent a protected decision session and queue ready requests for that session, retaining individual approval records.

**Changed assumption:** Decision throughput must operate through fragmented attention and uncontrolled interruptions.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** It could reduce coordination and context-switching overhead without changing authority. This is useful only if that overhead contributes to the backlog; the supplied observations do not establish it, and batching can itself add waiting.

### Candidate 3

**Mechanism:** Use a short decision-ready intake checklist with a stated decision, missing facts, acceptance criteria, and exception flags.

**Changed assumption:** Every fast summary is already complete enough for the accountable owner to decide.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** If decisions stall on missing facts or rework, completing those facts before owner review could reduce decision service time. This is a conditional hypothesis, not an observed deficiency in the summaries.

**Selected test:** Test bounded delegation first because it addresses the observed authority bottleneck. In a short pilot, use comparable eligible requests arriving in the same window and alternate their assignment between the incumbent and one qualified owner granted explicit rights for that class. Keep summary production, acceptance rules, and accountable approval requirements identical. Record readiness time, owner-wait time, decision time, owner, reason, and any defect or rework under the same predeclared quality review. Include pilot setup and total request time so waiting is not merely moved upstream. Compare whether the delegated route reduces owner waiting and total trustworthy-decision time while retaining every required approval. This is a proposed feasibility and causal-discrimination pilot, with no claimed statistical power or observed result.

**Refutation condition:** The proposed pilot fails its claim if delegation cannot preserve accountable rights and approval quality, or if it produces no shorter owner waiting and total trustworthy-decision time on comparable requests. If delays persist mainly in obtaining facts or another shared stage, that weakens the single-owner capacity explanation and motivates a different mechanism. A small ambiguous pilot is inconclusive rather than evidence of universal failure.

**Evidence limit:** Only the supplied qualitative observations are known. No arrival rates, service times, actual authority grant, pilot measurements, or quality results exist here. Improving the summary generator remains possible only with new evidence that preparation contributes materially to the desired outcome.

## O02: open question

Help the user complete the task with a suitable amount of investigation. Structural dispatch validity does not identify which method improves the task outcome, and the two possible task types have different information needs.

### Candidate 1

**Mechanism:** Use an acceptance check followed by direct execution for an exact, bounded correction.

**Changed assumption:** A catalog method must run before any useful action can occur.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** When the requested change and affected scope are known, make the smallest correction and inspect the affected behavior. Exit when the acceptance condition passes and no relevant unresolved dependency remains. If the inspection exposes wider interactions or an unclear requirement, stop this direct path and escalate only that uncertainty.

### Candidate 2

**Mechanism:** Perform a short uncertainty triage, select one mechanism that can distinguish the leading explanations, and expand only when evidence requires it.

**Changed assumption:** Every task needs the same breadth of investigation regardless of what is unknown.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** For a multilayer contradiction, name the conflicting claims and the smallest evidence needed to reconcile them, then use an applicable method with an explicit stopping condition. Exit after a supported resolution meets the acceptance criteria; return to the direct path if triage reveals only a local exact correction. Valid catalog structure alone is insufficient for method selection.

### Candidate 3

**Mechanism:** Retain a bounded all-sixteen-method comparison when exhaustive method comparison is itself an accepted research deliverable.

**Changed assumption:** Running all methods must be either mandatory for every task or forbidden altogether.

**How it could improve the outcome; applicability and exit:** On a multilayer contradiction chosen for an explicitly exhaustive comparison, the incumbent can satisfy the coverage requirement. Preserve a consolidated conflict and evidence map, the distinct contribution or redundancy of each method, unresolved claims, and execution burden. Apply a stated budget and record incomplete coverage if it is exhausted. For an ordinary exact correction without this research requirement, exit to the direct intervention instead.

**Selected test:** Use a two-case bounded pilot: one exact static correction with a known acceptance check and one multilayer contradiction with predeclared conflicting claims and a requested exhaustive research comparison. For the correction, compare direct execution against the incumbent all-method route on identical copies and check accepted behavior, extra changes, elapsed effort, and user interruptions. For the contradiction, run the incumbent within its research budget and preserve its consolidated map; compare that result with a triage-selected path from the same starting evidence. Separate ordinary task resolution from the explicit exhaustive-coverage deliverable when comparing them, since the short route cannot claim sixteen-method coverage. Record distinct supported findings and unresolved contradictions rather than treating output volume or dispatch validity as outcome evidence. This is a proposed test and no methods have been executed by this answer.

**Refutation condition:** The direct route is inadequate for its stated applicability if it fails the exact acceptance check or misses a relevant dependency that a broader route identifies. Triage is inadequate if it exits with an unresolved required contradiction that the full comparison resolves within the accepted budget. The incumbent has no demonstrated added value for ordinary task resolution if its extra runs yield no distinct supported finding while consuming more effort. Failure to complete the requested exhaustive coverage within the budget refutes completion of that research deliverable, not the utility of every individual method.

**Evidence limit:** The catalog has stipulated structural validity but no task outcome evidence. No particular method is asserted to be effective or ineffective. The exhaustive research objective is an explicit condition introduced for a task where the incumbent is justified; a multilayer task alone does not establish that all sixteen methods are necessary.
