# Executed anti-hammer strategies: synthesis

All sixteen strategies are completed for the bounded research case. Both independent audits pass, and all three finalization findings are resolved. The run contains 45 executed stages and two declared direct-exit records, covering all 47 required outputs. Twelve native helpers contributed concern reports, controlled exercises, and independent audits. The [completion review](audit/completion-review.json) records the authoritative whole-run verdict and repair history.

## What changed in the analysis

The strongest result is a conditional process for deciding, not a universal winner among methods:

1. State the outcome and constraints before selecting a capability.
2. Identify the uncertain causal or semantic distinction that could change the next action.
3. If direct evidence resolves it, make the bounded decision and retain a reopening trigger.
4. If concerns genuinely conflict, assign independent investigators and compare different mechanisms on the same problem.
5. Attack the hypothesis, then ask which assumption the attack exposes and what becomes possible if it changes.
6. Distill only after the useful expansion: preserve the consumer, evidence limits, next test, owner, and expiry.
7. Test at the smallest useful scale, retaining inconclusive outcomes and hard constraints.

The user-requested exhaustive run is a research deliverable. It does not establish that every future task should execute all sixteen methods.

## Actual observed findings

- The frozen S10 gate requires evidence equality but its local four-field checklist omits evidence. A controlled mutation showed that changing evidence alone passed that narrow comparator and failed the full-obligation comparator. The repaired local exercise reads the emitted human and machine records back and rejects jointly missing required fields.
- Every one of the 47 frozen step output-purpose strings repeats its route's final output. That is an evidence-stage ambiguity: a hypothesis and an observation can have the same purpose label. Actual run outputs therefore carry stage-specific operation, source evidence, result, and maturity. This is a demonstrated artifact ambiguity, not a recorded production misclassification.
- S07's generic missing-evidence stop can be read as forbidding the test intended to acquire missing evidence. This run distinguishes missing indispensable setup from a missing result that a feasible test measures.
- Three initially tempting attacks were rejected: structural passes do not contradict explicitly untested effectiveness; the S10 gate polarity was already corrected; and S14 already had a valid direct exit. Critique that invents a defect adds work without opening anything useful.
- The actual first reduction U0 returned a ranked proposal list but omitted owner, expiry, consumer, and unresolved evidence. The Proposer and independent Balancer repaired it into one owned decision episode. This is an observed local reduction-and-repair, not a claim of production improvement.

## Executed experiments

The primary native exercise used three independent agents with the same twelve synthetic cases: capability-first, outcome-first, and inversion-plus-expansion. All three selected the model-best action in all twelve. In the two open-ended cases, the arms produced overlapping mechanism families. The familiar tool won the summary-bottleneck case; delegation won the approval-bottleneck case; missing information led to measurement. These results do not show an added decision benefit from inversion or richer procedure on this small case set.

The factorial comparison crossed plain/structured process with capability-only/causal evidence. All four independent cells made four appropriate decisions. Without discriminating observations, both processes requested more evidence. With those observations, both selected three targeted interventions and retained one unresolved case. At fixed evidence, the action selections were identical. The case set supports the importance of supplying the missing distinction; it does not establish a general causal ranking of reasoning methods.

The capacity model evaluated nine baseline conditions and 27 nonbaseline interventions, for 36 condition evaluations total. Among the two single interventions, extra summary capacity had greater marginal benefit in five contexts, extra approval capacity in three, and they tied in one. The model's graph and capacities are stipulated, so this illustrates why the causal obstacle matters without claiming a measured effect in a real queue.

The paired-record exercise includes matching records, evidence-only drift, success-measure-only drift, and a required field absent from both records. Full obligation coverage detects the three invalid cases and emitted-file readback confirms the final matching pair's required fields and literal canonical rendering. This is not an automatic proof of arbitrary paraphrase equivalence.

## Inversion and destructive-to-creative results

Logical inversion worked as a disciplined question generator. Seven recorded dispositions distinguish valid contraposition under an explicit premise, invalid inverse/converse, necessity removal, role reversal, causal reversal, and a reversal that violates the user's required scope. The exhaustive Boolean check rejects inverse and converse with a concrete countermodel. A valid inference form cannot establish its empirical premise.

The most useful creative opening in this case came from attacking evidence handling: if more valid workflow artifacts still do not answer the outcome question, change what observation is acquired and who can act on it. This produced different mechanism families: evidence-conditioned next action; explicit disposition/decision rights; removal of redundant obligations; and bounded rules learned from counterexamples.

Attack-to-expand also closed without a new candidate when the attack was invalid or the constraint fixed. The user required execution of all sixteen strategies; “save effort by skipping them” was rejected because it changes the task. Expansion serves the outcome rather than rewarding novelty or option count.

## Limits that remain after execution

The experiment agents come from one runtime family; cases are authored, small, and unreplicated. The fixed-choice tasks test selection more than open-ended invention. Output length and step counts do not measure human review cost. No real organization was changed, no live delegation pilot was run, and no production efficacy was established. Those are boundaries of the findings, not hidden missing steps in the requested bounded research.

The next adoption decision can use the result now: prefer the smallest decision process that preserves the required outcome, exposed uncertainty, and evidence obligations; escalate when a named contradiction survives. Keep a portfolio because information generation, decision authority, work removal, and formal reasoning solve different obstacles.
