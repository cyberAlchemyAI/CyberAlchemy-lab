## Shared world and controller contract

Roster: Bastion (reduction/contact retaliation; stamina, range and hazards threaten it), Mason (walls/poison/slow/knockback; fragile, finite walls and self-entrapment), Veil (mobile magic/poison; fragile, cast recovery and repeated routes). Choose exactly one for the complete three-stage run. Keep mid-stage items and XP branch choices from R1, both manual and auto, and no party.

Provisional clock: simulation steps at 50 ms; public observation snapshots and command admission every 100 ms. Both controllers use that snapshot: visible geometry, current resources/status, own recovery, announced attack facts and hazards. Neither gets future seeds, hidden enemy intent, unseen positions or remaining random draws. Requests enter the next admission boundary. Auto evaluates its ordered rules once per snapshot and records the selected rule; manual requests pass the same command gate. These values are design assumptions, not demonstrated reaction fairness. Auto's consistent early response is allowed; human timing variance remains real.

An admitted action commits until its recovery ends; only declared dodge/guard cancel windows permit interruption. Movement and attack use declared compatible channels; a conflicting command waits or is rejected visibly. On completion, reevaluate from highest priority; fallback resumes only if no rule matches. No tick-by-tick replacement of committed attacks.

Shared execution policy validates resources, finishes admitted actions and follows the selected waypoint segment. It never dodges, heals or changes a route for the player. Up to six authored rules support composed conditions and a bounded action template: “follow this route while excluding warned polygons; allow monster contact unless stamina below reserve.” Every selected target and exclusion is visible to manual players too. Rule edits show displaced responsibility and are applied at paused item/XP choices or transitions. Authoring must express both deliberate contact risk and broken-guard recovery within this limit; inability is a defeating observation.

Lethal hazard issuance must find an escape trajectory during the full warning interval, accounting for current committed recovery, known compulsory displacement, command-boundary latency and existing obstacles. Hidden future events may not invalidate that trajectory before activation. Later voluntary casting, wall placement or route choice can close it and cause death; the death explanation identifies that later choice. If a newly issued compulsory event invalidates the only escape, delay/reposition that event. This preserves attributable mistakes rather than cancelling every danger.

## A: complete run with deliberate strategic sacrifice

Layers: survival purpose → adaptive build capability → full-run workflow → shared commands/encounter rules. Stage 1 rewards guarded melee contact and short funnels. Stage 2's announced ranged lanes force Bastion to spend stored retaliation on approach; wall-breaking charges force Mason to preserve a second exit. Stage 3's pursuit/leap alternation and hazard sweep force a retreat exception: abandon a currently profitable contact/funnel window to preserve health/stamina for the next pursuit window. Every boss retains ordinary contact and redirectable movement opportunities; its counter does not erase the build.

A's discriminating case: an authored “retreat on sweep warning even with full retaliation” rule sacrifices an expiring burst. Fixed hold-ground behavior retains that burst but risks lethal exposure; always-retreat behavior misses later pursuit windows. Manual can execute the same chosen exception. Compare identical build, seed and world with authored, fixed and manual command sources; this examines preparation value, not world novelty.

## B: contradictory outcomes depend on the public goal

Replace three immediate operators with one starting **bound contradiction**. A boss publicly promises one measurable objective: **touch hero**, **wound hero**, or **reach marked seal**. The token states target, deadline and exact success predicate. Binding empowers that attack and grants a payoff only when the promised objective fails. Physical events resolve normally either way.

The same fully guarded contact fulfills “touch” but defeats “wound.” Thus guarding earns a shield only against a bound wound promise; dodging or redirecting is needed against touch. A charge stopped by the hero before a seal defeats “reach” even if contact occurs. Goal and consequence can conflict without an inferred-intent judge. This is the candidate dependency difference, not proven originality.

Provisional economics: capacity two catalyst charges; start with two; collect one mid-stage refill per stage, capped at capacity. Binding spends one immediately and raises listed attack damage by 25%. Acquire a defensive payoff (temporary shield) or offensive payoff (stored retaliation) at an item choice; neither stacks beyond one stored payoff. Payoff energy is fixed from listed damage at lock and capped, so both dodged and blocked failures can pay. Bind after warning and before attack lock; no refund on cancel or failure. Only one highlighted commitment is bindable. Adds remain physically dangerous but emit no competing rhetoric tokens.

Resolution order: (1) freeze token, selected payoff and attack instance at lock; (2) resolve its ordered physical hits, prevention, guard break, collision and displacement; (3) apply same-boundary hazards/poison; (4) evaluate the promised predicate over that attack instance; (5) close token once, debit no further charge, emit one payoff if failure qualifies. “Wound” succeeds on any HP loss caused by that attack, whether guard breaks or stays intact. “Reach” succeeds on entering the seal before deadline, even if a wall was destroyed first. Multi-hit attacks close after the last committed hit/deadline. New shields never retroactively prevent step-3 damage or generate another token.

Counterplay: the boss telegraphs a delayed follow-through or obstruction-breaking variant before bind lock. Delay lets a player bait the strike, spend stamina repositioning and guard later; breaking a wall leaves time to sacrifice another barrier or dodge clear. Some normal attacks remain in each boss cycle. No counter guarantees payoff; no hidden variant consumes the only feasible response.
