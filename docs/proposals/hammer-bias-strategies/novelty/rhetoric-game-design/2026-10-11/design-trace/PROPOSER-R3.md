## Exact first unit and two tracks

Select a **complete three-stage survival run with three selectable heroes, one deployed, manual and authored auto, plus one bound-contradiction operator**. Each stage ends with a boss; each has a mid-stage item/refill and XP branch choice. Keep damage reduction, magic, poison, slow, knockback, movement plans and harmful random events including warned lethal collapse. This is the minimum fitting the user's context, not universal logical minimality.

**A: strategy and execution.** Bastion guards contact into retaliation but lacks range and pays stamina; Mason routes enemies through finite walls/poison but is fragile and can trap its escape; Veil combines mobile magic/poison but pays cast recovery and low durability. Stage 1 rewards contact/funneling; stage 2 introduces ranged approach and wall-breaking pressure; stage 3 couples pursuit/leap windows with hazard sweeps. Counterpressure retains contact and redirectable movement windows. Author a deliberate sacrifice of expiring retaliation or a wall to preserve the next useful window; manual timing executes that strategy.

**B: causal contradiction.** An enemy publicly promises **touch hero**, **wound hero**, or **reach marked seal**. Binding raises that attack's listed damage by a provisional 25% and spends a catalyst; failure of the locked promise releases a fixed capped shield or retaliation chosen at an item pause. Choose one operator, not three simultaneous rhetorical subsystems. Same physical attack, resources and payoff cap can require different responses under different promised goals. If ordinary effects reproduce the choices, retain the expression and withdraw the stronger novelty claim.

## Shared commands and explicit authority

Retain provisional 50-ms simulation and 100-ms public snapshots/admission. They are starting tuning values, not optimality or fairness findings. Both modes share resource costs, collision checks, commitment/cancel windows and public facts. Auto receives no hidden future state. Later measurement must examine manual input latency, dodge windows and game feel separately from strategic performance.

| Channel | Admission and continuation |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | One pending request; newest replaces it. Request expires after its next admission attempt. Admitted segment continues until completion, stop or replacement. |
| Discrete action | Same pending capacity/replacement/expiry. Illegal or recovery-incompatible requests are rejected visibly, never queued for later. Admitted actions finish unless a declared cancel window permits interruption. |

Auto reevaluates once per snapshot. Before admission, recheck its selected rule against the latest public snapshot; a false predicate cancels the pending request. A false continuation condition emits stop at the next admission. Manual release/stop works through the same channel; held guard renews its request. Rejected commands require a new request from either source. No stale attack backlog exists.

Six ordered authored rules plus fallback may combine predicates. Route commands are available in both modes. Excluding a warned polygon means: select the first **explicitly authored** alternate segment satisfying the exclusion; otherwise stop and reevaluate fallback next snapshot. Display the selected segment before admission; never invent a detour. Execution policy handles legality and chosen segments, not emergency survival. Players author safety exceptions themselves.

Hazards retain R2's temporal escape obligation at issuance, including recovery, displacement and admission latency. Later voluntary self-entrapment can kill. A newly issued compulsory event cannot invalidate the only escape; delay/reposition that event.

## One-shot rhetoric lifecycle and endpoints

Capacity two catalysts, start with two, one capped refill mid-stage; values remain provisional. One commitment is highlighted as bindable. Payoff energy is fixed at lock from listed attack damage up to the selected cap. One payoff slot: binding while it is occupied is rejected before cost; shields exhaust/expire and retaliation is spent/expires before another binding. No overwrite or stacking.

| State/transition | Cost and result |
| --- | --- |
| Issued → bound | Valid bind before lock spends one charge; warning states goal, target, deadline, counter and empowerment. |
| Bound → locked | Freeze attack ID, goal, target and fixed payoff. No rebinding. |
| Issued/bound → cancelled before lock | Player interruption yields no payoff; spent charge stays spent. Issuer invalidation refunds a spent charge, capped at capacity. |
| Locked → resolved | Completion, deadline or player interruption evaluates recorded goal once; failed goal yields one payoff. |
| Locked → invalidated | Issuer-caused contract invalidation cancels payoff and refunds charge; this is a specification fault, not a tactical success. |
| Any → dead | Hero death closes token without payoff or refund; no resurrection. |

Resolve physical hits/prevention/guard break/displacement, then same-boundary hazards/status, then token, then new payoff. Touch means at least one accepted weapon/body hit event from that attack ID, including blocked contact; invulnerable overlap is not accepted contact. Wound means direct HP loss from that attack's admitted hits through its final hit/deadline; poison is separately attributed and excluded, including delayed ticks. Reach means the enemy center enters the marked seal before deadline; later wall destruction/death does not undo recorded entry. Payoffs cannot prevent earlier damage or create tokens.

Starting counter specifications must name feasibility conditions. Delayed blow: waiting outside the arc then guarding requires current recovery plus approach time within its displayed windup and stamina for the listed block cost. Wall-breaking charge: dodge to the authored clear exit requires recovery plus travel/admission latency within warning and dodge stamina. If conditions fail, show the missing resource/path before spending; the player may decline or deliberately risk binding. A favorable outcome is not guaranteed.

## Worked paired design examples — not experiments

Assume an already-bound attack, identical empowerment, fixed shield cap **12**, and a future hit making that shield useful. For illustration only, value one shield point at one utility unit and one stamina point at half a unit. No additional value is created by changing the goal.

1. **Touch versus wound, same melee sweep.** Full guard costs six stamina; dodge costs fourteen; both avoid direct HP loss. Wound: guard scores 12−3=9 versus dodge 12−7=5. Touch: guard fulfills the promise and scores −3; dodge defeats it and scores 5. A goal-independent HP-avoidance reward favors guard in both. This example fails if actual timing/costs make dodge universally superior.
2. **Touch versus reach, same stoppable charge.** Guarded interception before the seal costs six stamina and terminates this charge; side-dodge costs fourteen and lets it reach the seal. Reach favors interception (9 versus −7); touch favors dodge (5 versus −3). Wall-breaking variants are excluded from this paired situation. A goal-independent avoidance reward favors interception in both. Binding value itself must also be compared with saving the finite charge.

Planned A controls include competent fixed hazard avoidance, fixed conditional retreat and authored rules with identical vocabulary/costs. Authors know stage-pattern families and observed encounters/items, not future seeds; revisions occur only at stated pauses. Compare across encounter variants, not just favorable seeds. Planned B controls match empowerment, catalysts and caps while replacing goal dependence with ordinary avoidance/block effects. No efficacy or novelty verdict is forced.

## Closure and honest closeout

Named interfaces: **command request/admission**, **public snapshot**, **encounter warning**, **token lifecycle**, **effect resolution**, **progression choice**. They recompose survival purpose → adaptive-build capability → full-run workflow; no unnamed coordinator supplies tactics.

Preserve wild expansion outside this first unit: sarcasm collisions, anchor betrayal/surprise, indirect effects and sound. Sound quality means bounded pitch/rhythm conformity relative to personal calibration, never beauty or loudness; equivalent non-microphone input remains available. Premortem: the core fails through authoring burden or effect-equivalent rhetoric; cap authoring and accept defeating comparisons. Frame expires with party deployment, different run structure or mandatory audio. **Flag:** fun, balance, responsiveness, comprehension and decision-level novelty remain unverified. Stop the three-round guard here; route the reconciled specification to invoke design, with no fourth round or implementation implied.
