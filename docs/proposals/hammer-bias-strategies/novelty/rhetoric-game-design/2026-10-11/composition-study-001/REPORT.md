# Combining methods to invent game mechanics

Date: 11 October 2026. **Status: the finite paper research is complete. Robot Talks and all three Deep conversations per composition are completed; overall design verdict: flag for untested feasibility, value and novelty.**

The research has produced six representative mechanics from eighteen initial drafts. The most distinctive questions concern cast-origin memory, a compulsory shove whose settlement origin can move, and hostile custody of an amended order. They are concrete proposals to investigate. Existing games supply many of their ingredients, and a conventional cached-route system reproduces the scout proposal. No worldwide-first mechanic or general creativity benefit is established.

Start with the [sixteen generator cards](../MECHANIC-GENERATION-STRATEGIES.md), the [eighteen-draft pool](GENERATION-POOL.json) and the [twelve reusable investigation rules](METHOD-LESSONS.md). The [main research report](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/hammer-bias-strategies/RESEARCH-REPORT.md) retains the earlier academic, analysis and game-design studies. This follow-up has its own sources, role counts and evidence limits.

## 1. What this research is trying to change

“Every problem becomes a nail” describes a trap in which a familiar tool supplies both the imagined problem and its solution. Listing more effects can preserve the trap. A generator instead changes a dependency: who performs an action, what must survive for it to succeed, when its consequence settles, what information an actor has, or what counts as victory.

The target is **strategies for inventing mechanics**. The game is a test case. Helping players invent strategies within those mechanics is a second target, requiring its own evidence.

The retained game frame is one hero chosen from three, three stages with a boss in each, acquisitions during a stage, demanding manual control and player-authored autoplay. Each proposed autoplay policy has six ordered tactical rules. Movement and a discrete command are explicitly separate proposed channels, with the first true rule owning both; they are not an implemented editor. Acquisitions can pause clocks and permit deliberate plan edits. They cannot discharge obligations, reset resources or secretly choose the best strategy.

Three different units matter:

- An **operator** transforms a relationship, such as making information physically contestable.
- A **composition** applies operators in order to a seed; each step uses the relation produced by the preceding step.
- A **mechanic** gives that construction a trigger, actor, persistent state, cost, observation, commitment and alternative choice.

A surprising title alone supplies no new action. A familiar operation can nevertheless support an unexpected purpose or experience. Those possibilities must remain independently testable.

## 3. The mechanics and their meaningful costs

### C01 — A threat becomes the custodian of your plan

A physical attack card travels with a living hostile courier. The hero can pay a finite seal to intercept and amend its lane. Only delivery before the ordinary scheduled attack admission uses the amendment. Killing the carrier, losing it to poison or delaying it past admission preserves the original attack and the spent seal. The boss does not wait for the player.

This makes “kill the threat” compete with “preserve the future action entrusted to it.” Autoplay can suspend new attacks while following a declared custody route; it cannot undo already admitted damage or grant the courier immunity. Ordinary combat remains available without the tool or refill.

Courier orders and delivery delays are established in the official Waterloo manual, and Strategos documents courier-based command. The candidate question is hostile, vulnerable custody of a player-amended attack. A direct paid targeting change with a delay is the competent simpler comparison. [Waterloo manual](https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/369390/manuals/SLIT_SoW_Waterloo_Materials_Manual_Ebook.pdf?t=1575554183), [Strategos developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3064810/Strategos/).

### C02 — Protection remembers where the cast began

Three real, costly cast interruptions consume finite residue credits and leave marks. A nondegenerate triangle becomes a temporary shared jurisdiction. An eligible cast admitted inside records an obligation to cross outward before it closes; a cast admitted outside has no such obligation even if it finishes inside. A cast owns its admitted boundary coordinates, so region expiry cannot erase its obligation. Failure keeps its cost.

The revealing comparison holds final position constant. A cast begun inside that never exits fizzles; an outside-origin cast that later enters can complete. Completion-position checking cannot distinguish those histories. An origin-sensitive conventional rule can, limiting what this example establishes.

Placed magic interference, spatial history and mutable rule text already have positive precedents. The remaining question concerns their failure-funded, symmetric cast-origin arrangement. The hero's preparation and outward movement remain costly and may prove repetitive rather than interesting. [Valve Riki record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=32), [Valve Disruptor record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=87), [Baba Is You developer description](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3).

### C03 — Your expressive control reveals a future expectation

A paid preparation supplies two ordered cardinal segments sharing one received knockback budget. Ordinary hit damage remains. The first blocking collision truncates the entire displacement; it does not donate movement to the second segment. A surviving actual endpoint updates a visible two-entry zone memory. Two equal fresh entries can request one finite coverage commitment. Static memory does not repeatedly trigger coverage, and later entries cannot retarget a committed response.

Two starting charges permit two teaching hits. Exploiting the resulting coverage can use ordinary movement; a third prepared shove requires an actual optional refill. The control slot is explicitly authored. This is endpoint recording with a fixed response, not trained machine learning or measurement of beautiful singing.

Directional influence and learned responses to repeated behavior already exist. The question is whether ordered precommitment and its publicly learned endpoint create a useful distinction from competent steering and a fixed coverage schedule. A path difference alone cannot establish that the two-zone response creates additional strategy. The final matched example confirms this limit: competent straight east reaches the same E zone and reproduces its memory response. Coverage now has an actual-arrival requirement, a bounded approach and an obstruction abort that preserves spent teaching costs. [Rivals developer patch notes](https://rivalsofaether.com/love-is-in-the-aether-valentines-update-0-15-9/), [ECHO developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES), [Hello Neighbor official description](https://www.helloneighborgame.com/home-en).

### C04 — Restraint changes where a consequence settles

Arming spends a finite charge. A qualifying hit within its half-open capture window still loses HP immediately, but its post-resistance vector becomes a four-second displacement debt. Movement preserves the debt. A newly admitted attack or timeout settles it once from the hero's current position using current collision geometry. Further hits remain ordinary and can spoil the plan.

A hero can receive a northward shove at A, withhold attacks while walking to B beside a wall, then attack to release it into that wall. This differs from returning to a marked past position: the stored vector remains, while its settlement origin changes. A competent timed knockback skill with these same release rights would reproduce the arrangement.

Autoplay therefore needs attack withholding to override ordinary offense. Waiting forever is unavailable; timeout remains compulsory. Damage, charges and the risk of later displacement survive the transformation. Stored kinetic energy and delayed position return are known ingredients. [Nintendo Stasis guide](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-runes-guide/), [Rogue Voltage developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1494560/_Rogue_Voltage/?l=english), [Valve Kunkka record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=23).

### C05 — Empowering an enemy can consume its entitlement

In one announced special encounter, ordinary attacks cannot directly defeat the boss. Its charges draw from a finite budget. A paid lane lease increases reach and nominal expenditure only if the boss's actual source lies in that lane when it locks the action. The hero tries to lure that source, survive the stronger attack and later reach an ordinary seal interaction. Adds and hazards remain threats.

The benefit is conditional. If only one budget unit remains, nominal cost two still spends only one while full empowered reach remains. The lease then supplies no faster exhaustion. A fixed player policy can prefer an unleased source, but success depends on actual positioning. Ordinary unleased charges also exhaust the budget; the lease is an optional risky acceleration.

Monster fatigue and posture-based defeat are established. The candidate question concerns player-authored spatial fees on enemy action entitlement, compared with a fixed number of attacks or a conventional exhaustion encounter. Fee arithmetic is specified; interesting reachable advantage is unmeasured. [Monster Hunter Generations manual](https://game.capcom.com/manual/MH_Gen/en-UK/page-46.html), [Sekiro official mechanics guide](https://support.activision.com/sekiro/articles/sekiro-shadows-die-twice-game-mechanics).

### C06 — The world changes before acquired instructions do

A public placard defines which source crossing opens a gate. A scout reads it, selects a finite route and retains that route until actual return and reinspection. A paid amendment changes current world law; the committed scout still carries its earlier acquired route. Another actor can cross the new source and open the gate for it. Acquired information gives no exemption from current physics.

A scout deadline limits new passage within that commitment; an earlier admitted passage owns its completion. Return follows declared geometry, and obstruction cannot produce remote reading. Autoplay's denial and use plans are explicitly selected; switching them requires a lawful pause or manual operation, not hidden runtime insight.

Physical rules, rewiring and behavior updates have positive precedents. More decisively, a conventional switch plus an inspected-and-cached route reproduces this proposal. Removing the version number while retaining the acquired route preserves the operation. This remains a useful construction and comprehension case, with a strong conventional equivalent. [Baba Is You](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3), [Gunpoint](https://store.steampowered.com/app/206190/Gunpoint/), [ECHO developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES).

## 4. What irony, sarcasm and Goat Simulator contribute

The [academic principles review](../PRINCIPLES-AND-APPLICABILITY.md) preserves competing accounts of irony, contextual comprehension findings and bounded creativity studies. Its findings motivate hypotheses; they do not establish that sarcasm trains lasting creativity or that an agent has the same cognitive mechanism as a person.

The useful transfer is to represent an attributed expectation, identify the criterion behind it, expose a scoped discrepancy and require reconstruction. Echoic and pretense accounts help articulate that distinction. Sarcastic tone remains optional and context-dependent. A pointed sentence supplies a starting discrepancy, while an action and defeating comparison supply the investigation. [Wilson and Sperber](https://www.dan.sperber.fr/wp-content/uploads/WilsonSperber_ExplainingIrony.pdf), [Clark and Gerrig](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1980s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Gerrig%2C%20R.J.%20_On%20the%20pretense%20theory%20of%20irony_%201984.pdf), [Huang, Gino and Galinsky](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/Huang%20Gino%20Galinsky%20OBHDP%202015_f4efb1e9-b842-4764-a292-ac4836c29cb2.pdf).

In these proposals, irony reverses expected utility: killing the messenger can ruin the plan, while strengthening a charge can shorten the boss's capacity. Contradiction is scoped to histories or positions; it is not acceptance of incompatible instructions for the same event. Indirection introduces a manipulable intermediary. Destructive-to-creative reasoning attacks an enabling dependency and requires a reconstructed operation. Surprise is an audience outcome to measure, rather than something a strange name proves.

The [Goat Simulator case](../GOAT-SIMULATOR-CASE.md) expands the value question. Its official description presents goat destruction and retained comic physics defects. The proposed inference is that changing an activity's actor, purpose and quality criterion can make familiar operations feel unexpected. Audience appeal, sustained interest and the causes of attention require separate investigation; a successful case does not isolate a general recipe for virality. [Official Goat Simulator description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/265930/Goat_Simulator/).

The wild singing direction remains available in the [earlier portfolio](../WILD-MECHANICS.md). This study's authored gesture and reserve musical-rest ideas do not measure singing quality. A future vocal system needs a defined measurable feature, feedback and equivalent nonvocal authorship before testing either input reliability or creative value.

## 7. How to reuse and test the methods

Use the [twelve investigation rules](METHOD-LESSONS.md) and generator cards on a fresh seed. Name the valued function, actor, trigger, state/history, resource owner, observation, commitment and meaningful loss. Apply two or three operators in order and save variants before converging. Preserve the unusual dependency while giving it a coherent rule contract, then compare it with a competent simpler operation.

Distill's useful unit is a **dependency-bearing action card**. Reducing it to “knockback,” “learning” or “buff” removes the very relation being investigated. Recompose the card into hero capability, encounter, acquisition and manual/auto authorship, with the required interface observations explicit. Two concept tracks organize the candidates; an evidence lane and a construction lane organize the work. Those are different divisions.

Robot Talks checks whether rules, authorship and meaning agree across concerns. Primary research narrows the claim. A discriminating example holds cost and relevant conditions fixed while changing one history or relationship. A result that the conventional substitute reproduces the choice is valuable and should remain visible.

For a causal method study, match seed, facts, expertise, construction obligation and time/output allowance across ordinary analysis, individual operators and compositions. Assess valid decision changes, source overlap, comprehension, strategic value and experienced surprise separately. Human creativity learning additionally requires delayed unaided transfer to unfamiliar tasks. A later prototype or experiment is a separately scoped next investigation; none is claimed here.
