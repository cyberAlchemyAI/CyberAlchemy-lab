# What the first mechanic pool adds, and what is already familiar

Date: 11 October 2026. **Status: provisional operation-level comparison of G0, before Robot Talks and the completed Distill loop.** This is parent research and interpretation, not an independent Balancer report. Later repairs must receive their own comparison rather than silently inheriting these judgments. Twenty-two game entries and twenty-three primary sources were inspected at the depths recorded in [SOURCES.json](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/hammer-bias-strategies/novelty/rhetoric-game-design/2026-10-11/composition-study-001/SOURCES.json). No game was played.

The evidence establishes positive precedents. It does not establish the absence of equivalent mechanics in these games or elsewhere. The exact difference below is a difference between the written proposal and the positively documented feature, not an exhaustive comparison with either game's implementation. Conventional substitutes are authored comparison proposals, not executed experiments.

## C01: put a controllable instruction in a hostile actor's custody

**Rule being compared:** amend a readable attack card carried by an enemy; the amendment takes effect only if that enemy reaches the boss's lectern. Killing the courier sacrifices the amendment. The player has to protect a threat because it carries their intended future action.

Useful enemy behavior has a precedent in ECHO's developer account of teaching enemies cooperation. Gunpoint already makes a device's triggering relationship editable. These defeat broad claims that using an enemy as a tool or changing an attack's trigger is itself new. [ECHO developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES), [Gunpoint description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/206190/Gunpoint/).

The proposed additional relationship is **dangerous custody before commitment**: an editable instruction is physically exposed, and continued survival of its hostile carrier is a condition of execution. That creates a candidate conflict between eliminating threats and preserving the delivery plan. A timer and direct lane selector provide a strong conventional substitute. If they reproduce the same choices, costs and failure possibilities, the courier adds presentation or escort framing rather than a further mechanical relationship.

**Provisional label:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. The unresolved question is whether carrier survival and position have independent strategic value. A courier that always succeeds reduces to delayed redirection; a courier that inevitably dies makes the option unusable. No playability or global originality finding is available.

## C02: make failure define a shared action jurisdiction

**Rule being compared:** three interrupted spells establish a temporary triangle. An eligible spell begun inside finishes only if its caster exits before completion. The player obtains protection by losing casts and must leave that protection to finish their own retaliation.

Riki's official Smoke Screen description already makes an area interfere with enemy ability use. Baba Is You establishes that manipulable rules can change object roles. Noita supplies a broader precedent for authored magic acting on a shared world. [Valve ability data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=32), [Baba Is You developer description](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3), [Noita developer site](https://noitagame.com/).

The candidate difference concerns **cast origin plus an escape obligation**, applied symmetrically and funded by actual interruption residue. It is not merely a silence field if a moving caster can deliberately begin inside and complete outside while a stationary caster cannot. Compare it with a conventional anti-magic zone of matched cost, duration and shape, then with a zone that checks only the actor's position at completion. Distinct outcomes at the same final position would isolate the relevance of remembered cast origin.

**Provisional label:** insufficient evidence for a novelty judgment; identifiable rule-construction hypothesis. The sources do not resolve close movement-conditioned casting equivalents. Eligible spell families, crossing detection, infeasible exits and whether the effect supports interesting encounters remain open. Do not grant originality because the region is triangular or because three failures pay for it.

## C03: author the movement imposed on you, exposing an expectation

**Rule being compared:** a two-segment gesture routes one future hostile knockback within its existing displacement budget. A disclosed opponent remembers completed endpoints and covers a repeated destination. The player can repeat an escape, then change the written route to exploit the resulting commitment.

Directional influence during hitstun is established in Rivals of Aether's official engine notes. ECHO establishes learning from player behavior. Their combination is the relevant challenge to this idea; drawing rather than using a stick does not by itself establish a new decision. [Rivals developer patch notes](https://rivalsofaether.com/love-is-in-the-aether-valentines-update-0-15-9/), [ECHO official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/551770/ECHO/?curator_clanid=34860364&l=greek).

The proposed extra relationship is **prior authorship of a bounded, bent hostile displacement and a public endpoint history**. A direction chosen only when hit cannot necessarily express the same two-segment commitment, but that is a hypothesis requiring an actual feasible trace. Compare four variants: ordinary directional influence, gesture routing without learning, learning about ordinary displacement, and the combined contract. If the combined version only needs a different final direction, its extra syntax adds authoring cost without demonstrating a useful new relationship.

**Provisional label:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. Distinctiveness depends on segment order, commitment and disclosed memory affecting available plans. The precise relationship between path length, initial force, collision and endpoint coverage is not closed at G0. Avoid claiming that being thrown, drawing paths or teaching enemies is individually unprecedented.

## C04: preserve an unwanted consequence but change where it settles

**Rule being compared:** one finite charge delays a received knockback vector. Damage resolves immediately. Moving and guarding preserve the debt; attacking or timeout releases it from the current location under ordinary collisions. Withholding an attack buys a chance to relocate the consequence, rather than remove it.

Breath of the Wild's Stasis provides a clear precedent for stored kinetic energy and later motion. Rogue Voltage makes energy storage and the timing of its use part of skill construction. These make storage and deferred discharge familiar. [Nintendo rune guide](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-runes-guide/), [Rogue Voltage description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1494560/_Rogue_Voltage/?l=english).

The proposed addition is a **compulsory hostile displacement debt carried by a still-mobile recipient**, whose attack releases it. The chosen release origin and the cost of withholding create the candidate relationship. Compare a fixed-origin delayed shove, a cancellable stored shove, an ordinary defensive shield, and the mandatory mobile debt. If cancellation or automatic safe relocation slips into the implementation, the borrowed escrow relation disappears.

**Provisional label:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement; a specific follow-up candidate. The inspected features support a comparison, not a claim that no game has already stored incoming knockback this way. Multiple hits, lethal damage, collision truncation, timeout order and warned instant-kill hazards need closure. No safety or tactical value is established by the word “escrow.”

## C05: strengthen a threat to consume its capacity to remain a threat

**Rule being compared:** direct damage is removed for a disclosed boss encounter. A player-leased lane makes a boss charge longer-reaching and more expensive in its finite attack-space budget. Budget exhaustion opens a defeat opportunity during recovery.

Monster Hunter Generations has monster stamina depletion and fatigue. Sekiro permits deflection to damage posture and create a deathblow opening. Shotgun King couples player upgrades with advantages for the opposing side. Those are substantial precedents for exhaustion, defeating through defense and coupled benefits. [Capcom stamina manual](https://game.capcom.com/manual/MH_Gen/en-UK/page-46.html), [Activision mechanics guide](https://support.activision.com/sekiro/articles/sekiro-shadows-die-twice-game-mechanics), [Shotgun King description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1972440/Shotgun_King_The_Final_Checkmate?l=english).

The proposed difference is a **player-authored spatial fee on enemy action entitlement**, with greater immediate threat accelerating exhaustion. Compare ordinary survival for a fixed number of attacks and a boss with a stamina meter. A new strategy is plausible only if choosing and exploiting the expensive lane changes expenditure and survival choices. Renaming attacks as rent payments cannot establish it.

**Provisional label:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. The hardest unresolved issue is whether every viable plan collapses to waiting out attacks. An announced boss mechanic can explore a different victory relationship while preserving the surrounding one-hero expedition. It does not demonstrate that permanently removing damage improves the whole game.

## C06: distinguish the world's current rule from an opponent's acquired rule

**Rule being compared:** a scout reads a physical gate placard and visibly commits to a route. The player can amend a source tile once. The world immediately follows the current clause; the scout follows its last-read version until its next inspection.

Baba Is You already turns physical text into rules. Gunpoint changes cause-and-effect links, and ECHO makes updating enemy knowledge an exposed part of play. Those defeat broad claims about editable laws, mutable switches and enemies with changing information. [Baba developer description](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3), [Gunpoint description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/206190/Gunpoint/), [ECHO official description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/551770/ECHO/?curator_clanid=34860364&l=greek).

The candidate arrangement is **a public distinction between enacted rule and acquired rule**, allowing a timed amendment to exploit a disclosed commitment. A conventional gate switch plus an enemy already committed to an attack may reproduce it. Compare equal-duration route commitments with and without rule-version dependence; if version identity never changes what the enemy can reasonably predict or choose, the visible version is explanatory UI rather than another mechanic.

**Provisional label:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. A “stale” enemy must follow its disclosed observation model rather than conveniently ignore something it would normally perceive. Which actors a gate constrains, how a flying counter relates to a gate rule, and what is observed during reinspection remain unresolved.

## Keep the rejected outputs as diagnostic controls

C01-V3 is close to action-checklist rewards carried by a fleeing enemy. C02-V3 is a conditional slow field financed by misses. C03-V3 is a shared accuracy benefit/liability and closely repeats the earlier MC01 structure. C04-V3 is an event-charged discharge. C05-V3 repeats the earlier empowered demolition/charge-redirection proposal. C06-V3 changes an ordinary trap's eligible target class. These are **authoring rejections**, with local relationship overlap or weak distinctiveness as their reasons; the external equivalence of every rejected mechanic has not been verified.

This matters for evaluating the methods: the same ordered generators produced both unusual relational proposals and conventional outputs. Eighteen drafts demonstrate application and construction, not a measured increase in novelty. The different initial seeds, selective development, arriving precedents and unmatched method orders prevent a causal conclusion that any combination or ordering outperforms ordinary analysis.
