## C01 — MG04 → MG07 → MG16

**Representative C01-V1: The enemy courier delivers your amended attack order.** A hostile courier carries a readable attack-lane card. The hero spends a seal to amend it and must preserve that dangerous courier until delivery locks the boss's next sweep. This changes who performs the targeting handoff and makes information exposure a physical dependency. The absurd dispatcher premise earns its place through a real operational inconvenience: killing a threat can destroy the plan you entrusted to it.

**Unusual reserve C01-V2: Your pursuer certifies the emergency exit.** A pursuing inspector records two turns and prints a one-use gate permission. Both sides can use the installed certificate. A simple route is easy to repeat but also easy for a pursuer to consume. The reserve is less direct and possibly too fussy; retain its transferable traversal permission for review.

**Rejected for development C01-V3: Monsters carry your performance review.** The authored action checklist becomes deferred XP on a fleeing witness. It fits the generators, but familiar bonus objectives plus fleeing loot appear sufficient to describe it. Rejection is provisional similarity to a control, not verified absence of novelty.

**Next pressure:** Replace the messenger with a timer and direct lane selection at equal cost. If no decision changes, the representative is delayed targeting with escort presentation. The certificate reserve likewise needs a comparison with an ordinary movement lock.

## C02 — MG03 → MG09 → MG06

**Representative C02-V1: Interrupted spells build a room nobody can finish casting inside.** Lost casts leave three floor marks. The claimed region imposes one shared eligibility rule: a spell started inside must leave before completion, otherwise it fizzles. The hero can create protection only by accepting actual interruptions, and must escape that protection to cast back. No hit points are refunded and no physical wall is manufactured.

**Unusual reserve C02-V2: A broken guard gives you right of way against the traffic.** A guard break preserves an authored retreat segment. Claiming it creates a short corridor whose acceleration applies to the hero only while an enemy crosses oppositely. It preserves a strange dependency on movement by the threat itself. The eligibility rule and collision risks may erase its usefulness; that is the next question rather than a reason to replace it with a free dash now.

**Rejected for development C02-V3: Your failed attacks make a moat for the healthy.** Missed spells create terrain slowing actors above a health threshold. Failure-funded placement and conditional slow already describe it well; little remains beyond those joined ingredients.

**Next pressure:** Compare the representative against a conventional anti-magic zone with matched duration and cost. Test whether cast-origin and boundary crossing create useful alternatives for both sides. Do not convert every enemy into a caster merely to make the region look powerful.

## C03 — MG10 → MG08 → MG05

**Representative C03-V1: Teach the boss your escape handwriting.** A drawn two-segment gesture routes one future hostile knockback within its existing displacement budget. A disclosed enemy remembers two completed endpoints and covers a repeated one. Steering is beneficial, but makes a future destination teachable and exposes a commitment. This is not simply the performed phrase becoming another musical boss attack. The learning component itself is familiar; the hypothesis concerns learning about a player-authored hostile displacement contract.

**Unusual reserve C03-V2: The boss learns your punctuation and your items must obey it.** Two identical four-slot phrases with one rest teach the boss where to pause. The hero's selected item is legal only at that same rest during the ensuing cycle. The resulting opportunity can be good or harmful depending on when an approach or cleanse is needed. Retain the awkward shared grammar instead of improving it into an ordinary damage buff.

**Rejected for development C03-V3: A perfect note teaches the boss perfect aim.** Shared precision couples a player benefit to an enemy benefit, but is close to MC01's coupled expressive power/threat tradeoff after replacing tempo with spread. Its generation sequence is valid; its distinct relationship is weak.

**Next pressure:** Check whether drawn knockback adds authorship beyond choosing a dodge direction. The reserve must be compared against independently selecting a boss pause and an item timing restriction; musical expression should not be mistaken for the decision structure.

## C04 — MG12 → MG15 → MG11

**Representative C04-V1: Keep the shove in escrow until you break your silence.** Borrow the causal idea of escrow without canceling the obligation. A finite anchor defers one hostile displacement vector while damage resolves immediately. Moving and guarding can relocate the future release; attacking or a four-second timeout releases it using current collisions. The player withholds attacks to arrange where the debt becomes real. This is displacement relocation, not borrowing another shield.

**Unusual reserve C04-V2: Let a recipe forget the first attack.** A vulnerable retort records two nearby hostile attack categories as an ordered recipe. Waiting erases the oldest category, so withholding a valid release can make room for a different result. This transfers maturation and pruning from fermentation rather than only its art theme. Requiring meaningful combinations without excessive recipe memorization remains unresolved.

**Rejected for development C04-V3: Leave the socket empty to charge the next spell.** A selectable event charges a later discharge. It faithfully combines borrowed process, typed mutation and withholding, but ordinary event-charged attacks and combo meters appear sufficient to cover it.

**Next pressure:** Deferred displacement creates a known compulsory event. Check it against hazard issuance, collision attribution and occupied geometry; no retroactive safe teleport or automatic release optimization is promised. Compare the retort with ordinary sequential crafting and enemy-action harvesting.

## C05 — MG13 → MG16 → MG01

**Representative C05-V1: Defeat the boss by making its attacks unaffordable.** Direct hero damage is removed for a disclosed encounter. The boss has finite attack-space lease tokens; attacks consume them. The hero spends collateral to lease one marked lane, increasing a charge's reach and its token expense. At zero, the boss's normal recovery opens a defeat seal. Empowering the threat spends its capacity to keep being a threat. The rental framing is absurd, but token expenditure and lane ownership must remain intelligible after removing that language.

**Unusual reserve C05-V2: Help the boss shine so you can drag its shadow.** A brighter lamp protects the boss yet enlarges its shadow handle. Hooks on that handle move the boss a bounded distance toward disclosed cuffs. Brightening, grabbing and surviving are distinct tasks. The strange manipulation surface is retained for precedent research; shadow puzzles are already a plausible equivalent and light physics must not become an unbounded simulation requirement.

**Rejected for development C05-V3: Give the boss a better demolition contract.** An empowered rush breaks a barrier the hero cannot damage. This strongly overlaps WM02 and the existing indirect boss-attack seed. Naming the boss a contractor adds little to its action.

**Next pressure:** If every legal plan simply survives the same finite number of attacks, the representative collapses into a survival objective. The stronger charge must alter where, when and how the player can accelerate expenditure. The defeated-state seal is a proposed boss-resolution operation, not a proven fit for the existing combat slice.

## C06 — MG14 → MG07 → MG15

**Representative C06-V1: Let the enemy read the law before you amend one clause.** A flyer defeats a physical maze, so retain route influence as a public gate rule. A scout reads a physical placard and commits its route for one cycle. The hero can amend the rule's source tile once, with the current rule and the scout's last-read version both visible. The world follows the current clause; the enemy follows its previously acquired information until reinspection. That difference is the hypothesized extra relationship beyond an editable switch.

**Unusual reserve C06-V2: Steal the exception that let the monster cross your wall.** An airborne bypass becomes a carried, one-use permission. A booth can change its eligible actor type to grounded. The countermeasure's exception becomes useful material rather than being defeated by a taller wall. This risks being only a transferable recolored key; preserve it for that discriminating comparison.

**Rejected for development C06-V3: A trap advertises which enemy class it punishes.** Replacing grounded targeting with airborne targeting is a familiar selector. Public feedback makes it understandable but does not itself add a contested-information relationship.

**Next pressure:** Compare the representative with a delayed gate switch, rule-editing puzzles and enemies following stale observations. A visible read version is not secret perfect knowledge. The reserve needs a clear operation beyond simply stealing a key that already opens the hero's gate.

## Handoff and unresolved constraints

All eighteen drafts are retained. The six representatives and six unusual reserves are prepared for the parent's next assigned sequence; no recursive Distill rounds were performed in G0. The next pass should preserve an unusual dependency long enough to attack it, and then record the resulting repair or rejection explicitly.

Several candidates require new public observations or editor predicates. These are additions to investigate, not claims that the existing six tactical rules can already express them. All proposed finite resources exclude recursive trigger income, unlimited XP or implicit optimizer decisions. Pending effects and boss phases need full state closure before implementation. Numerical durations, exact geometry, feasible escapes, controller feel, learning, balance, fun and prior-art distinctions remain open.
