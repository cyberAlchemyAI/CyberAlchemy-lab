## C01: decide unresolved delivery at attack admission

**B01, revise.** The ordinary boss attack scheduler does not wait for a card. At admission of its designated sweep, the attack owner reads the card outcome once. Delivered cards supply their frozen amended lane. A card that has failed or is still unresolved supplies its original lane. An unresolved card becomes `expired-at-admission` immediately, loses further delivery eligibility and is retired from the active-card slot. Its previously spent seal remains spent. A later arrival cannot change that attack or a later attack. Unamended cards can still deliver, with their ordinary lane unchanged.

Ordered same-boundary resolution is ordinary movement/damage/death, surviving arrival, deadline timeout, then attack admission. Thus a courier killed at that boundary cannot deliver; a surviving arrival at the deadline can deliver before timeout/admission. An earlier admitted fallback retires the request without suppressing the courier's ordinary enemy behavior. This preserves custody and timing as failure possibilities; it adds no attack postponement or hidden escort protection.

**B02, revise.** Loss of the owning boss phase, boss defeat or stage exit retires every outstanding card/request belonging to that owner. It produces no substitute attack, refund or later-stage delivery. Hero death ends the run and its pending state. Seals begin the run at two, have capacity two and carry across stages; a selected refill adds one up to capacity. These are explicit proposal parameters, replacing any unstated stage reset. They are not a validated progression economy.

**B03, route.** A target exclusion prevents deliberate selection of the named courier; it does not neutralize poison, area effects, reflection or obstructing walls. Ordinary collateral damage and path failure remain possible. The next control review must distinguish an authored strategy that knowingly accepts those risks from a hidden protector or route repairer. Six-rule policy construction is pending under B13.

**B04, defer.** The certificate reserve remains an undeveloped G0 proposal. Transfer, matching tolerance and atomic consumption need specification before promotion. No certificate mechanic is replaced with a conventional key in this reconciliation.

An authored branch illustration, not a simulation: issue at t=0, amend at t=2, admit sweep at t=4 while delivery is unresolved. The original lane is committed and the seal remains spent; arrival at t=6 cannot alter it. If delivery resolves at t=3 instead, t=4 admits the delivered lane. Arena timing that makes both plans feasible is still an encounter-design obligation.

## C03: make endpoint classification and coverage event-driven

**B05, revise.** Each encounter publishes a finite endpoint-zone map covering its playable floor. Zone identifiers and boundary membership are visible before gesture admission. The actor's floor anchor determines membership; if overlapping boundary definitions match, the lowest displayed zone ID owns that point. A position outside the published floor records the public sentinel `OUTSIDE`. It remains visible in memory but does not qualify for a guard-zone request. A lethal displacement terminates through the inherited death rule before any living-endpoint memory update.

The encounter owner supplies and displays this mapping; world motion supplies the actual floor anchor after collisions. The mapping remains fixed within the phase. A changed map or boss/stage exit clears endpoint memory and pending coverage requests, and expires pending gestures without refund. Gesture charges start the run at two, capacity two, and carry across stages; selected refills add one up to capacity. Exact map geometry and its learnability remain assumptions to investigate.

**B06, revise.** Only a newly resolved eligible gestured knockback evaluates the updated two-entry memory for a match. That displacement has an event ID. A matching non-`OUTSIDE` pair may create one request tagged with that event ID, if neither a request nor coverage is active. A later match while either is occupied updates public memory but creates no queued replacement. Static equal memory never retriggers by itself.

The request has a disclosed ten-second expiry and is consumed once at the next eligible coverage admission. The enemy then uses ordinary movement and the disclosed windup/three-second hold, without teleporting to the zone. If ordinary movement cannot reach an eligible zone at admission, the request is discarded, that failure is displayed, and normal enemy behavior continues. The already locked guard is never moved by subsequent memory. Completion, request expiry and owner loss release capacity. The durations remain tunable proposal values.

**B07, revise.** Two initial preparations can teach equal endpoints, leaving zero charges. Exploiting the guarded endpoint with a third gestured shove requires an explicit optional refill acquired before that preparation; the example must state it. Without a refill, the hero may exploit the declared coverage through ordinary movement or avoid the tool altogether. Neither baseline victory nor ordinary escape requires a random refill.

**B08, defer.** The shared-rest reserve remains G0. Conflicting teaching phrases, item availability/recovery and cycle ownership are unresolved. Its restriction on both actors remains the hypothesis, without an independently selected cooldown being substituted.

An authored trace: hits h1/h2 end in E and create request h2. A later h3 while that request is pending updates memory but creates no second request. The h2 guard completes; unchanged matching memory creates nothing further. A new eligible hit can create a fresh request only after capacity becomes free. This closes the scheduling instruction in writing; it does not establish control usability or a feasible escape.

## C05: restrict and schedule the encounter's action family

**B09, revise.** This special boss phase has only budgeted charge attacks, ordinary positioning and recovery. Adds can retain their ordinary attacks; their actions do not draw from the boss budget. There is no unbudgeted boss ranged attack or attack after exhaustion. Boss entry initializes six entitlement tokens. This phase-local action taxonomy replaces the ambiguous reference to unspecified “base attacks.”

The encounter owner publishes a repeated finite cycle: two seconds of ordinary positioning toward the hero's last displayed location, charge admission/lock, a one-second warning, execution, then two seconds of recovery. Ordinary movement and collision determine the source position at lock; no unseen optimizer places the boss in a leased lane. These values and the arena layout are untested design assumptions. A pre-lock interruption enters recovery without expenditure; an admitted charge spends once at lock, and post-lock interruption retains that spend and enters recovery. Any normal safety/admission rejection occurs before spending and returns to recovery with a displayed reason. At zero after final recovery, no new positioning/attack cycle begins; the one defeat seal opens. Existing adds and map hazards can continue.

This supplies an owner and termination behavior, not a guarantee that luring usefully controls source position. Reliable repeated interruption may make leasing unnecessary; unreachable useful sources may make it worthless. Preserve both as strategic-value questions.

**B10, revise.** The lease tool is an optional mid-stage acquisition; without it the hero can complete ordinary-budget charges. Collateral starts the run at two, capacity two, and carries across stages; it is not reinitialized at every boss. A selected collateral offer adds one up to capacity and competes with other items. The boss's separate entitlement resets to six only at new boss entry. Stage/boss exit retires its active lease and pending phase state without refund. Ordinary acquisition pauses freeze world events and all relevant timers; resumption preserves pending state under the inherited action handover contract. A selected tool persists as equipment; progression does not silently replace it.

**B11, revise.** Public feedback shows both nominal fee and actual spend, using `min(remaining entitlement, nominal fee)`. At one remaining token, an empowered charge can display nominal fee two, actual spend one, budget one→zero and its already frozen increased reach. No extra debt, refund or additional attack is implied.

**B12, defer.** The shadow reserve's protection has no adequate operative definition while direct boss damage is removed. Increasing resistance to pulling is one possible future interpretation, not a rule silently added here. Before promotion it must explain the cost of granting protection and its light/shadow relation. Shadow geometry alone and a meaningless damage-reduction statistic do not establish the proposed benefit/liability coupling.
