## Consequences for generation

Teaching enemies, asking an enemy to perform useful work, wiring triggers to effects, voice-based actions, spatial transformation, deliberate experimentation and paired player/enemy upgrades have substantial precedent. A candidate can use those ingredients, but its novelty claim needs to identify an additional controllable relationship or valued experience.

For each retained candidate, compare **who acts, which event triggers the rule, what state changes, who owns the resource, what is revealed, when commitment occurs, what cost persists and which player alternatives become possible**. Compare the proposed dependency, rather than its title, visual theme or genre label.

Record a concrete conventional equivalent where possible. If a fixed trap, normal reflected damage, independent upgrades or an ordinary conditional reward reproduce the choices and consequences, keep that result. If a different experience survives equivalent decisions, label and test that experience claim separately.

## Candidate judgment contract

Each final row needs a specific rule, at least two relevant existing-game comparisons, positive overlap with linked primary evidence, the exact proposed difference, and a defeating operation-level comparison. Additional targeted searches should use the rule's synonyms and include older or out-of-genre games when relevant. Search misses and marketing omissions leave uncertainty.

Use **known mechanic**, **familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement**, **distinctive candidate relative to the inspected set**, or **insufficient evidence**. The latter three do not establish worldwide originality. A finite comparison can substantiate positive precedents and a difference from specified descriptions; it cannot certify that nobody has ever built an equivalent.

Method application, method advantage, rule coherence, strategic value, perceived surprise, enjoyment and learned creativity are separate outcomes. This study can document actual agent construction and critique plus researched prior-art differences; no gameplay outcome is implied.

The initial pool was constructed while precedent discoveries were arriving. ECHO/Mosa Lina were supplied during G0, and Rivals of Aether/Monster Hunter/Sekiro during Proposer R1-PRE. Their influence is disclosed in the role receipts; no blinded or matched comparison of method orders is claimed.
