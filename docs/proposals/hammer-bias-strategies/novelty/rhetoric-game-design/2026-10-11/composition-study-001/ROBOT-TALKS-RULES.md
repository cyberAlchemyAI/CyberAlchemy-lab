## Gaps or Inconsistencies

The proposed refinements are uneven by declared design: three candidates have received a Proposer/Balancer/reconciliation conversation and three have not. This investigation does not treat raw G0 omissions as proof that a completed design has failed. It identifies the operations a subsequent round must make reviewable before claiming closure.

The shared hazard/admission and action-handover contract is referenced but is outside the fourteen frozen inputs. Its absence from this inspected set is an evidence limit, not a repository-wide absence claim. In particular, a later warning must not gain an unstated right to teleport an actor, rewrite a committed vector or refund a consumed resource. Whether any such operation is intended requires the actual common contract.

Authored ambiguity witnesses: a live preparation receives a hit on its expiry boundary; a source lease expires on charge lock; a stored shove reaches timeout on the same boundary as an attack and a second incoming shove; a gate edit coincides with scout inspection and tile crossing. These are input questions for deterministic closure, **not observed failures, executable tests or simulated results**. The findings deliberately do not select the missing branches.

## Local Tensions

- **RL01 (R01, R02).** The special rule fixes a future lane, endpoint response, vector or expenditure at a named boundary. Ordinary safety/admission may delay, relocate or reject a future action. A safety mechanism could rewrite or remove the relational decision; exact interface evidence is missing.

- **RL02 (R03, R04).** Origin/knowledge/time history carries the rule distinction. The current G0 operation lacks a full origin-to-completion, read-to-reinspection or simultaneous-finalization contract. Two readers can produce different legal outcomes from the same authored boundary case.

- **RL03 (R01, R05).** Finite resources and optional tools are supposed to recompose across three stages. Only refined odd representatives state a run/phase resource lifecycle; other proposals and reserve transactions are still G0. Costs, capacity release and pause behavior cannot be inherited by metaphor or assumed resets.

These are local tensions and evidence gaps. Cross-layer contradictions require the other investigators’ actual findings; this report does not invent their positions.
