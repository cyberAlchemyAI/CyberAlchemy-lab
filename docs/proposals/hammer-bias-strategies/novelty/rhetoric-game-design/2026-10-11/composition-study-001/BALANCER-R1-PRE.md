## C01: the courier must remain responsible for delivery

The proposed smallest unit has the right size: courier survival, transported amendment and attack commitment belong together. Inputs name the card, carrier, deadline and seal reserve; outputs name the handoff, expenditure and ordinary warning. Invalid amendments reject before cost, paid failure retains its seal cost, death before delivery fails, and delivery wins over same-boundary timeout. Those branches are useful closure work.

**B01 — unresolved delivery versus attack admission; closure; revise.** In `/candidates/0/rule_order/0` the card designates the next eligible sweep, but rule 4 takes the delivered lane or fallback without handling an unresolved card. Trace: issue at t=0, amend at t=2, boss reaches normal sweep admission at t=4, courier can deliver at t=6 before the t=8 deadline. Does t=4 commit fallback, wait, or classify the sweep as ineligible? A later delivery cannot overwrite an active attack. Specify the owner and disposition of this pending state, including whether any changed admission timing itself becomes a player choice. This is a written prerequisite, not encounter tuning.

**B02 — an orphaned card has no terminal branch; termination/frame; revise.** The same rule order retires the card after its physical sweep. If a phase transition removes that designated attack after delivery, an occupied active-card slot has no retirement event. Hero death ends the run, but ordinary boss defeat or stage transition is different. Declare how the card/request ends when its owner leaves the phase, and how seals and pending state cross the three-stage progression. Preserve spent costs and avoid an undisclosed refund/reset.

**B03 — target exclusion is narrower than courier preservation; hidden glue/variety; route.** `/candidates/0/manual_auto` lists a named-courier exclusion. A Mason poison area, wall obstruction or incidental reflected contact can still kill or stall it. This need not guarantee successful escort: failure is a legitimate choice. It does require distinguishing an authored target filter from a hidden protector that cancels collateral effects or repairs the route. Show a bounded policy that knowingly accepts those risks. Manual timing remains meaningful under the ordinary attack/recovery channel.

Three bosses and a mid-stage refill are named in `integration`; a feasible courier/escape arrangement is untested. That empirical uncertainty merits flag, not rejection. B01/B02 currently prevent a complete contract: **C01 block**. The reserve C01-V2 remains G0. **B04 — certificate ownership and matching; closure; defer:** its inspector prints a certificate, but pickup/transfer, turn tolerance and the first matching traversal's consumption boundary are not defined. Keep enemy consumption and transferable ownership when developing it.

## C03: the bend and the exposed endpoint must both matter

The unit preserves original damage, distance, collision and recovery while allowing prior authorship of a two-segment displacement. Costs reject invalid preparation before spending, expiry retains expenditure, and ineligible contacts do not consume it. Recording actual post-collision position prevents an invisible ideal-path solver. A shape alone would lose the threat acceptance cost; memory alone would lose the authored bend.

**B05 — zone membership lacks an owner; public interface/closure; revise.** `/candidates/1/rule_order/3` records the endpoint's named public zone, but neither inputs nor the rule define exhaustive membership, boundary ties or an endpoint outside named zones. Trace: the east leg collides exactly at an E/N boundary; choosing E can schedule a guard while choosing N can prevent it. Name the mapping and tie/fallback responsibility. Its numerical geometry can remain an assumption; the classification operation cannot.

**B06 — equal memory has no explicit scheduling consumption; closure/termination; revise.** Rule 4 says two matching endpoints cause the next eligible coverage phase to guard that zone. After the three-second hold, memory still matches. Is another phase scheduled automatically, or only on a newly resolved gesture? Two fresh matching endpoints arriving while a previous guard is locked also need an explicit pending-request disposition. Define the triggering event and what clears/replaces its request; the existing prohibition on rewriting a committed guard handles only one branch. A recurring guard is permissible if deliberately specified, not silently inferred.

**B07 — the betrayal illustration has an unpriced third preparation; resources; revise.** `/candidates/1/finite_costs` starts with two charges, exactly enough to teach matching endpoints. The illustration's later westward gesture needs a third charge unless a refill was acquired. Specify that prerequisite, or explain an exploitation using ordinary movement instead. Do not make the optional cache secretly mandatory for baseline victory. The question whether bends offer something beyond ordinary directional influence remains an unexecuted comparison.

The Bastion example carries one hero through three bosses; stage 2's poison/non-knockback pressure and a cleanse-versus-refill offer preserve meaningful acquisition possibilities on paper. Six-rule authorability and deliberate contact affordability are flagged unknowns. B05/B06 prevent unambiguous execution: **C03 block**. The reserve remains G0. **B08 — shared rest admission; closure; defer:** C03-V2 needs the disposition of two phrases teaching different rests, the selected item's absence/recovery, and exact cycle start/end ownership. Preserve the shared restriction; replacing it with unrelated boss pause and cooldown would erase the proposal.

## C05: spatial empowerment must alter threatening capacity

The expenditure boundary is stronger than G0: charge lock freezes reach/fee; misses and legal interruptions retain expenditure; no negative budget occurs; final recovery precedes seal opening; death is terminal; interrupted seal interaction leaves one opening. A lease is optional because ordinary charges also exhaust entitlement. These rules provide a credible local relation between greater immediate danger and reduced future commitments.

**B09 — the budgeted action family is inconsistent; closure/recomposition; revise.** `/candidates/2/rule_order/0` says base attacks spend entitlement, rule 2 spends it for an eligible charge, and rule 4 stops new charges. A boss with B=0 could still issue an unbudgeted ranged attack unless the phase explicitly consists only of eligible charges. Either declare that restriction or specify the finite, disclosed behavior of other actions. Also name the normal scheduling/recovery prerequisite that ensures an eligible charge can occur; “normally” cannot conceal an encounter director. This is an action taxonomy gap, not a demand for a general economy.

**B10 — stage resources and optional offers need one lifecycle; frame/hidden glue; revise.** `finite_costs` gives two collateral per phase, while `integration` offers a mid-stage tool/collateral acquisition. Identify whether collateral is initialized, carried, replenished or replaced at boss entry, and when the tool becomes available. A paused acquisition must preserve pending state under the inherited handover contract. The three-stage example can remain illustrative; its reset operation must be stated.

**B11 — nominal fee differs from expenditure near zero; feedback; revise.** With B=1, a leased charge has nominal fee 2 and empowered reach but spends only 1. The rule already closes subtraction; public feedback should distinguish nominal fee and actual spend rather than promising two tokens were removed. No rule change or refund is needed.

Manual lure, evade and seal interaction are distinct responsibilities; authored auto must express them without a source-position solver. Cheap reliable interruptions could dominate leasing, and uncontrollable boss source positions could make it useless. Those are counterexamples to tactical value, not evidence that the written relation is incoherent. B09 leaves **C05 block**. The shadow reserve remains G0. **B12 — protection may be a null liability; meaning loss; defer before development:** C05-V2 removes direct boss damage but increases protection. If protection only reduces damage, brightening supplies a longer hook surface at no relevant resistance cost. State the actual affected operation before claiming the assistance tradeoff. Do not silently substitute an ordinary grapple.
