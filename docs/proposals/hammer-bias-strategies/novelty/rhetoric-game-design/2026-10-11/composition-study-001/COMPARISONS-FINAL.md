# Operation comparisons for the final R3 proposals

Date: 11 October 2026. Version: [actual R3 contracts](CANDIDATES-R3.json). These are root comparisons of authored operations with primary descriptions and explicitly defined substitutes. Independent written-contract readiness is recorded separately by the Balancer. No game, controller or simulation was executed.

The source set remains twenty-five game entries and twenty-eight primary records. No source count is added by this analysis. Inspected feature descriptions establish positive precedents; missing details do not establish absence from the complete game.

## C01 — Custody is the dependency to compare

Waterloo documents courier-based order delivery and command delays; Strategos documents courier command linked to general position and commitment. The inspected material does not resolve hostile interception/amendment. [Official Waterloo manual](https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/369390/manuals/SLIT_SoW_Waterloo_Materials_Manual_Ebook.pdf?t=1575554183), [Strategos developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3064810/Strategos/).

Hold seal expenditure, chosen lane and scheduled attack time fixed. In the proposal, a living amended carrier delivers before admission and changes the attack; ordinary poison killing that carrier before delivery leaves the original lane. A direct targeting edit plus delay reproduces the choice of lane and time but loses its dependency on carrier survival. A competent conventional order-object system with vulnerable transport could reproduce that dependency. The inspected sources do not settle its broader existence.

The six-rule policy can preserve the courier by clearing new offensive requests. Existing projectiles, poison and already admitted attacks remain real collateral. This is a costly custody choice, not guaranteed protection. Resource carry, admission fallback and paid failure survive the final repairs.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. Strategic usefulness and broader originality remain unresolved.

## C02 — Origin memory survives equal completion positions

Riki supplies a placed magic-interference precedent. Disruptor supplies prior-position relocation and a delayed movement barrier. Baba Is You supplies mutable rule text. None of the inspected descriptions settles the exact failure-funded cast-origin duty. [Valve Riki record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=32), [Valve Disruptor record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=87), [Baba Is You developer description](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3).

Two authored histories share cast family, close time and final inside position. The first is admitted inside and never crosses outward; it fizzles. The second is admitted outside and later enters; it completes. A completion-position-only predicate cannot distinguish those histories. An origin-sensitive predicate can. The comparison demonstrates the need for that recorded state in the defined rule, not a new general spatial-memory primitive.

An inside-origin cast owns the admission triangle even after the live region expires. Three genuinely interrupted casts consume mana and finite residue credits to create it. Replacing that preparation with a paid placed region can preserve the completion law while changing the funding choices. Neither the value of deliberate failure nor preparation usability has been tested.

**Judgment:** insufficient evidence for originality; a precise origin-history and funding hypothesis worth a narrow comparison.

## C03 — A different path can still produce the same learned output

Directional influence during hitstun is established in Rivals' developer notes. ECHO describes enemy behavior updated from player actions. Hello Neighbor describes countermeasures to repeated routes. Their inspected accounts do not establish this proposal's exact movement or fixed two-entry response, and do not establish that such a relation is absent. [Rivals developer notes](https://rivalsofaether.com/love-is-in-the-aether-valentines-update-0-15-9/), [ECHO developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES), [Hello Neighbor official description](https://www.helloneighborgame.com/home-en).

The final paired toy example fixes start (0,0), displacement budget six, one charge, hit damage, pre-hit commitment timing and a wall at x=2 for 0.5≤y≤2.5. Ordered east-four then north-two movement passes below and around the wall to (4,2), in zone E. A straight heading aimed at that endpoint collides at (2,1), in W. That poor choice is not the competent comparator: straight east reaches (6,0), also E, at the same declared costs.

Consequently the ordered corner path differs literally, while competent straight control reproduces the endpoint-zone memory and E coverage output. A substitute allowing within-event direction changes can also reproduce the corner path. No distinct tactical payoff from its particular endpoint or intermediate path is established by this pair.

The active coverage owner now requires actual anchor arrival before windup, aborts on post-admission obstruction, and has a finite approach deadline. Abort retains spent gesture charges and teaching memory; it does not recreate a request. These repairs improve written closure without adding originality evidence.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients with a specified path arrangement. The present discriminator limits rather than proves its strategic novelty.

## C04 — The release origin moves while the vector remains

Nintendo's Stasis guide describes stored kinetic energy. Rogue Voltage describes energy storage and conversion. Kunkka's official description supplies a closer marked-position return, including an early return trigger. [Nintendo guide](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-runes-guide/), [Rogue Voltage](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1494560/_Rogue_Voltage/?l=english), [Valve Kunkka record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=23).

Fix immediate damage and the captured northward vector. Immediate settlement at A and settlement after withholding attacks while walking to B can meet different collision geometry and produce different endpoints. The proposal's release is calculated from current origin, so a return to a remembered capture location does not reproduce that history. A conventional timed incoming-knockback skill that permits movement, preserves the vector and releases on attack or timeout would reproduce it.

The meaningful relation is compulsory mobile consequence under restraint. Half-open arming, once-only settlement, ordinary further hits, paid cost and finite timeout are retained. A wall-truncated release is a proposed positional option; it supplies no general safety or defense superiority claim.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable settlement arrangement. Prior-art completeness, practical control and value remain unresolved.

## C05 — Spatial expenditure has a capped marginal benefit

Monster Hunter Generations documents monster stamina/fatigue, including weakened attacks and recovery through feeding. Sekiro documents posture, deflection and exposed deathblows. These establish alternate combat resources and consequences, without establishing this spatial fee. [Capcom manual](https://game.capcom.com/manual/MH_Gen/en-UK/page-46.html), [Activision mechanics guide](https://support.activision.com/sekiro/articles/sekiro-shadows-die-twice-game-mechanics).

A fixed-count exhaustion encounter can supply survival against a limited attack budget and a final claim interaction. The proposal additionally lets the player pay for a source lane whose lock-time membership alters reach and expenditure. If a six-unit budget pays two, two, one, one, four committed charges exhaust it; six ordinary one-unit charges also exhaust it. These are conditional arithmetic branches, not achieved encounter results.

At remaining budget one, the actual expenditure is one even when nominal cost is two, while full reach bonus remains. An old lease may therefore be a liability without depletion benefit. Luring the boss outside it is an authored alternative, subject to actual position and safety. A matched conventional source-dependent fatigue modifier could reproduce this relation; a fixed count alone does not include that choice.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients with a conditional spatial-fee arrangement. Useful reachability, voluntary lease uptake and enjoyment are unmeasured.

## C06 — Strengthening the substitute reveals equivalence

Baba Is You supplies physical rule editing; Gunpoint supplies editable trigger relationships; ECHO supplies updates to acquired enemy conduct. [Baba Is You](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3), [Gunpoint](https://store.steampowered.com/app/206190/Gunpoint/), [ECHO developer account](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES).

The proposal separates a gate acting on current law from a scout following a previously acquired route. A permanently fixed route cannot represent every inspection history. A conventional system that inspects the current switch, selects and caches its route, and retains it until reinspection can. Explicit version numbers are explanatory observations; removing them while retaining acquired route state preserves the operation.

The final repair also separates a common physical open-gate test from the scout's intent deadline. An unrelated hero is not constrained by the scout's clock. Third-party source crossings can help a stale scout before its deadline. These are coherent contract scopes, not additional novelty primitives.

**Judgment:** strong authored conventional equivalent. Retain as a comparative construction case and investigate experiential value independently.

## Limits, alternatives and next discriminators

All six representatives have at least two primary comparisons. The six G0 reserves retain insufficient evidence and unclosed contracts; C05-V2's undefined protection liability is explicitly deferred. The six rejected variants remain provisional resemblance judgments. No worldwide first, method efficacy, human creativity learning or audience surprise follows from these comparisons.

C02 and C04 are the first suggested discrimination targets because their proposed histories can be isolated at small scope. C03's negative endpoint-response result and C06's cache-route equivalent should remain in a future matched study. Removing them would overstate the generator's success.

The Goat Simulator lens remains relevant to another outcome: an unexpected actor, purpose or quality criterion can change experienced value with familiar mechanics. A source-equivalent operation may still support that hypothesis; it requires audience evidence rather than a claim of a new mechanical primitive.
