## C01: transport is established; hostile custody remains the hypothesis

**Current rule:** the boss's ordinary scheduler proceeds. At its designated attack admission, a delivered amended card commits its lane; an unresolved or failed card commits the original lane. Unresolved admission retires the request, without a refund or later overwrite. Ordinary damage can still kill the hostile carrier. This is the B01/B02/B03 proposal, not an independently validated encounter.

Scourge of War: Waterloo documents delayed courier command and composed messages with recipient and execution time. Strategos describes a tradeoff involving general positioning, orders and couriers. These supply closer logistics precedents than generic rewiring alone. The inspected sections do not determine whether either implements this proposal's hostile amendment/custody relation. [Waterloo manual](https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/369390/manuals/SLIT_SoW_Waterloo_Materials_Manual_Ebook.pdf?t=1575554183), [Strategos developer/publisher description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3064810/Strategos/). Initial positive comparisons GS01/GS06 remain in the G0 ledger.

**Conditional authored distinction:** hold the intended lane and attack-admission time constant. In one proposed branch, an amended courier survives and delivers before admission: the amended lane executes. In another, it dies before delivery: the original lane executes. A bare timer plus a lane selector, with no carrier-dependent cancellation state, cannot express this difference. A conventional escort objective that conditions the same selector on a vulnerable actor's survival can express it; adding “enemy” or “postal” language alone does not prove another mechanic.

Thus the next decisive question is whether preserving an actor that continues to threaten the hero imposes an additional controllable conflict beyond ordinary escort. If the carrier can be ignored, made safe or replaced by an identical risk meter, the proposed distinction weakens. If route/body/collateral choices constrain the paid delivery, those relationships deserve comparison. No such feasible encounter or authored policy has been demonstrated.

**Current judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement; greater confidence that courier-delayed instruction alone is familiar. The parent repair narrows the commitment rule, without earning a higher originality label.

## C03: countering repeated routes is established; public bounded teaching is the hypothesis

**Current rule:** a charge prepares two segments for one eligible received displacement. Actual collision-resolved endpoints enter a public two-entry zone record. Only a new eligible event can request coverage of a repeated zone; there is one occupied request/coverage slot, no static-memory retrigger, no teleport and no hidden inference. A third gestured shove requires an explicit optional refill, while ordinary movement remains available.

Hello Neighbor's official description promises countermeasures to repeated routes. That is a relevant precedent for opponents countering repeated movement, alongside the existing GS21 directional-influence and GS01 behavioral-learning comparisons. Marketing language establishes the advertised relationship; it does not identify the AI algorithm or prove runtime adaptation. [Official Hello Neighbor About/FAQ](https://www.helloneighborgame.com/home-en).

The written proposal supplies a disclosed fixed response. Calling it “learning” describes the player's causal interaction with its memory; it does not mean a model is trained. Repeating endpoints to create a known commitment is the hypothesis. Public memory and a two-entry trigger might change comprehension and planning, but are not automatically new mechanical ingredients.

**Conditional authored distinctions:** two equal latest endpoints produce a request only when the second arrives as a fresh eligible event; unchanged equal memory after coverage finishes produces none. Ordinary live directional influence and a pre-authored two-segment path also need a geometry case in which their attainable or committed routes differ. The event trace distinguishes a trigger from continuous polling, but supplies neither that geometry nor a viable strategic advantage.

A visible “guard this zone next” button coupled to a displacement charge is a strong conventional equivalent. If it recreates the same action choices, costs, opponent commitment and observation burden, endpoint teaching may add ceremony. Conversely, sacrificing a reliable escape to create an exploitable future guard is a candidate dependency to preserve until tested. The authored-policy and encounter obligations remain under B13 and the audit gate.

**Current judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. Added precedent defeats the broad claim that a monster responding to repeated routes is original. Pre-authored displacement, public history and controllable commitment remain the specific arrangement hypothesis.

## C05: the repaired ledger can differ from a fixed attack count

**Current rule:** the special phase begins with six entitlement tokens. Ordinary charges cost one. A charge locking from an active leased source lane costs two and gains reach; its actual spend is capped by remaining tokens. Pre-lock interruption spends nothing; admitted spend survives later interruption. The disclosed finite positioning/warning/recovery cycle ends in a defeat seal after budget exhaustion and final recovery. Adds and hazards remain.

The existing primary comparisons GS22 (monster fatigue), GS18 (defensive defeat openings) and GS08 (coupled player/enemy benefits) remain the positive precedents. They challenge broad claims about exhaustion, defensive victory and beneficial action worsening a threat. Their exact feature evidence and URLs remain in the initial comparison and source ledger, rather than being expanded into undocumented equivalences here.

**Conditional authored arithmetic:** assuming four charges can legally lock at eligible source positions, fees 2→1→2→1 take budget 6→4→3→1→0. Four ordinary one-fee charges instead leave two tokens. This is arithmetic from the proposal, not a simulated or feasible gameplay trace. A lease can affect more than one admitted charge while active; collateral is charged for admitting the lease, not for each attack. The example therefore does not infer collateral spend from its fee sequence.

The written transition is not identical to a strict “survive six admitted attacks” rule whenever the player can affect the two-fee admissions. It is close to an enemy stamina system with a player-influenced action price and exhaustion defeat condition. A conventional stronger-attack toggle could reproduce the fee/reach tradeoff. The spatial arrangement needs another comparison: keep all costs, durations and reach constant, and replace source-lane dependence with a direct toggle. If luring and source commitment add no reachable plan, the lane adds authoring burden without demonstrated strategic value.

The cycle repair supplies an owner, not a guarantee of luring. Repeated pre-lock interruptions can prolong the phase without spending tokens; useful leased sources can be unreachable; premature death can prevent claiming the open seal. These remain written obligations and empirical unknowns. A source-position fee is no originality proof on its own.

**Current judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable arrangement. Its ledger conditionally changes attack-count outcomes; the value and distinctiveness of spatially manipulating that ledger remain unproved.

## What changes for the generator methods

The closer precedents narrow the target of invention. Treat “courier command,” “route learning” and “exhaustion” as available ingredients, then ask whether the composition adds an operative dependency that simpler controls cannot retain. This is a reusable refinement prompt derived from these cases, not evidence that the method increases creativity.

The useful comparison has three layers: literal state-transition distinction; feasibility within the intended encounter and bounded control surface; and valued player experience. A difference at the first layer proves neither of the other two. Irony, absurdity and surprise can still carry a different experience even if a conventional mechanic reproduces the decision relation, as the earlier Goat Simulator case argues as an interpretation. That claim needs audience evidence, not renaming an equivalent mechanic.
