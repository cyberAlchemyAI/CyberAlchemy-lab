# What this construction study teaches about novelty analysis

Date: 11 October 2026. Parent synthesis from actual construction and critique. These are reusable investigation rules, not a measured improvement over ordinary ideation. Current proposal details remain versioned in the role receipts; final judgments belong to the final comparison.

| Investigation rule | Concrete application | What could defeat the claim? |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Change the dependency before selecting the effect. | C01 makes delivery by a surviving hostile actor a condition of an amended attack. | Direct targeting with an ordinary delay preserves every useful choice. |
| 2. Invert a beneficiary while retaining expenditure. | C05 empowers an enemy's committed action to alter its finite entitlement. | A matched fixed action count provides the same decisions without the spatial fee. |
| 3. Keep the loss when making failure productive. | C02 retains mana expenditure and real interruption while producing region marks. | Repeated preparation is merely a tedious purchase animation. |
| 4. Scope contradiction by state, history or position. | C02 distinguishes admission origin from completion location. | An ordinary origin-sensitive spell rule reproduces the relation; contradiction is only a description. |
| 5. Give indirection a vulnerable intermediate. | A courier, pending vector or acquired enemy route can be manipulated and can fail. | The intermediate changes neither choice nor experienced meaning. |
| 6. Turn attack into a reconstruction obligation. | A flyer defeats a maze; C06 rebuilds the dependency around current gate law and acquired routes. | The rebuild silently grants a blanket immunity or restores the old solution under another name. |
| 7. Preserve an unusual dependency through feasibility repair. | C04 keeps compulsory displacement and immediate damage while defining attack withholding. | Safety fixes erase the compulsory consequence and leave a familiar immunity. |
| 8. Remove the joke title and inspect the action. | C01's hostile custody relation remains after removing postal language. | Only presentation changes; then assess experiential novelty separately. |
| 9. Compare equal end states with different histories. | C02's inside-origin cast fizzles while an outside-origin cast can finish inside. | A competent substitute already records the necessary history. |
| 10. Strengthen the conventional substitute. | C06 is reproducible by an inspected-and-cached route plus current gate switch. | The supposed novelty disappears, a useful negative result for this candidate. |
| 11. Check marginal benefit at the boundary. | C05's remaining budget of one cannot pay two; empowerment can remain without depletion advantage. | An unqualified faster-exhaustion claim is contradicted by its own arithmetic. |
| 12. Separate invention, experience and learning. | A Goat Simulator-like unexpected purpose may matter even with familiar mechanical relations. | No audience surprise, durable appeal or delayed transfer has been measured. |

The constructive artifact is a dependency-bearing action card: trigger, actor, state/history, cost, observation, commitment, alternative and counter. Distill preserves that relation while reducing surrounding machinery. Robot Talks examines whether its rules, player authorship and meaning agree. Prior-art research supplies positive precedents; a conventional substitute tests how much additional machinery the choice needs.

Do not credit a stronger contract as a new invention merely because it has more clauses. Conversely, discovering a familiar relation need not erase an unusual purpose or valued experience. These are different questions with different evidence requirements.

The finite study produces proposals and explicit comparisons. A later controlled study must match seed, information, construction obligation and time/output budget across ordinary analysis, component operators and compositions. Human learning requires delayed unaided transfer tasks. Neither causal method superiority nor learned creativity follows from this paper exercise.
