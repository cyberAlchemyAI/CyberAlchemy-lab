## Proposed concern ownership

1. **Rules and simulation:** inspect events, state/resource ownership, cost admission, commitment, collision, expiry, interruption and stage transitions. Report missing or inconsistent branches. Exclude controller design, experience judgment and market originality.
2. **Control and authorship:** inspect whether public observations and bounded actions let a manual player or authored policy express the promised alternatives, acquisition priorities and ordinary escape. Identify concrete policy prerequisites and capacity conflicts. Exclude repairs to physics, inference about fun and market originality.
3. **Player interpretation and experience:** inspect whether feedback explains the unusual relation, whether surprise can become predictable, and whether the absurd or ironic premise survives without its title. Identify comprehension and experience hypotheses, without claiming human evidence. Exclude controller engineering, implementation choices and global originality.

Each agent, after approval, writes its own report with Key Findings (3–5 evidence-backed bullets), Gaps or Inconsistencies, Local Tensions and Questions for Synthesis. Cite exact file/line or JSON-pointer evidence and name uncertainty. Do not implement, run simulations, independently declare novelty or change another concern's contract. Evidence may overlap; questions should remain within the concern.

Root will join the three reports into cross-layer tensions, preserving what each layer holds, impact and evidence. The human then gives accepted/deferred/uncertain/rejected dispositions before changes based on that synthesis. The remaining Distill rounds use those actual dispositions.
