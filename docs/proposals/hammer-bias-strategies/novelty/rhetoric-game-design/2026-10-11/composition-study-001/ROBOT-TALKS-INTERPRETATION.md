## Key Findings

- **I01 — C01 preserves a title-independent reversal, but readable outcomes are more specific than “help an enemy.”** The written choice is preserving a dangerous carrier to commit an amended attack, or killing it and facing the ordinary attack. Parent reconciliation says the boss never waits and unresolved delivery expires at admission; collateral damage still matters. Thus the inversion survives the title: an enemy's continued danger is instrumentally useful. Interpretation needs a player to distinguish delivery success from fallback caused by death, deadline, attack admission or owner loss. Lists of public card/route/deadline/output fields establish a proposed information surface, not understanding or whether its timing is perceptible. **Evidence:** `CANDIDATES-R1-PRE.json` `/candidates/0/two_decisions`, `/candidates/0/public_inputs`, `/candidates/0/public_outputs`, `/candidates/0/meaning_destroying_split`; [reconciliation lines 9–19](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/hammer-bias-strategies/novelty/rhetoric-game-design/2026-10-11/composition-study-001/RECONCILIATION-R1-PRE.md:9); pool `/variants/0/feedback`. **Interpretation:** a coherent causal explanation is possible on paper; comprehension remains untested. **Readiness:** flag.

- **I02 — C03's visible learning has three different objects which its explanation must keep distinct.** The intended gesture, actual post-collision endpoint and coverage request are not interchangeable. ## Local Tensions

1. **LT-I01 — Discovery versus fair consequence.** The frame calls for demanding execution and possible lethal hazards, while several relationships require recognizing an initially counterintuitive benefit. Public written conditions do not establish a fair opportunity to infer them before irreversible loss. This is a design/evidence tension, not a finding that current gameplay is unfair. I01–I05 supply its specific cases.
2. **LT-I02 — Compact metaphor versus accurate causal model.** “Learns handwriting,” “escrow,” “rent” and “law” compress explanation, but the actual contracts depend on endpoint zones, damage retention, capped actual expenditure and versioned inspection. Using the compressed metaphor as the whole rule could hide the exact distinction which makes the arrangement interesting. I02–I05.
3. **LT-I03 — Surprise versus repeatable planning.** Authored autoplay requires reliable causal predicates; surprise from indefinitely obscuring those predicates would undermine planning. A discoverable expectation reversal followed by predictable operation is a possible reconciliation, not an observed experience. I01–I03/I05.
4. **LT-I04 — Claimed beneficial harm versus an undefined downside.** C05-V2 describes helping and resisting the boss, while the current reconciliation explicitly withholds an operative protection interpretation. A meaningful trade-off cannot be explained before its cost exists. I04. This is the strongest local reserve inconsistency.

These are local interpretation tensions. Cross-layer synthesis belongs to the parent and must use the other investigators' actual findings; none is a human-accepted design change.

## Questions for Synthesis

1. Which events can the rule owner expose early enough for a player to predict a consequential outcome, and which can only be explained afterwards? In particular: C01 fallback; C03 coverage denial; C04 timeout settlement; C06 inspection.
2. Can manual play and bounded authored control both distinguish the state pairs central to the relationship, without their observation surfaces implying different rules? The control investigator owns feasibility and capacity; this concern owns intended meaning.
3. Does a proposed mechanic still offer an intelligible alternative after the title and borrowed metaphor are removed? The twelve-row audit identifies retained relationships but cannot demonstrate player plans or enjoyment.
4. Which reserves remain useful experimental directions while unclosed, and which claimed interpretations should be deferred until an operative downside or matching rule is defined? C01-V2, C03-V2 and C05-V2 require explicit restraint.
5. Can a conventional substitute reproduce the player's explanation as well as the proposed action choice? Title-free diagrams and outcome predictions could supplement the still-unexecuted timer, directional-influence and fixed-count comparisons.
6. Which result is being claimed: a new generator construction, a new relation relative to an inspected set, a surprising first encounter, sustained strategic depth, or verbal humor? The artifacts support written construction; the others need their own evidence.

## Proposed interpretation tests — unexecuted

- **PT-I01, causal prediction:** give a title-free rule card and two minimally different traces. Ask for the next visible outcome and cost: courier arrives before versus after admission; same planned gesture ends in different actual zones; lease spends nominal two at remaining one; scout has old versus new read version. Predictions assess rule understanding, not fun or originality.
- **PT-I02, metaphor comparison:** compare the same full rule explanation with and without the borrowed label. Record what respondents say changes when helping an enemy or withholding an action. This tests whether the label assists understanding or supplies the whole apparent novelty. No participants, assignment or results are claimed.
- **PT-I03, expectation then transfer:** before the explanation, record the predicted effect of preserving, strengthening or waiting; after it, ask for a new, unaided outcome under a changed layout. Initial surprise, immediate comprehension and transfer would be separate measures. It does not by itself establish lasting creativity learning.
