## C02: origin history is an operative state distinction

Riki's documented area interference, Disruptor's spatial-history relocation/barrier descriptions, and Baba Is You's mutable rules supply positive precedents. They defeat claims that a magic-interfering region, spatial history or editable law is individually unprecedented. Their inspected descriptions do not resolve the specific cast-origin-and-exit obligation. [Valve Riki record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=32), [Valve Disruptor record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=87), [Baba Is You developer description](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3).

The actual proposal now records origin at admission, latches a real outward crossing and retains that obligation through region expiry. A conditional comparison can hold cast family, close time and final inside position constant. In one authored history, the cast starts inside and never exits: it fizzles. In another, it starts outside and moves inside during its movable commitment: it completes. A rule checking only position at completion cannot distinguish these histories. A rule that records origin can; adding that memory to the substitute reproduces this part of the proposal.

The funding relation also changes the choice: three genuine, costly interruptions consume finite residue credits before the region exists. It is not free protection purchased by labeling movement a failure. R1 allows a legally admitted player cancellation after windup as one actual interruption. Whether deliberate cancellation is an interesting preparation cost, or mostly repetitive bookkeeping, remains unproved.

**Judgment:** insufficient evidence for a mechanical originality claim; explicit origin-memory hypothesis. The state distinction is written, while viable preparation, practical six-rule expression and valued outcomes still need critique and empirical work. The region's triangular shape does not establish originality.

## C04: settlement origin differs from a remembered return position

Stored kinetic energy and deferred resource use are established in the inspected Nintendo and Rogue Voltage descriptions. Valve's marked-position return adds a closer delayed-movement precedent. [Nintendo rune guide](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-runes-guide/), [Rogue Voltage](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1494560/_Rogue_Voltage/?l=english), [Valve Kunkka record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=23).

R1 now preserves immediate hit damage, one vector and one settlement owner. A new attack admission or timeout applies that vector from the recipient's current position, using current collisions. It does not return to the capture position. Already admitted attacks are distinguished from new attack admissions. Further hits remain ordinary and can ruin the planned origin.

Compare two authored histories with the same captured vector: settle immediately at A, or withhold attacks and walk to B before settlement. Different collision geometry can produce different endpoints. A fixed-origin delayed shove or remembered-position return does not express that exact distinction. A conventional timed incoming-knockback skill that permits movement and attack-triggered release can reproduce it; its existence elsewhere is unresolved by these sources.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients with an identifiable, narrower arrangement. The real hypothesis is movable compulsory consequence under a cost of restraint. It does not become novel because the metaphor says debt. A safe wall-truncated release is a legal positional choice in the proposal, not evidence of general safety or superior defense.

## C06: rule version is not automatically a new mechanic

Physical rule text, editable trigger relationships and updated enemy conduct have documented precedents. [Baba Is You](https://hempuli.com/gamelist/index.php?rule=id&ruleid=3), [Gunpoint](https://store.steampowered.com/app/206190/Gunpoint/), [ECHO developer account](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/become-your-own-worst-enemy-in-echo?lang=es-ES).

R1 makes an acquired clause select one of two route tables at READ completion. The world checks the current source clause on actual crossing; the committed scout cannot switch its route until reinspection. Thus two scouts at the same current-world state can follow different routes if their acquired clauses differ. A single permanently fixed route cannot express both histories.

A stronger conventional substitute can: at inspection, select and cache a route from the current switch state; retain that route until the next inspection while the switch continues to control the gate. Removing the explicit version number while retaining the acquired clause/route also preserves the execution. This exposes a limit: version labels help explain stale knowledge but are not themselves an additional mechanical primitive. The proposed arrangement is close to a stateful gate-and-route puzzle.

The counterbranch in the proposal matters: another actor can cross the current NORTH source and open the gate for the stale scout. Acquired information does not exempt it from current physics. Whether visible inspection and public planning create a valued experience beyond the conventional substitute remains an audience and encounter question.

**Judgment:** familiar ingredients, explicit arrangement, strong authored conventional equivalent. No mechanically unprecedented status is supported. Preserve it as a comparative construction case rather than upgrade its novelty label because the event contract is longer.
