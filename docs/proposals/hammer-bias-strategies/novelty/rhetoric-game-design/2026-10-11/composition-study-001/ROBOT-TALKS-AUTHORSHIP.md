## Key Findings

- **A01 — Naming observations does not establish a six-rule policy.** The inherited editor permits six ordered rules with a small predicate set, an action template and an explicit fallback; it forbids path following from inventing dodges, healing or upgrades. All three pre-audit representatives explicitly mark editor fit pending. The parent then requires lethal escape, reserves, poison/recovery, special operations and acquisition ranking. C02/C04/C06 name additional auto responsibilities without a bounded construction. The unresolved obligation is to show both a special-choice policy and an ordinary fallback within the declared grammar, including priority when their conditions overlap. A bundled “do the strategy” action would hide the authoring work rather than establish fit. Acquisition ranking is a separate paused-offer responsibility in the inherited contract; its mere presence must not be counted as a seventh tactical rule. **Evidence:** `../GAME-DESIGN.md:62–64, 83–89`; `CANDIDATES-R1-PRE.json#/candidates/0/manual_auto/editor_fit`, `#/candidates/1/manual_auto/editor_fit`, `#/candidates/2/manual_auto/editor_fit`; `RECONCILIATION-R1-PRE.md:53`; `GENERATION-POOL.json#/variants/3/manual_auto`, `#/variants/9/manual_auto`, `#/variants/15/manual_auto`. **Readiness implication:** policy-expression closure remains unproved, distinct from human editor usability.

- **A02 — C01 custody and C06 stale knowledge require actionable time windows, beyond public status labels.** C01 exposes a delivery deadline and eligible sweep admission, while the parent scheduler proceeds at ordinary admission even when delivery is unresolved. Spending a seal is consequently a decision about whether interception, amendment and delivery can finish before admission; the eight-second card deadline alone is insufficient to establish that opportunity. A policy also has to preserve a hostile carrier while ordinary poison, area damage, reflection and walls remain active. Target exclusion prevents deliberate selection, not those effects. C06 exposes the current clause, last-read clause and locked route, but a deliberate after-read amendment also needs an observable inspection/commitment window and access to the sign within it. These are control prerequisites, not requests to postpone attacks, protect couriers or freeze scouts. **Evidence:** `CANDIDATES-R1-PRE.json#/candidates/0/public_inputs`, `#/candidates/0/manual_auto`; `RECONCILIATION-R1-PRE.md:9–19`; `GENERATION-POOL.json#/variants/15/concrete_rule`, `#/variants/15/feedback`, `#/variants/15/manual_auto`. **Readiness implication:** neither public visibility nor a named command proves a feasible custody/version strategy under the shared command-admission rules.

- **A03 — Deliberate failure and hostile contact need explicit control prerequisites.** C02 pays three interrupted casts to construct a region, then asks the hero to start a cast inside and cross out within the stated recovery window. Its policy must identify eligible interruption opportunities, the three distinct marks, cast origin, pending exit obligation, remaining region/cast time and an authored crossing segment. No specified command presently guarantees those interruptions or produces that triangle. C03 is more concrete: an authored gesture still pays the ordinary accepted hit, can be collision-truncated and teaches the actual endpoint. Its two initial charges can teach a repeated zone; a third gestured betrayal requires a previously acquired refill. A policy therefore needs an intentional-contact condition with health/stamina reserve, plus an ordinary-movement exploitation branch when no charge remains. A generic “avoid every hit” fallback cannot demonstrate intentional teaching; a generic “accept every eligible hit” cannot demonstrate its survival reserve. **Evidence:** `GENERATION-POOL.json#/variants/3/concrete_rule`, `#/variants/3/cost`, `#/variants/3/manual_auto`; `CANDIDATES-R1-PRE.json#/candidates/1/rule_order/1`, `#/candidates/1/rule_order/2`, `#/candidates/1/manual_auto`; `RECONCILIATION-R1-PRE.md:23–35`. **Readiness implication:** physical outcomes being defined does not prove the player can intentionally obtain and exploit them with the available commands and observations.

- **A04 — C04's withholding operation conflicts with an ordinary attack fallback unless that responsibility is authored.** The escrow candidate preserves immediate damage and releases the stored shove on the next attack or four-second timeout. Moving and guarding do not release it. An otherwise-attack fallback would settle the debt before the proposed relocation; the inherited plan’s nearest-target instruction alone says nothing about withholding. Conversely, indefinitely withholding attack is not available because timeout still settles it. The control obligation is to express withholding while moving/guarding, account for already admitted actions and pending requests, and restore ordinary offense after settlement or failed admission. Its public vector, timer and collision preview are useful prerequisites but do not establish a route that finishes safely within the hold. The retort reserve similarly needs an explicit choice to release or wait for oldest-record erasure while other survival actions continue. **Evidence:** `GENERATION-POOL.json#/variants/9/concrete_rule`, `#/variants/9/feedback`, `#/variants/9/manual_auto`; `#/variants/10/concrete_rule`, `#/variants/10/manual_auto`; `../GAME-DESIGN.md:73, 83–85`. **Readiness implication:** a restraint mechanic must have a restraint responsibility in the authored policy; ordinary path following or a safety correction cannot supply it silently.

- **A05 — Cross-stage resources and optional items change which special choices are available.** C01 seals, C03 gesture charges and C05 collateral now carry across stages with capacity two, rather than resetting at every boss. The policy must still function after early expenditure, an absent refill, or selecting cleanse instead of the special offer. For C05, nominal fee two at one remaining boss token spends only one, while increased reach remains; the extra collateral then does not reduce the number of remaining budgeted charges. A lease policy therefore needs the actual remaining entitlement and its chosen reason to accept the extra risk, rather than treating every available lease as acceleration. The inherited full-auto acquisition contract allows an authored ranking and deterministic fallback, but none is supplied for these representatives. Relevant reserves deepen this issue: C03-V2 can delay a selected item's activation until a learned rest; C01-V2 and C06-V2 add carried one-use permissions; C05-V2's operative protection cost is still unresolved. They are not ready-made solutions to the representative's control gap. **Evidence:** `RECONCILIATION-R1-PRE.md:13, 25, 31, 45–49`; `CANDIDATES-R1-PRE.json#/candidates/2/manual_auto`, `#/candidates/2/two_decisions`; `../GAME-DESIGN.md:52–56, 89`; `GENERATION-POOL.json#/variants/1/manual_auto`, `#/variants/7/concrete_rule`, `#/variants/13/player_choice`, `#/variants/16/manual_auto`. **Readiness implication:** optionality preserves an ordinary alternative on paper, but neither meaningful acquisition priorities nor three-stage authored continuity is demonstrated.

These are missing demonstrations, not claims of impossibility. The editor's exact supported predicate/action templates for the new operations are not enumerated. No quantitative timing feasibility or six-rule construction has been executed. A manually reachable action is not automatically expressible by the small editor, and a written policy fit would not establish intuitive authoring by humans.

## Local Tensions

1. **Ordinary survival priority versus deliberate strategic exposure.** C02/C03 require some failure/contact and C01 requires dangerous custody, whereas the ordinary fallback emphasizes warned escape and reserve preservation. Those priorities can coexist under distinct explicit conditions; their overlap has not been resolved in a construction. This is an authoring burden, not evidence that deliberate exposure must dominate.
2. **Public information versus operational opportunity.** Knowing a card is unresolved, a scout's rule is stale or a gesture endpoint is guarded does not by itself provide an admissible action and reachable authored segment before the relevant commitment. Source facts and action opportunity must be distinguished.
3. **Compact editor versus distributed strategy responsibilities.** Six rules constrain expression, while interruption, exclusion, withholding, acquisition and fallback add responsibilities. Fit cannot be established by counting names or by allowing an unspecified macro to make several tactical decisions.
4. **Local special choice versus a continuing three-stage plan.** Finite resources carry across stages; a locally attractive choice can remove later expressive options. The ordinary alternative remains important, but its existence is not proof that acquiring the special tool creates a useful plan.

## Questions for Synthesis

- Which relations have enough disclosed command and observation structure to support a bounded authored choice now, and which still require a new player-facing operation before a proposal can be composed?
- Does the rules concern supply a finite, publicly knowable opportunity for C01 custody, C02 exit casting, C04 deferred settlement and C06 post-inspection amendment, without a hidden scheduler or controller filling it in?
- Can the interpretation concern make causal failure understandable while preserving the player's responsibility for finite spending, harmful collateral and late voluntary commitment?
- What constitutes an honest six-rule fit for this study: named supported templates and fallbacks, without strategy-solving macros, and with paused acquisition ranking counted separately?
- Which reserve uncertainties warrant targeted follow-up before promotion, and which can remain deferred while the representative receives its remaining Distill rounds?
