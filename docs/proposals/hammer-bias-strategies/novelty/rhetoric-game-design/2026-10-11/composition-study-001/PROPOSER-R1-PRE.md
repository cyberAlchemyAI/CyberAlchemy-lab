## C01: a dangerous courier must survive your amended order

**Sequence:** MG04 actor transfer → MG07 contestable information → MG16 playable absurdity. The broad capability is influencing exposed enemy commitments. Its workflow is issue card, intercept, amend, deliver or fail, then execute an ordinary warned sweep. The **smallest coherent relational unit** is one physical courier, one amendable lane card and one future boss attack joined by delivery. It owns the handoff's outcome, not global pathfinding or the hero's strategy.

Public inputs are the card ID/lane, carrier position/health/route, deadline, amendment status, hero proximity and seal reserve, plus boss attack eligibility. Outputs are resource expenditure, card state, delivery/failure, queued lane and the ordinary boss warning. One active card, one amendment per card and capacity two seals bound the state. An optional mid-stage choice refills one seal, capped at capacity.

**Rule order:** Issue a card for the next eligible sweep, recording its ordinary lane and an eight-second deadline. Admit amendment only near its living carrier, before deadline, with a seal and no prior amendment; invalid requests reject before cost. Spend one seal and swap east/west. Resolve ordinary movement, damage and deaths. A surviving courier reaching the lectern by deadline delivers and freezes the lane; same-boundary arrival is checked before timeout. Death, timeout or failed routing selects the original lane. The next normal eligible sweep uses that result, with ordinary warning and hazard admission. It cannot overwrite an active attack. Resolve the sweep and retire the card once.

The hero makes two distinct decisions: preserve a dangerous courier versus kill it and accept the fallback; and redirect toward cache-side adds versus preserve a planned escape while leaving those adds alive. These differences require encounter construction, not just different lane labels. For example, seals 2 become 1 when east is changed to west. Delivery at second 6 freezes west. Killing the carrier at second 5 instead retains east with seals still 1. It never skips the boss attack or refunds the failed plan.

Manual play handles interception, target restraint and the later dodge/guard. Auto requires public card/deadline states, a courier exclusion while delivery is pending, an authored intercept route and an explicit fallback. Those are proposed editor additions; six-rule fit is unproved. A hidden repair route would erase the player's courier responsibility.

Mason illustrates integration: stage 1 exposes a simple delivery path; stage 2 adds a flanker and walls that can protect or stall it; stage 3 pressures timing near a boss phase transition. The refill competes with defense. World motion/death supplies delivery inputs; the rule emits an attack request; encounter admission issues its warning; ordinary commands supply response. No strategy manager chooses those commands.

Splitting away survival of the carrier or delivery dependence reduces the concept to direct lane selection plus a timer. Extend only a typed lane family or courier class after this unit is understandable. Expire it if delivery conflicts with feasible warnings, policy authors cannot preserve the carrier deliberately, or the matched timer control reproduces the useful choices.

**Reserve C01-V2 stays strange:** the pursuer's two turns print a transferable one-use gate certificate that either side can consume. Keep transfer and enemy consumption. Compare a two-turn movement lock plus enemy-operated gate; eliminate the certificate layer if neither extra relationship changes a viable choice.

## C03: write your incoming knockback, then expose its endpoint

**Sequence:** MG10 expressive structure → MG08 learned expectation → MG05 coupled liability. The capability is authoring a threat's consequence while exposing a learnable commitment. Its workflow is prepare gesture, accept hit, resolve displacement, record endpoint, then admit enemy coverage. The **smallest coherent unit** combines a two-segment gesture, an eligible hostile knockback and a visible endpoint-memory commitment. Removing hit cost makes a dash; removing endpoint exposure removes its coupled liability.

Public inputs are two directions and their length fractions, pending expiry, admitted hit/displacement magnitude, current geometry, actual endpoint zone, last two zones and current enemy coverage. Outputs are preparation admission/cost, resolved/truncated movement, memory and a declared future coverage warning. Capacity two charges, one pending gesture, two remembered endpoints and a ten-second preparation lifetime bound the state. Durations are assumptions.

**Rule order:** Admit a legal drawn gesture or equivalent segment choice; fractions sum to one and directions come from the disclosed set. Reject invalid input before cost; spend a charge and reject replacement while occupied. Resolve a hostile hit, prevention, HP loss and death normally. Rejected contact, poison, terrain/self damage and zero-knockback events do not consume the gesture. If the hero survives eligible displacement, consume it and use the attack's original distance budget along the segments. Collisions truncate normally; no invulnerability or recovery reset is granted. Record the actual named endpoint zone, including truncation. Two matching endpoints cause the enemy's next eligible coverage phase to guard that zone with visible windup and finite hold. Later memory updates do not move an already locked guard.

One decision is accepting the paid knockback to traverse a bend versus dodging and using ordinary movement. Another is deliberately repeating an endpoint to draw the future guard versus selecting an unremembered region. Learning alone is familiar. The proposed relationship is exposure caused by steering a hostile displacement, followed by an exploitable declared response.

An arithmetic illustration: distance 6 becomes east 4 then north 2. A wall after east 3 truncates at that actual zone, which still enters memory. Two resolved endpoints in E yield a warned E guard. A later westward gesture does not retroactively move that commitment. New lethal warnings must account for already committed compulsory motion; a later voluntary accepted hit can still create danger.

Manual drawing or segment selection has preparation and contact timing. Auto requires pending shape/expiry, eligible attack magnitude, memory zones and committed coverage, with explicit contact/reserve/alternate-route rules. It receives no hidden endpoint solver or simulated human mistakes. These observation additions and their fit beside survival/recovery are untested.

Bastion illustrates one-hero integration: stage 1 offers recognizable knockback; stage 2 introduces dangerous poison/ranged events that do not activate the tool; stage 3 exposes two-zone coverage. A mid-stage refill competes with cleanse. The authoring interface emits a segment contract; world resolution supplies actual hit/motion; enemy memory emits a coverage request; ordinary command selection owns the next action.

Extend by one gesture family or zone layout only after this version is learnable. Defer continuous drawing inference and persistent adaptive AI. Expire it if endpoint meaning becomes unstable, useful knockback disappears, deliberate teaching cannot be authored, or directional-dodge controls reproduce its decisions.

**Reserve C03-V2 stays strange:** a learned rest jointly determines a boss pause and the hero's item legality. Preserve two teaching phrases, one pending cycle and the shared restriction. Compare independently selected pause and item lock; eliminate shared grammar if it only adds waiting or changes no viable plan. No damage-buff substitute is introduced.

## C05: empower attacks to consume the boss's entitlement faster

**Sequence:** MG13 remove staple → MG16 playable absurdity → MG01 reverse benefit. The capability is turning an enemy benefit into loss of future threatening capacity. Workflow: lease a source lane, influence a charge commitment, survive increased reach, exhaust its budget, claim a defeat seal. The **smallest relational unit** joins finite attack entitlement, selected lane and conditional empowered fee. Budget alone becomes a fixed survival clock; a fee without empowerment becomes a shortcut purchase.

Public inputs are remaining entitlement, marked source lanes, boss position, lease/expiry, collateral, locked attack/recovery and seal state. Outputs are admitted lease cost, frozen reach/fee, token expenditure, expiry, seal opening and one boss-defeat result. Proposed values are six boss tokens, one normal fee, two leased fee, two collateral and one eight-second active lease. Direct hero damage to this disclosed boss phase is disabled; ordinary actions against adds remain.

**Rule order:** Admit one legal lease before cost, spending collateral without cancel/refill exploit. When the boss normally locks a charge, inspect its source position. A leased source adds two reach units and fee two; otherwise ordinary reach and fee one. Spend the smaller of remaining tokens and fee once; freeze both reach and fee at lock. Expiry cannot undo them. Resolve charge, damage, hazards and recovery normally. A miss or legal interruption keeps expenditure; interruption enters declared recovery. At zero, after final attack recovery, stop new charges and open the defeat seal. Adds/hazards continue. The hero must complete an ordinary interaction there; interruption leaves it open, death ends the run. Completion defeats the boss once and permits progression without healing or duplicate rewards.

The first decision is fewer empowered attacks versus more ordinary attacks. The second is leasing a cache-adjacent source versus a safer far source with extra travel. With B=6, fees 2,1,2,1 yield states 4,3,1,0 in four commitments, while six normal commitments also open the seal. This illustrates accounting only. If the player cannot influence where those attacks start, the spatial proposal has failed rather than become novel automatically.

Manual play controls lure, longer-reach response and final interaction. Auto needs source-position, locked-fee, lease and seal observations, plus an authored approach, escape and interaction route. Policy fit and affordable responses remain pending. Veil illustrates the chosen hero: mobile magic/poison handles adds; stage 1 introduces source lanes, stage 2 adds poison/travel pressure, stage 3 changes the disclosed reach response. Mid-stage collateral competes with cleanse. Optional leasing accelerates the phase; normal attacks already exhaust it, so no particular item is mandatory.

Encounter admission owns the frozen attack and fee; world resolution owns its physical harm; exhausted-state resolution owns seal availability; progression consumes one defeat result. This proposed boss-resolution change requires independent review against the user's intended outcome. Extend one eligible attack family or lane layout only; defer a general currency market. Expire if leasing is always dominant/useless, source position is uncontrollable, or fixed-count survival reproduces the plans.

**Reserve C05-V2 stays strange:** brightening protects the boss while enlarging a grabbable shadow. Retain protection, bounded hooks and three cuff targets. Compare a fixed shadow handle and ordinary hook under matched cost/protection. Reject the light coupling if it changes no useful positioning choice.
