## What the investigators establish

R01 finds that the parent repairs retain meaningful commitments: delivery-dependent amendment, fresh-event endpoint learning and charge-lock expenditure. I01–I05 find that the main relationships survive removing their titles. That supports preserving them for further construction; it does not establish that players understand, enjoy or find them surprising.

The original Balancer's block applies to original R1 contracts. The parent flag applies to proposed repairs. These are different versions, so their verdicts do not constitute a cross-layer contradiction. G0 omissions are also not failed completed rounds. This synthesis keeps those distinctions intact.

No role demonstrates verbal sarcasm as a mechanic, vocal quality as a damage measure, or a measured surprise response. Those directions remain research possibilities. The current transformations principally use expectation reversal, scoped contradiction and manipulable intermediates.
