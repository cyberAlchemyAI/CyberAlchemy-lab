# What is new in the proposals, and what remains unproved

Date: 11 October 2026. **Scope: existing written contracts after independent investigation, without synthesis-based revisions.** This is a parent comparison. It draws on twenty-five game entries and twenty-eight primary source records. Three investigator turns inspect the proposals; they are not independent originality judges. No game was played or simulated and no audience response was measured.

“New” has several different denominators here. Eighteen drafts were constructed for this study. A proposed arrangement can differ from a documented feature while using familiar ingredients. That does not establish that it differs from every operation in that game, still less from every game. Experienced novelty, strategic value and usefulness of the generator methods need separate evidence.

## The closer displacement precedent changes the claim

Valve's Kunkka description marks a hero's position for a later return, with an earlier return trigger available to the caster. Consequently, delayed position-dependent forced movement is already a documented ingredient. C04's candidate distinction concerns the carried vector and its current settlement origin, the recipient's attack-withholding control, and the retained compulsory consequence. The short ability description does not resolve every cancellation, death or collision interaction. [Official Kunkka ability record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=23).

Valve's Disruptor descriptions also document relocation based on prior position and a delayed barrier that constrains enemy passage. Those are positive precedents for spatial history and constrained movement. They do not establish C02's cast-origin completion rule as original, or show an exact equivalent. [Official Disruptor ability record](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=87).

## Reserves, humor and unexpected purpose

Six unusual reserves remain available: a pursuit-generated exit certificate, failure-funded opposing-traffic retreat, a shared learned musical omission, a recipe altered by waiting, protective light enlarging a shadow handle, and a stolen mutable bypass permission. Their novelty evidence is insufficient. The investigators identify unclosed matching, timing, ownership or liability contracts. C05-V2 especially has no defined protective operation in its damage-disabled phase; the metaphor cannot supply that cost. Keeping it available is different from promoting it as a completed invention.

The principal novelty hypotheses use expectation reversal, scoped contradiction and indirection. Verbal sarcasm has not been demonstrated by them. A phrase/rest rule is not a system that measures a person's singing quality. Public endpoint memory is not a trained adaptive model. These distinctions prevent an ambitious research direction from becoming an unsupported implementation claim.

The [Goat Simulator case](../GOAT-SIMULATOR-CASE.md) remains relevant to novelty of purpose, premise and experience. A familiar action relation can acquire a surprising use or comic frame. Whether that frame is effective requires audience evidence; a mechanical substitute comparison alone cannot decide it. Conversely, an absurd title alone cannot prove a changed choice. These are different questions to retain in the research.

## What Robot Talks contributes to the generator

The independent findings reveal which dependencies refinement might accidentally remove. Making the courier safe removes dangerous custody; silently moving a warning can change commitment; dodging every hit prevents deliberate shove authorship; ordinary attack fallback cancels planned restraint; unrestricted refunds eliminate a finite liability. These are written construction risks, not observed changes or results of accepted human dispositions.

A reusable evaluation sequence follows from the cases: identify the causal relation, remove its title, compare a conventional substitute, construct legal operation and policy witnesses, then test experienced surprise and usefulness separately. The present study demonstrates neither that this sequence is universally best nor that the method combinations outperform ordinary analysis. Initial seeds, selectively developed variants and arriving prior-art information confound order comparisons.
