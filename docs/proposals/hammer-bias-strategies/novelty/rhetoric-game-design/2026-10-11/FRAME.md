# Research and game-design frame

Date: 11 October 2026. Status: research and proposed design; no game implementation or gameplay evidence.

## Confirmed intent

Extend the existing hammer-bias research with irony, sarcasm, surprise, **contradictions**, and indirection. Use a concrete strategy-game design to investigate whether these directions create different player decisions and mechanisms.

The user clarified that the player chooses **one character from a roster of three**, rather than deploying a party. The deployed character survives **three stages, each ending with a boss**. Items are acquired during stages; XP and skill-tree choices change the build. Manual control rewards execution within a strategy, with a Soulslike mentality. Autoplay rewards creative preparation, movement plans and careful choices. Preserve damage reduction, magic, poison, slow, knockback, movement patterns, items, skill improvement, XP growth and harmful random map events, including possible instant kills.

User examples: a hazard-avoiding movement plan that accepts monster hits and uses reflection; a maze that funnels monsters; later levels that challenge those strategies; singing whose quality changes damage. Preserve unusual ideas while making costs, triggers, counterplay and observations explicit.

## Working assumptions

Top-down single-player arenas; continuous movement; roster choice fixed for the run; temporary run progression; authoring through a small ordered-rule editor. These are proposed starting choices, not further user decisions. A microphone is optional. Voice quality means calibrated pitch/rhythm performance, not aesthetic judgment. A non-microphone input may supply the same game value.

## Bounded method

Distill Deep continues the user's chosen depth: two proposal tracks, three recursive proposal/critique rounds, then parent reconciliation. Track A: authored tactics and manual execution. Track B: causal rhetoric and unusual interactions. Actual Proposer and Balancer subagents write their own bounded trace files. Parent owns research, reconciliation and report editing. Role conversations produce design evidence only; they do not measure fun, balance, human creativity or gameplay efficacy.

Two lanes remain separate until a construction has a causal contract: the evidence lane preserves claims, constraints and uncertainty; the construction lane changes an assumption and proposes an operation. Distillation must carry triggering conditions, costs, consequences, counters and a defeating observation into the next role.
