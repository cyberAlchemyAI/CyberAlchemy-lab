Two mechanical summaries exceeded the 350-word soft allowance by eight words each; their first saves were retained and charged. Total saved-output burden was 32,426 words.

No raw proposal was fully qualified. The mechanical block retains uncertain feasibility. In the workflow block the intact interface has agreed newness, family confirmation, differing observations and fit, but support remains unresolved. The scalar review resolved ten of thirteen differences, leaving three support labels uncertain and all ten family disagreements untouched. The broken interface has partial resource fit/contract because it proposes inspecting an unavailable record. This does not demonstrate interface synergy or an intact-versus-broken causal effect.

Original raw snapshots and post-review carry remain exact. The scalar resolution is a separate pre-compression assessment, charged as one actual role; it did not rewrite original grades or family links. There were no fresh post-review disputes or summary additions.

All four original held-out blocks and all six arms are prospectively admitted for informative confirmation. This is an unresolved pilot question, not a favorable efficacy result. Independent completion coverage audit is still pending, and the thirteen-study goal remains active.
