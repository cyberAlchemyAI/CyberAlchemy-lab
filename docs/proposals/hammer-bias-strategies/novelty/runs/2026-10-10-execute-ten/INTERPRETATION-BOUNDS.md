# Prospective interpretation boundaries

This study compares assigned process prompts over common case facts and shared causal-inverter/dependency-attacker concern reports. Ordinary and generic controls also receive those reports. Extra strategy comparisons measure the added assigned processing policy over this shared scaffolding. They cannot isolate the entire inversion, attack, Robot Talks or Distill component against an unassisted baseline.

Before launch, stage1 instructions for A and C must be identical apart from administrative IDs; likewise B and D. Upstream stages must not be told the downstream summary/residue routing. Every condition nonetheless uses independently generated stage1 and Distill artifacts. The comparison is not an ablation that feeds the same single Distill artifact's two branches to two builders. Source hypotheses, preservation and new construction remain different claims.

The family bounds encode disagreement and unknown equivalence/feasibility judgments. They are not statistical confidence intervals and do not estimate sampling uncertainty across repeated model runs. Four synthetic cases support exploratory local comparison. Additional qualified cards or a positive local contrast alone cannot establish general superiority.

The maximum native capacity fixture checks all532 output rows. Its short synthetic source descriptions do not test the maximum possible scored review input context. Actual review input/output sizes are recorded; any capacity/protocol failure remains visible and annuls numerical superiority claims. No true physical intervention, actual human workflow benefit or usable oracle is supplied in this study.

Administrative reviewer labels are hidden; operational content can suggest a strategy. Full semantic blinding is not established. Exact context sizes and model-internal compute are not equalized. Supplied archives and concurrent controls define local newness; global originality and whole canonical skill efficacy are outside scope.

The secondary diagnostic was declared before any scored call to identify qualified operations judged distinct from all qualified ordinary/generic control representatives. Unknown relations give no such credit. This distinguishes complementary operations from a larger number of probes while preserving the primary outcome and planned184 calls.
