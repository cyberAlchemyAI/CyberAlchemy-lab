# Prospective meaning checks

This is a casebound probe-specification study. The supplied case facts are synthetic. A prediction, construction, causal account or feedback path is a hypothesis unless supplied as an observation. A source ID does not convert speculation into measured evidence.

Qualification requires the protected outcome and hard constraints, available indispensable resources, genuinely different candidate/rival predictions on the proposed measurement, a clear predeclared defeat condition, and faithful use of supplied facts. Both independent assessors must affirm these requirements. Newness additionally requires both to judge the card distinct from every supplied ordinary archive entry. Unknown archive relations remain unresolved. Fluent wording and extra candidates provide no novelty credit.

Example failures: ban a tool word while keeping its operating dependency; rename an ordinary archive intervention; list two accounts that predict the same observed result; rely on an unavailable sensor without acquisition; erase a hard constraint; label a proposed feedback path observed; move labor to another actor while silently omitting that burden. Such cards may remain useful questions but do not qualify as feasible separating probes.

Example legitimate negative: zero cards plus explicit sourcebound no-opening explanation is valid negative output. A missing or invalid stage is a protocol failure and gates dependents; it is not a zero-performance result. The untouched cases still run after negative development results.

Example separating probe: vary one condition under fixed outcome and nuisance controls, specify the observable and opposite or materially nonoverlapping predictions, state what available resource records it, and declare a result that weakens the new account. A proposed measurement does not establish physical benefit or correctness of the account.

Equivalent relation means the same manipulated dependency and discriminating observation despite wording changes. Distinct requires an identifiable operational difference; differences of formatting, emphases, generic uncertainty or actor naming alone do not suffice. Unknown is permitted when detail cannot establish either relation. Do not force uncertain pairwise relations into transitive families.

All candidate/archive and candidate/candidate pair rows must be covered exactly once per independent assessment. Preserve disagreements. Contrast intervals derive from conservative family bounds; missing assessment or protocol failure leaves numerical contrasts null. No post-result rubric changes, scored retries, case replacement or physical/human benefit claims.
