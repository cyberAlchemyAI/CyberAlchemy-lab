# Interpretation boundary for the academic twenty

Prepared by root during execution. These are design implications and future research proposals, not scored outputs, completed experiments or an assessment of pending cards.

## What this execution can resolve

The native study can check whether an investigator actually changes a description, grammar, ordering, search allocation, retained procedure or information route; whether an independently invoked critic preserves the exact obligation; and whether a fresh builder reconstructs a supported operation. It can compare these outputs with explicitly rerun earlier instructions and ordinary controls, retaining missingness and disagreement. Finite replay checks stipulated action semantics, not real materials or human cognition.

## Why a tie needs careful interpretation

J fixtures disclose the whole supported component/property table, including alternative mechanisms. W fixtures explicitly authorize caching and list every required claim field. Strong ordinary controls can therefore discover every admissible alternative and attain the workload floor. A tie can show that a method adds no option on this disclosed model. It cannot show that people facing functional fixedness would already perceive the same affordances, that creativity is unlearnable, or that the complete method never helps. Conversely, a method's unusual terminology, different final choice or extra separating probe cannot establish a newly created mechanism.

Novelty relative to an earlier final choice differs from novelty relative to its proposal pool and critique. Retrieval of a stored schema differs from discovery; choosing a candidate differs from generating it; known-property recombination differs from inventing a property; admissibility expansion differs from a reduction in operator actions. Each deserves its own evidence statement.

## Next experiments that would add information

1. **Controlled discovery cost.** Keep the underlying affordances identical, but require every arm to use the same bounded query interface to inspect them. Freeze which facts are initially visible and charge each query. Compare new feasible families, useful outcomes and discovery cost. This would test search/discovery, unlike the present fully disclosed property table.
2. **Grammar membership with an oracle.** Freeze an explicit admissibility language and independently replay identical operation sequences under old and one-rule-new grammars. Keep hard function constraints fixed. This separates creation of expressibility from changes in mechanical feasibility.
3. **Ordering with matched production.** Randomly allocate fresh investigators to function-first or arrangement-first instructions, hold initial candidate count and resource caps fixed, preserve precursor artifacts, and evaluate blind. File order still cannot attest unobserved thought order.
4. **Persisted learning within the same learner.** Use a declared state-bearing learner or human participants; freeze practice, retention interval and heldouts. Compare active practice and no-training controls, then remove worked examples and immediate assistance. External-card retrieval should be a separate condition. Fresh stateless calls cannot establish retained unassisted learning.
5. **Feedback that generalizes.** Version a single external procedure field after an actual failure; give old and revised arms identical failure feedback on multiple unfamiliar near/far transfer tasks. Test both improvements and new failure modes. A better one-case wording is insufficient.
6. **Constructive attack versus criticism alone.** Reuse the exact same criticism across fresh builders, manipulating only invariant/permitted-variable support. Assess whether an absent-upstream feasible mechanism appears; do not credit recovery of an incumbent construction as creation.
7. **Actual beneficiary outcome.** Select a task with a real owner's evidenced burden. Measure total producer/beneficiary actions before and after, including setup, review and maintenance. Preferences and synthetic action forecasts alone cannot establish relief.
8. **Source variety with equal information.** Supply shared, disjoint and union source conditions with literal exposure logs, common essentials and blind causal-family evaluation. Separate repertoire diversity from option quality and evaluate actual work cost. Different personas or words alone are insufficient.

These proposals follow the verified academic foundations and problems exposed by the study design. They are not a universal recipe, a systematic review, a training result, or an additional set of executed native strategy cells.
