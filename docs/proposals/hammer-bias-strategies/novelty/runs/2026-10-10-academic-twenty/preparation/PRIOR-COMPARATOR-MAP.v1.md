## Inspecting the full earlier repertoire without generation leakage

1. **Freeze a reviewer-only inventory before generation.** Root should bind catalogs, contracts, manifests, literal prompts, actual raw/native outputs, summaries, residues, builder cards, fixed selection pools, raw reviews and terminal closeouts across ROOT, N, P, I, failed ten-operator, reference-explicit/standalone-coverage, composed-ten and composed-coverage scopes. Record file hash, namespace, case, stage, source fact IDs, chain validity and final disposition. Preserve failures and gates; distinguish actual saves from development fixtures and copied archives. Enumerating all scoped files is necessary for full repertoire review; this role performed selective verification and does not claim exhaustive semantic inspection.
2. **Separate method instructions from solved content.** Generators receive only their assigned current/older operative clause, common new facts and declared stage input. Remove historical worked examples, scores, successful schedules, novelty labels, reviewer reasons and this map's historical answer illustrations from generation packets. Fresh older controls apply old methods to new facts; they do not retrieve old winning answers. Use explicit path allowlists and literal input receipts. Describing an information boundary is not proof that unobserved filesystem reads never occurred.
3. **Lock first saves and stages before archive comparison.** Reviewers may inspect the complete prior repertoire only after current raw generation and requisite selection/training inputs are immutable. For held-out phases, complete independent source/case preparation and required earlier locks first; training authors and method revisers never see held-out answers or grades. The current map is preparation-only and reviewer/root-facing.
4. **Compare operational lineage at every stage.** Search normalized causal operations, variables, schedules, interfaces, predictions and defeat tests across proposal, attack, summary, residue and final card. Exact-string search finds candidates; semantic inspection decides equivalence and retains uncertainty. Compare all earlier catalogs for conceptual precedence and all relevant actual outputs for recovered operations. Historical cases with different resources support lineage/contextual judgment, not same-case causal effect estimates.
5. **Report two novelty ceilings explicitly.** A new final card relative to fresh O/older-control final cards is a final-repertoire addition. An equivalent operation in any inspected upstream record narrows it to recovery, preservation, selection or a particular probe delta. Confirmed raw exclusivity, survival through compression, final addition and full inspected-upstream distinction have different denominators. No local result establishes worldwide novelty.
6. **Use stage-matched contrasts.** AC08–AC09/AC20 manipulate context/exposure; AC14 manipulates pool/exchange timing; AC15/AC19 manipulate evaluation with generation fixed; AC16/AC17 manipulate practice/retained-state adaptation. They need older controls at those stages. Changes in information, output cap, runtime opportunity, selected-pool membership or scaffold exposure must be reported. Historical scores are never subtracted from fresh scores as if randomized causal comparators.
7. **Retain missingness and ceiling.** Invalid/missing/gated chains yield unavailable comparisons, not zero-effect observations. Valid no-opening needs source-backed method-specific reasons. Reviewer agreement and model replay do not establish real beneficiary outcomes, human acceptance, physical feasibility beyond supplied rules, persistent learning or equal hidden compute.

This map identifies what must be compared and where historical evidence can be inspected. Root's subsequent freeze, actual matched execution, semantic review and closeout determine which AC comparisons are completed and which claims are earned.
