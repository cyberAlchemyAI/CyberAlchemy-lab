Academic rationale does not validate these complete project adaptations. Human findings do not automatically transfer to agents. Bibliometric association is not causal evidence; measured writing similarity is not an established engineering-agent effect. Novelty is relative to the explicitly inspected repertoire. Actual construction of a reasoning artifact or finite-model replay does not establish deployment, physical feasibility, beneficiary benefit, weight updates or field originality.

For every row below, preserve actual prompts, supplied source bytes, role outputs and terminal execution witnesses. Record the required function; typed hard constraints, preferences, conventions and unknowns; manipulated variable; information/resources; exact attack, surviving invariant and permissible variable; selected summary or residue; and obligation-retention check. If one investigator performs all passes, report that path rather than claiming independent roles. Preparing a table, prompt, curriculum, route or comparison plan alone never satisfies execution.

The disposition codes used in each row are:

- **A — applied:** the operative clauses were actually exercised, evidenced in immutable outputs. Application can coexist with infeasibility, no additional novelty or no measured benefit.
- **N — explicit no-opening:** the procedure actually reached a source-supported terminal boundary preventing its assigned construction. Carry the exact refusal through the selected handoff. Equality with a comparator, by itself, is an applied no-benefit result rather than N.
- **NA — not-applied:** complete enough evidence shows omission or substitution of an essential operation. Useful substitute work does not repair it.
- **U — unknown:** essential execution, source, control or comparison evidence is unavailable or invalid. Missing artifacts and gated branches are not zero-effect observations.

A missing comparator can leave application A while the comparison remains U. Assess application, feasibility, repertoire addition, usefulness, burden and learning separately; never collapse these into one success flag.

## Prospective protocol and adapter gates

Before scored launch, replace the descriptive dispatch with a bounded execution contract while preserving its original file. Name execution owner, all twenty selected card versions, exact case/source hashes, condition units, prompts, role bindings, information boundaries, selected handoff branch, waves, write scopes and missing-data policy. Existing user authorization covers the requested execution; runtime admission and evidence gates still require actual artifacts, not another permission ritual.

1. **Context frozen:** require available method-specific controls, typed constraints, outcome and defeat criteria, ordinary/component/upstream comparison scope, budget and unit of analysis. Freeze training/holdout separation and AC17 feedback/revision timing. Obtain actual AC18 beneficiary evidence and AC20 route packs before admitting those cells. Unknown constraint status never permits relaxation.
2. **Adapter preflight:** use realistic unscored fixtures for numbered fact references, membership, branch selection and lifecycle failures. Literal source-reference grammar in prompts and validator must agree. Check missing output, invalid references, terminal refusal, malformed handoff and budget exhaustion paths. Pure fixture/replay success is no native strategy execution.
3. **Information and order:** enforce before/after saves for AC04/09/14/20, fresh boundaries for AC08/12/16/17 and fixed pools for AC15/19. Bind every handoff to saved bytes. Count any reused control only when its information, frame, outcome, resources and condition truly match; disclose correlated comparisons.
4. **Handoff faithful:** verify that the selected branch carries the exact assigned operation, invariants, resources, rival predictions, uncertainty and terminal refusal. Checking mechanical projection alone is insufficient. A summary dropping refusal while residue retains it fails this gate, as prior R07 did. No substituted connection or antecedent observation can satisfy the assigned move.
5. **Comparisons complete:** obtain independent application and outcome/repertoire judgments with preserved raw disagreements. Audit empty controls as actual source-backed refusals. Compare final cards against proposals, attacks, summaries, residue and intermediate builders to detect upstream duplication. Separate unavailable contrast, applied defeat and no-opening.
6. **Closeout/claim ceiling:** all actual roles joined and closed; files stable and bound to receipts. Reconcile scheduled dispositions, actual starts, FINALs, invalid outputs and gates. Leave earlier raw grades and failed comparisons unchanged. A separately declared fresh coverage study may repair coverage, never the original score.

## The 260-call ceiling and audit obligations

The ceiling is overall, including preparation, unscored native preflight, scored conditions, reviewers, auditors, report roles, failed starts and any additional native turns. The execution owner must freeze a ledger satisfying:

`actual calls already spent + remaining admitted calls + reserved required closure/audit calls <= 260`.

A gate without an invocation and pure deterministic tooling are dispositions/tool operations, not native calls. Conversely, failed or invalid native outputs consume calls. Do not multiply twenty by thirteen and assume overhead disappears. Reserve completion and report audit capacity before launching generation; admit new work only if its required downstream reviews fit the remaining budget. Optional rounds stop at repeated structure/no new evidence; three rounds is a maximum, not a completion substitute.

Budget compression may reuse genuinely matched baselines, batch independent reviews with explicit per-card judgments, or limit claim scope to a coverage pilot. It cannot drop essential comparisons, replace training by a curriculum, replace held-out use by a plan, invent beneficiary evidence or describe planned source diversity as delivered. If essential cells do not fit, retain their U/gated status and state that the all-twenty objective remains incomplete.

The audit must bind actual agent/role IDs, launch and join observations, terminal witnesses, literal prompts, first-observed locked output hashes and validation results; preserve create-exclusive save evidence and any timing departures. File mtimes are write observations, not exact spawn timestamps. Root-observed evidence does not certify unobserved pre-FINAL access or independently authenticate platform events. Report actual calls, word/token/time data when available, and missing cost data; word caps do not prove equal compute.

## Essential clauses agent evidence cannot establish by itself

Human incubation (AC08), human delayed unassisted retention (AC16), human motivation and measured beneficiary relief require corresponding human/world observations. They must remain unrun or unknown when only agent branches run. A finite replay can substantiate AC06's explicitly modeled failure; it cannot validate its physical rules. A localized forecast or separating-test design is not an executed field test.

This preparation role has not received a provenance-bearing beneficiary quotation and therefore cannot substantiate AC18's essential actual-person input. The later protocol must supply one; a genuine task preference is acceptable when its burden relevance is explicit. Agent source assertions alone also cannot establish a real material property or owner constraint absent the supplied evidence.

No agent-only coverage pilot demonstrates universal efficacy, field originality, retained human learning, agent weight changes or durable system improvement. For AC16/17, external-card retention and fresh test performance may support a narrow retrieval/procedural-adaptation result; declare exactly which assistance remains. For AC20, actual source routing can establish the manipulation, but superiority requires the matched comparison and its information differences. Missing controls or unrun clauses remain visible in the final report rather than being renamed successful execution.
