# Closed case and control design for AC01–AC20

Prepared 10 October 2026 by one native preparation role. This is a substantive case specification and feasibility assessment, not a scored execution, trained system, admission, or ranking. Root owns final protocol, fixture freezing, validation and runtime. No prior outputs or grades were inspected. Only the strategy catalog, academic foundations, C case inputs, preparation instructions, budget and supplied human utterances informed this design.

## 1. Design contract

Use a small, deliberately rich synthetic family, with separately declared intervention units. Each arm receives identical hard task facts, supported properties, resources, ordinary archive and outcome definition except for the prospective manipulation. Unknown facts are not free variables. All timings and capacities below are synthetic axioms, not physical measurements. All analog sources are common accessible source-bank entries.

A candidate must specify operations, identities, start ticks, consumed resources, resulting state and a separating test. The root must actually replay/validate that candidate; a plan to build or test is not application of a card requiring construction, failure feedback or transfer. Record feasibility, mechanism application, local repertoire addition, useful outcome and burden separately. An ordinary arm may discover every alternative. These fixtures deliberately give strong controls enough facts to defeat an apparent intervention benefit.

Primary generation endpoint: number of feasible, causally distinct mechanisms absent from the entire declared ordinary/component/upstream pool. Schedule variants and renamed parts are not automatically new mechanisms. Report completion and action burden alongside this count. Selection cards use justified choices, not a generation endpoint. Transfer cards use held-out performance and method choice. This is a coverage pilot with dependent cases; twenty cards are not twenty independent replications.

## 2. Mechanical family J: reusable alignment fixture

J0 requires two initially aligned panels to remain joined with retention at least four synthetic load units during ticks 3, 4, 5 and 6, then be separated with all installed retainers removed by boundary 10. No damage, substituted panel, discarded part or outside resource is allowed. Alignment remains fixed while retention meets demand. Below-demand retention during a required tick fails the outcome. Time has ten action ticks, 0–9. One operator performs at most one action per tick; each action completes at its next boundary. Resources cannot occupy two positions simultaneously.

Inventory: one intact tapper H consisting of detachable head M and rigid stem S; two removable pins P1/P2; one clip C; one sleeve V; one loop L; one alternate-position sleeve Q; and the fixed two-panel support. All are reusable. Pins each retain 2 units; clip retains 4; stem and sleeve jointly retain 4; head has ballast value 4 and loop limit 4. Q's retention theta is a fixed but initially unmeasured value in {0,4}. All arms receive this same uncertainty. Root fixes theta=4 in the fixture oracle before execution; selectors must not receive the oracle value before measurement. No confidence-based interpolation is permitted.

Permitted operations and exact effects:

| Operation | Preconditions and effect; each costs one operator tick |
|---|---|
| detach(H), assemble(H) | Convert intact H to free M/S, or free M/S back to H. No retention is created. |
| tap(Pi), pull(Pi) | tap requires intact H and free pin; installs that pin. pull removes installed pin. Installed pins are the only additive retainers: their capacities sum. |
| install(C), remove(C) | Install/remove clip on the panel pair. |
| install(V), insert(S,V) | Install free sleeve, then insert free stem into it. Joint retention is min(sleeve capacity, stem capacity). |
| withdraw(S,V), remove(V) | Withdraw stem, then remove empty sleeve. |
| place(M), route(L) | Put free head in the ballast recess; route free loop around the pair. With both installed, retention is min(head ballast, loop limit). |
| unroute(L), lift(M) | Remove loop, then free head. |
| install(Q), remove(Q), measure(Q) | Install/remove alternate sleeve; installed Q supplies theta retention. measure uses one tick, returns exact theta and changes no retention. |
| idle | No state change. |

At most one non-pin retaining assembly may be installed at a time; it cannot combine with pins. No adhesive, welding, elastic extrapolation, manual sustained support or unspecified operation is available. Installation/removal prerequisites must be checked; an available part is not an available simultaneous assembly. Q is a genuine missing-evidence candidate before measurement, not proven infeasible. Unlisted operations remain unsupported.

The ordinary archive contains reusable two-pin fastening and clip fastening, with their operation rules above. The full property table is still available to the ordinary arm, which may derive sleeves or ballast. The soft convention for AC03 is “express every connection using discrete fastening”; root must label it a benchmark convention, never a mechanical law. The AC11 soft preference is at most b impact actions, b in {2,1,0}; violation affects preference satisfaction, not hard outcome validity. Deadline, demand and inventory stay fixed across this ladder.

## 3. Workflow family W: evidence-linked research reporting

W0 is a synthetic miniature of reporting research, not the user's actual twenty-card report. It has four claim identities E1–E4, one shared academic-rationale source A, one shared prior-repertoire source K, and the actual human-preference source U described below. Seven readable immutable source objects are initially present. Each Ei contains a fixed method-application fact, finite-model outcome fact, comparison fact and claim ceiling. Root supplies exact source tokens before execution; generated prose is not a source token.

Hard output: four distinct claim cards, each citing its Ei plus A, K and U, keeping application, outcome, novelty and evidence ceiling distinct; then a compiled report retaining all four cards and citations by boundary 12. No claim may turn synthetic facts into observed human or field effects. Twelve ticks, one operator/action per tick. Actions: read(source) caches an exact reusable token; write(i) consumes one tick and requires all four cited tokens cached; compile consumes one tick and requires all four valid cards. Cached tokens persist without additional reading cost. reread is allowed and costs a tick. The cache and source graph are available to every arm. Reads, writes, compilation, missing citations and repeated openings are logged separately.

Known ordinary repertoire includes source caching and an evidence table. Therefore merely rediscovering a cache is not novel. Different burden paths must change a concrete operation or produce a discriminating justified refusal. AC06 can replay an unsupported “four summaries are sufficient” construction: missing comparison/ceiling fields violates the original output contract. Reframing to an evidence-linked claim must preserve that contract, not add a conveniently new requirement.

For W1 far transfer, use three claims and six sources: E1–E3, A, K, U; eleven ticks. A is replaced at boundary 4 with a new exact version token. At that boundary any cached old A token is invalidated; all later written cards require current A, and compile checks that every included card cites current A. An earlier card can be rewritten in one tick with current tokens. The update is known prospectively, common to all arms, and requires no outside repair. This changes a relation relevant to retaining/reusing evidence; treating W1 as W0 under renamed nouns fails.

Both ordinary and intervention selectors choose at most two implementation candidates and, separately, at most one evidence-gathering candidate. Implementation recommendation, unresolved investigation and rejection are different decisions. Root replays the fixed prescriptions and preserves first assessments. Unknown theta remains unknown to selectors unless both arms receive the same measurement result at the same stage/cost. This permits a legitimate missing-evidence reason alongside task violation and unfamiliarity, without making the latter an observed human bias.

Predeclare dimensions/order and an identical set-size cap. AC19 uses the same pool/evidence/stakes but compares ordinary reasons with cited reasons separated into infeasibility, missing evidence, burden, outcome mismatch and unfamiliarity. Cosmetic changes to labels cannot establish improved choice.

## 7. AC16/17 retained state and AC20 route mechanics

For AC16 root must actually save a procedure-only artifact after T1/T2 practice: property extraction, preserved invariant, applicability cue, source mapping, construction obligation and refusal boundary. It must omit all holdout facts/answers/grades. Save an exposure-matched ordinary-practice artifact separately. Declare exact paths and hashes. No artifact is created by this preparation role; its existence and transfer remain indispensable execution evidence.

For AC17 save unchanged v1 and revised v2 after a cited real training failure. Modify exactly one named cue/condition/obligation; keep exposure and remaining text fixed. Use separate fresh contexts on an additional untouched partition, or prospectively split holdouts between AC16/17. Reusing a holdout after feedback is not untouched evaluation. Root must freeze that split before practice. No artifact-only update establishes improvement.

For AC20 use two actually executed source routes from the common bank: R-M retrieves DEV.C3/C5/C6/O3/P1/P2 (material delivery, capacity and release); R-I retrieves CONF.C4/C5/O5/O6/P1/P2 (eligibility, duplicate delivery and recognition). Save pre-method proposals independently before joined assistance. Distinct routes mean different recorded selectors and no proposal exchange, not different personas. The sources share synthetic authorship and one JSON container; they are independent retrieval/operation routes, not independent empirical datasets or model weights. If stronger source independence is claimed, that essential evidence is unavailable here.

Compare two routes receiving the same seed body against the diversified pair; also compare both routes receiving the full union with different assigned retrieval order, so equal information and changed routing can be examined separately. Keep unique-source totals, excerpt lengths, examples, calls and tokens explicit. Diversified-versus-shared information differences must be reported; source count alone is not equivalence. All sources remain commonly accessible. Candidate pool quality and between-output similarity are separate outcomes. Freeze the actual pre-assistance candidate pool before exposing assistance; P-J0 cannot substitute for that independent-generation record.
