## Keep two lanes

The outcome lane checks the function, constraints and burden. The invention lane changes the selected element. Robot Talks should retain incompatible predictions until a separating observation is available.

The constructive attacker supplies: failure signature, surviving function, unavailable shortcuts and evidence-bound uncertainty. Distill supplies a compact core and a separately attributable residue. Declare which branch a fresh builder receives before generation.

## Require a next move

The fresh builder returns:

1. A concrete operation someone can perform with the available resources.
2. The closest archived operation and the operational difference.
3. Two rival accounts and their opposing predictions.
4. A separating observation, with timing and required resources.
5. A condition that would defeat the opening.
6. Displaced burden, dependencies and unresolved unknowns.

Retain an explicit no-opening if no different operation survives. An unavailable indispensable resource defeats feasibility; a renamed archived operation does not earn newness.

## Investigate the composition

Declare four comparators: ordinary process, primary technique alone, secondary technique alone, and composition. Give each comparator only its declared technique instructions. The shared packet contains facts, constraints, resources and ordinary actions; it must not supply another comparator's method hints, generated construction or judgments. Keep the facts and opportunity to propose actions fixed, while recording any difference in supplied input size or runtime.

Preserve raw first attempts. Lock independent archive and candidate-pair judgments before polishing proposals. Keep genuine no-opening, uncertainty, equivalence and delivery failure separate. Test the selected recipe on a fresh confirmation case after development judgments close.

Freeze the native call budget, exact machine condition IDs, literal prompts, interpreter and scored output parents before execution. The prepared Dispatch Spec route is a structural design candidate; it does not itself establish novelty or benefit.
