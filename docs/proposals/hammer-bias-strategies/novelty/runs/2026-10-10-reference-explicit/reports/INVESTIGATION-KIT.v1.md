# Make analysis create a new next move

The hammer conundrum becomes an investigation when we change something the analysis process does and ask whether that change produces a usable new action. A surprising sentence is a starting prompt. The deliverable is an intervention, construction, question or measurement with a prediction and a way to reject it.

This is a readable derivative of the ten frozen strategy cards, not a new scoring rule or an additional experiment. Actual execution and review belong to the current run's immutable report and raw artifacts. These mechanisms remain candidates for investigation.

## Prepare the problem

Record the valued outcome, hard constraints, supplied observations, available resources and ordinary operations already considered. Identify the preferred tool's actual operating dependency. Give facts and ordinary operations stable IDs. For a literal hammer, specify whether the task is joining, positioning, releasing or signalling; each function is a different problem.

Keep two lanes. The outcome lane preserves the required function, constraints and burden on the people doing the work. The invention lane changes one dependency, causal arrow, carrier, sequence or interaction. Keep assumptions attached to the resulting proposal.

## Ten strategies

| ID and strategy | Starting sentence | What the process changes | Required new next move and challenge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S01 Tool deprivation | “Take away the hammer; keep the job.” | Remove its operating dependency while the outcome lane holds the requirement. | Construct another route to that function. Check that the dependency has actually disappeared rather than been renamed. |
| S02 Causal return-path inversion | “Is the hammer teaching the world to grow nails?” | Treat tool use shaping later requirements as a causal hypothesis. | Interrupt that return link while initial demand and the required outcome stay fixed; compare with a sham. No predicted later-demand change weakens the account. |
| S03 Attack to fresh builder | “Break the assumption; build with the opening.” | An attacker names one broken dependency and the outcome that survives. A fresh builder receives that failure. | Return a replacement, justified requirement removal or separating experiment. Reinstalling the attacked dependency defeats the proposed escape. |
| S04 Carrier migration | “Give the joint the hammer's job.” | Transfer a function into the object, arrangement or environment; then attack the new carrier's dependency. | Specify the transferred operation and all displaced effort. Reject a transfer that merely hides the same work in another actor. |
| S05 Delayed convergence | “Let the disagreement choose the experiment.” | Robot Talks keeps two incompatible predictions alive through the handoff. | Construct one feasible action on which the accounts predict different observations. More debate with the same next action supplies no new opening. |
| S06 Residue-fed construction | “Give the discarded footnote a turn at the controls.” | Distill retains a source-bound residue beside its compact summary; a fresh builder receives the residue. | Turn an omitted condition or unresolved conflict into a variable, operation or question. Repeating the summary or inventing a discarded fact fails the proposal. |
| S07 Protected anomaly | “Make the awkward fact change the next move.” | Preserve an inconvenient observation and develop rival accounts of it. | Manipulate a condition that separates the accounts. Mentioning the anomaly more prominently without a different action is insufficient. |
| S08 Rival backcasting | “Two successful futures; one choice where they split.” | Trace two plausible successful futures with different dependencies back to their earliest divergence. | Return a feasible present choice that distinguishes the paths. An unavailable indispensable resource remains an unresolved acquisition step. |
| S09 Interface ablation | “Keep both tools; interrupt their conversation.” | Connect two parent functions, then break only their link while preserving each parent. | Propose a signal or operation that depends on the interaction. Compare intact connection, co-presence, broken connection and each parent alone. A parent-only explanation defeats the added mechanism. |
| S10 Objective boundary inversion | “Find where more solution creates more problem.” | Vary the preferred intervention to search for an adverse outcome boundary. | Construct a detector or an operation that avoids that regime. Fix the boundary prediction before confirmation; choosing it after observing results cannot confirm it. |

S02 targets **later demand shaped by tool use**. S10 targets **the present outcome worsening as intervention increases**. S01 challenges logical necessity by constructing the valued outcome without the dependency; reversing a causal sentence alone does not do that.

## Turn destruction into creation

The attacker hands off three things: the dependency broken, the specific failure and the outcome that survives. The builder must turn that failure into a replacement or a variable we can manipulate.

For example, suppose pressing one corner of a cover lifts the opposite corner. The attack questions whether increased closing pressure causes opening through a pivot. The constructive move is to use pressure location as a diagnostic variable: move the pressure point while holding the cover arrangement fixed, then record which corner lifts. A rival predicts uniformly improved grip. A position-dependent reversal would separate these two accounts; no reversal would weaken the pivot proposal. This is a proposed probe, not an observed mechanism or a demonstrated replacement closure.

## The handoff

1. Supply the same facts to the causal investigator and dependency attacker.
2. Apply the selected mutation while preserving the outcome lane.
3. Distill into a core and a separate source-bound residue. Retain disagreements and resource assumptions.
4. Give a fresh builder the declared branch and common facts. Return anchor, mutation, construction, rival, probe, opposing predictions, defeat condition, resources, burden and unknowns.
5. Have two independent reviewers assess the raw proposal against the ordinary archive. Compare operational meaning; do not reward new vocabulary alone.
6. Preserve invalid delivery, genuine no-opening, uncertain judgment and equivalent operation as separate outcomes.

The frozen study crosses immediate/delayed convergence with summary/residue input and includes ordinary and generic creativity relays. Its Dispatch Spec describes an explicit custom native route. Structural route validation does not establish full canonical skill effectiveness.

## Reusable instruction

> Preserve the supplied outcome and hard constraints. Apply one named mutation from the table. Give a fresh builder the broken dependency, incompatible predictions or declared Distill branch. Return a concrete action we could take next, the closest ordinary rival, an observation on which they predict different results, and a result that would make us abandon or revise the proposal. Cite supplied facts, disclose unavailable resources and account for displaced work. Then write one sentence naming the new operation. If no opening survives, say so explicitly.

Judge local novelty against the supplied ordinary archive and concurrent proposals. A new operation with a usable discriminating prediction can be worth testing. More words, more cards and confident agreement cannot establish useful novelty or real-world benefit.
