## Concrete constructions worth investigating

These are exact examples from locked model judgments. “Qualified” means both reviewers accepted the five axes, available indispensable resources and distinction from all three supplied ordinary operations. It is local proposal qualification, not an exercised experiment or a guarantee of originality.

**Separate evidence freshness from evidence coverage.** WD-U14-C02, from residue-fed construction, proposes crossing zero versus one new full-suite run with tail versus full inspection. Record the following requested action and a citation supported by viewed evidence before allowing more execution. This makes “we need a fresh run” distinguishable from “we need a fuller view.” A retained full view that cannot support commitment defeats its sufficiency claim; unchanged next requests across views leave the proposed visibility-driven demand link unsupported. Both reviewers qualified the card. **Move redistribution to a different carrier, then remove it.** PC-U05-C01 proposes six fixed low-to-high syringe transfers with rotation off, compared with zero transfer and fixed rotation. Match the operation cutoff and quiet interval, then measure the independent depth-sample proxy. Migration failure when the rotation control succeeds defeats that substitution; both migration and zero transfer succeeding fail to support a transfer-specific contribution. Both reviewers qualified the card. No proposed trial was performed. ## What the judgments teach preparation

The original raw repertoire contains nineteen WD cards, sixteen PC cards and thirteen WC cards; PD has no cards because common-source delivery failed. Eight WD, nine PC and seven WC cards meet local qualification. These are individual card counts across different cases, with possible equivalence, not twenty-four distinct strategies or comparable family totals. WD has one additional uncertainty-eligible card. The original case-wide inference remains invalid.

The fresh case has three raw cards, no qualified cards and one uncertainty-eligible card. Both reviewers judge the A own-tray card equivalent to the first B own-tray card. One also judges both equivalent to the ordinary own-tray reminder. More careful observation and wording can improve an existing operation without making it a new operation. The report-timing candidate changes a manipulated variable, but its valued-outcome uncertainty prevents qualification.

Several original methods and the generic O/G scaffolds also reach retained-view interception or immutable-key selection. Their convergence prevents attributing those constructions uniquely to a named technique. The next composed recipes therefore need ordinary, primary-only, secondary-only and composition comparators, plus a fresh confirmation case after development judgments close. Neither reviewer agreement nor more cards substitutes for that comparison.

For logical inversion, identify the claim: necessity, tool-induced later demand, or harm from more present intervention. Each needs a different test. For destructive-to-creative work, require a surviving function, a fresh builder's construction and an attack on the new dependency. Distill's core and residue must remain source-bound; declare the branch the builder receives.
