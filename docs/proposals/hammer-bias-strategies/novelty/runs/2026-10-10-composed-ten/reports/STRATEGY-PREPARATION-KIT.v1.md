# Preparing strategies that can add novelty

This kit prepares investigations. Each strategy must deliver a changed operation or a source-backed explanation of why the case supplies no opening. A novel phrase is insufficient. Novelty is assessed against the supplied ordinary archive, each component's delivered repertoire, and upstream component ideas; usefulness is assessed separately.

The starting question is: **What becomes possible when we change the way the analysis searches?**

## One preparation card per strategy

Record these fields before execution:

1. **Preserved function:** the outcome the person still needs, stated without naming the preferred tool.
2. **Ordinary repertoire:** the current operation and its strongest available rival.
3. **Operator:** exactly what the strategy removes, reverses, protects, transfers, or delays.
4. **Construction obligation:** the concrete action, arrangement, measurement, or connection the builder must produce.
5. **Required controls:** the actual resources, observables, and interventions needed. Missing controls are explicit boundaries.
6. **Handoff:** summary or residue, declared in advance; source facts and inconvenient details survive.
7. **Defeat:** a prospective observation or existing equivalent operation that would defeat the proposed addition.
8. **Burden:** whose actions, time, consumables, and maintenance increase.
9. **Comparison:** ordinary process, each component alone, and the composition on the same supplied case.
10. **Disposition:** applied; source-backed no opening; not applied; or unknown. Keep creative success separate.

## Two lanes and three handoffs

The construction lane asks what new action becomes possible. The challenge lane asks what existing operation already covers it, what breaks it, and who pays for it. Robot Talks supplies distinct roles with conflicting obligations; duplicate prompts do not provide that separation.

**Proposal → attack and distillation → fresh builder.**

The proposer gives a source-bound candidate. A separate attacker identifies a failed assumption and a function worth preserving. Distillation carries those constraints and the protected anomaly into the predeclared summary or residue branch. A fresh builder receives that branch and common facts, then constructs an operation and its defeat test. Residue-only construction is a deliberate restriction, not permission to invent omitted facts.

The attacker must deliver material a builder can use: “this fails because X; Y must survive; change Z.” The builder must show what it retained and what it rebuilt. Logical inversion opens a counterfactual; reconstruction turns it into an investigable operation.

## What the first locked case teaches

The development case provisionally favors R04 as a local addition to the final builder repertoires. Full manual transport lost a labor comparison. The fresh builder retained a single manual trip as an early-arrival bridge, then restored powered batches for the remaining carriers.

Under the supplied finite model, the changed schedule and its closest rival both complete twelve correct releases by minute 13 with seven occupied transfer-clerk minutes. Their arrival traces differ. Delaying the initial manual bridge by one boundary predicts eleven releases. This is a concrete reconstruction and a specific dependency test; it supplies no predicted advantage on the stated ranking.

Several other strategies reached missing-control boundaries. Later-demand strategies need observable later behavior; link-only strategies need an independently changeable connection; the harm-trigger strategy needs physical resets. A future case should supply those controls only when they actually exist. Preserving those boundaries makes preparation more useful than inventing a successful-looking example.
