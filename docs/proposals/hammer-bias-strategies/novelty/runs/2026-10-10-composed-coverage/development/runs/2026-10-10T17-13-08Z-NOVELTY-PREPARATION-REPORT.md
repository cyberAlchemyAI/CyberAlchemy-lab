# Preparing novelty in the analysis process

Saved 2026-10-10 17:13:08 UTC. The bounded execution and method-coverage work are complete. Novelty, usefulness and method application are separate judgments.

Prepare a strategy as a mutation of what the analysis can do next. Require a construction and a defeat test. A new metaphor, another critique or an extra role does not by itself change the available operations.

Start from a function stated without the preferred tool. “Drive more nails” already embeds the hammer; “keep the two parts joined for the required load and service access” leaves alternative operations open. Before choosing a strategy, record the ordinary repertoire and strongest rivals, actual resources, controls, observables, burden and unknowns.

The reusable strategy card is:
- Preserve: the exact valued function and fixed constraints.
- Mutate: one named removal, inversion, transfer, protected anomaly, delayed commitment or measurement.
- Attack: the failed dependency, what must survive, and what may change.
- Construct: one feasible action, arrangement, connection or probe.
- Handoff: prospective summary or residue, preserving source facts, rival accounts, inconvenient details and missing-control boundaries.
- Defeat: an existing equivalent operation or an observation that would reject the proposed addition.
- Compare: ordinary analysis, component A, component B and their composition, including upstream proposals and distillations.
- Account: actor time, displaced work, upkeep, consumables and unavailable controls.
- Judge separately: method application, local novelty, usefulness and uncertainty.

Use two lanes. The challenge lane owes the builder material: “This fails because X; Y must survive; change Z.” The construction lane owes a changed action and a distinguishing test. Robot Talks assigns these conflicting obligations to distinct roles. Distill carries the chosen summary or residue to a fresh builder. Dispatch Spec declares who owns each step, what information crosses each handoff, what gates the next step and what evidence closes the route.

The route is: source-bound proposal → independent attack/Distill → fresh builder → repertoire comparison → independent judgments. Declare the branch in advance. A residue-only builder may use common source facts, but may not invent resources omitted from the packet. A summary-only builder must still receive the critical inconvenient facts. Missing controls produce a precise no-opening result, never a substitute question.

Reusable attacker prompt: “Attack the dependency rather than polishing the conclusion. Name the assumption that fails, the valued function that must survive, a source-supported variable that could change, the displaced burden, and two opposing predictions. Preserve any unavailable-control boundary.”

Reusable distiller prompt: “Produce the smallest handoff from which a fresh builder can reconstruct the move. Keep the exact function, declared dependency, protected anomaly, rival accounts, controls, burden and defeat condition. Put a method-specific no-opening in the selected branch when the required move is unavailable.”

Reusable builder prompt: “Using only common facts and the selected handoff, construct one feasible changed operation and a probe that separates its rival accounts. Identify what you retained and rebuilt. If the case supplies no opening, cite the precise missing control. Do not substitute a different connection or a present harm measure for later adaptation.”

Reusable repertoire-auditor prompt: “Compare the operation and probe against ordinary analysis, both components and their upstream proposals/distillations. Identify the smallest actual delta. Treat recovered ideas as recovered ideas. Check usefulness independently and preserve unknowns.”

To generate further strategies, pair one search operator with one construction operator. For example: inversion→residue reconstruction; migration→failure instrumentation; backcasting→link-only ablation. Reject redundant combinations unless the second operator changes the constructed action or the discriminating test. Run bounded proposal/critique rounds, then freeze instructions and controls before evaluation.

Use two kinds of cases. Coverage cases deliberately supply the controls needed to test application. Later evaluation cases should be independently prepared without advertising the method's mechanism. Neither synthetic case establishes practical benefit. An apparent novel operation must survive comparison with upstream component ideas, a feasible resource check and a prospective failure test. Retain negative results: they identify where the process requires better evidence or controls.
