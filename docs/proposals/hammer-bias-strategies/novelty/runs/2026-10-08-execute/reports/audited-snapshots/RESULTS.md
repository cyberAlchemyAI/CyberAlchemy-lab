# Results: generating and preserving novelty during analysis

Status: all planned attempts, semantic assessments and synthesis complete; independent completion audit pending.

The experiment found useful local openings from specific generative operations, with mixed transfer across cases. It also exposed two separate ways an analysis process can hide an opening: compression can lose its distinguishing causal relationship, and recommendation ranking can leave it outside the displayed choices. These findings motivate focused changes to generation, preservation and presentation.

The practical next design is in [Preparing the next novelty investigation](reports/next-investigation-design.md): ten prospective process strategies with comparison conditions and rejection tests. Start with criticism that must construct an alternative, Distill that preserves the causal relation and uncertainty, and a recommendation slot for one qualified distinct alternative. These follow-ups have not been executed.

## What was executed

The frozen scope contains 120 scored workflow attempts and one separate unscored preflight:

| Stage | Design | Valid complete workflows | Failed attempts |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Discovery | Twelve operators plus ordinary and generic-creativity controls; two cases, two repetitions | 56 | 0 |
| Combination supplement | Three operator combinations plus matched control; two cases, two repetitions | 6 | 10 |
| Confirmation | N08 and N12 plus both controls; four untouched cases, three repetitions | 47 | 1 |
| Scored total | All scheduled attempts retained | 109 | 11 |

Lane Z developed and challenged the incumbent. Lane A performed either ordinary analysis, a generic creativity instruction, or the specified operator against the same outcome and evidence. The neutral Distill stage followed both saved lane outputs. Combination attempts added an independent critique stage. Each case batch received two independent anonymous reviews, followed by one bounded adjudication of declared differences. The eight batches require 240 independent pair assessments; the final accounting records their locked completion.

Cases and comparison archives were independently authored synthetic material. Reviewers compared mechanisms, hypotheses and questions against those incomplete local archives. A qualifying alternative required local novelty, usefulness, an observable distinction, preserved constraints, retention through Distill and resolved judgment. Exclusive operator credit additionally required an A-origin contribution absent from both pooled controls, including uncertain equivalence links. A zero qualified count does not mean zero useful ideas.

The [execution addendum](EXECUTION-ADDENDUM.json), [selection rule](reports/preflight-audit.md), source hashes and prompts were frozen before their respective stages. No generated answer was repaired or retried. Finalist selection preceded confirmation prompt preparation and generation. The combination supplement did not influence selection.

## What happened to the combinations

Each of C1, C2 and C3 completed two of four workflows. All four matched C0 controls failed before Distill, so the supplement cannot establish completed-workflow superiority for any combination. Ten critique outputs failed the frozen fit enum contract; the prompt had omitted its permitted values. Their saved text remains unchanged and the dependent Distill calls were explicitly skipped.

The 73 available raw openings yielded 22 retained distinctions, three losses, one uncertain retention despite an available Distill output, and 47 unobservable retentions because Distill was skipped. Three lost relationships were concrete: a confirmed manifest driving export selection, occupancy enforcing closure, and insertion resistance deflecting a guide to create a visible cue. Labels survived while the operative relationship disappeared. The last is a candidate destructive-to-creative conversion with uncertain feasibility, not a tested physical device.

See [combination results](reports/combination-results.md) for all conditions, causal losses and source references. A prospective run should fix the common output contract before freezing new trials; selective replacement of these failures would change the evidence.

## Novelty must survive the whole process

All 216 discovery raw openings survived full Distill. Nevertheless, all exclusive qualifying alternative contributions in both discovery cases disappeared from their own first-two view under the frozen ranking. H2 showed the same pattern: all seven qualifying family-by-trial occurrences remained in the full outputs but were absent from the first two. That ranking breaks otherwise equal scores in favor of earlier ordinary-lane candidates.

The language-circle case also lost a joint after-round feedback relationship through compression. Its novelty was already uncertain, so the loss does not prove destruction of a qualified novel mechanism. It does demonstrate loss of a causal distinction. In H4, Distill changed unknown equipment flexibility into a negative assertion; both judges flagged it and the adjudication preserved it as unsupported. The raw, compressed and displayed candidate sets therefore need separate reporting.

Across confirmation, only three of 33 qualifying family-by-trial occurrences appear in their first-two view. All nine qualifying H4 occurrences remain outside it. These are case-specific presentation sensitivities, not a measure of human adoption or effectiveness.

This evidence supports investigating preservation and visibility; it does not establish that always presenting the most novel idea improves a human decision. Qualified familiar options remain valuable, and speculative novelty must retain its uncertainty.

## Execution quality and claim limits

The recorded generation scope used 368 fresh native role invocations from 379 nominal roles, with eleven dependent skips. Of those invocations, 357 produced valid role outputs, including the three preflight outputs; eleven failed. There are 367 saved generation artifacts. H034/Z reported a write failure after using an unavailable command and saved no output. Its size and contents remain unknown; its valid A output is retained and D was skipped. No missing content was reconstructed.

Two valid saved outputs, H042/A and H043/Z, were joined after authoritative lookups showed their native handles absent following continuation. Their terminal messages were unavailable. The receipts distinguish these two recoveries from the 366 observed terminal responses. Fresh contexts and read-scope instructions are behavioral controls, not operating-system isolation or platform attestation.

Actual tokens, internal model calls, seeds and hidden compute were unavailable. Known saved generation text totals 116,101 words; the sixteen reviewers and eight adjudicators saved another 97,064 words. Case/archive authoring, preflight auditing, orchestration and final auditing are additional work outside those counts. Saved words and task-input words are proxies; known saved text does not establish complete computational cost. The largest observed task-input proxy was 1,859 words, below the declared 2,500-word input policy. Soft output targets were instructions, not enforced equal budgets. [Execution accounting](reports/execution-accounting.json) preserves the exact scope and unknowns.

The original integrity checker intentionally remains unchanged: it classifies the eleven failed roles and eleven skipped dependencies as 22 pending role entries. The explicit failure-disposition audit reconciles those entries with supported terminal outcomes. Completing the investigation does not make the failed workflows successful.

No physical, organizational or user-decision probe was executed in the world. These are model-assessed distinctions in synthetic cases, not claims of worldwide originality, statistical superiority, general method effectiveness or measured real-world improvement. Repeated model outputs are not independent organizations.
