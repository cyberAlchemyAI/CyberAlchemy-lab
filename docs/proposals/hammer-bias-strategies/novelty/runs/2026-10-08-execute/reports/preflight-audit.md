## Proposed exact advancement rule for the pre-launch freeze

The following is a prospective operationalization, not a claim that the original prose already specified every detail. The parent should accept it in the frozen addendum or replace it before viewing scored outputs.

1. **Valid exposure.** An operator needs its two planned, independently assessed run units on each of the two discovery cases. A failed or truncated unit is an execution-quality limitation, not a zero-novelty score. Incomplete exposure makes that operator ineligible for this screen, with the reason retained; do not retry selectively.
2. **Family adjudication.** Assign method-anonymous family IDs using actor, operation, target, causal path, operating conditions, discriminating predictions, and assumptions. Mere parameter variants remain one family. A combination is a separate family only when its stated interaction changes an observable prediction. Duplicate IDs and Distill merges count once. Do not infer family difference from different wording or headings. Do not use unchecked transitive clustering: a disputed equivalence remains uncertain and is excluded from credited distinctions until the bounded adjudication resolves it.
3. **Qualified family.** Both judges, or the one permitted adjudication, must agree that a family is new relative to the frozen local archive, has a specific observable distinction, has a plausible or direct link to a named outcome or decision, and preserves the case's hard commitments. Conditional frame branches are reported separately. Unresolved judgments do not qualify. A retained label whose causal distinction was erased by Distill does not qualify as retained.
4. **A lineage.** For operator advancement, the qualifying causal distinction must appear in the saved raw A output and survive Distill, including an explicit A-to-merged-output mapping where needed. A family present only in ordinary Z does not qualify for operator attribution. A family independently present in both lanes is labeled shared Z/A origin, not uniquely caused by the operator. Distill-only additions do not qualify; report them separately.
5. **Conservative control overlap.** For each discovery case, pool both repetitions of both concurrent controls. Compare the operator's A-origin qualified, post-Distill families with *all intelligibly specified raw and post-Distill control families*, including Z and A. A mechanism-equivalent family found in either control is overlap, even if Distill later dropped it. Such a result can be reported as a retention difference but not credited as newly generated operator novelty. Matching must include the meaningful conditions and predictions; an invalid superficial resemblance is not automatically equivalent. Unresolved control equivalence receives no exclusive-contribution credit. This conservative rule is stricter than comparing post-Distill counts alone and must be disclosed.
6. **Eligibility.** On each of the two discovery cases, require at least one qualified, retained, A-origin family absent from the pooled controls under rule 5. One observed appearance is enough for eligibility; report whether each family recurs across the two repetitions. This is a bounded screening rule, not statistical significance or proof of causal efficacy.
7. **Full and first-two reports.** Report the complete capped family set for every unit and the first two distinct retained families under a fixed method-anonymous relevance ordering: commitment-preserving before uncertain/conditional/violating, direct named-outcome or decision link before plausible link before no link, specific discriminator before vague before missing, then earliest canonical raw ID (Z1, Z2, A1, A2). For merged families use their earliest constituent ID. The first-two report is a prespecified sensitivity view, not a replacement for the full-set eligibility rule. Report how results change under it.
8. **Per-case contribution.** For each eligible operator let `U1` and `U2` be the number of distinct exclusive families under rules 2–5, unioned across the two repetitions of each case. Two repetitions of one family count once. Preserve recurrence counts separately. Never multiply ordinal judgments into a creativity score.
9. **Equivalent finalists.** Two eligible operators are equivalent for this screen only when their exclusive family sets are adjudicated equivalent on *both* cases, not merely because their counts coincide. Collapse each confirmed equivalence group to one representative using the frozen seeded ordering below. Ambiguous equivalence cannot justify silently claiming two different finalists: resolve within the bounded adjudication allowance; otherwise flag the pair and do not select both. Keep the unresolved alternative in the report.
10. **Dominance with honest burden.** Compare `U1` and `U2` (higher is better), the number of distinct post-Distill families presented as usable despite an agreed hard-constraint violation (`V`, lower is better), unresolved fit among post-Distill families presented as usable (`F`, lower is better), and total emitted generation-output words across that operator's four discovery units (`B`, lower is better). Deduplicate fit families within each run before summing. Conditional ideas explicitly parked for further authorization/evidence are not falsely counted as recommendations to violate a constraint. These dimensions remain separate. X dominates Y only if every dimension is observed, X is at least as good on all five, and strictly better on at least one. If either cost is missing, the pair is incomparable for dominance; missing is never zero. Estimated implementation burden is reported qualitatively but is not used to break ties without a separate frozen comparable scale. Word count is emitted-output burden, not compute cost, latency, or usefulness.
11. **Final choice.** Take the non-dominated eligible equivalence representatives. If there are more than two, use the preregistered seeded ordering; take at most two, skipping an unresolved-equivalence pair rather than asserting they are distinct. The prospective seed is `novelty-2026-10-08-finalists-v1`; sort ascending by the hexadecimal SHA-256 of UTF-8 `seed + "|" + operator_id`. Record the ordering. This is a reproducible seeded pseudorandom tie selection, not an outcome-driven ranking. No eligible finalist means no confirmation. One finalist means 36 confirmation units; two means 48. Execute the separate combination supplement irrespective of finalist count.
