Qualified means locally new, specifically observable, potentially useful, fit-preserving, retained and resolved under the original criteria. Familiar useful ideas and novel but uncertain ideas remain in the full evidence. Word counts are output length, not compute cost.

**N08 generated a repeated novel interaction.** H001, H006 and H011 all used the same identity to govern both pending work and which completed preview could become current. The predicted interaction reduces obsolete processing and wrong-current selections. Reviewers accepted local novelty in family C06, but pending-work replacement, conditional publication, interface behavior and affordable implementation/probing remained unresolved. All three survived Distill; none qualified as fit-preserving. This is a repeated generation signal with an unresolved feasibility boundary, not a confirmed advantage.

**N12 returned familiar families.** Its overlay previews, fixed-pair identities and input/selection diagnostics matched the local archive. They may still be useful; this case did not establish an exclusive new contribution from the operator.

**The generic creativity control supplied the one qualified new question.** In H012/A2, a bounded test crosses interruption with a persistent receipt of the selected cut and caption excerpt, while holding waits, labels and completion order fixed. It asks whether a receipt specifically reduces interruption-related selection errors and time. That question, C08, occurred once in three repetitions. No interruption effect has actually been measured.

The paired case-union differences are 0 versus ordinary analysis and -1 versus generic creativity for each finalist. These are descriptive counts within one synthetic case, not statistical superiority claims. Neither finalist has exclusive qualifying A credit. The generic control's C08 also survives the frozen first-two view; the discovery ranking effect does not imply that every alternative is hidden on every case.

The overall confirmation stage remains incomplete. H2 and two different-domain cases must still be collected and assessed; this result neither retunes the operators nor replaces those cases.
