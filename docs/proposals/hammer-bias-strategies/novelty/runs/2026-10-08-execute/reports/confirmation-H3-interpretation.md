## Concrete openings and overlap

**N08 specifies interactions that can be investigated separately.** C07 resets shared context at a speaker-role handoff and compares context-only, roles-only and combined conditions. C09 crosses fresh shared context with private language support. C10 crosses that support with protected reciprocal speaking turns. These concern different dependencies: entering a shared context, making a language demand usable, and obtaining time to use support. Each qualifying family appears once.

**C08 is the recurring N08 contribution.** Participants explain a dated paper task to one another, check it against the card or example, and correct mismatches. The date identifies the right version while peer checking exposes interpretation errors. H030 and H033 qualify; H026's related proposal depends on an unverified between-session paper pickup point and does not qualify. The predicted complementary benefit remains untested.

**N12's C11 asks a sharper diagnostic question.** H032 independently varies familiar versus fresh scenarios and optional versus existing support. The proposed distinction separates context's effect on entering an exchange from support's effect on useful practice when context is held fixed. This is an assessable question, not an observed explanation of the supplied attendance data.

**Other useful contributions overlap controls.** N12's C03 publishes multiple future dated tasks before a participant might miss a weekly handoff. It qualifies twice, but the creative control also generates it, and H034's preserved ordinary A output contains the same family with unobservable retention. C13 separates recognizing the right date/source from identifying the task correctly. Ordinary Lane Z generates it in H025, N08's Z generates it in H026, and N12's A generates it in H032. It cannot be attributed exclusively to either finalist.

**Generic creativity also transfers.** Its C05 creates reciprocal informational dependence: each partner supplies something the other needs to discover, then roles reverse. This qualifies in all three repetitions. C06 replaces changing preparation assignments with an enduring invitation for the participant's next attendance; it qualifies once. These remain locally new relative to the supplied archive, not globally original language-teaching methods.
