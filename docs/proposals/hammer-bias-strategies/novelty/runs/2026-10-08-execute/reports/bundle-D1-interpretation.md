Two independent anonymous reviews plus one bounded adjudication produced 17 case-local families for 38 raw openings. The family labels include 11 locally new, 3 equivalent, 2 uncertain and 1 parameter variation. These raw-text judgments include failed trials and do not establish working combinations or exclusive improvements over controls.

In the single complete trial, B004/C3, four openings were retained and A1 lost a distinctive causal relationship. Its confirmed manifest was supposed to drive export selection; the compressed output kept identity and reconciliation language without preserving that dependency. This is the reviewers' assessment of the supplied text, not an executed software result. In the other seven trials, retention is unobservable: 33 entries remain uncertain with no invented post-output IDs.

Both C0 controls failed before Distill. A completed-workflow comparison against C0 is therefore unavailable. Failed trials are not scored as zero novelty, and their observed partial word counts are not represented as complete workflow costs.

The recurring contract error is specific: the frozen critique prompt does not enumerate the allowed fit values, while the frozen validator requires a fixed enum. The original answers and validator remain intact; any repaired interface belongs to a future separately specified run.
