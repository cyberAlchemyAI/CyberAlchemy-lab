## What the new possibilities actually are

**N08 / C05: connect customer geometry to staged allowance calibration.** A correct allowance on the wrong table geometry can still fail; correct geometry with a wrong allowance can also fail. H015/A1 and H024/A1 propose crossing these two inputs before releasing cutting cards, using bounded comparisons of component-only and combined corner mockups. H024 explicitly asks whether geometry correction has a larger effect with a calibrated allowance. The other members predict combined benefit without establishing superadditivity. C05 also appears in H017/A1, but that occurrence does not qualify because it assumes the four nominal-only orders are among the six customers willing to supply measurements. Thus raw recurrence is 3/3 and qualifying recurrence is 2/3.

**N08 / C11: couple wash compensation to layout selection.** H015/A2 and H017/A2 ask whether dimensions corrected for two-axis shrinkage can be paired with grain- and print-compatible layouts that preserve washed fit within the area-waste ceiling. The proposed offcut measurements and paper rejection gates make the next investigation assessable. They do not establish that a feasible production layout exists. The distinctive contribution is the fit/yield coupling, with comparison against compensation or layout selection alone.

**N12 / C09: temporarily remove corner capture to locate the source of poor fit.** H021/A2 proposes releasing temporary pocket stitching and comparing straight-edge fit before and after. Persistent shortfall points toward a panel deficit; improvement when only the pockets are released points toward local capture. Working backward from a compliant future cover produced a diagnostic intervention here. The compliant pocket designs themselves matched an archived family and retained material/access uncertainty.

**The generic creative control also added a recurring question.** C03 in H014/A2 and H020/A1 tests whether seam feed or alignment accumulates distortion after accurate cutting. This different family qualifies in two of three repetitions. The finalist observations therefore coexist with useful novelty from the generic control.

## Comparison, compression and remaining uncertainty

The full-set case-union differences are +2 versus ordinary and +1 versus creative for N08; +1 versus ordinary and 0 versus creative for N12. N12 and the creative control have equal counts but different families. These are descriptive comparisons in one synthetic case, not population-level superiority or proof that the prompts caused the contributions.

Every trial's frozen first-two view contains the familiar C01 and C02 families. All seven qualifying raw occurrences across six trials disappear from that view, including the creative control's two occurrences. The full Distill artifacts retain them. This is an effect of the prespecified ranking, which favors fit, usefulness, observability and then earlier raw IDs; it does not establish what a human reader would choose or what another ranking would expose.

One post-Distill fit issue remains explicit: H017/D3 presents the required nominal-order/customer overlap without establishing it. The adjudicator resolved the disagreement about recording this issue, not the underlying availability uncertainty. The case also preserves an uncertain equivalence between C01 allowance correction and C08's underspecified geometry-to-pattern transfer rule. No missing relationship was invented to force a merge.

Together with H1's lack of a qualifying finalist advantage, this is a context-dependent result. The two transfer cases remain necessary; these findings do not retune the frozen strategies or change their assessment criteria.
