## The concrete openings

**C04: compatibility matching coupled to duration allocation.** [H037/A1](../outputs/H037/A.json) and [H046/A1](../outputs/H046/A.json) compare the same declared needs under inherited bookings, compatibility matching alone, minimum-plus-extension allocation alone, and their combination. The proposed interaction changes which complete rehearsal needs can fit while protecting weekly minima and booked performances. Useful alternative windows and durations must exist. Better performance than each parent alone does not by itself prove a superadditive effect.

**C05: exchanges coupled to a single commitment point.** [H037/A2](../outputs/H037/A.json) and [H043/A1](../outputs/H043/A.json) keep original reservations in force until every affected group consents, then commit all replacements together in the authoritative ledger. Matching alone can find an exchange without coordinating access rights; the ledger alone need not find usable alternatives. The new relation is the all-consent commitment boundary and retained-original rule. The component and interrupted-handoff comparisons in H043 remain proposed tests.

**C08: preauthorized replacement coupled to authoritative reassignment.** [H046/A2](../outputs/H046/A.json) connects acceptable replacements for released slots to a ledger commitment. Queue-only, ledger-only and combined replays compare accepted placements, conflicting confirmations and handling time. A weekly minimum is protected before release. This appears once and concerns released-slot placement, distinct from reciprocal exchanges.

**C06: a person can be the shared scarce resource.** The generic creative lane finds this in [H038/A1](../outputs/H038/A.json), [H041/A2](../outputs/H041/A.json) and [H045/A1](../outputs/H045/A.json); N12 finds it in [H039/A1](../outputs/H039/A.json). Essential musicians might belong to multiple groups, so two available rooms can still create incompatible attendance obligations. Interviews, comparisons with timely sessions and an accepted staggered schedule could distinguish this explanation from room scarcity. Actual overlap and feasible alternatives are unknown. It is locally new against the supplied archive and shared with the control, not an exclusive N12 contribution.

Linked booking cycles, C07, appear in creative H045/A2 and N12 H048/A1. Their distinction from ordinary global compatible reassignment remains uncertain. They retain no qualified-novelty credit. This uncertainty was preserved rather than forcing a novel family or an archive-equivalent merge.
