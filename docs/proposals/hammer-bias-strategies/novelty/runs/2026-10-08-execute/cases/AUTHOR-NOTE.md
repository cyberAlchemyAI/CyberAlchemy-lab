# Synthetic case author note

Seven packets were independently authored for this run: one unscored preflight (P0), two discovery cases (D1-D2), two related-domain confirmation cases (H1-H2), and two transfer cases (H3-H4).

H1-H4 are held out. Their contents must not be used for prompt tuning, candidate selection, or discovery-stage method decisions. Shape and hash validation may occur without using their substantive content.

All observations are stipulated synthetic data. They are not records of real people, organizations, products, or experiments. Numeric detail supports bounded reasoning within each case and supplies no external empirical validation. Samples are small, several measurements are incomplete or self-reported, and neither causal explanations nor feasible improvements are guaranteed by the packets. The cases do not cover high-stakes domains or establish general performance across tasks.

No method catalog, repository content, prior output, sibling work, or web material was read to author these packets. They contain no prescribed actions, answer keys, candidate recommendations, or diagnostic labels. Word counts were checked in memory before files were written; each packet contains 350-500 whitespace-separated words across string values, including identifiers and provenance.
