# Prepare strategies that manufacture a new next move

A strategy earns its place by changing a dependency, revealing a different prediction, or making a previously unavailable intervention possible. Clever wording is a prompt seed. The research artifact is the next move it creates.

These are future research proposals. They do not modify the frozen thirteen studies, add scored calls, or establish a novelty advantage. Novelty is evaluated against an explicit supplied archive and concurrent comparators; global originality is outside this preparation.

## Ten operations to investigate

| Strategy | Prompt that starts the operation | What must emerge | How to challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tool deprivation + two lanes | What must still work after the preferred tool disappears? | Lane one fixes the valued outcome; lane two proposes a different dependency that can achieve it. | Compare actual tool removal with banning only its name. Restore the vocabulary and check that the changed operation survives. |
| Logical inversion + boundary search | Under what conditions does more of our solution create more of our problem? | A causal return path or boundary condition, followed by an intervention that breaks it. | Compare return-link removal with a sham; hold the starting problem and valued outcome fixed. A contrapositive alone does not count. |
| Destructive attack + fresh builder | Destroy one dependency. What becomes possible because it failed? | A replacement, a removed requirement, or a test derived from the specific failure. | Compare with critique-only and an ordinary builder relay. Reject constructions that secretly reinstall the attacked dependency. |
| Robot Talks + delayed convergence | Which two explanations require us to do opposite things? | Explicit incompatible predictions and one probe that separates them. | Give both convergence conditions the same concern reports. Compare immediate agreement with retaining the conflict until probe design. |
| Distill + residue resurrection | What did the clean summary have to throw away? | A discarded condition that generates a fresh question, mechanism or probe. | Feed a fresh generator the summary or the saved residue, with the same base facts and allowance. Judge new creation separately from faithful preservation. |
| Carrier migration + constructive attack | Could the object, environment or arrangement do the tool's work? | The function moves to another carrier without losing the outcome. | Account for displaced work, then remove the new carrier's key dependency. A renamed or outsourced hammer receives no credit. |
| Protected anomaly + rival accounts | Which awkward observation would our preferred account rather forget? | Two accounts that predict different effects when one condition changes. | Keep the anomaly supplied in both arms. Test whether protecting it changes the proposed intervention, not just how prominently it is described. |
| Backcasting + incompatible futures | If two futures both succeed, where must their paths first diverge? | A feasible present action that distinguishes their different dependencies. | Compare rival futures with one preferred future and ordinary reasoning. Reject paths whose first step needs unavailable resources. |
| Mechanism collision + interface ablation | What can the interaction do that neither parent can do alone? | A new signal, control relation or operation attributable to their connection. | Compare intact connection, mere co-presence and a broken connection while preserving both parents. Test cancellation and false signals. |
| Feedback inversion + demand removal | Is the hammer solving nail demand, or manufacturing it? | A changed specification, habit or interface that reduces future dependence. | Hold initial demand equal. Reject apparent improvement caused by dropping the outcome or exporting the burden to another actor. |

The two inversion strategies examine different targets: boundary search asks when the present intervention becomes harmful; demand removal asks how using it shapes later problems. Preserve this distinction when comparing them.

## A worked opening

Suppose the supplied observation is that pressing one corner of a cover lifts the opposite corner. The familiar response is to press harder or add more fastening force. Invert the assumption: perhaps pressure is producing the opening through a pivoting relation. Attack the present force path, then ask a builder to use the revealed movement as a diagnostic signal or to relocate the closing constraint.

The new next move is a proposed pressure-location probe: keep the cover and closure arrangement fixed, move the point of pressure, and record which corner lifts. A position-dependent reversal would favor the pivoting account over a predeclared account that predicts only uniformly improved grip. No reversal would weaken that particular opening. The supplied observation does not by itself prove a pivot, a feasible replacement closure, or a globally original design. Other accounts can remain live.

## First successor pilot: can rejected material generate useful new probes?

Test delayed convergence and residue-fed generation as two factors. This pilot asks whether the process can produce a distinct, feasible separating probe; it does not score confident agreement or fluent prose as novelty.

Prepare four untouched case packets in advance: two development cases and two held-out cases, balanced between a physical reasoning scenario and an information-workflow scenario. Supply the outcome, constraints, facts, resource availability and ordinary archive. If there is no usable oracle or test representation, score probe specification only and leave actual benefit unmeasured. Do not relabel a reused case as held out.

For each case, prepare two shared concern reports. One investigator reverses the assumed causal arrow; the other attacks a named dependency and records the surviving outcome. Both concern reports must be identical across the four experimental cells. Charge shared preparation once and report its cost separately. Their use is a shared component; this design cannot isolate the effect of inversion or constructive attack.

| Cell | Convergence | Input to the final fresh probe builder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Resolve disagreement immediately | Main distilled summary |
| B | Keep incompatible predictions explicit | Main distilled summary |
| C | Resolve disagreement immediately | Material excluded from the main summary |
| D | Keep incompatible predictions explicit | Material excluded from the main summary |

Every cell follows the same three calls: a converger, a Distill role, then a fresh builder. The converger changes only its assigned disagreement policy. Distill always produces both a main summary and a residue record; the assigned branch decides which reaches the builder. Give every builder the same source facts, outcomes, constraints and a fixed supplement size. Preserve omitted and unresolved material with source IDs; do not fabricate residue or silently import facts absent from the source. If equal supplement size cannot be achieved without distortion, retain the actual size difference as a limitation and do not claim a matched-context comparison.

Add ordinary-reasoning and generic-creativity relays, each with the same three-call stages, source packet, shared concern reports, output allowance and final builder cap. All six conditions receive the same supplied facts and concerns; their assigned processing policies differ. Each final builder may propose at most two probes. These controls judge whether the combination does more than supply another discussion stage. They do not give an equal-compute claim unless actual calls, context, output and costs are reconciled.

The proposed four-case allowance is 72 condition-stage calls, eight case-level independent assessments and eight shared concern calls: 88 native calls before any separately declared preparation or completion-audit overhead. Each case's two independent assessors cover all six final conditions together, with administrative method labels withheld. This is a prospective ceiling to bind in an executable schedule; zero calls have been spent on this successor pilot.

The primary planned outcome is the number of distinct probe families that have differing predictions, available test resources and a clear defeat condition, per attempted case. Use two independent assessors with method labels withheld; retain disagreements about equivalence and feasibility. Report candidate counts, qualified families, uncertainties, saved words and actual calls separately. Confirm contrasts on the two untouched cases even when a development result is negative, unless a predeclared resource prerequisite fails.

Freeze source packets, ordinary archive, stage prompts, limits, contrasts and scoring examples before any scored call. Freeze raw final proposals and their two independent assessments before evaluating compression or display. An idea that first appears in a summary is a fresh proposal and needs its own assessment; it is not retained source content.

The planned interaction contrast is D minus C minus B plus A, alongside both main effects and comparisons with the two relays. A positive difference in proposed card counts alone is insufficient. The useful result is a qualified new probe, or a clear negative finding that the process only repackages existing operations. No numerical superiority claim is possible until these proposals are executed under the frozen comparison.

## Route preparation

Dispatch Spec binds the source packets and hashes, exact role inputs, separate outcome and invention lanes, branch rules, handoffs, first saves, budgets and evidence for advancing. The accompanying dispatch is a descriptive route, structurally checkable with the sibling repository validator. It launches no workers. Runtime readiness and technique effectiveness remain to be established by their own evidence.

The preparation sequence is: fixed outcome and archive → causal inversion and dependency attack → four matched convergence/Distill branches plus two ordinary relays → fresh probe builders → independent raw assessments → untouched-case confirmation → report mechanisms, uncertainty and costs.
