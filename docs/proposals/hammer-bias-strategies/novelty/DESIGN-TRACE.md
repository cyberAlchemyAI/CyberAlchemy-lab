# Novelty investigation design trace

Intent: prepare new strategies for investigating novelty in the analysis process after the user found the first proposed sentences analytical rather than novel. Target: a reusable operator catalog and comparison protocol. The existing Deep preference is retained. The work is preparation, not execution of a new trial suite or alteration of the completed sixteen-strategy run.

## Evidence and assumptions

The previous synthesis and independent experiment review establish the narrow action-selection tie, limited open generation, shared runtime family, and explicit absence of production efficacy. They support investigating generation directly, without asserting why the earlier methods tied. The user's sentence feedback is evidence of dissatisfaction with the delivered phrasing, not a measured creativity score or an endorsement of one later sentence.

Working meaning of novelty: a mechanism, explanation, discriminating question, or declared frame branch that changes the local possibility set. Global originality is unassessed. Existing outcome commitments and hard constraints remain binding on candidates offered as solutions; conditional branches label their changed premises.

## Smallest coherent unit and closure

Selected unit: one traceable generative move, at the workflow/process level, carrying a baseline, assumption, operator, concrete mechanism or question, outcome/constraint disposition, discriminator, next probe, evidence status, owner, expiry, and compression lineage.

Inputs are the supplied case evidence and operator instruction. The output becomes one candidate record. The evaluator adds archive matches and comparisons. The synthesizer recomposes candidate records into a bounded portfolio and next-test decision. Responsibility for generation, archive comparison, compression, and synthesis is explicit; a slogan alone loses that behavior.

Evolution pressure is concrete: repeated use across different domains and output kinds. The small extension boundary is a typed operator ID and candidate kind. A general creativity engine, automated global-novelty search, ontology promotion, and deployment machinery remain deferred.

## Remaining uncertainties and navigation

No strategy's novelty benefit is established. Case packets, reference archives, runtime bindings, and resource enforcement must be frozen at launch. Those are explicit experiment prerequisites; they do not prevent delivering the requested strategy preparation. A full comparison may select zero finalists. The exemplar domains are not valid transfer holdouts.

Start with README.md, select the generative uncertainty, and inspect that strategy's card. The next lifecycle owner is the experiment investigator using experiment-harness. Four dispatches describe a single-operator comparison and three candidate combinations; their structural validation is independent of any effectiveness claim. Global novelty and live adoption remain separate inquiries.
