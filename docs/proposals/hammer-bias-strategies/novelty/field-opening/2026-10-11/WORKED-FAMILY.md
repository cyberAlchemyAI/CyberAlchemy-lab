# A worked family: movement controls what arrival accomplishes

**Date:** 11 October 2026. **Status:** four authored paper instances of a candidate grammar. No independent creator, playable implementation or simulation supplied these examples. Their existence demonstrates a written construction exercise, not an empirical field-opening effect.

## 1. Seed, invariant and permitted variation

The starting material is the previous [Creature's Errand proposal, LG02](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/hammer-bias-strategies/novelty/literature-longevity/2026-10-11/WORKED-EXAMPLES.md). This extension reuses it rather than reclassifying it as an independently discovered mechanic.

**Candidate grammar:** an entity carries a resource; arrival commits a consequence determined by a public destination, time, capacity or authorization condition; the player uses legal movement effects to influence that arrival.

**Preserved invariant:** arrival is an observable event with a declared consequence; the carrier remains vulnerable to its declared interactions; already committed effects cannot be retroactively changed. Movement can change a decision only if it changes destination, arrival time or the relevant public state. Every variant needs a viable way to respond and explicit resource/cooldown costs.

**Variation axes:** destination, time, receiving capacity and authorization. These specify design choices, not numerical evidence of novelty. Multiplying parameter counts does not measure the number of useful distinct mechanics.

The game envelope remains one chosen hero from three, three stages with bosses, mid-run items, manual execution and player-authored autoplay, provisionally limited to six ordered rules. Each instance is a separate condition to specify and test. They are not merged into the earlier closed contracts.

## GF01 — Redirect the charge

**Construction:** The carrier seeks the nearest reachable accepting dock, with a disclosed tie rule. A red dock turns the charge into reinforcements; a blue dock makes it a limited player resource. Maze walls change path length/reachability; knockback changes the carrier's position under a finite charge/cooldown budget. Killing the carrier removes the charge.

**New choice within this instance:** redirect, defeat or tolerate a delivery, weighing wall placement and knockback against ordinary combat needs. This largely restates the prior seed for use as a family reference.

**Manual/autoplay:** Manual timing can interrupt movement. Autoplay needs accessible dock/route observations and legal directional control. A mere statement that the agent knows the shortest route is insufficient; the observation/action interface must support it.

**Ordinary equivalent to inspect:** escorting, redirecting a payload, or path defense with beneficial and hostile goals.

**Ablation:** Give both docks the same consequence. If route choice still produces the alleged benefit through conventional positioning, the destination relation adds nothing to that benefit.

**Defeating observation:** one route dominates every viable build, or a competent ordinary payload model reproduces the proposed operations. No historical novelty is claimed.

## GF02 — Arrive inside the window

**Construction:** Use one dock. A public boss-phase window accepts a charge as a limited defensive resource; arrival outside the window creates a bounded hostile effect. Slow and knockback can delay arrival, each at a declared cost. The carrier has a finite approach and cannot be stalled indefinitely without consuming scarce resources.

**Changed relation:** destination remains fixed; arrival time determines value. The player may shorten a route, spend a delaying effect, defeat the carrier or prepare to absorb the disclosed penalty.

**Manual/autoplay:** Manual play executes the delay at the chosen moment. Autoplay requires observable phase/time, carrier position, effect duration and cooldown, with predicates expressible within its authored rules. Exact timing and event order remain to be fixed.

**Ordinary equivalent to inspect:** a timed delivery objective or escort synchronized with a periodic gate.

**Ablation:** The dock always grants the same resource. Delay then has only conventional combat effects; test whether any added decision survives.

**Defeating observation:** a fixed wait reproduces every useful outcome, timing is unobservable or no affordable response exists. A relation difference on paper does not establish enjoyment or a new primitive.

## GF03 — Prevent a second arrival from overflowing

**Construction:** A dock can hold one charge until a publicly scheduled discharge. The first delivery fills it; a second arrival before discharge creates a bounded overload. Two vulnerable carriers approach along visible routes. Movement effects let the player order arrivals or redirect one toward another disclosed endpoint.

**Changed relation:** value depends on receiving capacity and order. The same carrier, route and effect can be helpful or harmful depending on the dock's current state.

**Manual/autoplay:** Manual play can execute an interception. Autoplay must observe occupancy, upcoming discharge, carrier states and available effects. Placement and item choices determine whether that sequence fits six ordered rules.

**Ordinary equivalent to inspect:** a finite buffer or queue with timed service, overflow and routing control.

**Ablation:** Give the dock unlimited capacity. If the alleged sequencing benefit disappears, it identifies the capacity dependency, not historical originality.

**Defeating observation:** ordinary queue control supplies the same operations and outcomes, or the second delivery can always be killed without any tradeoff. The candidate may remain a usable themed instance.

## GF04 — Make arrival conditional on a public authorization

**Construction:** The carrier reaches a boss-linked dock. A visible authorization token makes that arrival drain a bounded amount of shield; without authorization it adds shield. The hero obtains one expiring token by completing a disclosed earlier action. Arrival consumes the token if present, exactly once. Legal movement can delay arrival until the token is acquired, or accelerate it before expiry.

**Changed relation:** movement and an earlier action jointly determine the arrival's effect. Both token state and carrier state are public. Token generation, lifetime and effect amount require a fixed event contract; this proposal supplies no hidden authority or retroactive exception.

**Manual/autoplay:** Manual control executes the token-producing action and interception. Autoplay needs expressible predicates for token availability/expiry and carrier movement, plus an affordable plan. The hero's action remains within the chosen strategy.

**Ordinary equivalent to inspect:** a keyed/authorized delivery with a deadline, or escorting a resource after an enabling switch.

**Ablation:** Make authorization permanent or irrelevant. Check whether the proposed preparation/timing decision survives under an ordinary equivalent.

**Defeating observation:** the token is a renamed conventional key, the schedule is fixed and trivial, or the condition cannot be authored through public state. No new mechanic family is certified.

## 2. What the construction exercise establishes

The four cards share a declared relation while varying which public condition controls the arrival consequence. GF01 is a retained seed instance; GF02–GF04 are authored variations. None is classified as a newly invented historical mechanic. Strong familiar equivalents are visible, and a useful contribution could instead be a learnable design representation or a player's unfamiliar experience.

The music analogy is limited: a recognizable principle may support varied work and opportunities for further creation. This exercise does not identify Vini Vici's musical grammar or demonstrate that these game cards are comparably influential.

## 3. Prepared discriminating test

Give independent designers either the grammar plus the same examples, examples alone, or a competent ordinary routing grammar. Fix source information, construction budget, obligations and the number of requested outputs. Ask for a withheld variation and a later unaided instance. Grade viable decisions, equivalence, family coherence and usefulness separately; preserve disagreement.

A positive grammar-versus-examples result could support an instruction or representation benefit. A tie with ordinary routing would limit the proposed method's contribution. Independent construction under an assigned prompt would still not establish a socially recognized field. That claim needs actual unprompted adoption, divergence and persistence.

This test remains unrun. The exercise adds four documented cards, with only three new variations of the existing seed; no execution count, measured generation advantage, player-learning benefit or broader originality result is added.
