# Ten strategies for investigating field-opening novelty

**Date:** 11 October 2026. **Status for every card:** prepared and unrun. These authored strategies investigate whether an invention enables a reusable family of work. They are adaptations of the research, not validated general methods. Source IDs refer to [SOURCES.json](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/hammer-bias-strategies/novelty/field-opening/2026-10-11/SOURCES.json).

## FO01 — Extract the grammar, then remove the example

**Inputs:** An exemplar, inspected predecessors, intended users and the valued function.

**Operation:** Describe components, relations and constraints. Mark inherited parts. Write the smallest rule that allegedly explains a useful difference. Remove the exemplar and use that rule to construct three meaningfully different instances.

**Construction:** Turn a successful maze level into a rule about controlling what arrival accomplishes, then vary destination, timing and capacity.

**Comparison:** Equal information and budget with examples alone, ordinary decomposition and an existing equivalent principle.

**Outcome:** Valid useful descendants after duplicate/equivalent mechanisms are removed; shared relation and distinct consequences.

**Defeating observation:** Only the original instance works, variants repeat its surface, or the extracted grammar is an established equivalent with no new opening for the intended users.

**Basis:** FS10, FS13. **Claim target:** reusable generation, distinct from worldwide originality.

## FO02 — Invert one exclusion, retain its purpose

**Inputs:** A convention that excludes a proposed family and the reason for that convention.

**Operation:** Express the exclusion literally. Reverse or relax one condition. Preserve its valued purpose through a different relation. Construct an old-compliant example and two varied examples using the changed rule.

**Construction:** Replace the assumption that every monster's purpose is attacking the hero with a public delivery objective, while preserving threats and meaningful strategic costs.

**Comparison:** Ordinary goal-based enemy design and a version that merely renames attacking. If a competent existing rule already permits the same actions, claim an access/representation change instead of formal expansion.

**Outcome:** Distinct feasible choices and a family whose members remain legible and useful.

**Defeating observation:** Relaxation breaks the valued function, creates arbitrary exceptions or only reveals options already available under the unchanged rule.

**Basis:** FS10; prior destructive-to-creative research. **Claim target:** a specified constraint change.

## FO03 — Build a bridge to an adjacent audience

**Inputs:** Two audiences, their actual conventions and a candidate principle.

**Operation:** Find a familiar entry cue and an unfamiliar consequential relation. Preserve the relation while adapting presentation or learning sequence. Record which users can understand and create with it.

**Construction:** Teach an action-game player a routing mechanic through a familiar dodge/knockback encounter before asking for an authored autoplay policy. A music study would separately inspect actual listening conventions rather than infer them from genre labels.

**Comparison:** Existing presentation, a conventional tutorial and a version with the unfamiliar relation removed.

**Outcome:** Comprehension, unaided construction and audience-specific uptake; familiarity and skill are measured separately.

**Defeating observation:** The bridge only increases clicks, replaces the distinctive relation or benefits solely from more instruction time.

**Basis:** FS03, FS04, FS09 as attributed crossover accounts. **Claim target:** access, not component invention.

## FO04 — Generate a family before polishing a flagship

**Inputs:** A seed rule and a small set of declared variation axes.

**Operation:** Produce a normal instance, a boundary case, an inversion and a counterexample. For each, state the shared relation, changed decision and known equivalent. Repair the grammar if it needs a special exception for every member.

**Construction:** Apply movement-to-consequence control to destination, time, capacity and authorization. Keep effects and stage resources explicit.

**Comparison:** Ordinary brainstorming under the same source information, candidate count and construction budget.

**Outcome:** A family with multiple valid consequential variants and disclosed equivalence, rather than raw output volume.

**Defeating observation:** Variants are decorative, invalid or interchangeable; the ordinary route generates the same useful family with less effort.

**Basis:** FS10, FS12, FS13. **Claim target:** generative yield under bounded conditions.

## FO05 — Lower the cost of making, without fixing the answer

**Inputs:** A difficult practice, its required knowledge and the intended new creators.

**Operation:** Identify one entry barrier. Supply a representation, constrained editor or teaching aid that preserves substantive choices. Then ask users to create without the aid or with a new instance after a delay.

**Construction:** An autoplay editor exposes observable state and six ordered rules, with explanations of consequences. It leaves route, item and priority decisions to the player.

**Comparison:** A competent ordinary editor/tutorial with equivalent information; assisted output and delayed transfer are different outcomes.

**Outcome:** Entry time, viable constructions, variation and retained unaided performance.

**Defeating observation:** The tool supplies the strategy itself, only increases superficial variations or yields no retained ability beyond the baseline aid.

**Basis:** FS10, FS11 motivate examining representation and practice; they do not establish this intervention's efficacy. **Claim target:** access and learning.

## FO06 — Ablate the seed rule

**Inputs:** A purported family, its defining rule and competent equivalent systems.

**Operation:** Remove the rule while preserving components and ordinary capabilities. Reconstruct each descendant under the comparison. Identify exactly which decisions, outcomes or interpretations disappear.

**Construction:** Remove the destination-dependent delivery outcome while leaving knockback and pathfinding. Check whether the alleged delivery strategy becomes ordinary enemy positioning.

**Comparison:** Feature-absent baseline, renamed feature, and a strong ordinary equivalent.

**Outcome:** The minimum dependency shared across descendants; mechanical, information and experiential effects are scored separately.

**Defeating observation:** Every worthwhile descendant survives unchanged, or the rule only alters vocabulary. If only experience differs, revise the claim and test actual audience response.

**Basis:** FS10, FS13 as conceptual motivation; ablation is an authored method. **Claim target:** contribution beyond an appealing explanation.

## FO07 — Hand the principle to an independent creator

**Inputs:** A frozen grammar, example set, source pool and evaluation obligations.

**Operation:** Give another creator the grammar and shared evidence. Withhold one example and ask for a new member. Track which supplied information appears in the result and whether the creator changes the rule.

**Construction:** A designer receives arrival/consequence control and must build a level using a previously unused interaction while retaining public state and feasible strategies.

**Comparison:** Another creator receives equal-budget examples without the explicit grammar, or an ordinary established grammar. Prompts and available information must be fixed.

**Outcome:** Independent viable descendants, withheld-example generalization and transfer after assistance is removed.

**Defeating observation:** Outputs reproduce supplied examples, depend on concealed new information or cannot meet shared obligations. Assigned creation is not unprompted cultural adoption.

**Basis:** FS10, FS11. **Claim target:** transmissibility. No independent creator or agent was invoked here.

## FO08 — Test the promise in the name

**Inputs:** Identical material and truthful but differently framed descriptions.

**Operation:** Compare a future-standard framing, a neutral description and a literal account of the intended principle. Collect expectations before exposure and response afterward; use separate fresh participants for first encounters.

**Construction:** A design family is introduced through a future-classic promise or neutral rule description, with identical playable content. Do not falsely claim established acclaim or invent historical adoption evidence.

**Comparison:** Same material, access and creator identity; framing alone changes.

**Outcome:** Expectations, recall, appreciation and voluntary reuse at a later opportunity.

**Defeating observation:** The name changes anticipated prestige without affecting learning, useful construction or subsequent behavior. An initial framing effect does not establish enduring value.

**Basis:** The FS01 title provides the research prompt; FS11 motivates classification questions. **Claim target:** anticipatory framing, not a verified self-fulfilling prophecy.

## FO09 — Audit invention, access and popularization separately

**Inputs:** Dated predecessors, the candidate artifact, attributed accounts and adoption records.

**Operation:** Build three timelines: earliest documented component/relation; entry into an audience or practice; and wider recognition/distribution. Assign claims to those timelines. Compare predecessor novelty and adoption rather than treating low visibility as nonexistence.

**Construction:** Place the 2006 related progressive catalog, the April 2015 album and later crossover accounts on separate documentary tracks.

**Comparison:** Prior innovative work without similar distribution; prominent work with familiar components. Missing historical records remain missing.

**Outcome:** A contribution description tied to actual evidence, including lower access cost or changed standards when earliest invention is unsupported.

**Defeating observation:** The alleged invention predates the exemplar, uptake is already established or observed reach depends entirely on external exposure. A corrected origin claim need not erase a separate contribution to access.

**Basis:** FS01–FS09, FS11. **Claim target:** historical contribution with a bounded ceiling.

## FO10 — Follow descendants and dropouts over time

**Inputs:** A defined candidate branch, competing branches, dated works, independent creators and consistent audience measures.

**Operation:** Freeze a classification rule before selecting successes. Record precursor ambiguity, actual reuse, divergent descendants and abandoned paths. Separate creator testimony from resemblance. Track changes in classification and exposure across fixed periods.

**Construction:** A prospective music-lineage corpus tests whether the claimed branch expands, persists or converges into repeated imitation. A separate game study tracks independent mechanics built from a shared rule.

**Comparison:** Rival branches and unsuccessful descendants matched where feasible on period, access and distribution; no causal conclusion from simple temporal succession.

**Outcome:** Independent productive reuse, lineage diversity, persistence and audience share with stated denominators.

**Defeating observation:** The family depends on one creator, variants are copies, rivals have equal persistence, or changing genre labels manufacture apparent dominance.

**Basis:** FS11–FS13. **Claim target:** field formation and endurance, not individual retention.
