## Limits

This checks coherence and boundedness, not that the operators improve creativity or that the eventual study has adequate statistical power. Fixed role instructions and two independent judges reduce some confounds but do not create population-level evidence or eliminate recognizable operator content. Follow the protocol's unknown and disagreement dispositions. Complete case authoring, prompt freezing, archives, access boundaries, and budget calibration before any scored run.
