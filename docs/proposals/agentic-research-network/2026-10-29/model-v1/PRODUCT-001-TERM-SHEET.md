## 3. Contribution and benefit schedule

Every sponsor selects an explicit **bundle** before paying. The agreement records Product ID and version, sponsor identity, cleared currency, restricted or unrestricted funding, refundability, vesting event, contribution measure `V`, `F_limit`, `k_F`, Product revenue fraction `beta`, fixed `F_cap`, payout term, audit/appeal rights, access quota/cost, IP option, expiration and termination. Contributors use a separate U schedule for independently accepted durable advances; W pays bounded task execution. F and U use the same Product revenue statement and settlement calendar but different earning evidence. No sponsor is made a research contributor merely by paying.

For a chosen F benefit, `F_j = min(F_limit_j, floor(V_j/k_F))`; the sum of pre-reserved sponsor limits cannot exceed `F_cap`. An unpaid pledge and refunded unspent amount vest nothing. A sponsor can stop future payments and retain previously vested F for the original contract term. F pays `floor(F_pool × F_j / F_cap)` from eligible Product distributable revenue after the period snapshot; it creates no guaranteed redemption. For a chosen no-charge service benefit, `Q_j = min(Q_cap_j, floor(V_j/k_Q))`; reserve `Q_j × marginal_cost_cap` against Product cash and specify the use window and eligible Principal. A chosen IP license requires the named asset and rights clearance. **A contribution threshold may qualify a sponsor to negotiate an ownership transaction; it does not execute one.** See the [sponsor model](economics/SPONSOR-PARTICIPATION.md).

The sponsor benefit is **one priced bundle**. If the sponsor chooses F plus service access, both costs are recognized against the same funding agreement and Product budget. Funding is booked once. The Product owner must disclose the combined opportunity cost of a license or exclusive field and must keep the rights needed for the projected Product receipts. If it cannot, those receipts are removed from the solvency model.

## 4. Candidate cash waterfall and stop rule

For each settlement period: reconcile actual Product cash receipts and deductions into signed net Product contribution `N` and nonnegative distributable `D`; reserve refunds, disputes, sponsor access and W fulfillment; compute U and F payouts from the **opening** issued-unit snapshots; pay valid operating, review and research commitments; test `cash − refundable funding − undelivered access reserve − other committed liabilities ≥ floor`. `alpha + beta ≤ 1`; both pool denominators are fixed before issue. Payouts to holders are individually floored and rounding dust remains with the Product/program treasury. No reserve or token transfer manufactures external cash. The [formal model](economics/FORMAL-MODEL.md) and [circular-loop review](economics/CIRCULAR-ECONOMY-RESEARCH.md) give the broader constraints.

Pause new research and new sponsor benefits when the next period fails the floor, issued-unit cap, IP permission, access capacity, or Product revenue evidence gate. Existing contractual liabilities then need their own resolution. A period-end arithmetic pass does not prove intraperiod liquidity or payment finality.

## 5. Synthetic two-sponsor test contract

These are **arbitrary C units**, not a price, stablecoin transaction, offer, customer forecast or proposed live allocation. They exercise the [multi-sponsor runner](economics/multi_sponsor_scenarios.py) and its [machine result](economics/fixtures/multi-sponsor-results.json).

| Parameter | Synthetic value |
| --- | ---: |
| Opening treasury / headroom floor | `1,000 C` / `500 C` |
| Product `N=D` in sale periods | `1,000 C` |
| U and F pools | `20%` and `10%` of `D`, with `1,400 F` fixed cap |
| Sponsor A | Clears and vests `900 C` in period 1; reserves/earns `900 F`; no access benefit |
| Sponsor B | Clears and vests `500 C` in period 2; reserves/earns `500 F`; five no-charge service units at `30 C` cost each, `150 C` reserved |
| Period costs apart from access | `200 C` operations/review, `400 C` research, `100 C` W service |
| Sponsor exit | Both make no further payments from period 3; their F and B's unused access remain active for the assumed term |

With Product `N=D=1,000 C` in periods 3–5 and one B access use per period, the simulator pays A `64 C` and B `35 C` each sale period; U receives `200 C`. One C of the F pool per period is rounding dust. End cash is `913 C`; two B service units remain reserved at `60 C`, so headroom is `853 C`. Without the access package, end cash/headroom is `1,003 C`. With no Product receipts after sponsor exit, the next `700 C` spending period fails the floor and F pays zero. At `N=D=600 C`, one Product period passes and the next fails. A hypothetical IP assignment that removes Product selling rights blocks booking the forecast Product receipt. These are conditional arithmetic outcomes, not product demand or sponsor yield.

## 6. Evidence and decisions to turn this into a pilot

1. **Product and rights owner:** confirm whether Engineer Assessment may be used as the Product 001 pilot; appoint actual Product, research, IP, privacy and treasury Principals. Complete the asset-specific rights and Product/Network agreement.
2. **Research baseline:** freeze one bounded case or evaluator research question, closest prior work, novelty criterion, discriminating experiment, independent review and permitted source data. Use the existing [synthetic pilot protocol](PILOT.md) before participant data.
3. **Real unit economics:** measure delivery, AI inference, human review, support, accommodations, acquisition, incident and access-fulfillment costs by assessment; use the Product's `PS-17` definition. Get an actual customer or permitted sponsor signal before forecasting recurring `N`.
4. **Sponsor agreement:** select a bundle, exact vesting/refund rules, term, caps, rights, currency, custody and cash waterfall. Run multi-sponsor, delayed-sale, withdrawal, cost-overrun and IP-removal stress with those terms.
5. **Admission and offer:** follow [Lab admission](ADMISSION.md), authorize a bounded pilot, and obtain Product-specific legal, tax, securities and privacy review before requesting money, granting IP or issuing any asset. [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/faq) describes ownership and use-right questions in sponsored research; Brazil's [CVM cryptoasset guidance](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/pare040.html) and [ANPD research-data guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-lanca-guia-orientativo-sobre-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-para-fins-academicos) are starting sources for the relevant jurisdiction review, not an approval of this design.
