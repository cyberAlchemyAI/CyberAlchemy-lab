# Policy coverage research

Status: source review for a candidate product/research policy set, 2026-10-26. The pilot's jurisdiction, controller and employment-use route have not been selected. The sources below identify policy questions and design constraints; they do not by themselves decide the applicable law or issue this project's policies.

| Primary source | Design implication in this project |
| --- | --- |
| [ANPD research-data guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia-orientativo-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-para-fins-academicos-e-para-a-realizacao-de-estudos-e-pesquisas) | Research purpose, basis, sharing and participant expectations require explicit review. This supports separate purpose/notice, research-use and export policies. |
| [ANPD data-subject rights guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/titular-de-dados-1/direito-dos-titulares) | Rights intake, correction/withdrawal/deletion lineage and review of automated decisions need a defined route if applicable. |
| [ANPD incident communication guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/comunicado-de-incidente-de-seguranca-cis) | Incident classification, responsible owner, processor coordination and notification need a policy and rehearsed evidence flow. Exact duties depend on jurisdiction and event. |
| [GDPR Article 5 and Article 22 text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) | If applicable, storage limitation and solely automated consequential decisions make retention and employment-use gates material. No GDPR applicability is assumed for the pilot. |
| [EEOC employment tests guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures) | If used for hiring in a covered setting, job relevance, adverse impact and alternatives need separate evidence; a fun challenge score is insufficient. |
| [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) | Browser accessibility and alternative response paths need defined acceptance checks, including before a timed assessment is released. |
| [NIST AI RMF core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) | AI scoring and detector risks need named governance, mapping, measurement and management across versions and use contexts. |

The [fourteen-policy inventory](README.md) also comes from this project's existing product and research sources. The first-pilot priorities are purpose/notice, retention, assistance, administration, accessibility, case release, scoring/feedback, research recruitment/use, access/incident, vendor, rights and claim limits. Integration reconsideration is already supported by a candidate Challenge Contract. A later employer-facing release would require a new scope and exact evidence/approval for selection use.

Policy choices still needing empirical work include: whether video is necessary, whether the proposed assistance baseline is acceptable and fair, ASR/score errors by language and mode, equivalent accessible administration, participant understanding of feedback and rights, deletion across backups/vendors, and whether the three-session measure predicts an independent target outcome. Their experiment designs and release gates stay separate from policy issuance.
