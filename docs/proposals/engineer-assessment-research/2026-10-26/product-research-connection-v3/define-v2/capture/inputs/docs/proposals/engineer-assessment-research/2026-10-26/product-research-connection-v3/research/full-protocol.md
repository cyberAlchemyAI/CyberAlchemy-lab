# Complete prospective experiment protocol: EA-PROT-0.1

Status: executable **design template**, no approved recruitment or run. Date: 2026-10-25. This protocol specifies sequence, estimands, randomization, measures, analysis, failure decisions and artifacts. The role-specific parameters in §1 must be resolved and registered before participant contact; filling them with convenient values after viewing outcomes would invalidate confirmatory inference. The three product sessions remain at most ten minutes each; additional research tasks are separate, consented validation measures, not part of a proposed 30-minute candidate experience.

The first [MVP pilot](../software/EXPERIMENT-OPERATIONS.md) targets **mixed software developers** for formative usability, case and scoring-process evidence. It precedes the named-role validation stages below and cannot satisfy their role-specific transfer or hiring estimands. Its own protocol and collection decisions must still be registered before inviting people.

Use the candidate meanings in [definitions.json](../model/definitions.json) for cases, ratings, independent criteria, calibration/holdout sets and selection comparisons; the protocol does not independently redefine them.

For the protocol's symbols, `P` is the target pool, `C` the fixed selection capacity, `T` the outcome horizon, `Y` the independent outcome, `π` a frozen decision policy, `U(π)` the registered utility at capacity `C`, `L` the existing longer pipeline, `Q` the equal-time comparator and `δ` the non-inferiority margin. Their concrete values and measurement rules belong in the run registration.

## 1. Registration sheet that makes a run executable

Freeze in a dated, versioned record: employer/role/level and work analysis; source population and recruitment channels; geography/language/access modes; inclusion/exclusion rules; candidate use of external help/AI; session order and exact ten-minute timing; case-bank IDs and exposure history; rubric/scorer/ASR versions; primary criterion and time horizon; comparator pipeline `L` and equal-time work sample `Q`; decision capacity `C`; minimum material differences and non-inferiority margin `δ`; outcome ascertainment and data access; subgroup analyses allowed by law; number of employers; sample and precision simulation; interim looks/stopping rule; missingness strategy; security/retention schedule; preregistration location and independent reviewer. The accountable owners must approve collection and use. No number in this document silently fills these choices.

## 2. Study architecture and participants

**Stage A, job/content study (E01–E02).** Obtain critical incidents and consequential tasks from incumbents, managers and customer/product counterparts for one role/level. Independently rate each task's importance, frequency and required proficiency. Map each proposed dimension to task evidence and mark constructs that do not survive. Build at least three independent case families per session with multiple variants per family; experts blind to generator identity review solvability, role relevance, cultural/access assumptions and defensible alternatives. Cognitive interviews with diverse volunteers check what participants think the task asks. This stage cannot produce a predictive hiring claim.

For E02, archive generator prompt/model/seed, all candidates and rejection reasons. Compare variants at two levels: surface similarity and underlying solution mechanism. Reviewers independently label answer leakage, repeated trick, ambiguity, missing facts, multiple defensible solutions, construct coverage, expected time and accessibility; adjudicate with reasons before release. Hold out whole mechanism families for later tests and keep exposed teaching examples out of live scoring. Report acceptance rate, duplicate rate by level, reviewer agreement and post-release exposure/retirement. Automated similarity is a screening aid, never proof that two cases are equivalent.

**Stage B, measurement and transfer study (E03–E05A/B/C, E07).** Recruit consenting engineers across experience and response modes; include intended applicant population as soon as possible rather than relying only on challenge enthusiasts. Give the three short product sessions plus independent criteria on different cases. A balanced incomplete-block design assigns at least two cases per session to a research subset to estimate case variance while a separate subset receives the intended one-case-per-session design. Counterbalance case family, order, rater assignment and response mode where feasible; hold reveal timing fixed within each case version, and vary it only in a registered experiment. Predeclare which variables are randomized; log allocation and deviations. Record baseline role experience, domain familiarity, riddle/game exposure and permitted assistance without treating them as automatic ability adjustments.

For E03, build a calibration set before the prospective sample with high/low, unusual-valid, concise, fluent-but-wrong, inaudible and accommodation cases in each session. Raters score independently, revise anchors only in this development phase, then freeze them. Test the frozen AI against new adjudicated answers for dimension error, evidence-span correctness, rank/threshold changes and consistency under meaning-preserving paraphrase; preserve failure examples. For E04, use the crossed multi-case subset to estimate person, case-family, variant, rater, mode and interactions; run D-study projections specifically for **one** case per session. The operational design is tested rather than inheriting the research battery's reliability.

**Stage C, prospective decision study (E06/E08).** After measurement quality is acceptable, recruit a separate applicant-like cohort from multiple employers/time periods. Freeze scores before outcomes. Where lawful and agreed, run the assessment in shadow mode alongside existing selection, so it cannot affect opportunities or labels. Give all participants a common, separately scored work sample to obtain an outcome available beyond hires. Follow later job outcomes only for those who enter comparable roles; record who was hired, why and their opportunity to perform. A randomized opportunity or a justified alternative design is needed before claiming top-`C` hiring utility for the entire applicant pool. Challenge feedback is separately randomized or phased; its engagement data are not pooled into selection-validity claims.

Recruitment is stratified for role level, language/mode and experience ranges needed for the target use. Record refusal, noncompletion, exclusion, disability accommodation and source channel. No concealed exclusions after outcome inspection. Because correlations, case-by-person variance and subgroup precision are unknown, determine `N` by simulation from pilot variance under realistic clustering, attrition, base rates and smallest material effect; publish power/precision curves and a minimum analyzable sample per stage. Do not use a round convenient sample size as a validation claim.

## 3. Administration, masking and data flow

1. Obtain consent for each purpose separately: fun challenge/feedback, research scoring, recording retention, contact for follow-up, optional subgroup audit, and employer use if ever authorized. Explain case/tool rules and alternate response modes. The candidate may withdraw under the approved protocol.
2. Capture the exact case version, allowed information, timestamped reveal, 10-minute cap, recording quality and response mode. Permit a documented practice item that is never scored. Technical failure gets an announced retry/reassignment rule, not an implicit zero.
3. Produce raw ASR, candidate-corrected transcript if offered, and a blind human transcription subset. Separate identifiers. Raters receive standardized text/artifact evidence and are blinded to candidate identity, other-session scores, AI scores, comparator results and future outcomes. Direct-media review is a separate mode experiment.
4. Two trained experts independently score a connected calibration sample; rotate raters across case families. Include anchor, unusual-valid, concise, fluent-bluff and counterexample responses. Adjudication occurs only after independent raw ratings are preserved. Blind experts score the independent work samples; different observers score team behavior.
5. Freeze evidence extraction prompt/model, rubric, transformations and analysis code before validation. Log hashes, versions, access, errors and every rescore. An AI change creates a new version requiring bridging; no silent within-cohort replacement.

## 4. Independent criteria and comparators

| Session | Criterion available to all Stage B/C participants | Later job criterion when available | Equal-time and rival comparator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unseen creative engineering task: a constraint-rich repair/invention with blind separate novelty, usefulness and feasibility ratings. | Blind review of opportunity-adjusted improvement/design/problem-solving artifacts, where feasible. | Equal-time conventional reasoning task; prior riddle exposure and verbal-fluency measures. |
| 2 | Unseen written/diagrammed architecture work sample using a different domain and hidden requirement change; blind design-quality rating. | Blind artifact review for requirement fit, reliability/operability, tradeoffs and maintainability; separate from supervisor halo. | Equal-time design sample, current technical interview, domain/stack familiarity. |
| 3 | Standardized pair/team exercise with a real coordination dependency, assigned counterparty and observable message, repair and credit decisions. Pair assignment is randomized and partner effects estimated. | Multi-source, behavior-anchored coordination evidence when role opportunity exists, with assessor masking. | Structured work SJT, pure game, best-action vs likely-action framing, and existing reference/interview. |

Criterion raters must use separate anchors from session raters and be blind to candidate assessment scores. The primary criterion for each session is declared before the run; later outcomes are not interchangeable with an easy proxy. A supervisor's general “good engineer” rating alone is insufficient. Measure criterion reliability and partner/team/context variance. Record whether each person had the opportunity to demonstrate the target behavior.

## 5. Primary estimands and analysis

**Measurement:** For each session, estimate absolute decision dependability `Φ_s` and the probability that a candidate's relative placement or threshold classification changes under plausible case/rater/mode replacements. Report variance components and D-study projections for the actual one-case design. Anchor-level agreement is secondary.

**Transfer:** On a held-out cohort, estimate `ρ_s = corr(S_s, Y_s)` and `Δ_s`, the difference in prespecified predictive loss/decision value when `S_s` is added to the other two sessions plus baseline covariates, compared with adding the equal-time comparator. Use cross-fitting only within development data, then a locked external employer/time/case-family test. Report confidence/credible intervals, calibration where applicable, and raw as well as context-adjusted associations. Include confound checks for familiarity, fluency, experience and response mode. Exploratory models cannot rescue a failed preregistered score without a new study.

**Construct separation:** With the multi-case subset, compare correlated three-session factors against a common interview-performance factor and inspect whether each session better predicts its aligned independent criterion than the other two criteria. Predeclare the model comparison, fit diagnostics and smallest meaningful alignment difference. If factors are not separable, report a general verbal-case performance construct rather than three independently validated abilities.

**Combined selection claim:** For capacity `C`, define `U(π)=E[Y_job | selected by policy π at capacity C]` only in a design with outcome identification for the relevant pool. Primary contrasts are `U(π_3x10)-U(L)` with prespecified non-inferiority margin `δ`, `U(π_3x10)-U(Q)` with a superiority/materiality margin, and an absolute quality floor. Also estimate false rejection of later strong performers, cost and candidate time. If outcomes exist only for those selected by `L`, estimate within-hire prediction only and explicitly withhold pool-wide `U` and “hire the best” claims. Examine each session's ablation and interactions; gains of a composite do not validate a weak session. Account for employer clustering, capacity/base-rate differences and multiple primary comparisons in the registered analysis.

**Missingness/selection:** Show a flow diagram from invitation through follow-up by channel/mode. Predeclare inverse-probability/weighting or bounded sensitivity analyses only where assumptions are defensible and measured; report worst/best plausible missing-outcome bounds. Do not impute unobserved job performance for rejected candidates as fact. Do not use post-treatment opportunity variables as casual score corrections without a causal design.

**Fairness/access:** By session and intersection where precision permits, report participation/completion, technical failure, ASR evidence deletion, criterion-level residual error, form/rater/mode differential functioning, score calibration, threshold instability and false rejection against independent criteria. Report denominators and intervals; a null significance test with low power is not evidence of parity. Evaluate transcript correction and text/diagram alternatives for construct and criterion equivalence. Any legal group data collection or subgroup reporting requires purpose, consent and jurisdiction review.

## 6. Decision and stopping rules

Before Stage B, set minimum acceptable absolute dependability, largest tolerated threshold-flip probability, smallest material `Δ_s`, margin `δ`, subgroup precision/disparity tolerances and candidate-burden ceiling through role-owner and independent psychometric review. Values are intentionally unset because role stakes and consequences differ. Stage progression is based on intervals and practical effects, not only `p < .05`.

Stop or redesign a case family if it is leaked, insoluble, asks for protected/personal information, lacks fair alternatives, or produces form effects that cannot be equated. Stop AI scoring use if it changes valid answers, misses critical evidence, or produces unacceptable mode/subgroup errors. Stop a session's hiring interpretation if one-case reliability is inadequate, the independent criterion transfer fails, or a simpler equal-time comparator is at least as useful under the declared burden/cost tradeoff. A failed Session 3 game may lead to a work-case redesign; it cannot be relabeled successful by scoring “niceness.” A failed three-by-ten hypothesis permits adding cases or abandoning the limit. Null, negative and inconclusive results remain publishable research outputs.

## 7. Run package and reproducibility

Each run deposits: registered protocol and amendments; role/task map; immutable item manifest and exposure decisions; generator prompts and rejection log; rubric anchors; sampling/allocation log; consent/data schedule; anonymized response/criterion linkage under controlled access; independent rater and AI raw outputs; ASR mode comparison; preregistered analysis code; missingness flow; effect/variance estimates with uncertainty; subgroup limitations; deviations; adverse events; and an explicit `supported`, `contradicted` or `inconclusive` finding per claim. Public material must avoid identifiable answers and reusable live items. The [scoring specification](scoring-model.md) is versioned alongside every run.
