# Lab artifact-carrier and capability audit

Audit date: 2026-10-26. Scope: this project's current files against the Lab's selected [definition catalog](../../../CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/lab/reference/definitions.md), [artifact-type catalog](../../../CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/lab/reference/artifact-types.md), and on-disk selected schemas. This is a **local integration audit**, not Lab admission, Project State, a research run, or authority to collect candidate data.

## The four layers that must stay separate

1. **Semantic Definition:** what a Product, Experiment, Criterion Rating, etc. means. The project currently has 78 candidate local terms in [definitions.json](../../model/definitions.json); ten Lab meanings were selected in the original historical Define Packs, six access meanings in a later candidate Pack, and seven further Lab meanings plus two local metric meanings in the [new historical preparation](PRODUCT-RESEARCH-CONNECTION-RESULT.md). None of these Packs grants admission.
2. **Artifact Type / carrier contract:** required machine fields, semantic bindings, source direction, human projection, validation and claim ceiling. A definition does not create its carrier. The Lab's [Artifact Type meaning](../../../CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/lab/reference/records/def-cal-definition-artifact-type.md) states this separation.
3. **Artifact instance:** a particular versioned record such as this Product identity, E06 design or future scoring run. A schema pass is evidence about shape, not standing or truth.
4. **Decision and effect:** owner decisions, source custody, observed execution and outcome evidence are separate. Neither a candidate record nor a prepared Definition Pack grants collection, hiring or publication authority.

The [structural check](../../tools/validate_lab_carriers.py) currently reports **34/34** candidate records conforming to the selected on-disk Lab schemas, with local source paths resolving. It does not check semantic admission, reference existence in Lab Project State, permission, run execution or scientific validity. Its result can change if the selected Lab schemas change.

## Are we using all Lab capabilities?

**No.** The Lab catalog exposes many capabilities beyond what this product currently needs. Using every Lab carrier would create records with no real subject or evidence. The relevant chain currently stops at a prepared exact meaning Pack, a local ontology, schema-conforming candidate records and unapplied relation proposals. It has not reached Research Run, Experiment Run, Measurement Result, Finding, Decision, Product Capability realization or runtime adoption. That is an accurate stage boundary, not a failed experiment.

Prioritize the next applicable capabilities in this order: (1) current-host and semantic/carrier owner review; (2) a compatible standalone Hypothesis preregistration route; (3) role-specific Measurement Method and case/rubric carrier contracts; (4) permissioned Research/Experiment Runs and Results; (5) Evidence Sets, Findings and owner Decisions; (6) implementation/realization and employer use only after the evidence and decisions support them.
