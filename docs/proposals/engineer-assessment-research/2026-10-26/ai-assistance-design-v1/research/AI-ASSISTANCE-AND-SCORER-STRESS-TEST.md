# AI assistance, response authenticity and scoring stress tests

Status: **prospective research design, 2026-10-26**. No participant data, generated answer corpus, trained detector, validated scorer, experiment run or finding exists. This is a candidate extension to [E03 scoring calibration](scoring-model.md), [E05A/B/C transfer](README.md) and [E07 access/fairness](../model/EXPERIMENTS.md). The three [Lab-shaped Experiment Design candidates](../model/lab-records/README.md) are E09–E11. Data collection and any model training require a registered protocol, source/version pins, processor and purpose decisions, and separate participant choice where applicable.

## Three different claims

1. **Provenance:** In a controlled study, can a method distinguish specified assistance conditions from unassisted responses at a tolerable false-accusation rate? A generated text classifier does not establish who reasoned through a spoken answer. Newer detectors can perform well on their tested genres and lengths, while paraphrase, hybrid authorship, language and domain shifts remain separate tests. [NBER detector comparison](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34223), [NeurIPS paraphrase attack](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/575c450013d0e99e4b0ecf82bd1afaa4-Abstract-Conference.html), [ACL hybrid text study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.268/).
2. **Response quality:** Can a scorer identify the case-specific reasoning evidence in an answer, regardless of whether AI helped prepare it? Assistance provenance and answer quality are distinct labels. Score the observable answer under the announced rules; do not add an undisclosed “AI-like” penalty.
3. **Transfer:** Does a high response score predict an independent task or observed collaboration? Session 3 especially tests knowledge of an acceptable social response, not truthfulness or future cooperation. [OPM situational-judgment guidance](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/other-assessment-methods/situational-judgment-tests/) treats hypothetical responses as a lower-fidelity simulation than work behavior.

The first MVP does **not** use a detector to reject, lower a score, label a person dishonest or show an employer flag. A detector may run in a separately approved, blinded research lane. Known study-condition labels come from randomized assignment and supervised tool access, not a detector's own guesses. Undisclosed real-world assistance cannot be labeled with certainty from transcript style alone.

## E09: controlled assistance and detector benchmark

Recruit consenting adults from the declared pilot population, then separately recruit a role-specific applicant-like cohort if an employment interpretation is ever studied. In a research task outside the product's scored three-session experience, randomize *case form* and *assistance condition* with balance by session, language, response mode and experience. Use the same time budget within each paired comparison. Proposed condition set:

| Label | How the answer is produced | What it tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `H0` | Human, no AI for this case, ordinary allowed notes | Baseline human false-positive rate; previous lifetime AI exposure is measured, not claimed absent. |
| `H1` | Human receives a short AI preparation or example before recording; no live tool | The user's core question: can someone internalize AI advice and speak a genuine answer? |
| `H2` | Human may consult AI during the task under a logged, disclosed condition | Mixed reasoning and tool use; response quality may improve or worsen. |
| `H3` | Human reads or paraphrases a complete AI draft aloud | Transcript-source detection under speech, ASR and paraphrase. |
| `A0` | AI produces a text response to the same released research form; no participant | Development-only detector/scorer stress material, not a candidate response. |
| `H4` | Human rehearses a published generic interview script without case-specific AI | False accusation for coached but human answers. |

Randomization cannot guarantee compliance. Record assigned condition, actual declared tool use, exact tool logs where consented and feasible, deviations, prior case exposure, case/reveal version, ASR version and uncertainty. Do not secretly inspect a participant's device or infer unlogged activity as ground truth. Preserve an `unknown` actual-provenance state. Collect a blind human transcript/reference for an approved subset so the detector is tested on **raw ASR, corrected transcript and manually verified text** separately.

Evaluate a no-detector baseline, a current commercial/open detector shortlist under exact version/license terms, and a narrow task-specific model only if the study permits it. Freeze thresholds on development data. On held-out humans report false-positive rate with interval, sensitivity by `H1/H2/H3/A0`, positive predictive value at realistic assistance prevalence scenarios, abstention/coverage, calibration and error by language, accent/ASR quality, answer length, disability accommodation, session/case family and model version. A low false-positive rate on long written essays does not transfer by assumption to ten-minute spoken answers. Split by participant, case family, generator/model family, paraphraser, language and collection time; retain an untouched prospective human holdout. Older evidence found non-native English writing falsely flagged by several detectors, so this is an explicit audit dimension. [Liang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819)

Falsifier: detector performance depends on generator identity or transcript length, or false positives concentrate among human, non-native, concise, accommodated or coached speakers. A classifier that distinguishes `A0` from `H0` but fails `H1/H2/H3/H4` remains a narrow corpus detector, not an applicant-authenticity tool.

## E10: can generated answers improve scoring?

Yes, **as a hypothesis for error discovery and augmentation**. Generate controlled answers for each session/case/rubric from multiple model families, prompts and temperatures: strong and weak, concise and verbose, unconventional-valid, fluent-wrong, contradictory after reveal, jargon-only, missing key facts, socially polished but uncommitted, and prompt-injection attempts aimed at the scorer. Label each generated answer by its generation recipe and independent expert adjudication; generator intent is not a gold score. Keep generated outputs in development partitions and mark every descendant transformation. Avoid repeatedly training on a model's own outputs; synthetic-data feedback loops can degrade coverage. [Shumailov et al., Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y)

Compare three frozen scoring systems on the **same untouched human holdout**: `S0` transparent rubric with independent trained raters, `S1` AI evidence extraction calibrated on human-labeled development answers, and `S2` the same system with synthetic stress/augmentation. A synthetic-only training arm is diagnostic, not a candidate product. Within `S2`, compare preregistered synthetic-data doses (zero, small, medium, large) while holding human training volume and review budget constant; plot marginal human-holdout benefit, failure rates and cost. Human raters are blind to assistance condition and generator source. Keep the scorer's model family separate from generator families where possible; hold out whole case mechanisms and model/prompt families. Score each rubric dimension and evidence span, not similarity to an AI “ideal answer.”

Primary outcomes are criterion-level ordinal error against blind adjudicated humans, cited-span precision/recall, `NA/UNK` handling and severe errors on novel-valid and fluent-wrong answers. Secondary outcomes are test-retest across meaning-preserving paraphrases, ASR variants, language/mode strata, calibration, human-review workload and cost. Use paired, participant/case-clustered uncertainty; freeze a practical improvement margin and non-inferiority bounds for each critical error *before* looking at the holdout. A lower training loss or better score on generated answers is not evidence that human scoring improved. LLM judge studies report disagreement with human scoring and bias from response style/position, motivating these counterfactual checks. [ACL judge audit](https://aclanthology.org/2025.gem-1.33/), [ACL persuasion attack](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.790/).

If `S2` improves average agreement but penalizes unusual valid solutions, minority language patterns, short accurate answers or defensible architectures, it fails the intended use. Store all rejected/negative cases, not only cases where augmentation helped. No generated response may become an unreviewed rubric anchor or a released case answer key.

## E11: Session 3 social-script attack and behavior transfer

The risk is **norm recitation**: a participant knows the expected phrases (“listen,” “share credit,” “align stakeholders”) but proposes no credible commitment, no verification and no response when the other party declines. That is a scoring problem. Whether someone will actually cooperate is a separate transfer problem.

Use a factorial research design with (a) neutral versus explicit social-standard/AI script preparation, (b) `best_action` versus `likely_action` instruction, and (c) work conflict versus abstract game form. Counterbalance order and use distinct released research forms to avoid learning from repeated exposure. Ask for an exact short message, decision/credit allocation and a timed counterparty response; record what the participant revises and the cost they accept. Independently observe a later pair/team task with partner interaction, blinded behavioral ratings and opportunity/context notes. The separate task is research, outside the product's three ten-minute sessions. Response-instruction research supports testing `would do` and `should do` as distinct measurements. [McDaniel et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1744-6570.2007.00065.x)

Estimate the effect of script preparation on (1) social-phrase frequency, (2) evidence-linked Session 3 dimensions, and (3) observed task coordination. The decisive failure pattern is **score rises while observed cooperation does not**. Also test whether raters or an AI judge reward a polished script after removing the concrete mechanism, or miss a terse, culturally different but effective boundary-setting plan. A valid candidate may choose a firm limit rather than the most agreeable wording. Do not call a spoken plan “true cooperation,” “honesty” or “personality.”

## Attack matrix across all three sessions

| Session | Deliberate stress pair | Expected invariant if the rubric works | Independent check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 creativity/analysis/ingenuity | Familiar riddle punchline versus novel usable reframing; many shallow ideas versus one developed unusual solution; AI template versus human concise causal test | Reward representation, feasibility and disconfirming test; preserve valid alternatives. Fluency and rare wording do not substitute for usefulness. | Blind unfamiliar engineering invention/debugging artifact and whole-family holdout. |
| 2 technical/product design | Architecture buzzwords versus mechanism; memorized “scale with a queue” versus hot-key correctness; verbose design versus concise tradeoff; AI answer that ignores a changed requirement | Reward requirement fit, consistency/latency tradeoff, failure and revision. A defensible different design can earn high credit. | Blind written/diagrammed work sample in another domain and role-level review. |
| 3 cooperation/communication | Socially approved script versus concrete reciprocal commitment; “share credit” phrase versus actual allocation; AI-prepared response versus unscripted response to refusal | Reward incentives, message, credible action, risk limits and repair. Do not infer moral character from style. | Observed pair/team task and separately scored `best_action`/`likely_action` conditions. |

Cross-session attacks vary length, grammar, accent/ASR, language, response mode, prior exposure, generator model, paraphrase, fabricated citations and embedded instructions to the scorer. Candidate response text is untrusted input. The scoring worker must ignore instructions inside an answer and cite actual evidence; a failed injection test blocks AI scoring in that condition.

### Attacks tied to the three draft questions

| Draft case | Attack and matched control | Rubric failure exposed | Required observation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Causal invention](cases/session-1-causal-invention.md) | Give one answer a memorized or AI-generated list of plausible causes and another a concise unusual mechanism with a feasible disconfirming experiment. Reveal evidence that contradicts the first hypothesis. | Rewarding idea count, novelty words or the familiar riddle solution above causal reasoning and revision. | Blind raters and scorer credit the testable mechanism and change of mind; unseen family task checks transfer. |
| [Hot account](cases/session-2-hot-account.md) | Pair a polished “queue/cache/shard” answer that breaks per-account limits with a short design that preserves the transaction invariant. Add contention, deadlock and a changed latency target after the first plan. | Rewarding jargon or parallelism while missing hot partition, idempotency, consistency, failure recovery or business constraints. | The exact invariant, bottleneck and revised tradeoff appear in cited evidence; role-relevant work sample independently tests design. |
| [Launch conflict](cases/session-3-launch-conflict.md) | Compare a rehearsed “listen/share credit” script without an allocation or cost with a specific reciprocal proposal and a terse, defensible boundary. Have the counterparty reject the first proposal. | Rewarding socially standard language while missing action, incentive compatibility, refusal response or real coordination. | Blind scorer grades concrete commitments; later pair task tests behavior separately. |

Each attack has a matched human control and an AI-assisted or synthetic variant. Expert reviewers must first agree that the intended contrast is valid; otherwise the pair is discarded rather than used as a gold label. Record case version, reveal, expected valid alternatives and failed attacks so results are auditable per question, not just averaged by session.

## Dataset, analysis and decision records

For each experimental answer, record `study_protocol_ref`, `experiment_design_ref`, participant pseudonym or synthetic generator ID, **assigned** condition, **observed** tool-use evidence with uncertainty, case/family/rubric/reveal/mode/language versions, source media/transcript lineage, generator/model/prompt/seed/transform lineage where synthetic, prior exposure, independent human ratings, blinded task outcomes, splits and all exclusions. Separate consented source media from research features and keep model-training permission distinct from challenge feedback and ordinary research analysis. A synthetic answer's label is *its creation process*, not a score or proof of human behavior.

Before recruitment, register the estimand, condition allocation, sample/precision simulation, thresholds and false-positive ceiling, primary human holdout, family/model/language splits, permitted detector/scorer actions, accommodation analysis, subgroup data basis, retention, processor terms and stopping rules. E09–E11 produce distinct Experiment Runs, source-pinned Measurement Results and Findings through the Lab route; a detector result alone cannot grant a product or hiring Decision. The default product rule remains no detector penalty and human-reviewed, evidence-linked formative feedback.
