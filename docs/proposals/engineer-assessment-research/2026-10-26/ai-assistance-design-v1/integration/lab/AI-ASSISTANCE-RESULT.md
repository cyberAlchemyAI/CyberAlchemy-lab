The [three Experiment Design/v1 candidate records](../../model/lab-records/README.md) bind these tests to the project, existing inquiries, product capabilities and protocol. They have no run, evidence or finding references and have no authority effect.

Synthetic answers are development material; their benefits must appear on an untouched human holdout, including valid unusual and terse answers.
