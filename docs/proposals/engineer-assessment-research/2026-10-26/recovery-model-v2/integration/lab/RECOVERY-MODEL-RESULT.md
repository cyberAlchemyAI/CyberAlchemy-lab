# Session recovery modeling result

Status: **candidate design and review input; no Lab admission or live policy effect**. The [protocol](../../software/RECOVERY-PROTOCOL.md) answers connectivity, crash, capture, upload and downstream failures. Its [20 source-bound Recovery Rule records](../../model/recovery/index.json) are concrete candidate specifications; the [Recovery Decision](../../model/recovery/synthetic/recovery-decision.json) and [Upload Attempt](../../model/recovery/synthetic/upload-attempt.json) examples are explicitly synthetic shape witnesses. No participant event, actual media transfer, owner Decision or case replacement has been recorded.

The three new meanings are in the [81-definition source](../../model/definitions.json), [individual Definition/v1 candidate records](../../model/definition-records/index.json) and [ontology proposal](../../model/ontology.json). The four new [Artifact Type Requests](../../model/recovery/README.md) compare Administration Policy with Lab Policy/v2, Recovery Rule with Lab Policy/v2, Recovery Decision with Lab Decision/v2 and Upload Attempt with Lab Operational Observation/v1. Each has a distinct local closed schema and an unadmitted descriptor; none claims Lab Artifact Type/v3 conformance. The [current coverage](../../model/carriers/coverage.json) accounts for 142 selected-carrier and 117 local-carrier candidate instances, 14 Artifact Type Requests and 13 explicit gaps. The earlier [235-record review result](CONCRETE-CARRIER-COVERAGE-RESULT.md) remains an exact historical cut.

The rule is to **retain the same case and original deadline for a transport interruption** when local capture is intact and required reveals were delivered. A finished recording can resume multipart upload within the policy window. A lost recording or missed reveal leads to technical void/review; replacement is a linked successor in the same slot only if a released compatible form and approved retry policy exist. New cases are never generated as an automatic response to a failed connection. The [draft Administration Policy](../../model/recovery/administration-policy-candidate.json) records proposed timed values and has no release decision. The [gap register](../../model/carriers/gaps.json) leaves the issued Administration Policy, browser timing assurance, and equivalent replacement inventory open.

A [frozen source cut](../../../CyberAlchemy-lab/docs/proposals/engineer-assessment-research/2026-10-26/recovery-model-v2/manifest.json) pins the relevant project files and selected Lab request/definition registry evidence for review. It is not an owner application or admission. The current checks prove definition/source parity, rule/source parity, schema conformance, request shape, coverage accounting and Markdown links; they cannot prove browser continuity, fairness, case equivalence or participant authorization.
