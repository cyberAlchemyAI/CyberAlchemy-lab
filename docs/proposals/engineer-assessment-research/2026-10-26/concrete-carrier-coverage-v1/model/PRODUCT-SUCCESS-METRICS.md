# Product success scorecard — `EA-PSM-0.1`

Status: candidate measurement and decision specification. It instantiates the local [Product Success Metric meaning](definitions.json) for this product. No pilot, employer validation or commercial launch has produced values. Participant-facing answer metrics are specified separately in [Participant Metrics](../software/PARTICIPANT-METRICS.md); those values do not measure product success by themselves.

The [product–research trace](PRODUCT-RESEARCH-TRACE.md) binds each metric group to a capability, experiment, possible finding and product decision. A scorecard value without that lineage remains descriptive.

## Decision question by phase

| Phase | Product decision | Primary evidence | Must also hold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P2 private mixed-developer pilot | Is the three-session challenge useful and operable enough to continue? | All-three completion `PS-02` **and** understood/actionable feedback `PS-04/05`, with burden `PS-03`. | Access, custody, case and transcription quality `PS-06/07/08/16`; no hiring claim. |
| P3 named-role measurement | Do the three sessions yield sufficiently stable and distinct work-relevant evidence? | One-case design dependability `PS-09` and each session's independent transfer `PS-10`. | Case/mode/subgroup error, role-task linkage and accepted precision. |
| P4 prospective hiring-thesis study | Does the compact policy identify later strong performers at fixed capacity? | Independent selection utility `PS-11`: absolute floor, non-inferiority to the longer pipeline and superiority to equal-time comparator. | False rejection, calibration and fairness/access `PS-12/13`, valid outcome coverage and holdouts. |
| P5 product-value decision | Does the validated use save meaningful effort and sustain an offering? | Employer/candidate time and total cost `PS-14`, actual employer value/adoption `PS-15`, unit economics `PS-17`. | Current validity, rights and operating quality; an employer's stated interest alone is insufficient. |

No target numbers are chosen here. For each phase the accountable owner and research lead must register the target, minimum acceptable precision, comparison, sample/observation window and stop rule **before** seeing its outcome data. A dashboard color or metric value has no authority effect; the claim ladder and owner decision remain in [PRODUCT-MODEL.md](PRODUCT-MODEL.md) and [AUTHORITY.md](AUTHORITY.md).

## Metric register

`N(...)` denotes a count under the frozen protocol. Every ratio reports numerator, denominator, observation window, exclusions, uncertainty interval and missingness. Use unique participants for people ratios, exact attempts for process ratios, and exact case versions for item measures; never interchange them. Stratify only where purpose and precision allow. Do not suppress failures or withdrawals from the denominator because they hurt the result.

| ID / value tested | Operational measure and denominator | Source; phase and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PS-01` Invited-to-start conversion | `N(eligible invitees starting scored Session 1) / N(eligible invitations delivered)`; separately report redeemed, declined, unreachable and eligibility unknown. | Invitation and session-event ledger; P2 recruitment friction. Recruitment channel matters; this is not validity. |
| `PS-02` Three-case completion | `N(starters with three valid committed responses within policy) / N(starters of scored Session 1)`; also per-slot continuation and reasons for technical failure, withdrawal and timeout. | Server receipts, assignment and event ledger; P2 primary journey measure. Publish mode/channel strata with intervals, not only a completer-only rate. |
| `PS-03` Candidate burden | Distribution of wall minutes from first case shown to final upload receipt among all starters, with unfinished attempts and censoring reported; separately `Σ recorded_seconds ≤1800` for compliant completions, plus participant-rated burden and access effort. | Server events and optional survey; P2/P5. A ten-minute recording cap does not mean the whole journey takes 30 minutes. |
| `PS-04` Feedback comprehension | `N(report readers correctly identifying that results describe case answers, missingness and no hiring decision) / N(readers answering the fixed comprehension items)`; report response rate against all reports delivered and sensitivity treating nonresponders as unknown. | Frozen post-report questions; P2. Measure understanding, not merely report views. |
| `PS-05` Actionable feedback | `N(respondents able to name a specific, feasible next practice step tied to their cited answer) / N(report recipients answering the fixed actionability item)`; report survey response rate. Optional later follow-up records tried/not tried among those who permitted contact. | Blinded coding of short responses and follow-up; P2/E08. Satisfaction alone is insufficient; self-reported practice is not skill improvement. |
| `PS-06` Access and completion gap | `PS-02` and `PS-04` by approved response mode, language, accommodation route and other lawfully collected audit strata; report difference/ratio with interval, cell sizes and missingness. | Events and separately governed audit data; P2–P5 guardrail. A raw gap is a signal for investigation, not a causal explanation or a score correction. |
| `PS-07` Evidence loss from transcription | In a predeclared human-verified audit subset: `N(answers whose criterion rating or evidence status materially changes between raw ASR and verified source/corrected transcript) / N(audited answers)`, per session/mode/language where supported. Report `UNK` and correction frequency separately. | Paired pinned scoring runs; P2/P3 guardrail. Register “material” before audit; no unreviewed ASR can silently set feedback. |
| `PS-08` Case health | `N(administered case versions held/retired for ambiguity, exposure or unfair elicitation) / N(administered case versions)` plus affected administration count, prior-familiarity rate and reason. | Item-bank evaluation/exposure/disposition; P2/P3. A low retirement count alone does not prove item quality. |
| `PS-09` One-case measurement dependability | Generalizability `G` and absolute-decision `Φ` for the **operational one-case-per-session** design, with variance components and interval; report rater/mode/case contribution and near-threshold instability. | E04 crossed case/rater/mode study; P3. Multi-case battery reliability cannot substitute for one-case reliability. |
| `PS-10` Session transfer and added value | For each session, held-out association and incremental predictive value for its aligned independent creative/design/observed-coordination criterion beyond the other sessions and named equal-time comparator, with uncertainty. | E05A/B/C blinded work criteria; P3. A narrated cooperation answer must be checked against actual coordination. |
| `PS-11` Fixed-capacity selection utility | For a predeclared role, pool, outcome `Y`, horizon and capacity `C`: `U(π)=mean(Y among the C people selected by policy π)` or another preregistered consequence-weighted utility. Estimate `U(3×10)`, `Δ_L=U(3×10)-U(longer pipeline)` and `Δ_Q=U(3×10)-U(equal-time comparator)` on the **same held-out pool and C**, with intervals. | E06 independent outcomes; P4 strong-thesis primary. Predeclare absolute floor, non-inferiority margin for `Δ_L` and superiority margin for `Δ_Q`. Incomplete outcomes require a registered missing-label analysis; observed hires alone do not identify full-pool utility. |
| `PS-12` Missed-strong-performer rate | Among people who meet a predeclared independent high-performance criterion and have outcome opportunity, `N(not selected by policy at C) / N(strong performers)`; compare all policies at the same C and report outcome missingness. | E06; P4 error and human-consequence measure. The performance band is fixed without assessment scores. |
| `PS-13` Decision error and fairness | False rejection, calibration and utility differences by approved audit groups and response modes at the same role/capacity, with intervals, cell counts and missingness; investigate error source and access path. | E07/E06; P4 guardrail. No universal fairness conclusion from one cohort or aggregate score distribution. |
| `PS-14` Total decision effort | Candidate wall minutes plus employer/reviewer/support minutes and infrastructure/vendor cost per **eligible assessed person** and per decision, including failed attempts, appeals and adjudication; compare longer and equal-time policies at equal role/pool. | Time logs, vendor invoices and process ledger; P5/E08. Report cost components; time saved cannot excuse lower outcome quality. |
| `PS-15` Employer use and value | Among employer pilots with an authorized use, `N(teams completing the agreed workflow and requesting/renewing a subsequent cycle) / N(eligible pilot teams)`; pair with observed workflow time and selection outcome, not intent survey alone. | Contract/workflow receipts and E08; P5. Report small samples and reasons for nonrenewal. No employer access in first MVP. |
| `PS-16` Trust and rights operation | `N(verified rights requests resolved within the owner-approved service period) / N(verified requests due in period)`, plus unresolved backlog, unauthorized-access incidents, deletion/correction propagation failures and affected people. | Rights/audit/incident ledger; P2–P5 stop guardrail. The service period is selected before collection; severe custody or permission failure stops new collection independently of a percentage. |
| `PS-17` Unit economics | For an authorized paid offering: `(recognized product revenue − variable delivery, scoring, review, support, payment and incident costs) / paid assessments` over a declared accounting window; show acquisition and fixed research/build cost separately. | Finance and operations ledger; P5. A positive variable margin is not proof of validated assessment value or overall profitability. |

## Registration and dashboard rules

Each metric version records: `metric_id`, phase/capability/claim, owner, exact population and eligibility, source versions, event or outcome definition, numerator/denominator, observation window, exclusions, missing-data rule, subgroup/mode plan, uncertainty method, comparator, target or margin, stop/continue rule and result/decision link. An experiment run reports planned and observed versions, deviations and negative findings. The first pilot can populate `PS-01`–`PS-08` and `PS-16`; `PS-09`–`PS-15` and `PS-17` require later designs or a paid offering.

The product dashboard presents **phase and evidence status** (`not started`, `collecting`, `estimated with limitations`, `decision-ready`, `contradicted`) beside every value. A phase is not “successful” because one metric improved. Pilot continuation needs useful feedback *and* workable completion without unresolved access, case or custody failures. The hiring thesis needs the registered `PS-11` comparisons plus `PS-09/10/12/13` supporting evidence. Economic success requires value after costs and an authorized scope; it cannot promote a weak hiring claim.

For E08, compare feedback/product variants under a preregistered randomized or phased design where feasible. Keep recruitment channel, voluntary participation, responder bias, practice effects and employer selection of pilots visible. Pilot metrics describe the invited sample; transport to applicants, roles and employers is a separate question. Small cohorts report intervals and counts, not only percentages. Publish aggregate findings only under the accepted purpose and privacy rules.
