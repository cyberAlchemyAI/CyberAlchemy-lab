# Reader study: using CyberAlchemy Lab documentation

## Purpose

Observe whether readers who are new to the Lab can find and use the documentation. Recruit 5–8 people across engineering, operations and product roles if available. This is a formative study, not a population estimate.

Use an anonymous participant code. Do not collect names, customer data or employer details. Ask permission before taking notes or recording. A reader may stop at any point. Recordings are optional; the response form works without them.

## Setup

Share the documentation folder with its neighboring Craft documentation folder, or serve their common parent. Begin at `start-here.html`. Give the reader the [response form](reader-study.html). Do not share this facilitator guide until the tasks are finished.

Allow 25–35 minutes. Ask readers to say what they are looking for and why they chose a passage. Avoid teaching the answer while a task is in progress. If someone needs help, record the intervention. Ask about confidence and confusion after the answer, before discussing the expected interpretation.

## Tasks and rubric

1. **Find the permission.** “An agent has been assigned to investigate a missing export. Find what determines whether it may inspect the data or deploy a change.” Success: locate Delegation/current authority and distinguish it from assignment, criterion and view. Deployment requires a separately applicable grant/gate.
2. **Prepare the assessment.** “Use the Northstar files and prepare the shortest assessment you would send for review.” Success: S1/Northstar/status=active/E12/method 2; actual missing-ID comparison; 200 absent; unresolved cause, wider impact, containment/recovery; candidate/review standing. Counts alone are insufficient.
3. **Inspect standing.** “Open the Work example and explain whether it is accepted and what would establish that.” Success: candidate specimen; schema validity or admitted type does not accept it; exact applicable owner decision and standing evidence required.
4. **Explain continuation.** “At 10:10 the task view expires. The permission period ends at 10:15. What can the agent do next? What survives if the session ends?” Success: pause; fresh usable views and current permission needed; durable Project State survives; terminated machine instance/session memory do not transfer to a new session.
5. **Read Craft capacity.** “What happens at 4/5, 5/5 and 6/5? Does Accept reserve a place?” Success: strict `<` yields Accept/Reject/Reject; pure Calculation leaves capacity unchanged; reservation is a separate state-changing Operation, not established by classification.
6. **Transfer to a new case.** “An export for another account has 1,000 required identifiers and 1,000 output rows. Twenty-five required IDs are missing and replaced by other IDs. Values for the remaining rows have not been checked. What can you conclude, and what remains open?” Success: identifier coverage fails despite equal counts; no conclusion about value accuracy, cause, recovery or wider scope; select the new account/filters/snapshot/output before applying a criterion.

## Evidence to record

For each task: elapsed time, answer, pages/anchors used, completion (independent / assisted / unsuccessful / not attempted), assistance, errors, confidence and comments. Retain quotations only with permission. Use anonymous IDs in the report.

After the session, classify issues as navigation, language, concept distinction, evidence selection, task sequence or environment/accessibility. Do not classify hesitation alone as a model error. Match each material misunderstanding to the passage or interaction involved, propose a targeted revision, and check that revision with the reader or a later participant.

## Reporting

Report individual task observations and recurring patterns, with participant role/familiarity and browser/environment. Do not infer statistical success rates from 5–8 participants. Store returned response files locally with consent; do not publish participant data in the documentation site or search index.

No participant session has been run merely by creating this package. The implementation ledger records reader observations separately.

The downloaded response adds task_set=documentation-and-whole-site-v2 while retaining the original study identifier. Keep older six-task responses as that earlier task set.

Select the documentation block, the whole-site block, or both before a session. Allow breaks and record unattempted tasks. For tasks 7–12, observe the chosen path and reasoning without naming pages or controls. Evaluate task 8 against the stated local foundation, ongoing product work and captured edition. Robot scenarios are exploratory proposals, not established deployments. In task 11, account, active filter and fixed S1 bound the required identifiers. In task 12, Calculation returns a pure result and supplies no action permission. A reservation Operation or deployment requires its own applicable authority and lifecycle conditions.
