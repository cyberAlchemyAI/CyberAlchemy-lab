## Blocking audit

The previous goal turn made progress: it finished the remaining map-view review and prepared the manual environment checklist. This turn closed the missing essay/disclosure browser coverage. The same external dependency—actual human observations—has remained across the original release turn and two goal continuations. Existing browser processes have completed; there is no live participant job or verified process wait.

Further required work depends on receiving the reader JSON files or notes and actual assistive-technology observations. The user is arranging readers and has said this will take time. The plan's success criteria are retained; a portable release and prepared forms do not replace the study. The goal can resume when that evidence is supplied, then review recurring problems, make targeted revisions and perform follow-up checks.
