01 / The name

From lead to gold.

Alchemy gave us an image of transformation: starting with something available and working toward something we do not yet know how to make.

We start with an intention, something to understand, change or create. The work is to express it as knowledge, models and systems another participant can examine and use.

Cybernetics gives us a way to study that work. Observe the result, compare it with the intention, respond to the difference and reconsider the understanding behind it.

The name holds an ambition and a responsibility together. We want to transform things, and we want to understand what we are changing.

02 / Our culture

Two incessant questions

What if?How to?

We describe ourselves as a band of deviants and renegades. Curiosity takes us into subjects we still need to learn.

Learning comes first. We expose assumptions and let evidence and criticism challenge our ideas. Being wrong belongs in the work. We need to notice, understand the mistake and change course.

Enter the unfamiliar.
Read, ask, experiment. Expertise is something we work toward.
Examine the premises.
Use epistemology to question what we know, how we know it and what would change our minds.
Welcome correction.
Apply the scientific method. Keep counterexamples and failed experiments available for scrutiny.
Care about the human effort.
Study what people need to understand, review, correct and recover machine work.

03 / Research

Questions worth
working through.

We study reality with the intention of expressing parts of it in machines. Our models, their limits and our own judgments need examination too.

01Intelligence & learning
How do people and machines form models, learn from experience and use what they have learned in unfamiliar situations?
02Novelty & creativity
How does a new idea arise? What makes it useful? How could a machine explore possibilities and judge its own attempts?
03Representing reality
What happens when we translate a concept, a physical process or a social practice into a definition, a model or code?
04Human–machine ergonomics
How can people express intentions, inspect machine reasoning and intervene without carrying an ever-growing burden of supervision?

04 / Research into software

CyberAlchemy Lab

An open-source
company brain system.

A company accumulates knowledge through projects, people, decisions and mistakes. Keeping it useful requires continuing work.

The Lab organizes that knowledge around definitions, evidence and declared change rules. People and agents can recover project meaning, claim support and permission for the next action.

05 / The longer horizon

Intelligence, with
direction and correction.

Our ambition includes more general artificial intelligence. Questions about learning, intention, culture and judgment are a place to start.

Alignment belongs in that inquiry. How does increasingly capable AI stay responsive to human intentions and values when people disagree, circumstances change and a stated goal leaves things unsaid? Read the alignment survey

We study how to express intentions, examine behavior and correct mistakes. The Lab provides a practical setting with explicit meanings, bounded permissions, evidence and accountable changes.

Those structures help us formulate the questions. Establishing good behavior still requires evaluation, stress testing and oversight of actual execution. Explore research on evaluation and oversight
