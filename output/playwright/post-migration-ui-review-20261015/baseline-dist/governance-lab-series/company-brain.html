The work continues.
The context has to continue too.

A customer reports a problem. Someone reads logs, forms a hypothesis and proposes a change. A colleague returns the following morning and needs to recover the basis: the data examined, what “fixed” means, why the proposal exists and who can approve it. That recovery is work in its own right.

An agent session can leave code, an assessment or a plan. The next session needs an account of its meaning and status. A connected record lets a reviewer examine the basis of the explanation, rather than depend on how convincing it sounds.

We’re building the Lab for software, research and operations teams coordinating people and agents across projects. Continuity is part of their work.

Give the company’s knowledge
a structure it can use.

We call the Lab an open-source company brain system. The ambition is practical: connect knowledge to the work it informs with enough structure for people and machines to inspect it.

That knowledge includes important meanings, project intentions, evidence gathered and decisions changing what is accepted. Records and declared connections give those facts identifiable places.

When reading a saved explanation, we still need to understand its role. Is it defining a meaning, recording an observation, proposing a change or authorizing an effect? Those roles have different requirements and limits on use.

Meanings, formats and records

A definition states meaning and boundaries. An accepted exact version is a basic unit of authority over meaning within an accountable owner’s declared purpose and scope.

Definition
States a term’s meaning and where that meaning applies.
Artifact Type
Sets the contract for a kind of record, including its meaning bindings, format and validation requirements.
Artifact
An identifiable, typed and versioned record—for example, this particular investigation’s Work record.

Connections also have rules. An Ontology View displays selected subjects and relationships while retaining identities and the sources it depends on.

Knowledge that can guide work.

AuthorityWhat governs meaning and action?
Which accepted meanings and rules apply, and who may act within which scope? The accepted completeness criterion governs the assessment. A separate, scoped permission determines what the agent may inspect or change.
ProvenanceWhere did this come from?
Where did this information come from, and how was it produced? Identify the source snapshot, account, filters, output, comparison method and recorded result that support the omission claim.
LifecycleWhat can change from here?
What state is this record in, and what conditions allow it to change? The definition, proposed code and incident each have their own state and requirements for change. A proposed fix proceeds through its assessment and owner decision.

Now a team has a basis for examining the claim and response. Evidence supports the assessment. An accountable decision authorizes a change. A proposed fix stays proposed until its applicable route is completed.

Connect the need to the work

Intent gives the work a purpose. Product identifies a continuing offering, Project coordinates a bounded effort, and Work records its attempts. Individual tasks can then move ahead while their purpose remains recoverable.

  1. Intent The need

    Customers need their complete eligible data. Intent Resolution selects a response and a way to assess whether it satisfies that need.

  2. Product The promise of value

    The platform promises eligible export through a Product Capability. Particular implementations realize the outcome.

  3. Project and Goal The coordinated effort

    The repair Project coordinates the effort. Its Goal states the outcome and evidence needed for achievement.

  4. Work The bounded job

    Investigate the reported omission, using named inputs, constraints and expected results.

  5. Agent Assignment The participant’s job

    Assignment binds the agent to investigation. Permission comes separately from the applicable authority or delegation.

An incident, examined.

In the fictional Northstar case, a customer reports missing export records. The agent’s bounded investigation covers saved sources, output and logs, identifier comparison, candidate assessment material and a proposal.

The completeness definition fixes account, filters and snapshot. That lets the comparison establish a scoped omission. Cause, wider impact and recovery still need investigation.

The assignment identifies investigation. Permission to change source data, deploy a fix, accept a definition or close the incident has to come from the relevant authority route.

A reviewer can follow the criterion, inputs, comparison and proposal. Each part has a basis to examine. The full walkthrough follows that account.

For this export, completeness requires every selected identifier from the declared account, filters and fixed snapshot in the saved output. That examines identifier coverage. Value accuracy and data-access permission need their own requirements.

What the next agent inherits.

Project State supplies continuity through saved meanings, records, decisions, evidence, connections and histories. Working memory belongs to one session. When that machine session ends, its instance ends too. A later agent recovers durable knowledge through fresh checked views.

An Agent Session is temporary. Context View supplies task information, while Ontology View selects relevant concepts and connections. Both identify source versions, limits and refresh conditions.

If a view expires, refresh it or pause. Continuing requires usable context and current permission. The saved identifier comparison lets another agent inspect its basis and continue investigating cause.

The identifier comparison can survive the session, so another agent can inspect its basis and continue investigating cause. A saved lesson can also be examined through later use and benefit evidence.

Learning is another question to examine. A saved lesson can be tried in later work. To claim improvement, we need evidence of actual use and an assessment of the resulting benefit.

Use Craft for the software design

Craft is the software-design Product within the Lab. Its tools express responsibilities, architecture constraints and supported construction from model to code.

Craft separates a Rule’s condition, a Calculation’s derived value, a Query’s retrieval and an Operation’s state change. In its capacity example, the classifier accepts only a request strictly below supplied capacity. Reserving capacity is a separate Operation.

The Craft model describes design and implementation. Lab records connect that work to purpose, ownership, evidence and decisions. Generated code and checks can be Work outputs, with acceptance and deployment governed through their own authority routes.

A practical setting
for the alignment question.

AI alignment examines whether behavior follows human intentions and values. Expressing those fully is difficult, and people disagree. Training, evaluation and control are parts of that research. Ji et al., AI Alignment: A Comprehensive Survey

The Lab gives us a setting for some of those questions. Do clearer meanings reduce misunderstandings? Would bounded permissions and visible evidence help expose a bad action sooner? Can Project State preserve corrections as agents change?

We can formulate and test those questions. Governance records make intentions and decisions inspectable. Actual behavior needs evaluation under changing conditions, adversarial exercises and oversight. These are complementary lines of research. Anthropic’s alignment research

More general intelligence is part of the ambition. As capabilities grow, we need better ways to express direction, examine behavior and change course.

Begin with one piece of work.

Choose a project where people keep rebuilding context. Ask what its important words mean, what supports current claims and who is allowed to make the next change. That gives us a concrete place to begin.

The incident, system map and reference library provide routes into the model. For software behavior, the Craft article follows explicit design within the wider Lab.
