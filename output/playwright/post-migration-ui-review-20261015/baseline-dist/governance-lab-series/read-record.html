Read a record and its standing

Identify the exact record, contract and selected status before relying on it.

Result and prerequisites

Start with the record, selected contract and applicable registry or decision evidence. Your reading note should identify exact content, format, integrity, standing, scope and claim limits. A filename or diagram leaves those facts to be checked.

These steps inspect saved material manually. The documentation supplies examples and contracts, rather than a live registry query.

1. Select the record and its contract

Open the complete Work example and record node_id and schema_version. Use the Work reference to identify the exact format and required fields. Keep source identity and digest with the note so another reader can recover the same content.

node_id does not necessarily identify a version. Follow the selected storage and versioning contract before concluding which content or standing applies.

2. Establish standing independently

This specimen declares lifecycle_state: candidate and authority_effect: none, with no acceptance evidence. Record it as “candidate documentation specimen.” Real material needs its standing selection and exact owner decision. An accepted type leaves each instance to its own admission route.

The library shows standing selected in captured material. A file can keep its authored candidate wording while a later decision establishes effective standing. Save both facts and identify the applicable selection.

3. Read scope, evidence and connections

Read owner, scope, inputs, outputs and evidence, then state the strongest supported conclusion. Open the sources before relying on them. For connections, check direction, ordered kinds and qualifiers. A local relation_refs field still needs the complete assertion behind it.

The Northstar Work belongs to the repair Project. That membership supplies no deployment permission, established repair or incident closure. The connection examples identify the required membership qualifier.

Standing
Candidate documentation material; no acceptance decision supplied.
Scope
S1 / Northstar / status=active / E12 identifier coverage.
Permitted conclusion
The record describes investigation Work and its expected outputs. Its existence does not establish success or permission.
Next route
Use the accountable owner’s review route for proposed acceptance or changed effects.

A common mistake is treating the latest-looking filename as current. Also check whether a schema pass was mistaken for acceptance, evidence links remain unresolved, or a derived view was treated as another authority source.
