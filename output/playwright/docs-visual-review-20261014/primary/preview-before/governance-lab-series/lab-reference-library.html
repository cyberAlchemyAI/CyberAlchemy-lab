Terms you will meet in the records
Scope
Where a meaning, rule or permission applies, and its limits.
Contract and schema
A contract states requirements. A schema expresses structural rules in a machine-readable form. Meeting a schema does not by itself prove truth or grant permission.
Reference and binding
A reference identifies another record; a binding explicitly associates particular records or versions. The binding’s own contract determines what that association means.
Canonical
In representation rules, the designated machine form with its declared approved origin. This is separate from semantic authority. A generated result can have a canonical type without becoming a second writable source of meaning; accepted definitions govern meaning through their own scope and decisions.
Projection or view
Information derived from selected source records for a particular question. An ontology view presents concepts and connections; a context view prepares information for work. Their source selections and omissions matter.
Qualifier
An extra detail required on a connection, such as version, scope, role, period or evidence.
Invariant
A condition the model requires to hold across the cases it covers. Stating an invariant does not prove that every implementation satisfies it.
Residue and claim ceiling
Residue is required matter that remains unresolved or unpreserved. A claim ceiling is the strongest conclusion the available record or evidence justifies.
Replay and cursor
Replay reconstructs a recorded state from its event history. A cursor identifies a position in that history.
Fingerprint or digest
A content identifier calculated from a record’s bytes, often using SHA-256. It supports checking which contents were captured; it does not establish their truth.
How to read the machine field names

Keys such as source_ref stay exact so you can compare the explanation with the schema. _ref points to another record, and _version_ref selects an exact revision. The schema identifies permitted reference kinds.

In JSON schema, required lists mandatory fields, properties describes them, enum restricts alternatives and $ref points to a schema section. A discriminator identifies type through a field and value. These are reading clues. The complete contract and schema govern the requirements.

Part I · Governance / Essay 01 · 4 minute read

Authority begins with meaning

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

An AI sales agent prepares a renewal quote. The number looks plausible. Before anyone sends it, though, we need to understand what counts as a renewal, which pricing rule applies and who has permission to commit the company to the price. An approval button records a choice. The definitions and rules explain what that choice means.

CyberAlchemy Lab organizes project knowledge and work through identifiable records of definitions, plans, software, evidence and decisions. People and agents use those records to recover what applies to a task. A contract states requirements. Admission formally accepts an exact record or revision within scope. It supplies neither proof of truth nor evidence of deployment.

A Definition establishes meaning for a stated purpose. For the quote, “renewal” could mean extending an existing agreement rather than selling a new service. That distinction changes what the agent searches and checks. Its authority comes from acceptance of an exact version within the owner’s scope. A copied definition has a source trail; deciding whether that version governs in the Lab is a separate step.

Other records carry other responsibilities. An Artifact Type governs the required machine format and human views of a record. A Relation Kind governs a connection’s meaning, direction and permitted endpoint kinds. Policies state obligations or restrictions, and a Constitution combines rules for its subjects and scope. We need identifiable owners, versions and limits for each.

The agent might be able to calculate a discount while lacking permission to offer it. Assignment gives it a job; Delegation grants named actions within subject and time limits. A Principal is the identity recognized at an authorization or accountability boundary. A Role describes a position and expectations. None of the job, role or technical ability substitutes for permission.

NIST’s attribute based access control gives a useful comparison. It evaluates the requester, subject, operation and relevant conditions against rules. The Lab also needs the definitions and record requirements making that question meaningful. The comparison helps us reason about permission, without establishing that the Lab implements the NIST model. NIST SP 800-162

The same quote can be read through authority, provenance and lifecycle. We ask what governs and who is permitted to act, follow how the information was produced, then examine states and permitted changes. Other properties still need their own records.

The Governance-Axis Independence Invariant keeps kind, status, evidence confidence, commitment confidence, provenance and owner distinct where a decision needs them. An invariant is a condition the model must preserve. The Lab also tracks residue, meaningful unresolved or unpreserved work. An accepted pricing rule could still have limited evidence and unexamined customer segments, even when its owner is firmly committed.

A definition can affect later work through explicit links to a schema, validator, task selection or decision. Following that influence means identifying the applicable contract, implementation and observed effect. The definition itself executes no code and grants no blanket permission over related actions.

The inspected snapshot contains a tension worth keeping visible. The proposed governance module emphasizes decision rights, while accepted Authority and related definitions cover meaning and rules more broadly. Reading them together does not accept a revised module. That needs review and a decision. The sales workflow here is an illustration.

Now the sales agent has something to work from: an accepted meaning of renewal, quote requirements, pricing policy, permissions and required evidence. If something is missing, it can identify the gap and responsible owner. Another participant can later examine the same basis for the work.

Following the evidence

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

A customer reports missing export rows. The agent finds a completion log and a screenshot with fewer rows than expected. Those records support different claims: what the job reported, and what someone saw under particular conditions. The Lab keeps the records, origins and supporting conditions so the assessment can distinguish them.

Provenance is the account of those origins and production steps. For the export, we need the request, inputs, software version, execution conditions, output, observation time and responsible recorder or transformer. A later reader should be able to distinguish an original observation from a summary, adaptation or generated view.

W3C PROV describes entities, activities and agents, giving us vocabulary for information, producing processes and responsible participants. The Lab separately records whether a source or revision has been accepted for a governing purpose. Knowing its origin helps assessment. Acceptance still needs its own record. W3C PROV-DM

Evidence work has stages. A Source identifies an origin and its content identity. Observation records research or experiment data with conditions and uncertainty; Operational Observation covers ordinary project work. A Measurement Method declares measurement, units, tools, sampling and uncertainty. A Measurement Result records an execution. An Evidence Set gathers support and contrary material for a question. A Finding states a conclusion within that support.

“The output contains 9,800 rows” needs an identifiable output and counting method. To say 200 required rows are missing, we also need an inclusion rule and identifier comparison. A claim that new pagination code caused the omission needs further causal evidence. Pagination retrieves data in successive batches. Saving the intermediate records lets someone inspect each step toward the conclusion.

Evidence supports a claim under stated conditions. One successful input leaves larger or changing datasets to be examined. Repeating the demonstration helps assess repeatability while preserving the original scope. The evidence needs the subject and version, claim boundary and conditions for continued use.

A digest identifies exact file bytes. A digital signature can bind an approval to a proposal and signing key under policy. Both help detect substitution. They leave truth, test sufficiency and the signer’s applicable permission to their own evidence and authority records.

This matters when importing CyberAlchemy v2 work. A faithful copy can still await Lab acceptance. An imported graph might omit current project objects. A source ledger records the transfer under declared rules, including renamed identities, missing links and older versions left unresolved. The Lab calls those meaningful leftovers residue.

Confidence needs a subject too. Evidence confidence concerns support for a claim; commitment confidence concerns an owner’s intention to proceed. A team can commit to an export redesign while reliability evidence is preliminary. Recording acceptance should preserve the uncertainty future experiments need to address.

A provenance view selects relevant sources and connections. It lets an agent follow the export through software, inputs, measurements, selection decisions and conclusions. The view needs coverage, exclusions and freshness, with unresolved references visible. Any conclusion still depends on the underlying records.

The assessment should say which comparison ran, what output was observed, which software revision applied and what is still uncertain about cause. It can name the owner for the next decision. Provenance makes the reasoning inspectable; evidence determines its support.

Changing a governed system

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

The investigation suggests revised code, a different record format and new success criteria. Reviewing them together is convenient, provided the owner can distinguish their effects. Code changes behavior. A format changes stored representation. Criteria might change completion’s meaning. The Lab records the intended effects before acceptance.

Lifecycle gives each subject its states and permitted changes over time. A record format can be accepted while a particular record is still a draft. Selected software can await activation. A graph can represent a proposal as proposed. A session ends while its project continues. We need each subject’s state to understand those facts.

The Lab separates three changes. A Semantic Epoch is an owner-selected accepted meaning version; a material owner-determined change to export inclusion rules needs a successor. A Representation Revision changes machine encoding while preserving meaning, with equivalence evidence, validation and owner selection. A Derived Refresh regenerates and validates a view from unchanged canonical input under an exact generator binding. It adds no meaning, representation revision, decision or authority effect. A generator change follows its tooling route.

A proposal identifies exact subjects, versions and relevant file bytes, requested effects, authority limits and invalidating conditions. A Decision chooses an outcome within those limits. A Decision Bundle puts separate decisions at one review checkpoint. Their owners, targets and effects stay separate, so accepting one supplies no automatic acceptance for the rest.

An Admission Transaction applies an already authorized bundle. It checks authorization, targets and starting state, then applies the declared consequences together. Accepted records, receipts, histories, Project State and generated indexes can be part of that result. Project State is saved knowledge outside any agent. The transaction has only the permission supplied by its decisions.

The inspected records show a governance approval covering record contracts, the Admission Transaction definition and connection rules, with consequences applied together. A later approval accepted the local runtime and declared operational records. Each approval identifies its scope. Deployment and publication still have their own authorization requirements.

Two histories let us recover the result. Project Semantic Event History records complete resulting states and predecessors. Project Governance Event History records decisions, authorization, policies, challenges and evidence for those changes. Both identify the same change events, letting a reader connect what changed with why it was accepted.

Event sourcing retains an ordered change history and reconstructs state from it. Replay performs that reconstruction from recorded inputs. The Lab also links a separate authority history. Reconstructing a state leaves the acceptance of its inputs to the applicable governance records. Martin Fowler, Event Sourcing

The local implementation supplies bounded evidence. A signed proposal identifies changes and expected starting state. An exclusive writer lock prevents competing local writes. Prepared transaction records precede the accepted-state reference update, and recovery recognizes completed history and unfinished work. The recorded tests concern local process interruption on the selected filesystem, without establishing power-loss durability or coordination across machines.

A Challenge Contract sets eligible participants, reasons, evidence, responsibility, timing and resolution requirements. A Challenge Event records one eligible challenge. Missing-row evidence could challenge a completeness finding. Filing it creates a matter requiring resolution. Suspension, withdrawal or revised meaning still needs the applicable rule and authorized decision.

An accepted change can leave an earlier Context View stale. The view identifies task information, source versions and use conditions. The runtime can require a fresh checked selection tied to accepted state. The next agent recovers the change and authority from project records, with no need to inherit a private conversation.

What the Lab organizes

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

A software company wants customers to export data reliably. Intent records the outcome and constraints before a solution is chosen. Intent Resolution records the owner’s response and success assessment. That response might be product work, a project or a research question. Choosing it gives direction, with delivery still to establish.

The Product is the continuing offering, such as the customer platform. Its Product Thesis explains the audience, problem, value and propositions needing evidence. A Product Capability states an outcome such as reliable export. A service, command or agent workflow could realize it, each with its own identity and lifecycle.

A Capability Realization Binding links specific capability and implementation versions, roles, compatibility, ownership, adoption posture and replacement conditions. Several implementations can participate, and delivery can change while the capability keeps its identity. The binding leaves adoption, installation, activation, conformance and customer benefit to the appropriate decision or evidence.

A Project organizes an effort with purpose, scope, exclusions, goals, responsibility, evidence and lifecycle. Its export-repair Goal might require no omitted identifiers under a selected comparison. Work names a bounded activity and its inputs, participants, result and constraints. That activity can contribute to the Goal, whose conditions and owner-controlled state change still govern achievement.

The platform continues beyond the repair Project. The Project can examine several implementations before any product adoption. Membership, adoption and demonstrated value therefore need separate records. Later work can recover what was investigated, selected and observed.

A Method describes repeatable transformation of inputs into results, with actions, roles, checkpoints and unresolved work. A Craft Method specializes construction into smaller governed tasks and their recombination. Define makes behavior and limits explicit. Design selects an arrangement; Plan describes how work proceeds. The resulting records use the selected contracts.

Implementation identifies a technical realization with sources, interfaces, build or deployment materials and evidence. Workflow orders software Operations and their connections, decisions and recovery. Construction continues through conformance, operation, recovery and later change. Smaller tasks need owners, recombination and stop conditions. Residue keeps meaningful unfinished or unpreserved work visible.

A Coordination Context saves why information is needed and what the work concerns. It has exactly one Product anchor and optionally one Project and one primary Goal. A Project might have several goals while this context selects one. A material change to those anchors or purpose needs a successor context identity. Consumers and source-selection rules belong in the record too.

Sales and billing might both use “customer” while referring to different facts. A Bounded Context establishes consistent local meaning, and a Context Map records directional relationships between particular model versions. Domain-driven design gives a useful account of those coherent meanings and explicit model connections. Martin Fowler, Bounded Context

A Context Profile supplies reusable construction and resolution rules. A Context Request identifies task or consumer needs, selection and freshness. A Context View supplies the fixed result with exact sources, exclusions and expiry. These records keep separate identities, so a refresh leaves the purpose to its own governing change route.

Project State retains records, definitions, connections, decisions, evidence and histories. A Ledger is an ordered collection with entry, integrity, correction and reconciliation rules. Some histories add append-only requirements and event reconstruction. The general Ledger meaning makes no blanket claim that every collection is immutable or every entry true, accepted or authoritative.

The export agent now has a route through the need, capability, remaining project conditions, method, software and evidence. The reference library supplies exact meanings and record requirements. We can follow that route while learning the identifiers along the way.

How the records fit together

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

“The experiment evaluates the export capability” works in conversation. For a record, we need to identify the design or run and capability version. We also need to distinguish evaluation from evidence support or software adoption. Each connection should answer its own question.

A Definition governs meaning within its owner’s scope through scientific and formal wording. Plain-language and domain voices explain it. An Artifact Type governs the record kind’s machine format and required views. An artifact instance identifies a particular versioned record. Type acceptance and instance acceptance are separate admissions.

A Schema states structural requirements, including kinds, fields, constraints and connections. Passing its checks leaves evidence strength, source freshness and action permission to other records. W3C SHACL illustrates explicit graph-structure checking. This comparison establishes neither Lab use of SHACL nor acceptance through a structural pass.

The Four-Facet Artifact Invariant applies to governed actions on load-bearing artifacts, state or meaning. Before downstream governance, the action must bind, create or record typed residue for classification, schema, a human continuation view and a machine-readable view. Missing facets need reason, owner, evidence and a continue, defer or block outcome. The Canonical Representation Invariant separately designates one machine representation. That designation concerns form and origin, without itself proving truth or authority.

Registries select exact definitions and contracts. Artifact Type Resolution uses an exact reference or declared discriminator, reporting ambiguity instead of guessing from filenames. An older reference can resolve while a newer version is current. An Artifact Type Request records a reusable-contract gap for review, without supplying the missing accepted contract.

A Relation Kind declares meaning, direction, permitted ordered endpoint pairs, required details and inference limits. A Typed Relation instantiates the rule between specific versions. This snapshot has 41 kinds and 550 permitted ordered pairs. Source and target are not interchangeable, and separate lists do not permit every possible combination.

Membership, intended contribution, evaluation and evidence support need different connections. member-of, contributes-to, evaluates and evidence-bears-on record those facts, including support or opposition where required. Experiment Design and Experiment Run may evaluate Product, Product Capability, Product Thesis and Capability Realization Binding. Product connections are available alongside research connections, with no promised favorable outcome.

The Typed Connection Extension Rule governs expansion. Reuse a fitting meaning and constraints. An additional ordered pair needs review and an accepted successor contract; a different semantic question needs a distinct relation kind. Ownership, evidence, versions, lifecycle and challenge rights remain explicit. Registering an endpoint kind grants no extra connections.

Non-collapse asks whether concepts describe different facts. Assignment can precede delegation, capability can precede adoption, and evaluation can fail. Formalization expresses those distinctions logically. A witness or countermodel demonstrates a distinction under assumptions. Consistency asks whether the combined requirements hold together in the selected theory.

The inspected assurance work covers sources for 183 selected definition identities and partial translations. It reports zero verified whole-definition proofs. A proof checker’s accepted statement still needs a justified relationship to the intended definition. Lean’s documentation explains that boundary. A Lab claim requires adequate evidence connecting the formal statement to its source meaning. Lean, Validating a Lean Proof

An ontology organizes concepts, things and relationships. A Node represents a specific subject and revision. An Edge represents a Typed Relation or an explicitly declared local connection. Local appearance does not make a connection global. Graph and source identities stay separate, and drawing an edge supplies no authority to change its source.

A Model Instance Binding links a local instance to a shared parent model while preserving owners and authority. An Ontology Cut reconstructs a consistent state from selected event-history positions; a View selects from it for a purpose. A lifecycle graph needs sources, coverage, exclusions, unresolved references and freshness. Completeness is assessed within that scope.

An agent’s working session

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

A new agent starts the export investigation on Monday. Last week’s review is unavailable as private recollection. The agent needs selected software, current evidence, the project goal and tool permission. The Lab prepares an explicit session for one temporary machine instance and saves useful knowledge outside it.

Actor classifies identifiable participants. Person, Team and Principal identify a human, continuing group and boundary-recognized identity respectively. Principals support authentication, authorization, ownership or accountability. Role describes a position; Responsibility describes what someone answers for. Team Membership and Role Assignment record separate facts. A reference leaves person, account and role identities distinct.

Agent classifies an operational actor. Its Profile supplies reusable behavior, inputs, outputs, tools, evidence, escalation and stop conditions. An Agent Capability describes technically possible attempts and their input, output and failure requirements. An Instance identifies a concrete realization and accountability. Concrete participant kinds use their own records without requiring a duplicate universal Actor record.

Assignment identifies expected participation in work, intent, project, goal, run, role or responsibility, with limits and deliverables. Delegation separately grants scoped permission. Diagnosing an export might permit reading selected material and proposing a fix while excluding customer communication and production changes. The job and permission need separate records.

Saltzer and Schroeder describe least privilege and complete mediation: limit permissions to the work and check authorization whenever access occurs, including relevant changes to earlier grants. Those principles help explain why an old instruction has limits. They provide design guidance rather than evidence of enforcement on every Lab path. Basic Principles of Information Protection

A Session binds one instance and assignment to Context and Ontology Views, policies, permissions, inputs, budgets and termination conditions. The machine instance belongs to that session and ends with it. Profiles and historical session records can persist. The operating worker and its working memory are temporary.

Project State supplies saved knowledge with each record’s standing intact. Including a proposal in an accepted state does not accept the proposal. A Context Request identifies session needs; a Context View gives a fixed source selection with versions and use conditions. An Ontology View supplies a connected selection from a consistent cut. Both retain source authority limits.

Session Memory holds temporary notes, alternatives and reminders, then ends with the session. Useful material can enter the applicable reflection, evidence or lesson route. A private note has to follow the recording process before becoming Project State. Later agents recover their position through saved records and fresh views.

The inspected local runtime keeps bounded notes in process memory and clears them on closure. A session records accepted-state reference, source selection and expiry. Changed state reference, changed source digest or expiry invalidates continued context use. The agent needs current checked context through the applicable route.

A saved context packet is an output of preparation. The runtime’s context-only export closes its session after preparing that packet. A later consumer needs its own freshness and permission checks. Keeping the file preserves neither the earlier machine instance nor expired grants.

Agent Action records attributable proposals, attempts, completion, rejection or failure, with session and inputs. A Tool supplies a versioned invocation interface. Availability, ability, action and observed effect are different facts. A Work Outcome Assessment compares expected and observed results within its scope.

The agent can recover current information, inspect assignment and permission, choose allowed tools and record actions, evidence and unresolved work. Proposed Project State changes follow review and authorization. After closure, another agent recovers accepted results through new views without inheriting private memory or unrestricted grants.

How experience becomes useful

See this in the incident · Compare the relevant concepts

In a separate hypothetical episode, an export fix works for the original customer and later fails for a larger account. The agent suggests comparing adjacent batches before reporting success. Retrieving those batches is pagination. Advice for future work needs evidence, conditions, predicted benefit and a result contradicting the prediction. Saving it preserves the advice. Later use tells us whether it helps.

The Operational Learning Cycle starts with identifiable work, participants and conditions. Sessions and actions yield results, feedback, measurements and residue. Operational Observation records ordinary-work information; research and experiment runs use Observation. Agent Reflection records a revisable diagnostic interpretation for review. It is a report, without requiring retention of private internal reasoning.

An Operational Lesson makes the advice specific. Here it could apply while data changes between batch requests, excluding fixed-data exports with a different integrity contract. It predicts detection before delivery. A falsifier would show the check missing the known failure or rejecting valid output without useful detection. The evidence bounds the advice.

Lesson Application records actual use or deliberate non-use of an exact version. Presentation, selection, use, correct use and success are different facts. Lesson Outcome Assessment examines predicted benefit and contradicting conditions against observations. Work Outcome Assessment asks what the task achieved. A completed export leaves the lesson’s causal contribution to be assessed.

Research Inquiry records a scoped question with sources, method and stopping conditions. Research Run records the investigation. Hypothesis states a testable proposition. Experiment Design declares conditions, variables, comparisons, measurements and stop rules; Experiment Run records execution, deviations and observations. Planning, execution and favorable outcomes each need their own account.

Experiments can address research, product propositions or implementation-capability relationships under declared endpoint and direction rules. For exports, runs could compare dataset size and concurrent changes with an identified method. evaluates records the assessed subject. Evidence connections describe claim support, and a Finding states the bounded conclusion.

Failure Mode describes a possible requirement violation. Failure Occurrence records an actual or suspected episode, including uncertainty. Failure Assessment separately examines occurrence, pattern, cause, impact, containment and recovery. Service restoration can leave cause unresolved. We should preserve that uncertainty before proposing a general lesson.

An Incident organizes response when related failures or uncertain impacts need an owner, timeline and resolution. Failure Control Binding associates an exact pattern version with a measure having one primary role: prevention, detection, containment, recovery or compensation. The intended treatment leaves installation, execution and effectiveness to their own evidence. Closure preserves history, uncertainty and follow-up.

Classification supplies declared categories for finding relevant records. A Classification Scheme defines axes and permitted values; an Assignment binds values to a subject and revision. The Classification Orthogonality Invariant requires distinct decision or checking purposes, with dependencies explicit. An impact label supplies no conclusion about cause, recovery or permission.

Feedback Record preserves an attributable correction, rejection or other response. Communication Preference records presentation, language or detail under conditions; Preference Application Assessment checks applicability to an output. A preferred review format leaves permission unchanged. Improvement Signals identify possible opportunities, while owner-reviewed Improvement Targets route proposed work with evidence and responsibility.

Park and colleagues studied observations, retrieval, reflection and planning in a simulated community, reporting contributions to believable behavior. The study provides an architectural precedent within its scope. It proves neither export improvement from a Lab lesson nor implementation of the Lab’s temporary-session contract. Generative Agents

Observations, lesson versions, applications and assessments persist as project records. Later agents obtain them through current Context and Ontology Views with source versions identified. Those records can inform work. Changing policy, software, a profile or trained weights is a separate governed change, rather than an automatic learning-cycle permission.

Sources, interpretation and remaining work

The research package captures 484 source files from 8 October 2026, with content fingerprints. Primary literature

  • W3C PROV-DM

    Provenance vocabulary identifies what existed, which activity produced it and who participated. It helps follow a Lab record to its origins while leaving authority to separate records.

  • NIST SP 800-162, ABAC

    Attribute-based access control considers requester, subject, action and conditions. Actor permission is one question within the Lab’s broader authority model.

  • Saltzer and Schroeder, Basic Principles of Information Protection

    Least privilege limits grants to the task. Checking access on each use explains why an earlier permission needs reconsideration before later actions.

  • W3C SHACL

    SHACL declares constraints on graph fields and connections. It helps explain structural validation, without establishing that the Lab implements it.

  • Martin Fowler, Event Sourcing

    Event sourcing retains changes and reconstructs state from history. The Lab separately records the authority behind governed changes.

  • Martin Fowler, Bounded Context

    A Bounded Context establishes where a model’s vocabulary applies. The same word can have different scoped meanings, with connections between models needing explicit rules.

  • Park et al., Generative Agents

    The study examines agents using observations, retrieved memories, reflections and plans in simulation. It provides an external-memory precedent within that scope, without establishing business reliability or Lab conformance.

  • Lean reference, Validating a Lean Proof

    A checker can verify a formal theorem. Relating that theorem to the intended meaning is another obligation. The assurance discussion keeps both questions visible.

  • Mensh and Kording, Ten simple rules for structuring papers

    The editorial method asks for a central contribution, attention to what readers know and a logical progression. Those questions informed the essay structure.
