01 / 14 Writing · story systems

Whisper

What if a story could remember itself?

A writing studio where intent stays visible and the world behind a story can become explorable—not another box that turns a prompt into prose.

Current artifactA reviewable intent → plan → draft → validation lifecycle.
Open the project

Vision

An agentic writer studio—where AI can carry out bounded, reviewable steps—with a knowledge graph: a map of characters, places, encounters, timelines, and connections that stays available while people write and read.

What exists now

A reviewable composition lifecycle that preserves audience, structure, voice, evidence, movement, review, and what the team learned.

Not yet

Not a finished writer studio or a live story knowledge graph. Broader transports and quality outcomes remain unproved.

Next experiment

Keep one bounded story map synchronized with one narrative excerpt as characters move, meet, and remember.

Possible collaborators

Writers, editors, narrative designers, readers, and knowledge-graph thinkers.

Talk about Whisper →

Possible contribution, not an open role. Confirm welcome and scope with a human owner.

A constrained arena where agents compete under explicit rules and a human can inspect legality, decisions, and replay before believing a ranking.

Current artifactA runnable local Kuhn-poker arena, bot contract, replay, and receipt.
Open the project

Vision

An arena for testing agent decisions where rules, seats, actions, randomness, and results stay reproducible and inspectable.

When a live conversation helps

45 minutes. One useful next move.

A focused session can turn interest into one bounded experiment. The page remains the shared project surface; people decide together whether a conversation should continue.

0–5

Orient

One question: which project, if any, is worth understanding more deeply?

5–12

Scan

Notice what you could help, learn, challenge, test, or refer.

12–29

Open

Visit two or three projects whose current state or uncertainty earned attention.

29–39

Shape

Turn interest into a bounded offer with effort, scope, owner, and stop condition.

39–45

Read back

A human confirms what was understood, what remains open, and what happens next.
