## Cross-area ownership

Lab capability development is recorded as Lab Work, coordinated by its Project and described in its Product. Applicable definitions/policies remain with their semantic owners. Implementation can consume Craft and Ontology Lifecycle without absorbing those Products or their authority.

Instance-local application/configuration can evolve independently while retaining explicit compatibility with selected shared contracts. A common release needs a tuple of selected component, contract and environment revisions; one package version alone cannot stand in for all of them.
