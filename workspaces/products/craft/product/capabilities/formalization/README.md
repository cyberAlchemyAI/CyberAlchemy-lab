# Formalization

**Form and inspect typed formal claims**

Enable a meaning owner and reviewer to form supported typed FormalClaims and explicit definition/interpretation bindings while retaining scope, source identity and unsupported residue.

**Bind formal projections, cases and evidence**

Enable a reviewer to bind supported compiler/formal projections and case/evidence joins to their exact source and claim, preserving the boundary between specifications, checked projections and proved results.

Acceptance conditions and exclusions are part of the current capability definition. Candidate descriptions and imported definitions do not establish runtime qualification.
