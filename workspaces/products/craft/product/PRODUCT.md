# Craft Product

Craft helps people carry a feature from intended behavior to a built system and evidence about how it behaves. In the Customer Success example, the rule that suggests renewal remains recognizable in the selected application and its checks. A passing check records its limits; it does not decide whether the rule is right for a real business or whether the system is ready to release.

This is the Product's draft promise. Current capability descriptions have proposal-only standing, and the complete construction and feedback loop still needs qualification. Start with [Understand Craft](../docs/README.md) for a short explanation.
