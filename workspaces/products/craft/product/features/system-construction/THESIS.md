## Project Identity

Craft is both a Product and a Project under distinct typed identities. The Product owns the product proposition;
the Project coordinates this work; and `CAV2-D20` owns the Method. The first Goal makes one candidate Product
Feature precise enough for owner review before feature-definition admission, architecture selection,
construction, or migration begins.

## Product Context

The Craft Product Thesis supplies the immediate purpose and value context for this Product Feature. The broader
CyberAlchemy Product Thesis is the parent context for the Craft Product. Together they require owner-controlled
meaning from intent through implementation and observed reality, evidence-bounded claims, and explicit owner
decisions for consequential transitions.

The context constrains the feature; it does not prove that the feature is desirable, adopted, implemented, or
valuable.

## Product Feature

The candidate System Construction Product Feature gives an owner one connected path for defining a product and domain,
selecting exact architecture and implementation realizations, constructing a contained target system,
independently checking conformance, observing bounded runtime truth, and evolving the system without silently
breaking its meaning.

`Product Feature` is used here as a project-local candidate term. This package does not claim that the term or
this instance is already admitted into the canonical CyberAlchemy definitions or product ontology.

## Thesis

If a product owner can express purpose, domain meaning, system obligations, exact realization choices, and
evidence expectations in one owner-controlled continuity chain, then Craft can reduce repeated architecture and
integration work while producing systems that are faster to construct, easier to explain, independently
checkable, and safer to change.

## Problem

Product purpose, domain meaning, architecture, implementation, operation, and evidence are usually fragmented
across tools and handoffs. Teams repeatedly rediscover decisions, generators silently choose implementation
details, conformance checks prove only static shape, and later changes drift away from the meaning that justified
the system.

## Value Proposition

Craft should help an owner turn a product feature into a working, explainable system whose implementation can be
traced to its meaning and changed without silently violating it.

## Value Mechanism

1. Bind product context and owner boundaries to typed product, business, and system meaning.
2. Select exact architecture and language-profile realizations instead of relying on hidden generator defaults.
3. Construct deterministic, dependency-closed target artifacts.
4. Validate declared relations, operations, workflows, effects, recovery, and other meta-type obligations.
5. Observe bounded runtime evidence independently from construction.
6. Preserve residue and route an evidenced successor state to its explicit owner.

## First-Order Principles

1. Product context precedes feature construction.
2. Product, Project, Method, Product Feature, Goal, capability, implementation, and evidence remain distinct identities.
3. Owners retain authority over meaning and consequential adoption decisions.
4. Exact realization selection must be explicit and machine-checkable.
5. Construction must be deterministic and dependency-closed.
6. Conformance observation must remain independent from construction.
7. Runtime evidence must be bounded, attributable, and replay-safe where required.
8. Evidence never becomes authority by accumulation.
9. Safe recovery and evolution are product behavior, not operational afterthoughts.
10. Construction and validation machinery must remain replaceable.

## Intended Outcomes

- **Continuity** — each generated implementation obligation traces to owner-controlled meaning.
- **Construction speed** — reusable exact realizations reduce recurring design and integration work.
- **Conformance** — static and runtime drift is detected at the appropriate boundary.
- **Evolvability** — change, recovery, compensation, rollback, and migration preserve explicit contracts.
- **Replaceability** — runtimes and generators can change without losing source meaning.
- **Human control** — owners can understand, challenge, accept, or reject consequential transitions.

## DomainSpec v2 Rename And Lineage

DomainSpec v2 is the former name of the Craft Product and the label retained by its current source and
implementation paths. The proven meta-type, profile, construction, conformance, operation, workflow, recovery,
and integration assets belong to the same Product lineage. A later physical migration must classify exact legacy
assets as moved current capability, archived provenance, separately owned dependency, or rejected residue. The
naming decision implies no completed file move, import rewrite, consumer migration, or authority relocation.

## Name Boundary

The private Craft Product, Project, Method, and Product Feature do not replace the public Arcanum `craft`
capability, which owns repository-ledger behavior under `.craft/`. The public capability is a narrower ledger
realization; display-name equality does not merge identity, authority, implementation, lifecycle, or visibility.

## Evidence Strategy

The thesis should be tested through:

- exact preservation of selected working DomainSpec capabilities;
- construction of one bounded target system from an owner-selected feature slice;
- deterministic replay and artifact identity checks;
- independent static and runtime conformance;
- recovery, compensation, migration, and rollback evidence; and
- product-owner, builder, operator, and reviewer burden observations.

One successful target cannot prove the whole thesis. Failure must distinguish a defective realization from
counterevidence against the feature model.

## Revision Criteria

Revise the realization—and, if evidence is systemic, this thesis—when continuity costs more than it saves,
owners cannot distinguish their meaning from generated projections, exact realization selection blocks useful
adaptation without evidence benefit, construction cannot integrate with real projects, independent conformance
duplicates construction defects, runtime observations cannot remain bounded, safe evolution cannot be preserved,
or a materially simpler feature provides the same guarantees.
