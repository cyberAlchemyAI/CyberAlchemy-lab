## What we are building and why

Create six useful, bounded applications from business models and selected implementation profiles. Their repeated technical mechanisms belong in one proposed shared source repository, `craft-ai-profiles`. The six business repositories consume it and the existing Craft/AlchLang CLI pair; they do not carry compiler forks. These are proposed repository names and layouts, not newly created GitHub repositories.

AlchLang is where an application author describes business meaning: records, conditions, decisions, exact calculations, state changes, permitted actions and required external business contracts. Craft checks that model and selected realizations, generates executable application structure and exposes the business graph, system graph and business-system bridge. A profile selects how an existing semantic capability is realized. A native adapter implements its actual technical I/O once. An Interface alone never performs a network call.

The smallest useful reusable unit is **one authorized, persisted work item with a checked outcome**. It has a typed cause, a legal business transition, a bounded implementation step and recoverable result. It works without AI. A model adds a proposal; business Rules, Policies, Calculations and Operations still decide what can be committed. Several declared stages compose into a workflow. When adaptive investigation is required, an optional bounded ARE run occupies one such stage; it cannot replace authored business decisions or own a second business queue.

First configure the existing generated protected application for a local CRM subset. In parallel, configure a differently shaped invoice decision using the already supported generic decision path. These are focused domain exercises, not repeated foundation acceptance. Then add the new model proposal and reconciled CRM update. Do not wait for a universal platform, every provider or a hosted scheduler before seeing a real generated application.

## Shared executable contracts

The new AI/source-access/continuation contracts are proposed, not copy-paste syntax accepted by the current release. P01/C01/C02 define and lower only their unsupported deltas; existing business/permission/transaction syntax stays reused. The design avoids inventing an AlchLang Float or a second general-purpose programming language.

**Identity and permissions.** A trusted adapter verifies configured RS256 OIDC tokens using allowlisted issuer/JWKS origin, audience, validity and key policy. Server-controlled subject membership maps to tenant/grants and original Auth Foundation owners; caller headers, arbitrary JWT roles and LLM results cannot mint grants. User, service and source credentials stay separate. Business input may contain a record ID but cannot select a different authority tenant. Entry permissions remain beside the Entry. Business Queries receive only checked required context fields, while technical resource handles remain runtime-owned. A local signed-token fixture proves the crypto path, not qualification of a production issuer.

**Work and continuation.** A work item captures tenant, checked source/profile/workflow identity, immutable business cause and input digest, revision and stage. A claim adds owner/fence/lease/deadline and attempt bounds. Stage kinds are local Operation, model observation, document processing, index mutation, provider command and named event/approval wait; an optional agent-run stage delegates its internal loop to ARE under the separate contract. Authored Operation/StateMachine/Policy decisions produce one of: advance; complete; business rejection; wait; review/unknown; bounded retry-later; cancel. Persist that decision and its relevant revision. The driver follows it and cannot decide a business branch. Retries remain on the same declared stage and consume a bounded attempt; no dynamic graph or unbounded retry. Local-only workflows have no accidental provider/event prerequisite. Stage outputs are typed persisted entities queried by later stages via correlation keys, rather than hidden host dataflow.

**Fences and effects.** Check the current claim at dispatch and commit. A late result cannot commit under an expired claim; its observation can support reconciliation. Cancellation/timeout prevents future local progression but does not prove a provider did nothing or a model was free. Provider command identity includes tenant, Entry/action, canonical payload, target/account, relevant object/approval versions and profile semantics; transport attempts are separate. Preserve prepared, dispatched, confirmed, definitively-no-effect and unknown evidence. A changed action is not the same retry. Unknown mutation suspends for inspection or review; compensation requires the existing no-effect/authority conditions. Source/workflow version drift requires explicit compatible resume/migration rather than silent replay under new semantics.

**Model computation and budget.** Reserve conservative exposure atomically in a service-owned SQLite ledger before request dispatch. Budget identity is billing-account/tenant/application allocation; a shared cap across applications requires those applications to use the same authoritative ledger owner through its port. This does not control unrelated spending on the provider account. The reservation binds request/model/prompt/destination/input contract and attempt. Persist egress/dispatch intent before I/O. Transitions distinguish reserved, dispatched, observed usage, unknown exposure and reconciled/manual settlement. Concurrent jobs cannot consume the same allowance. Crash/timeout/cancel/missing usage retains conservative exposure; stale responses cannot refund another attempt. Price-policy revision and maximum possible charge must be available to enforce money caps; without a defensible bound permit token/attempt admission only and do not promise a hard monetary cap. Explicit retry needs another reservation; no implicit SDK retries. Output is candidate/refusal/incomplete-invalid/unavailable/usage-unknown, never an authoritative fact. Tools are disabled in the first model port. The shared join reserves budget first, then records source-owned release/enqueue and dispatch intent before I/O. The common R02 owner supplies a single-use release ticket bound to attempt/fence/destination/expiry; crash recovery tests every boundary, retaining conservative budget and disclosure state without holding a transaction over network I/O.

**Exact values and technical ranking.** Money/totals/business priority remain exact authored Calculations. Vectors stay in a native technical resource with checked dimensions, finite values and norm bounds. Native ranking uses full-precision cosine plus lexical ranking, fused by reciprocal rank fusion `1/(60+lexicalRank) + 1/(60+denseRank)`, missing ranks contribute zero. Filter authorized versions first; order each list and fusion ties by canonical document/version/chunk identity. Return ordered evidence references/ranks, not a generic confidence or integer truth. Validate zero/negative/huge/nonfinite/near-equal vectors and top-k ties against an independent reference. Any future business threshold needs a named calibrated score contract; optional business reranking is explicit and cannot reintroduce denied documents.

**Source authority and A2→A3 composition.** First composition is co-located, with one authoritative source/access/index owner supplied by A2 and consumed through generated read/revalidate/release ports by A3. Each app still has one application-runtime owner; their data handles are not interchangeable. A3 does not read A2 tables or validate against an independent stale mirror. A standalone A3 example seeds the same legitimate source-owner API with fixture data. Separately deployed distributed consistency is future work.

A source identity includes tenant/connector/object ID; version includes hash, content/ACL/policy/index-generation revisions and active/tombstoned state. A source-owned snapshot/result reference binds principal/scope, evidence versions, expiry and current authority clock. Protected lookup filters eligibility before content exposure. Source owner serializes a fresh check with release/enqueue of context into the trusted model egress path, then separately with release of the final answer/cache response. That checked release is the local authorization linearization point. A later revoke cannot retract already released/in-flight bytes. An upstream synchronizer needs an explicit freshness limit; unavailable/stale authority fails closed. The first connector's local ACL is authoritative; Google Drive/SharePoint synchronization is not claimed.

The trusted dispatcher captures an immutable dependency manifest of **every source context actually exposed to the model**, bound to model attempt, prompt/output identity and work item. Final answer, cache value, approval and any derived external write retain that manifest, including the union of dependencies across combined/model-stage outputs. The manifest is owned by the runtime; a caller cannot forge a reduced list. A model-selected citation list is not the dependency list; omitting a citation cannot erase a confidentiality dependency. Revoke or replace any dependency and the conservative first implementation rejects/recomputes release.

Derived output release checks the actual recipient/audience and sink/purpose as well as the invoking principal. An operator who can read internal knowledge cannot thereby disclose it in a customer reply or campaign. First support uses explicitly customer-shareable knowledge and customer-scoped ticket facts; the source owner still authorizes the current actual recipient before send. Approval binds the same full-context manifest and output bytes. Final outbound provider dispatch consumes a current source-owned release for that actual recipient/sink; Entry write permission alone is insufficient. A changed recipient, source version or audience invalidates release/approval.

Cache partition includes tenant, actual recipient/audience/sink, principal authorization scope, complete context dependencies, policy/access/content/index versions, query and model/prompt identity. Cache hits still revalidate. A tombstone blocks new releases before physical cleanup. Chunks/vectors/blobs/cache/failed payload retention and source classification are covered by deletion policy; cleanup failure stays visible and cannot resurrect access. Auditable accounting metadata is redacted and separate from sensitive payloads. Check approved destinations before any embedding or model egress.

**Approval.** Bind approval to exact action/candidate digest, tenant, target account/resource, current object/source/policy versions, audience/recipient snapshot, expiry and amount/currency where relevant. Recheck authority and subjects immediately before dispatch. Changed audience, contact preference, ticket revision or invoice total invalidates approval. Routine automatic actions require an authored Policy; a model cannot approve itself.

**Ingress.** First supported inbound mode is authenticated manual submission through generated Entries. No webhook listener or implicit trusted provider payload. Provider-specific verified webhook adapters are a distinct optional W01 task: exact raw bytes/signature or documented mechanism, configured account/resource binding, replay window/inbox identity, size/schema limits and durable acknowledgement. Injected Request tests exercise the real verification path without hosting. Do not advertise webhook support until that adapter passes.

### One concrete path through the model

For CRM, the business author supplies a lead model, evidence relations, eligibility condition, exact score/routing calculation, allowed transitions and update policy. Profile selection chooses Node/SQLite/work items, typed model observations and HubSpot contacts. Craft resolves their checked contracts and generates the protected receive/review/sync handlers, persisted entities/journal and dispatch/inspection wiring. The model may propose an industry from supplied evidence. An unsupported proposal is rejected by authored checks. The worker waits for required approval, then sends the approved exact update using the selected connector. A lost acknowledgement produces unknown/reconciliation, never a guessed success or automatic duplicate update. The graphs retain both business reasoning and concrete technical ownership.

The DAG keeps the reused local CRM and invoice slices independent of new infrastructure. Six business-model tracks can start after registry inspection P00; four isolated connector packages proceed after their new effect contracts stabilize. No per-capability governance, sealed candidates or repeated proof gates; use focused functional checks while building and one consolidated result after the full chosen reference target works.
