A bound source contains an exact request and requirement revision, nonempty complete named criterion list, versioned actions/outputs, and exact criterion/action/output evaluations at its context, scope and epoch. Before runtime, prove quantified fulfillment soundness/completeness over every named criterion and exhibit linked-but-unfulfilled source counterexample. Compute yes/no/unknown per criterion from exact source action/output membership and accepted status; absent evidence yields unknown, explicit denial yields no, and contradictory duplicate assessment identities refuse.

Then create closed schemas and fixed adapter under e03. Generate complete owned positive fulfillment and linkage-without-action witnesses, plus independently evaluated multi-criterion cases. Test stale request/requirement, unrelated output, missing action/criterion, conflicting assessments and context drift.
