# Bounded redelegation implementation review

The separate `bounded-redelegation-v1` profile composes versioned hops between explicitly role-bearing principals. Every intermediate principal must have both roles. Each supplied gate/revocation assessment has the exact hop and query frame. Missing gate or revocation evidence stays unknown; a negative gate, revocation, expiration or denied redelegation prevents an effective chain. Exact source snapshot identity and all input evidence are checked before traversal.

Permission sets and half-open intervals narrow from parent to child. The universal non-amplification theorem says every ancestor of a reported effective chain permits the queried scope/action at its epoch. Universal word enumeration establishes that every sequence of source hops of lengths one through the declared bound is considered. The concrete witness checks the complete positive and unknown path sets and verdict against that universe, so Python cannot authorize a partial successful traversal.

Nodes are capped at 32, hops at 64, depth at 4 and enumerated words at 4096. The depth is part of the statement. No within depth means no complete simple supported path within that depth; it does not mean no unbounded path. Cycles remain in the source but are excluded from candidate simple paths; empty/reflexive paths establish no authority. A work cutoff is a resource refusal before search, rather than a negative theorem or partial positive. A missing gate can still leave a candidate unknown.

Three actual owned final-source kernel witnesses cover positive two-hop composition, denied child widening and unknown ancestor gates. Separate closed-schema/normalizer controls exercise expired/revoked ancestors, missing gate, denied redelegation, broken join, cycles, foreign context/epoch, duplicate assessments, stale snapshot identity, role mismatch, explicit depth and work cutoff. Those preflight controls are in-process tests, distinct from the three complete compiled/replayed witnesses.

The actual input snapshot, generic/concrete Lean source and pinned imports are bound to every completion. Event ownership remains process-local and checked after supervisor cleanup. Saved/new-client results do not become fresh events. The original direct-delegation profile and accepted sources remain unchanged and independently selectable. This candidate proves consequences of supplied source/gate/revocation truth; it does not authenticate those facts, infer independent admission, or grant unrestricted/live authority. The JSON emitter and same Lean kernel retain their stated trust boundaries.
