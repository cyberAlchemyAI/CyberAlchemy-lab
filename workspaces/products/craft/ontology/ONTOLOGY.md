# Craft Ontology

Craft owns its semantic inputs and configuration. The shared ontology-lifecycle application records representations and derives views. Current authored graphs, identity relations, schemas and source bindings are functional inputs. Evidence-only records, validation receipts, snapshots and saved runs are not imported.
