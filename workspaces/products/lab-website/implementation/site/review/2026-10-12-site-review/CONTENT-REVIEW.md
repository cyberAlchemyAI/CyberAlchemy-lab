## Current source comparison

## Research attribution

The 27 external URLs linked by the delivered pages yielded content through the research tool. The review checked the newer empirical references, architectural precedents and attribution boundaries. Link retrieval is not a reproduction of their experiments.

The Craft discussion's iterative-code claims match the stated scope of [SlopCodeBench v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24755v2) and [CodeTaste](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/thillen26a.html). They provide reasons to investigate successive changes; neither establishes Craft's effectiveness.

The robot series treats [DreamerV3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104), [Eureka](https://eureka-research.github.io/) and [DrEureka](https://eureka-research.github.io/dr-eureka/) as precedents for particular learning mechanisms. Application to the hypothetical arm still requires its own evidence.

The provenance and structural-validation comparisons retain the boundaries of [PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) and [SHACL](https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/). The Lab is not presented as automatically implementing or conforming to those standards.

## Editorial judgment

No new technical contradiction was identified in the reviewed explanations. The largest remaining editorial problems concern finding the right explanation, identifying what is available now, and keeping speculative lesson branches easy to distinguish. Comprehension and transfer need actual reader observations.
