How experience becomes useful

An export fix works for the original customer, then fails for a larger account. An AI agent proposes advice: compare adjacent data batches before reporting success. Retrieving data in successive batches is called pagination. In CyberAlchemy Lab, advice for later work needs supporting evidence, use conditions, a predicted benefit and a result that could contradict it. Saving the sentence preserves it. Examining later use establishes whether it helps.

The Operational Learning Cycle starts with work whose participants and conditions can be identified. Sessions and actions produce results, feedback, measurements and residue—meaningful unresolved or unpreserved work. An Operational Observation records ordinary-work information; Observation is the distinct record kind used during a Research or Experiment Run. An Agent Reflection records a revisable diagnostic interpretation. It can identify a suspected mistake or useful practice for investigation. It is a report suitable for review, not a requirement to save private internal reasoning.

An Operational Lesson makes that advice precise. It could apply to exports retrieved in batches while the dataset changes, excluding fixed-data exports with a different integrity contract. It predicts that the check will detect missing rows before delivery. Its falsifier is a result contradicting that prediction: the check misses the known failure or rejects valid output without useful detection. The advice remains limited to the conditions its evidence supports.

A Lesson Application records use or deliberate non-use of a specific lesson version in later work. Showing it to an agent, selecting it, using it, following it correctly and obtaining a successful outcome are distinct facts. A Lesson Outcome Assessment examines the predicted benefit and contradicting conditions against observed results. A Work Outcome Assessment instead asks what the task achieved. Completing an export does not alone show that a lesson caused improvement.

A Research Inquiry records a question and its scope, source rules, method and stopping conditions. A Research Run records what an investigation actually did. A Hypothesis states a testable proposition. An Experiment Design states the planned conditions, variables, comparisons, measurements and stop rules. An Experiment Run records execution, deviations and observations. A plan, its execution and favorable results have separate records.

An experiment may address research, evaluate a product proposition or assess an implementation’s relationship to a capability. The connection catalog states which record kinds may connect, in which direction. For the export, runs could compare dataset sizes and concurrent changes using an identified counting method. An evaluates connection records what was assessed. Evidence connections record how results bear on a claim. A Finding states the conclusion and its limits.

Failure records also separate different questions. A Failure Mode describes a possible way a kind of thing could violate a requirement. A Failure Occurrence records an actual or suspected episode and can remain uncertain. A Failure Assessment examines occurrence, pattern, cause, impact, containment and recovery independently. Restoring the service does not resolve its cause. Keeping that uncertainty visible prevents a premature general lesson.

An Incident coordinates response when related failures or uncertain impacts require an owner, timeline and resolution. A Failure Control Binding associates a specific failure-pattern version with a measure having one primary role: prevent, detect, contain, recover or compensate. It records intended treatment. Installation, execution and effectiveness need their own evidence. Incident closure retains history, uncertainties and follow-up obligations.

Classification organizes these records using declared categories. A Classification Scheme defines separate questions, called axes, and permitted answers. A Classification Assignment records values for a particular thing and revision. The Classification Orthogonality Invariant requires each axis to serve a distinct decision or checking purpose, with dependencies stated explicitly. An impact label cannot also establish cause, recovery or permission. This lets agents retrieve relevant records without treating labels as conclusions.

Feedback Record preserves an identifiable response such as a correction or rejection. Communication Preference records preferences about presentation, language or detail under stated conditions; Preference Application Assessment examines whether a particular preference applies to an output. A convenient review format does not broaden permission. Improvement Signals identify possible weaknesses or opportunities; owner-reviewed Improvement Targets route proposed work while retaining evidence and responsibility.

Park and colleagues studied stored observations, retrieved context, reflection and planning in a simulated community, reporting contributions to believable behavior. This supplies an architectural precedent within that study’s scope. It does not establish that a Lab lesson improves exports or that the study implements the Lab’s temporary-session contract. [Generative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442).

The project retains observations, lesson versions, applications and assessments as saved records. Later agents obtain them through current Context and Ontology Views: task information and a selected connected model with source versions identified. These can inform future work. Changing a policy, software, agent profile or trained model weights is a separate governed change. The learning cycle does not grant automatic permission to modify itself.

The export failure can therefore yield an inspectable account, advice for stated conditions, evidence of later use and a reviewed improvement. For the next agent, that account becomes usable input rather than private recollection. The project learns through records that can be challenged, revised and compared with later outcomes.
