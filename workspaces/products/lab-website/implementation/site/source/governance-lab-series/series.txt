They distinguish accepted definitions and contracts from implemented software, recorded past executions and proposed work. The explanation uses sources captured on 8 October 2026 and the exact accepted project state selected in that research. This explanation does not approve changes or authorize actions.

The accompanying reference catalog contains every definition, record-format binding, connection-rule kind and endpoint kind selected as current in the inspected registries. A record-format binding identifies the contract that applies to a record kind or a section of a shared schema. Connection tables list the allowed directional source–target kind pairs. The catalog is complete for those registry selections; this does not establish ontology coverage of the whole repository.

The library opens with a reading guide. Each definition has a short editorial introduction alongside its exact governing statement, original explanation and boundaries. Definitions that share a name in different workflow scopes have distinct labels. Status, version, schema fields and content fingerprints are explained. Earlier accepted versions remain in separate historical lists.

The primary literature helps explain record origins, permissions, reconstructing state from events, scoped meanings, graph validation, agent memory and formal proofs. Each source note identifies its contribution. The comparisons do not establish compliance with an external standard or equivalence to another implementation.
