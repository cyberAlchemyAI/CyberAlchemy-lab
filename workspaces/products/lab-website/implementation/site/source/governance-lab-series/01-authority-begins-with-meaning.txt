Authority begins with meaning

An AI sales agent prepares a renewal quote. It finds a customer, reads a pricing document and produces a plausible number. Before the quote is sent, three questions remain: what counts as a renewal, which pricing rule applies and who may commit the company to that price? An approval button can record a choice. The definitions and rules give that choice its meaning.

CyberAlchemy Lab is a framework for organizing project knowledge and work. It keeps identifiable records of definitions, plans, software, evidence and decisions so people and software agents can inspect what applies to a task. Its governance model asks which accepted meanings, record formats, connection rules and permissions apply, for which purpose and under whose responsibility. A contract is an explicit set of requirements. Admission is formal acceptance of a particular record or revision within a stated scope; it is not evidence that the record is true or that software has been deployed.

A Definition establishes what a term means for a stated purpose. A definition of “renewal” might distinguish extending an existing agreement from selling a new service. That distinction changes which records the agent searches and which cases it checks. The definition has authority only through its specific acceptance and the responsible owner’s scope. Copying a definition from an earlier project records where it came from. A separate decision establishes whether that version counts in the Lab.

An Artifact Type specifies the requirements for records of a particular kind, including their required machine format and human-readable views. A Relation Kind specifies a kind of connection, its direction and the kinds of things it may connect. A Policy states applicable obligations or restrictions. A Constitution combines rules for the things and scope it governs. Each governs a different question. Their owners, versions and limits must be identifiable.

Permission to act is another part of authority. The agent may be able to calculate a discount without being allowed to offer it. An Assignment identifies its job. Delegation records explicit permission for specified actions, things and time limits. A Principal is an identity recognized for authorization or accountability at a particular system boundary. A Role describes a position and its expectations. A job assignment, a role and a technical ability do not replace permission.

Access-control research offers a useful comparison. NIST’s attribute based access control evaluates information about the requester, the thing accessed, the operation and relevant conditions against rules. This makes a permission question precise. The Lab must also identify the definitions and record requirements that make that question meaningful. The comparison explains a design idea; it does not establish that the Lab implements the NIST model. [NIST SP 800-162](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/162/upd2/final).

Authority, provenance and lifecycle are three ways to inspect the same thing. Authority asks which meanings and rules apply and who may act. Provenance traces where information came from and how it was produced. Lifecycle describes the states something may occupy and the permitted changes between them. These questions provide a starting point, while other properties remain separately recorded.

The Governance-Axis Independence Invariant requires kind, status, evidence confidence, commitment confidence, provenance and owner to remain distinct when they matter to a decision. “Invariant” means a condition the model must preserve. The wider model also tracks residue: meaningful unresolved or unpreserved work. An accepted pricing rule can have limited evidence, strong owner commitment and unexamined customer segments. Acceptance does not erase those limits or automatically improve its confidence.

An accepted definition can influence future work through explicit links. Its meaning may constrain a schema—the fields and constraints for a record—a validator, the information selected for a task or a later decision. To inspect that influence, follow the definition through the applicable contract and implementation to the observed effect. The definition itself does not execute code, change a database or grant every apparently related permission.

The inspected source snapshot contains an important distinction. The proposed governance module emphasizes decision rights, while accepted Authority and related definitions cover meaning and other rules more broadly. This essay explains those records together. It does not accept a revised module; that requires its own review and decision. The examples illustrate the model rather than a deployed sales workflow.

The sales agent can now find the accepted meaning of renewal, the requirements for the quote, the selected pricing policy, its own permissions and the evidence needed before sending. If a required element is missing, it can identify the gap and the responsible owner. Governance supplies information the agent can use while doing the job, and records that another participant can examine afterward.
