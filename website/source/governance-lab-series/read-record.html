Read a record and its standing

Identify the exact record, contract and selected status before relying on it.

Result and prerequisites

You will prepare a short reading note that identifies a record’s exact identity, selected format, content integrity, standing, scope and claim limits. Have the record, selected contract and applicable registry or decision evidence available. A filename or diagram label cannot supply those facts.

This is a manual inspection of the materials. The documentation offers examples and reference contracts; it does not query a live registry.

1. Select the record and its contract

Open the complete Work example. Record its node_id and schema_version. In the Work reference, select the exact format version and its required fields. Keep the source identity and digest alongside the reading note.

A node_id is not necessarily a version field. Use the selected storage/versioning contract to identify the exact content; do not infer version or standing from its spelling.

2. Establish standing independently

The example says lifecycle_state: candidate and authority_effect: none. No owner acceptance is supplied. Write “candidate documentation specimen” in the note. For real material, check the applicable standing selection and exact owner decision; an admitted Artifact Type does not admit every instance of that type.

The reference library presents the standing selected in its captured source material. A source file can retain its authored candidate wording even when a later decision governs its effective standing. Preserve both facts and identify which selection is applicable.

3. Read scope, evidence and connections

Check the owner, scope, inputs, outputs, evidence references and claim ceiling (the strongest conclusion its scope and evidence support). Open the referenced material before treating it as support. For each connection, inspect its direction, ordered endpoint kinds and required qualifiers in the declared relation rule. A local relation_refs entry does not contain the complete relation assertion.

For Northstar, Work belongs to the repair Project. It does not authorize deployment, establish a repair or close the incident. The connection examples show the required membership qualifier.

Expected output and next step

Identity and format
Work specimen, exact saved JSON; Work/v1 selected.
Standing
Candidate documentation material; no acceptance decision supplied.
Scope
S1 / Northstar / status=active / E12 identifier coverage.
Permitted conclusion
The record describes investigation Work and its expected outputs. Its existence does not establish success or permission.
Next route
Use the accountable owner’s review route for proposed acceptance or changed effects.

Common failures: reading the latest-looking filename as the selected version; treating a schema pass as acceptance; relying on unresolved evidence links; reading a view as a competing authority source.
