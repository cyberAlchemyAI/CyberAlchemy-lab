Review of extension integration and the inherited semantic backlog

The current checker has accepted bounded components. The missing capability is a composed, owner-resolved qualification whose obligations correspond to the intended source meanings. Original R06/G4 are complete (35/35 tasks) and are not reopened. This review proposes new work; it executes no successor runtime or proofs.

**What the number 263 actually means.** The source ledger contains 353 obligations: 90 already discharged and 263 unsupported. The retained 90 are 82 structural signature/qualifier-shape checks (two per kind) plus eight scoped qualifier meanings across F0’s three kinds. The 263 comprise:

| Missing claim | Count | What must be supplied |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualifier meaning | 74 | Exact typed decoding, owner resolution, revision/role/frame correspondence and source mapping. |
| Operational effect | 41 | An independently defined intended effect, claim tier and checker/refinement proof. |
| Forbidden inference | 148 | Exact conclusion meaning plus scoped non-entailment countermodel; full state/closure invariants where prohibition is global. |

These are source claims, not 263 failing theorem executions or 263 relation kinds. Every kind has residual obligations, including conforms-to (5), declares-effect-on (4) and materializes-consequence (5). F0 proved eight narrow qualifier meanings; it did not prove all surrounding prose. The 38 other legacy meanings remain unsupported as complete named profiles. Later v2 maps five selected fragments and explicitly defers 36 wider meanings. Neither that map nor seven accepted extensions automatically discharges any inherited ID.

Seventeen operational-effect statements use the same generic “records only” wording. Their source questions distinguish the intended relations, but their effect text alone does not define a nonvacuous proof target. The 74 unresolved qualifier claims use 69 distinct field spellings. The 148 forbidden claims use 83 distinct label spellings; shared spelling does not guarantee shared meaning. For example, authority may mean a declared scope, effective representation, permission or an institutional role. Resolve these differences in an owner-reviewed glossary.

The E03 generic model supplies a concrete additional formalization target: positive fulfillment has an independent quantified theorem, while negative verdict selection relies on adapter well-formedness checks for request/requirement/frame. The public normalizer rejects stale frames, so this is a missing generic bridge/verdict theorem rather than evidence of a stale-frame public acceptance bug. The successor must also choose whether an explicit no evaluation may refute fulfillment when its action/output is unaccepted. See the [formal definition](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/craft/formalization/candidates/relational-theorem-checker/v3/e03/FulfillmentSemantics.lean) and [normalizer](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/craft/formalization/candidates/relational-theorem-checker/v3/e03/fulfillment_adapter.py).

**Two worked gaps.** In E00, a root revision can accompany an atom or its negation. Different definition identities prevent a same-input contradiction, but a consumer that trusts the root revision alone still does not know the owner’s AST. In E03, removing one of two criteria and recomputing the supplied source produces a different valid fulfilled snapshot. The proof is valid relative to that smaller universe; owner-required completeness is missing. The existing [boundary observations](/home/vlad_/projects/CyberAlchemy-lab/craft/formalization/candidates/relational-theorem-checker/v3/execution/reviews/2026-10-10-seven-extensions-r06-g4/boundary-observations.json) record both cases without claiming false theorem acceptance.

**The composition gap.** A yes expression, yes conformance, valid delegation path and fulfilled requirement cannot be combined unless their exact revisions, subjects, targets, scope, context, snapshot/epoch and owned policies agree. Eligibility must consume a complete independently derived qualification and actual exact review. E04 currently checks a supplied eligibility value. Add explicit cross-profile mapping/refinement and a composed eligibility theorem. Avoid requiring a future commit receipt to prove the eligibility of its own commit.

**How to close a source obligation honestly.** For delegation_ref, resolve the exact delegation and prove agent/principal/role/frame agreement, not merely that the string is nonempty. For materializes-consequence’s effect, separate consistency with a supplied receipt from evidence of actual authorized atomic execution. For “evidence support does not imply truth,” define the conclusion and construct a model in which valid supporting evidence exists while the claim is false. This proves non-entailment; it does not say the claim is always false. A no-authority inference invariant across rules requires a stronger closure/state proof.

**Completion and remaining trust.** Full inherited completion requires the exact 263 ID set discharged with owner-reviewed meanings, source refinements, current exact proofs and actual mapping/runtime evidence, while retaining the 90 earlier discharges. All newly introduced integration obligations and mandatory independent closures must also pass. A retired, narrowed or unsupported source claim stays in a separate disposition count; it cannot quietly reduce the unfinished count. Owner disagreement or missing external provenance blocks the affected claim. Formal proofs remain conditional on explicitly named external facts, clock/cryptography/host assumptions and intended-meaning review.

A valid proof and the intended definition are separate checks. The independent checker plan therefore pins both, following [Lean’s official validation guidance](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/ValidatingProofs/). Byte parsing uses [RFC 8259](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8259) as a grammar baseline with a separately defined narrower profile.
