# Artifact Lifetime: proposed definitions, types and logic

The genus is intended-lifespan classification of a canonical artifact, with exactly the currently defined ephemeral and perennial values. Ephemeral means work intended to be produced, consumed and retired; perennial means a standing reference intended to endure and be maintained. These are intended classes, not an observed survival time, regenerability classification, maturity stage or authority outcome.

Use distinct descriptive sorts LifetimeAssignment, ArtifactReference, CanonicalKind, AuthorityKind, MaturityStatus, Durability, IntendedLifespanReference, OwnerReference, OwnerNoteReference, PropositionReference, AssertionRecord, AssertionSign, AssertionUse, TimePoint, Context and Token. EPHEMERAL and PERENNIAL are distinct Durability constants. POSITIVE and NEGATIVE describe assertion-record polarity, while LIVE, HISTORICAL and QUOTED describe record use. Opposed recorded claims must not be encoded as P and logical not-P simultaneously true in a classical model: the records are data, not the model's own truth valuation.

A selected assignment binds one exact artifact reference, kind, authority classification, maturity/status, effective durability and intended-lifespan declaration. Treat canonical-artifact applicability as a separately qualified declaration rather than silently importing the whole Artifact theory. Reference declaration and nonempty handles are necessary shape guards; artifact existence, responsible-owner identity, intended lifespan and accepted canonical qualification remain separate meanings requiring owner correspondence.

Keep kind-default durability distinct from effective instance durability. The scope explicitly assigns per-kind defaults and permits an instance override with an owner note. When effective durability differs from the declared kind default, require a declared nonempty note bound to the selected artifact, responsible owner and chosen durability. The note declaration does not authenticate the owner or prove actual override authority. When default and effective durability agree, do not invent a mandatory override note.

Authority kind and maturity/status remain orthogonal to effective durability. Required witnesses should include the same coarse authority kind with both lifespan classes and the same maturity value with both classes. Also include two instances of one fine kind whose effective durability differs through the explicit owner-noted override branch. Do not impose a function from authority kind or maturity to durability merely because the default table correlates certain examples. Do not confuse a regenerable projection with an ephemeral lifespan.

Artifact's frozen partial theory currently derives its durability projection from the selected fine kind. Artifact Lifetime separately allows an effective instance override. The current sources require a precise default/effective-durability and kind-granularity bridge. Fine versus coarse kind distinctions alone do not resolve the explicit owner-note exception. Record ART-CONSUMER-FINDING-001 as unresolved; do not rewrite the frozen Artifact packet, silently assume source precedence, or claim a common-meaning model. A proposed bridge may map Artifact's field to a kind-derived default and Lifetime's field to effective durability, but actual field meaning and owner acceptance remain unproved.

Represent the live-contradiction rule through assertion records bound to artifact, proposition, time and context. A selected perennial assignment cannot have both live positive and live negative records for the same proposition at the same scoped time/context. An ephemeral assignment permits that recorded opposition; it does not require every actual ephemeral artifact to contain contradictions. Supply one hypothetical nonvacuous ephemeral witness with opposed live records and one without them to demonstrate permission without forcing contradiction.

Historical assertions, quoted resolution narratives and temporal supersession are separate record uses. A perennial append-only history can retain an earlier positive record and a later overriding negative record while only the current applicable record is live. A perennial Decision can narrate the contradiction it resolved without endorsing both sides as current live assertions. If a selected supersession declaration claims to retire the earlier record, require exact old/new artifact and proposition bindings and an explicit local order or temporal marker; do not turn those declarations into authenticated chronology or a verified actual resolution.

The selected current Durability vocabulary has two tags. The possible future append-only/monotonic third value is an owner-gated extension, not an available current constant. Unknown selected tags fail the current guards. Outside-scope assignments remain explicitly outside those selected rules. Do not silently impose a global total history order, transitive event discovery, uniqueness of artifact identities, append-only storage correctness or complete truth detection.
