## Exact binding, attribution and evidence

Every selected present assignment must:

1. Be a Classification Assignment whose exact subject revision belongs to its selected subject.
2. Use an axis declared in its exact Scheme version and a value whose selected axis/version match and whose answer is allowed there.
3. Have a named basis, a recorded attributing action, the exact selected attribution tuple and a recorded evidence-set identity.
4. Have recorded Method/Implementation references when the selected `MethodInUse`/`ImplementationInUse` conditions hold.

A named action is not proof it executed.
